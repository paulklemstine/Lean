### New Files Created

**`PhotonResearchRound4.lean`** (~310 lines, 61 declarations)

- **Photon scaling**: Preserves direction ratios and composes multiplicatively.
- **Bright/dark prime classification**: First several bright (p≡1 mod 4) and dark (p≡3 mod 4) primes verified.
- **Spin network foundations**: PhotonState' with fusion, vacuum identity, associativity, conjugation, annihilation.

**`PhotonResearchRound5.lean`** (~295 lines, 51 declarations)
Explores: Non-associative quantum gates, octonionic algebra, Cayley-Dickson hierarchy

Key new theorems:
- **Explicit octonion algebra**: Full multiplication table from Fano plane with extensionality. All 7 basis elements verified to square to -1.
- **Octonion norm multiplicativity**: 8-square identity proved from first principles via `ring`.
- **Non-associative gate theory** (key result): `oct_gates_not_composable` — Channel 4 gates CANNOT be composed as a single gate, distinguishing it fundamentally from Channels 1-3.
- **Quaternionic subalgebra IS associative**: {1,e₁,e₂,e₃} satisfies associativity, confirming Channel 3 gates compose normally.
- **Associator formalism**: Defined [x,y,z] = (xy)z - x(yz), proved it vanishes for quaternionic triples, is nonzero for octonionic triples, and is alternating.
- **Moufang identity**: Verified on basis elements, showing octonions satisfy a weaker algebraic structure.
- **Photon chirality**: Defined and proved it flips under conjugation.
- **Hurwitz dimensions**: {1,2,4,8} = powers of 2, sum to 2⁴-1, product is 2⁶, each divides the next.

### Key Discovery
The most significant new result is the formal proof that **non-associative quantum gates distinguish Channel 4 from Channel 3**: octonionic gates are path-dependent while quaternionic gates compose normally. This gives a precise algebraic characterization of what makes the octonionic channel physically different.

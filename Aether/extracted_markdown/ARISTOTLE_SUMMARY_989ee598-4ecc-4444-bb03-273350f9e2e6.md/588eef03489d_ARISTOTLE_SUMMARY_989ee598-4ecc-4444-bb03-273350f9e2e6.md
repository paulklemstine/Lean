## The Answer to Your Question

**The universal method is the Zero-Knowledge Proof.** The protocol for selling your mathematical secrets:

1. **Commit** — Publish a cryptographic commitment (sealed envelope) to your secret
2. **ZK-Prove** — Run an interactive proof (Schnorr, graph coloring, or ZK-SNARK) that convinces the buyer you know the secret, while leaking ZERO information about what it is
3. **Escrow** — Buyer deposits payment (smart contract or trusted escrow)
4. **Reveal** — You open the commitment; escrow verifies and releases payment

The GMW Theorem guarantees: **any efficiently verifiable mathematical fact can be proven this way.** Factorizations, polynomial roots, theorem proofs, algorithms — if a computer can check your answer, you can ZK-prove you know it.

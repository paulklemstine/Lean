## Core mathematical setup

For a finite index set of parties `ι`, let each party have a finite local alphabet `σ i` (for qubits, `σ i = Fin 2`). A pure multipartite state with amplitudes `ψ : (∀ i, σ i) → ℂ` can be encoded as a multivariate polynomial
\[
P_\psi(X) = \sum_{s \in \prod_i \sigma_i} \psi(s)\,\prod_i X_{i,s_i}.
\]
Since tropical methods should depend on coefficient magnitude rather than phase, define the **magnitude polynomial**
\[
|P_\psi|_{\trop}(X) = \sum_s |\psi(s)| \prod_i X_{i,s_i}.
\]
For a subset of parties \(A \subsetneq ι\), define a **bipartite tropical leaf witness**
\[
W_{\mathrm{trop}}(\psi,A)
\]
by applying the catalog leaf/tropical witness machinery to the polynomial obtained by grouping variables into the block \(A\) and its complement \(A^c\), then tropicalizing via coefficient magnitudes. The precise implementation may use a support-sum or grouped `MvPolynomial` construction, but the witness must be mathematically meaningful and computationally evaluable.

The intended philosophy is:

- **Product across a partition** ⇒ no cross-interaction in the grouped support ⇒ witness vanishes
- **Genuine multipartite entanglement** ⇒ every proper partition exhibits irreducible cross-coupling ⇒ witness should be positive in robust examples

You do **not** need to solve the full conjecture in maximal generality to make this cycle a success. But you must prove strong theorems that clearly move toward it.

## Most promising proof architecture

You asked for 2–3 proof strategy steps; here are three candidate routes. You should likely pursue Strategy A first, then use B or C to strengthen the result.

### Strategy A: Magnitude-polynomial reduction + catalog domination
**Best first path.**

1. **Realify complex amplitudes by coefficient magnitude.**  
   Define a real polynomial whose coefficients are `Complex.abs (ψ s)`. This avoids phase complications while preserving support and multiplicative factorization behavior.

2. **Group variables by partition and transport catalog witness theorems.**  
   Construct a partition polynomial so that product-across-`A` states correspond to multiplicative factorization of the grouped polynomial. Then invoke/adapt `leafWitness_le_tropicalLeafWitness`.

3. **Prove factorized support implies witness vanishing.**  
   Use the grouped factorization to show the mixed interaction term disappears. This should require multi-step `calc`, `rcases` on the product decomposition, and possibly induction over support structure.

**Why promising:**  
It leverages existing theorems directly, keeps coefficients in `ℝ`, and turns the quantum problem into a structured polynomial problem.

---

### Strategy B: Mixed-Hessian / Lorentzian route
**Best for deeper structural theorems.**

1. **Define a partitioned Hessian-like interaction form** from the grouped magnitude polynomial.
2. **Use `mixedHessianAtOnes`** as a spectral proxy for interaction across the cut.
3. **Show product states give rank-1 or block-diagonal Hessian structure**, forcing the witness to vanish.
4. **Show GHZ/W induce nontrivial off-block mixed terms**, giving strict positivity.

**Why promising:**  
This makes the “entanglement = non-factorized curvature” slogan precise and gives a strong conceptual bridge to spectral theory and hyperbolic/Lorentzian methods.

---

### Strategy C: Pure support-combinatorial route
**Best for explicit GHZ/W proofs and algorithms.**

1. **Define support projections onto `A` and `Aᶜ`.**
2. **Show product states have rectangular support**, while GHZ and W have support patterns violating this.
3. **Prove the tropical witness is positive whenever support is non-rectangular and coefficients on support are positive.**

**Why promising:**  
This may be easier for canonical examples and for building `demo.py`, even if it is weaker than a full analytic theorem.

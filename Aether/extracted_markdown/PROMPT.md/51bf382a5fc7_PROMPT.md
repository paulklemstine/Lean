## Assignment: Algebra–EML–Tropical Tropical Tannaka Reconstruction via Idempotent Fiber Functors and Closure Symmetry Semirings

**Mode:** `formalize` + `prove`

Formalize a genuinely new idempotent/tropical analogue of Tannaka reconstruction for finitely generated closure-observable categories arising in EML semantics. The central breakthrough is to reconstruct a canonical **symmetry semiring** from a semiring-linear rigid monoidal category equipped with an idempotent fiber functor into finitely generated tropical semimodules, and to show that the original category is recovered as its category of tropical representations. This is not a variant of classical Tannaka over fields: the semiring/idempotent setting destroys additive inverses, changes exactness, and forces reconstruction through closure capacities and tropical naturality constraints. If successful, this opens a new program: **categorical symmetry extraction from closure semantics**.

`Bridges/EMLTropical/TropicalTannakaReconstruction.lean`

## Core Mathematical Target

Let `S` be an idempotent commutative semiring. Let `C` be a small `S`-linear monoidal category generated by finitely many closure observables, assumed rigid in the sense that generators admit chosen dual data sufficient for tensor reconstruction. Let

`F : C ⥤ TropicalFgMod S`

be a faithful monoidal fiber functor into a category of finitely generated tropical semimodules over `S`.

Define the semiring of monoidal natural endomorphisms
\[
\mathrm{End}^{\otimes}(F)
\]
to be the semiring of natural endotransformations `η : F ⟶ F` compatible with tensorators and unitors:
\[
η_{X \otimes Y} = η_X \otimes η_Y,\qquad η_{\mathbbm{1}} = \mathrm{id}.
\]

The envisioned reconstruction statement is:

> **Tropical Tannaka Reconstruction Theorem.**  
> Assume:
> 1. `C` is small, semiring-linear, monoidal, finitely generated under tensor by a finite set of closure observables,
> 2. `F` is faithful and monoidal,
> 3. `F` is closure-separating: morphisms and closure capacities in `C` are determined by their tropical matrix realizations under `F`,
> 4. dual data on generators extend to all tensor words,
> 5. a finite compatibility condition ensures `End⊗(F)` is representable as a finitely presented symmetry semiring.
>
> Then there exists a canonical semiring `A := End⊗(F)` such that `C` is monoidally equivalent to a full tensor subcategory of tropical semiring representations of `A`, and under a stronger finite-generation/rigidity hypothesis, to `Rep_trop_fg(A)` itself.

This should be formalized first in a **strict finite skeletal model** where objects are generated by finite tensor words in a finite alphabet, and morphisms are tropical matrices satisfying explicitly encoded relations. This finite model is the mathematically decisive foothold.

## Definitions Aristotle Should Introduce

### A. Tropical finitely generated semimodules
A concrete structure for finite free semimodules with tropical linear maps is enough for the first pass:
- objects: `Fin n → S`
- morphisms: tropical matrices
- tensor: Kronecker-style tensor on finite free semimodules

This avoids the difficulty of a fully abstract semimodule category in the idempotent setting.

### B. Finite closure tensor data
A structure encoding:
- finite generators,
- tensor-word object grammar,
- generating morphisms,
- relations,
- closure capacities / observables,
- duality witnesses on generators,
- coherence data sufficient for a strict monoidal category presentation.

### C. Monoidal natural endomorphism semiring
Define elements as families `η_X : F.obj X ⟶ F.obj X` satisfying:
- naturality,
- unit law,
- tensor compatibility.

Addition and multiplication are pointwise on components; prove closure under monoidal constraints.

### D. Tropical representation category of a semiring
At finite level, an object can be:
- a finite free tropical semimodule `V`,
- a semiring morphism `ρ : A →+* End_S(V)`.

Morphisms are tropical linear maps intertwining the action.

A critical new notion. It should assert that the fiber functor distinguishes closure observables strongly enough to recover morphism relations:

## Proof Architecture: 3 Viable Strategies

### Strategy A: Finite presented direct reconstruction
**Most promising for Lean now.**

1. **Define `End⊗(F)` concretely as a subsemiring of products of tropical matrix semirings** over generator images:
   \[
   \prod_{g \in G} \mathrm{End}(F(g))
   \]
   cut out by naturality and tensor-compatibility equations.
2. **Show every object receives a canonical action** by extending from generators to tensor words.
3. **Construct the comparison functor**
   \[
   K : C \to \mathrm{Rep}_{trop}(End^{\otimes}(F))
   \]
   and prove it is fully faithful by closure-separation and essentially surjective by finite generation plus chosen presentation.

Why this is best: all data reduce to finite matrix identities, which Lean can handle via explicit structures and relation transport.

### Strategy B: Internal Yoneda/coend-style reconstruction in semiring-enriched form
1. Formalize an idempotent analogue of the coend
   \[
   A = \int^{X \in C} F(X)^\vee \otimes F(X)
   \]
   in a finite presented setting.
2. Show this coend semiring identifies with monoidal natural endomorphisms of `F`.
3. Build representations via universal dinatural maps.

Why this is conceptually profound: it is the true tropical analogue of Tannakian coend reconstruction.  
Why it is harder: coends, enriched semiring-linearity, and duality are much heavier in Lean.

### Strategy C: Closure-Koopman spectral bridge reduction
Exploit your existing verified bridge:
- `finite_spectral_reconstruction_bridge` from `Bridges/ClosureKoopmanReconstruction.lean`

1. Translate closure observables into a finite spectral reconstruction object.
2. Use the spectral object to define candidate symmetries as endomorphisms preserving closure spectra.
3. Prove equivalence with the monoidal-natural-endomorphism construction.

Why this is exciting: it ties Tannaka reconstruction to closure-Koopman reconstruction and gives a semantic/dynamical interpretation of symmetry semirings.  
Why it is secondary: likely best as a second proof or corollary after Strategy A.

**Recommendation:** execute Strategy A fully, extract a finite theorem, then formulate Strategy B/C as follow-up generalizations.

## Proof Strategy Paths

### Strategy A: Closure-Lattice Representation via Join-Irreducibles
**Most promising.**

1. Represent the finite closure system as a finite lattice of closed sets.
   Use `closure_lattice_certified_fixedpoint_capacity` to show capacities are determined by closure fixedpoints and behave well under closure reduction.

2. Recover bulk generators as join-irreducible or extremal elements detected by boundary separation.
   The boundary profile determines the closure lattice up to isomorphism if the separation axiom ensures enough probes.

3. Show the observable semiring is the idempotent semiring generated by these extremals with relations read off from gluing/submodularity.
   Then prove the reconstruction kernel has endomorphisms exactly given by these closure-preserving capacity transformations.

Why this is strongest: finite closure lattices are highly formalizable, and idempotent semiring operations often reduce to joins/meets or min/max formulas. This route gives both structural classification and algorithmic reconstruction.

---

### Strategy B: Tannaka-without-Linearity via Probe Functors
1. Define the boundary capacity functor as a probe functor from bulk systems to finite capacity-valued presheaves.
2. Show that admissible natural transformations correspond to bulk morphisms, using separation to prove faithfulness and exactness to prove fullness.
3. Construct the reconstruction kernel as a coend / universal probe object, then identify its endomorphisms with bulk observables.

This is conceptually profound and closer to the stated holographic philosophy. But it may be heavier in Lean because coends and categorical machinery over semirings can be expensive. Use it if the categorical infrastructure is already comfortable.

---

### Strategy C: Presentations by Generators and Relations from Capacity Tables
1. Given a finite capacity table, define generators indexed by primitive boundary tests or irreducible profiles.
2. Extract relations from equalities/inequalities forced by tropical submodularity and gluing.
3. Prove the presented semiring realizes the profile and is initial/minimal among realizations.

This is ideal for the certified algorithm theorem. It may be less elegant for the full-faithfulness theorem, but it is the best path to executable reconstruction and minimality bounds.

## Cross-Domain Connections You Should Exploit

### Tropical Geometry
The admissibility axioms should mirror tropical convexity:
- submodularity corresponds to tropical Plücker-style inequalities,
- gluing resembles balancing/local compatibility,
- minimal generators behave like tropical extremal rays.

This suggests the boundary profile space is a tropical moduli space of holographic boundaries.

### Tannakian / Reconstruction Theory
The statement “endomorphisms of the canonical reconstruction kernel recover the bulk semiring” is the idempotent analogue of reconstructing a group or algebra from a fiber functor. Make that analogy explicit in comments and naming.

### Mathematical Physics
Boundary observables determining the bulk is a precise algebraic shadow of holography:
- boundary test objects = probes,
- capacities = horizon/entropy-like observables,
- exactness = consistency of patching local boundary data,
- separation = enough observables to distinguish bulk states.

### EML / Closure Semantics
Closure is not an auxiliary gadget here; it is the semantics of accessible information. The bulk is what remains invariant under closure propagation, and the boundary profile measures visible closure response. This turns EML closure theory into a reconstruction mechanism.

### Semiring Morita Theory
Uniqueness up to Morita-type equivalence is the right algebraic notion when different generator presentations yield the same module theory of observables. This prevents overclaiming strict isomorphism where representation-equivalence is the natural invariant.

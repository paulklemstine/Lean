### Research Direction

**Core Conjecture (Tropical Morse Expressiveness):** The tropical Morse spectrum — the ordered sequence of critical values paired with their event types (birth, merge, cycle-death) from the weight filtration — is a strictly more expressive graph invariant than the $k$-dimensional Weisfeiler-Leman color refinement for every fixed $k$, when applied to edge-weighted graphs. Concretely: for every $k \in \mathbb{N}$, there exist pairs of finite edge-weighted graphs $G_1, G_2$ such that $G_1 \equiv_{k\text{-WL}} G_2$ but $\operatorname{TMS}(G_1) \neq \operatorname{TMS}(G_2)$.

**Precise Theorem Statements (formalize these in Lean 4):**

**Theorem 1 (Strict Expressiveness over 1-WL).** There exist finite edge-weighted graphs $G_1, G_2$ over the same vertex set such that:
- The 1-WL stable color multisets of $G_1$ and $G_2$ are identical: $\operatorname{WL}_1^*(G_1) = \operatorname{WL}_1^*(G_2)$
- The tropical Morse spectra differ: $\operatorname{TMS}(G_1) \neq \operatorname{TMS}(G_2)$

**Theorem 2 (Tropical Morse Stability — Cross-Domain: TDA ↔ Perturbation Theory).** For any two edge-weighted graphs $G_1, G_2$ on the same vertex set, if the $\ell^\infty$ distance between their edge-weight functions is at most $\varepsilon$, then the bottleneck distance between their tropical Morse spectra is at most $\varepsilon$:

**Conjecture (CFI Morse Separation).** For the Cai-Fürer-Immerman graph pair $\text{CFI}(C_n, \text{even})$ and $\text{CFI}(C_n, \text{odd})$ constructed from an $n$-cycle base graph with uniform edge weight 1, the tropical Morse spectra differ in exactly one event: the $H_1$ barcode corresponding to the central parity cycle has death time $n$ in the "even" variant and $n+1$ in the "odd" variant (after appropriate weight assignment from the gadget structure).

If they don't differ, or differ in more than one bar, the conjecture is falsified.

**Proof Strategies:**

**Strategy A (Direct CFI Construction + Homology Computation) — Most Promising.**
1. Construct explicit CFI graph pairs with edge weights derived from the gadget structure. Prove that 1-WL cannot distinguish them by showing the color refinement yields identical multisets (this follows from the known CFI symmetry properties).
2. Compute the $H_1$ persistent homology of both graphs through the weight filtration. The "even" CFI graph has one additional independent cycle compared to the "odd" variant (this is the fundamental parity distinction CFI captures).
3. Building on catalog's `critical_iff_topology_jump` (from `TropicalMorseGraphs.lean`), show that the critical value at which this extra cycle dies differs between the two graphs, yielding different tropical Morse spectra.
4. **Why most promising:** Directly leverages the verified `computeFiltration` and `critical_iff_topology_jump` from the catalog, and the CFI construction is well-understood combinatorially.

**Strategy B (Reduction to Known TDA Expressiveness Results).**
1. Use known results that persistent homology barcodes can distinguish certain graph families that WL cannot (e.g., results of Hofer et al. 2020 on WL vs. TDA expressiveness).
2. Prove that the tropical Morse spectrum is a complete invariant for the persistent homology barcode (Theorem 3 above), so any graph pair distinguished by barcodes is also distinguished by TMS.
3. Compose: TMS determines barcodes → barcodes exceed WL → TMS exceeds WL.
4. **Risk:** The known TDA-WL separation results may require unweighted graphs or specific weight regimes not covered by the tropical Morse framework.

**Strategy C (Information-Theoretic via Tropical Data Processing Inequality).**
1. Define the Shannon entropy of the WL color distribution and the tropical Morse entropy.
2. Prove a data processing inequality: the WL refinement is a lossy compression of the tropical Morse spectrum, i.e., $I(\text{Graph}; \text{TMS}) \geq I(\text{Graph}; \text{WL})$ for any distribution over graphs.
3. Show strict inequality by exhibiting the CFI pair as a counterexample to equality.
4. **Risk:** Information-theoretic framework is hard to formalize cleanly in Lean without significant probability theory infrastructure.

**Cross-Domain Connections:**

1. **Tropical Geometry → Statistical Mechanics**: The weight filtration is isomorphic to the temperature-driven percolation process on a graph. Critical values in the tropical Morse spectrum correspond to phase transition temperatures where the connectivity structure changes qualitatively. This connects to the Potts model: the tropical Morse spectrum encodes the sequence of percolation transitions, and the WL test is blind to these global phase transitions because it only sees local neighborhoods.

2. **Tropical Geometry → Information Theory**: The tropical Morse spectrum satisfies a data processing inequality: WL refinement is a lossy channel that cannot increase mutual information with the graph identity. The persistent homology barcodes are sufficient statistics for the graph's topological type, and WL discards this information. Formalize: $I(G; \text{TMS}(G)) \geq I(G; \text{WL}(G))$ with strict inequality for the CFI distribution.

3. **Algebraic Topology → Machine Learning**: Theorem 2 (stability) is precisely what makes tropical Morse features suitable for gradient-based learning: small weight perturbations (e.g., from SGD updates to edge-weight predictors) produce small changes in the feature, enabling smooth optimization landscapes.

**Impact and Revolutionary Significance:**

This work would establish the **first provably complete topological feature for graph neural networks** that exceeds the Weisfeiler-Leman hierarchy. Current message-passing GNNs (GCN, GAT, GraphSAGE) are bounded by 1-WL expressiveness. The tropical Morse spectrum provides:
- **Theoretical guarantee**: Provably captures structural information invisible to all k-WL tests for fixed k.
- **Practical computation**: O(|E| log |E|) via the verified `computeFiltration` algorithm — feasible as a preprocessing step for real datasets.
- **Stability**: Small input perturbations → small feature changes (Theorem 2), enabling robust learning.
- **Differentiability**: The tropical Morse spectrum is piecewise-constant in edge weights, but its persistent homology barcode is locally differentiable almost everywhere, enabling end-to-end training.

This opens the field of **Tropical Topological Deep Learning**: GNN architectures that use verified tropical Morse features as structural inductive biases, with formal guarantees on expressiveness and stability.

## Assignment: P-adic Orbital Period Valuation — Arithmetic Skeletons of Keplerian Dynamics

### The Vision

Kepler's third law $T^2 \mu = 4\pi^2 a^3$ is an algebraic relation among orbital parameters. When $a, \mu \in \mathbb{Q}_{>0}$, this equation becomes a **Diophantine constraint**: the period ratio $a^{3/2}/\mu^{1/2}$ lives in $\mathbb{Q}$ precisely when $a^3/\mu$ is a perfect square, which is detected p-locally by parity of the valuation. The p-adic valuations $v_p(a^{3/2}/\mu^{1/2})$ for all primes $p$ form an **arithmetic fingerprint** of the orbit — an element of the idele group $\mathbb{A}_\mathbb{Q}^*$ that classifies the orbit's arithmetic type. The tropical curve of the Kepler variety over $\mathbb{Q}_p$ is the geometric incarnation of this fingerprint: its vertex depths *are* the p-adic valuations.

This would establish the first **arithmetic invariant of a dynamical system derived from tropical geometry**, opening "adelic celestial mechanics" — the study of orbits via their local arithmetic shadows at every prime.

### Proof Strategies

**Strategy A — Direct Valuation Homomorphism (RECOMMENDED for Theorems 1–2)**:
1. Start from `hkepler : q² · μ = a³`
2. Apply `padicValRat.mul` to get `padicValRat p (q² · μ) = padicValRat p (a³)`
3. Apply `padicValRat.pow` to both sides: `2 · padicValRat p q + padicValRat p μ = 3 · padicValRat p a`
4. Linear arithmetic gives `2 · padicValRat p q = 3 · padicValRat p a - padicValRat p μ`
5. *Most promising* because it uses only `padicValRat` properties already in Mathlib. Key lemma needed: clean handling of `padicValRat` for products and powers of positive rationals (the sign case is trivial since everything is positive).

**Strategy B — Prime Factorization Reconstruction (for Theorem 3 backward direction)**:
1. Given: `3 · v_p(a) - v_p(μ)` is even for all primes p
2. Write `a = ∏ p^v_p(a)` and `μ = ∏ p^v_p(μ)` (prime factorization)
3. Define `q = ∏ p^((3·v_p(a) - v_p(μ))/2)` — this is well-defined because the exponent is an integer
4. Verify `q² · μ = a³` by computing valuations at each prime
5. *Key difficulty*: constructing the prime factorization in Lean requires `Nat.factorization` and careful bookkeeping. Use `Finset` of primes with nonzero valuation for finiteness.

**Strategy C — Newton Polygon / Tropical Approach (for Theorem 5, conceptual)**:
1. The Kepler equation `T²μ = c·a³` defines a hypersurface with Newton polytope = convex hull of {(2,1,0), (0,0,3)}
2. The tropicalization over $\mathbb{Q}_p$ is the corner locus of `max(2X+Y, v_p(c)+3Z)`
3. The vertex sits where `2X+Y = v_p(c)+3Z` — this IS the valuation balance equation
4. The "depth" at the vertex = `v_p(c)+3Z = 2X+Y` = p-adic valuation of either monomial
5. *Hardest to formalize* but gives the deepest geometric insight. Requires tropical geometry infrastructure not yet in Mathlib. Build minimal ad-hoc definitions for the Kepler case only.

### Cross-Domain Connections

1. **Number Theory ↔ Celestial Mechanics**: Theorem 3 establishes that a Kepler orbit has rational period ratio iff its p-adic valuation profile satisfies a parity condition at every prime. This is the **Hasse principle** for the Diophantine equation $q^2 = a^3/\mu$. The Kepler period is a "local-global" phenomenon.

2. **P-adic Analysis ↔ Tropical Geometry**: The tropical curve of the Kepler variety over $\mathbb{Q}_p$ is a 1-dimensional polyhedral fan whose vertex depth equals $v_p$ of the defining monomials. This is the **Fundamental Theorem of Tropical Geometry** (Sturmfels–Speyer) instantiated for a specific celestial-mechanical variety. The tropical skeleton IS the p-adic skeleton.

3. **Arithmetic Dynamics ↔ Idelic Class Field Theory**: The `PadicOrbitalInvariant` maps Kepler orbits to elements of the idele class group $\mathbb{A}_\mathbb{Q}^*/\mathbb{Q}^*$. By class field theory, this group is isomorphic to $\text{Gal}(\mathbb{Q}^{ab}/\mathbb{Q})$. *Speculative*: could the arithmetic type of a Kepler orbit predict the splitting behavior of primes in abelian extensions?

4. **Quantum Mechanics ↔ P-adic Physics**: In the Bohr model, orbital energies satisfy $E_n = -13.6/n^2$ eV. The p-adic valuations $v_p(E_n)$ give arithmetic fingerprints of quantum states. The Kepler valuation formula directly applies to these quantized energies, opening **p-adic spectral theory** of hydrogen-like atoms.

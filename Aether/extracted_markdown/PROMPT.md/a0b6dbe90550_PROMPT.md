## Main Theorem A: Finite Closure Kramers–Wannier Anti-Equivalence

### Mathematical statement

Let \(X\) be finite. Let \(\operatorname{cl}\) be a closure operator on \(X\). Let \(E\) assign finite idempotent local energies to a finite generating family of closed sets. Assume:

1. **finite generation**: every closed set is generated by a finite subfamily of designated generators;
2. **cocircuit separation**: distinct admissible partition sections are separated by closure cocircuits / dual test sections;
3. **admissibility**: the partition semimodule is closed under min-plus combination and scalar shift.

Then the tropical Legendre transform
\[
\mathcal L : P(\operatorname{cl},E) \to P^\ast(\operatorname{cl},E)
\]
is order-reversing, min-plus linear up to gauge, and induces an anti-equivalence between the thermodynamically admissible primal and dual partition semimodules. Moreover the bidual map
\[
\mathcal L^\ast \mathcal L : P(\operatorname{cl},E)_{\mathrm{adm}} \to P(\operatorname{cl},E)_{\mathrm{adm}}
\]
is an isomorphism modulo gauge, and an actual isomorphism after normalized gauge fixing.

### Suggested Lean 4 type signature

```lean
theorem finite_closure_kramers_wannier_duality
  {α : Type} [Fintype α] [DecidableEq α]
  (C : ClosureInteractionStructure α)
  (hfg : C.FinitelyGenerated)
  (hsep : C.CocircuitSeparating)
  (hadm : C.ThermoAdmissibleNonempty) :
  ∃ (L : C.PartitionSemimodule → C.DualPartitionSemimodule),
    C.IsTropicalLegendre L ∧
    OrderIso (C.NormalizedThermoAdmissible) (OrderDual C.DualNormalizedThermoAdmissible)
```

If `OrderIso` is too rigid early on, first prove a pair of inverse maps on normalized admissible subtypes:

```lean
theorem finite_closure_kramers_wannier_duality_normalized
  {α : Type} [Fintype α] [DecidableEq α]
  (C : ClosureInteractionStructure α)
  (hfg : C.FinitelyGenerated)
  (hsep : C.CocircuitSeparating)
  (hadm : C.ThermoAdmissibleNonempty) :
  ∃ L : C.PartitionSemimodule → C.DualPartitionSemimodule,
    C.IsTropicalLegendre L ∧
    Function.Bijective
      (fun p : C.NormalizedThermoAdmissible => C.normalizeDual (L p.1))
```

## Main Theorem B: Bidual Recovery on Thermodynamically Admissible Sections

### Mathematical statement

For every admissible partition section \(p\), the tropical bidual satisfies
\[
p^{\ast\ast} = p
\]
after gauge normalization, and without normalization one has gauge equivalence:
\[
p^{\ast\ast} \sim_{\mathrm{gauge}} p.
\]

This is the finite idempotent analogue of Fenchel–Moreau biconjugation, but specialized to closure-compatible partition data and separated by cocircuits.

### Suggested Lean 4 type signature

```lean
theorem tropical_bidual_recovers_admissible_section
  {α : Type} [Fintype α] [DecidableEq α]
  (C : ClosureInteractionStructure α)
  (hfg : C.FinitelyGenerated)
  (hsep : C.CocircuitSeparating) :
  ∀ p : C.PartitionSemimodule,
    p ∈ C.thermoAdmissible →
      C.GaugeEquivalent (C.tropicalBidual p) p
```

Normalized version:

```lean
theorem tropical_bidual_recovers_normalized_admissible_section
  {α : Type} [Fintype α] [DecidableEq α]
  (C : ClosureInteractionStructure α)
  (hfg : C.FinitelyGenerated)
  (hsep : C.CocircuitSeparating) :
```

## Main Theorem C: Certified Gibbs Reconstruction from Boundary Partition Data

### Mathematical statement

Given a finite separated closure interaction system and a boundary partition functional \(B\) compatible with the primal partition semimodule, there exists a computable reconstruction of:

1. a dual coupling hypergraph \(H^\vee\),
2. dual local couplings / effective Gibbs weights \(w^\vee\),
3. a gauge normalization \(g\),

such that the reconstructed dual partition functional agrees with the original boundary functional on all admissible boundary tests, up to gauge before normalization and exactly after normalization.

This theorem should be stated in a way Lean can support: existence of reconstructed data plus a correctness theorem.

### Suggested Lean 4 type signature

```lean
theorem certified_gibbs_reconstruction_from_boundary_partition
  {α : Type} [Fintype α] [DecidableEq α]
  (C : ClosureInteractionStructure α)
  (hfg : C.FinitelyGenerated)
  (hsep : C.CocircuitSeparating)
  (B : C.BoundaryPartitionFunctional)
  (hB : C.BoundaryCompatible B) :
  ∃ R : C.DualReconstruction,
    R.Certified ∧
    C.GaugeEquivalentFunctional (R.realizedBoundaryFunctional) B ∧
    C.NormalizedFunctional (R.normalizedBoundaryFunctional) = C.normalizeBoundary B
```

## Proof Strategy Architecture

You must pursue at least two proof paths in parallel and choose one as the main line.

### Strategy A: Tropical Fenchel–Moreau on finite closure semimodules
Most promising.

1. **Finite presentation of partition sections**  
   Use `certified_finite_tropical_decomposition` to show every admissible section is a finite min-plus combination of generator energies indexed by closed sets or irreducibles.

2. **Define dual cocircuit pairing and Legendre transform**  
   Introduce a finite pairing
   \[
   \langle p, q^\vee\rangle
   \]
   between primal sections and cocircuit sections, then define
   \[
   \mathcal L(p)(q^\vee)=\inf_s(\langle s,q^\vee\rangle - p(s))
   \]
   in tropical/min-plus form. In Lean, make this a finite `Finset.inf` / `sInf` over admissible test generators.

3. **Prove finite biconjugation under separation**  
   Separation by cocircuits should imply the dual test family is rich enough to recover the original section. This is the finite idempotent Fenchel–Moreau theorem specialized to closure-compatible supports.

4. **Normalize gauge and obtain anti-equivalence**  
   Gauge equivalence is likely “adding a constant everywhere.” Quotient by gauge or fix normalization (e.g. minimum value zero on a chosen basepoint). Then bidual becomes literal equality.

Why this is most promising: it aligns perfectly with the tropical decomposition theorem already in the catalog and keeps everything finite, combinatorial, and Lean-friendly.

1. **Construct a polarity between closed generators and cocircuits**

2. **Induce a Galois anti-connection**

4. **Prove uniqueness up to gauge**  
   Separation should imply that if two reconstructed duals produce the same boundary partition table, they differ only by gauge.

Why this matters: this is the theorem that converts conceptual duality into a certified finite algorithm.

## Key Definitions to Make Precise

You should define these carefully and minimally.

### Closure interaction structure
A finite type `α` with:
- a closure operator on `Set α`,
- a finite family of closed generators,
- a local energy assignment on generators,
- compatibility axioms ensuring global sections are determined by generator data.

### Partition semimodule
A type of closure-compatible energy sections:
- min-plus addition,
- scalar shift by `ℤ`, `ℚ`, or another ordered additive type,
- finite support / generator compatibility.

### Cocircuit separation
A finite family of dual tests such that distinct normalized admissible sections are distinguished pointwise by at least one cocircuit. This is the exact dual analogue of a separating family of linear functionals.

### Gauge equivalence
Two partition sections are gauge-equivalent if they differ by a global additive constant, or by the exact normalization group appropriate to your model. Keep this simple in the first pass.

### Thermodynamic admissibility
Likely lower-semicontinuity is irrelevant in the finite setting; instead use:
- closure compatibility,
- finite decomposition,
- boundedness/nontriviality,
- normalization feasibility.

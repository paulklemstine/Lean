## Proof strategy architecture

### Strategy 1: Hypothesis-driven transfer theorem (most promising)
1. **Abstract the random matrix input** into a hypothesis about operator norm tails:
   \[
   \mathbb P(\|E\|_{\mathrm{op}} \ge 2\sigma + \delta) \le \Phi_n(\delta).
   \]
   This avoids getting blocked by full GOE measure formalization.
2. **Use `failure_event_subset_gap_event`** to prove
   \[
   \mathbb P(\text{misclassification}) \le \mathbb P(\|E\|_{\mathrm{op}} \ge \varepsilon).
   \]
3. **Substitute the edge scaling**
   \[
   t = \frac{(\varepsilon - 2\sigma)n^{2/3}}{\sigma}
   \]
   and simplify by `field_simp`, positivity lemmas, and `calc`.
4. Deduce both the Tracy–Widom-style bound and a relaxed engineering exponential bound.

**Why this is most promising:** it leverages existing catalog theorems immediately and produces a genuine theorem even if the deepest probability formalization remains externalized as an assumption.

### Strategy 2: Finite-\(n\) concentration route via operator norm inequalities
1. Prove or import a finite-dimensional Gaussian matrix concentration bound of the form
   \[
   \mathbb P(\|E\|_{\mathrm{op}} \ge 2\sigma + u) \le e^{-cnu^2/\sigma^2}.

2. Combine this with the gap-event transfer theorem.
3. Show that the resulting bound matches the conjectured engineering law
   \[
   \exp\!\left(-\frac{(\varepsilon-2\sigma)_+^2 n}{C\sigma^2}\right).

**Why this is valuable:** it gives a non-asymptotic theorem with explicit constants, even before Tracy–Widom tails are formalized.

### Proof strategy: concrete route

#### Strategy A: discrete support via singleton masses, then KME separation
This is the most promising route for a complete Lean proof now.

1. **Define discrete support by singleton nonvanishing**:
   ```lean
   def MaxitiveMeasure.suppDiscrete ...
   ```
   Then `x ∉ suppDiscrete μ ↔ μ {x} = 0` is immediate.

2. **Build singleton witnesses**:
   Construct `φx : X → ℝ` with `φx x = 1` and `φx y = 0` for `y ≠ x`, or the codomain variant best adapted to the tropical integral. Prove:
   ```lean
   tropicalIntegral μ φx = μ {x}
   ```
   or at least that it determines `μ {x}` by an existing witness-reconstruction theorem.

3. **Transfer KME equality to equality of witness integrals**:
   Use the existing idempotent RKHS reconstruction theorem to show:
   ```lean
   kernelMeanEmbedding μ = kernelMeanEmbedding ν →
   tropicalIntegral μ φ = tropicalIntegral ν φ
   ```
   for all witness functions `φ` in the admissible family.

4. **Deduce support equality pointwise**:
   For each `x`, if `x ∉ suppDiscrete μ`, then `μ {x} = 0`; via the singleton witness and KME equality infer `ν {x} = 0`; hence `x ∉ suppDiscrete ν`. Symmetry gives equality.

5. **Deduce full measure equality**:
   Under Dirac separation, recover all singleton masses. Then use finite maxitivity and decomposition of any set into finite union of singletons to conclude `μ = ν`.

This strategy is algebraically clean, computationally explicit, and likely aligns best with the finite-projective dual separation already in the library.

#### Strategy B: witness-duality first, support reconstruction second
This is conceptually deeper and prepares the zero-dimensional compact extension.

1. Define support topologically:
   ```lean
   x ∈ supp μ ↔ ∀ open s, x ∈ s → μ s ≠ 0
   ```
2. Prove the clopen-witness characterization in zero-dimensional compact spaces:
   `x ∉ supp μ ↔ ∃ clopen s, x ∈ s ∧ μ s = 0`.
3. Convert clopen sets to tropical witness functions using the support-reconstruction infrastructure from idempotent functionals.
4. Use equality of KMEs to show equality on all such witnesses, hence equality of support.
5. Restrict to finite discrete spaces to recover point masses and then the whole measure.

This is the route that most clearly opens the Stonean extension and the support-duality program, but it may require more topology API work.

#### Strategy C: finite-projective duality / extensionality through basis coordinates
Use the tropical Serre–Swan / finite-projective dual separation results as a coordinate theorem.

1. Show that on finite `X`, the feature family generated by singleton-separating witnesses is finite-projective.
2. Realize `kernelMeanEmbedding μ` as a coordinate vector of evaluations on a separating dual basis.
3. Prove coordinate equality implies singleton-mass equality.
4. Conclude by extensionality of finite maxitive measures.

This may give the strongest conceptual theorem—KME as a full dual coordinate system—but depends more heavily on exact existing algebraic APIs.

### Why this matters to the research program

This theorem is the missing identifiability bridge between three infrastructures that were previously parallel:

1. **Tropical kernel mean embeddings** gave a representation of maxitive measures in an idempotent Hilbert/RKHS-like world.
2. **Support duality for idempotent functionals** gave geometric reconstruction of supports from vanishing/witness data.
3. **Finite-projective dual separation** gave algebraic separation of points by dual coordinates.

What you are proving is that these are not just analogous stories—they are the same reconstruction mechanism seen from analytic, geometric, and algebraic sides.

Concretely:
- `kme_eq_imp_support_eq` says the embedding is not merely feature-extractive; it is support-faithful.
- `kme_eq_imp_measure_eq_of_dirac_separating` says that under a sharp enough kernel, the tropical KME is fully identifiable, not just up to coarse geometry.
- The clopen/zero-dimensional extension points toward a Stone-dual theory of idempotent probability, where supports are recovered from clopen spectra and KMEs become functorial avatars of maxitive measures.

This opens several new directions immediately:
- algorithmic reconstruction of sparse maxitive measures from finite witness coordinates;
- tropical analogues of characteristic kernels and MMD-type distances;
- categorical equivalences between maxitive measures, idempotent functionals, and tropicalized dual modules;
- support learning for morphological / max-plus / tropical neural models.

In other words: this is the theorem that upgrades the existing pipeline from “representation theory” to “inverse theory.”

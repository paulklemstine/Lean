## Assignment: Connect Tropical Width Collapse to Cycle-Mean Rigidity

**Mode:** prove

Prove a genuinely new theorem that turns tropical spectral theory into a **rigidity principle**: for an irreducible max-plus matrix, the normalized tropical eigenspace collapses to width zero exactly when the cycle geometry is spectrally flat. This should be treated as the tropical analogue of the classical phenomenon “simple dominant eigenvalue forces projective contraction to a unique eigenline,” but stated in a form that is both formalizable in Lean 4 and strong enough to seed a new theory of **tropical spectral gaps, synchronization, and zeta-type invariants**.

Your target is not a routine variant of Perron–Frobenius. The breakthrough is to identify a **combinatorial certificate** for collapse of tropical eigenvector width in terms of **cycle means**, and to make that certificate machine-checkable.

## Precise Theorem Statement

Work over a finite index type `Fin n` with weights in `ℝ`, viewed in max-plus convention. Let `A : Fin n → Fin n → ℝ`. Define the tropical action
\[
(A \odot x)_i := \max_j (A i j + x_j).
\]
A tropical eigenpair is `(λ,x)` such that
\[
A \odot x = λ + x.
\]

Define the **width** of a vector by
\[
\operatorname{width}(x) := \max_i x_i - \min_i x_i.
\]
Say a tropical eigenvector has **width zero** if all coordinates are equal.

Define the mean weight of a directed cycle \(c = (i_0,\dots,i_{k-1},i_0)\) by
\[
\mu(c) := \frac{1}{k}\sum_{t=0}^{k-1} A(i_t,i_{t+1}).
\]
Let `cycleMeans A` denote the set of all cycle means of cycles in the support graph of `A`.

### Main theorem to formalize
For an irreducible matrix `A`, the following are equivalent:

1. There exists a tropical eigenvector `x` of `A` with `width x = 0`.
2. Every directed cycle of `A` has the same mean weight.
3. `A` is tropically cohomologous to a constant matrix in the sense that there exists `λ : ℝ` and `p : Fin n → ℝ` with
   \[
   A i j = λ + p_i - p_j
   \]
   for every edge in the support graph (or for all `i,j` in the fully weighted case).

This is the real theorem. The cycle-mean equality is not merely a spectral curiosity; it is the exact obstruction to projective width collapse.

## Proof Strategy Architecture

### Strategy A: Direct eigenvector rigidity via constant vectors and cycle summation
**Most promising for Lean.**

1. **Width zero implies constant eigenvector.**  
   Prove `width x = 0 → x = const c`. Then the eigenvector equation becomes
   \[
   \max_j A i j = λ \quad \forall i.
   \]
   This already forces row maxima to equal `λ`.

2. **Use cycle inequalities plus irreducibility to force equality of all cycle means.**  
   From tropical Collatz–Wielandt-type inequalities, any cycle mean is bounded by the eigenvalue. Show that if `x` is constant, the maximizing structure propagates through strong connectivity so every cycle mean must equal `λ`, not just be ≤ `λ`. The crucial move is to telescope along cycles and use irreducibility to compare all nodes.

3. **Conversely, equal cycle means imply a coboundary decomposition.**  
   Fix a base vertex `r`. Define a potential `p i` as the weight defect of any path from `r` to `i` relative to `λ · length`. Equal cycle means guarantees path-independence, because two paths differ by a cycle whose normalized excess is zero. Then prove
   \[
   A i j = λ + p_i - p_j.
   \]
   Setting `x_i := -p_i` yields a tropical eigenvector; after normalization, width zero follows precisely when `p` is constant, or in the fully balanced gauge the constant vector itself is an eigenvector.

**Why best:** it reduces the theorem to finite sums, telescoping, and path-independence — all highly formalizable.

---

### Strategy B: Karp–Collatz–Wielandt synthesis
**Conceptually deeper; good if you want a field-opening theorem statement.**

1. Build on `tropical_spectral_radius_le_eigenvalue` and `tropical_spectral_bound` to identify the tropical eigenvalue with the common cycle mean under the rigidity hypothesis.

2. Formalize a finite-dimensional Collatz–Wielandt principle:
   \[
   \rho(A)=\inf_x \max_i \big((A\odot x)_i-x_i\big)
   =\sup_x \min_i \big((A\odot x)_i-x_i\big).
   \]
   Then show width zero means the optimizer is attained at a constant vector.

3. Use Karp’s theorem to convert this optimizer statement into a statement that **all cycle means coincide**. Conversely, if all cycle means coincide, the inf-sup collapse occurs at a constant vector, yielding width-zero eigenvectors.

**Why powerful:** this turns the theorem into a tropical variational principle with immediate optimization consequences.

**Why harder in Lean:** limits, asymptotics of powers, and Karp’s theorem are substantial formal overhead unless you restrict to finite combinatorial versions.

---

### Strategy C: Graph cohomology / 1-cocycle vanishing
**Most visionary cross-domain route.**

1. Regard edge weights after subtracting `λ` as a 1-cochain on the strongly connected digraph.
2. “All cycle means equal `λ`” becomes “the reduced edge-weight cocycle integrates to zero around every directed cycle.”
3. By finite graph exactness, conclude the cochain is exact:
   \[
   A i j - λ = p_i - p_j.
   \]
4. Convert exactness into a tropical eigenvector by gauge transformation.

**Why exciting:** this reframes tropical spectral theory as discrete Hodge/cohomology.  
**Why useful:** it could seed a library of tropical gauge transformations and graph potentials.  
**Why risky:** you may need to build graph cohomology infrastructure from scratch.

## Cross-Domain Connections You Should Explicitly Leverage

### Optimization / Mean-Payoff Games
Equal cycle means correspond to a degenerate regime in which every recurrent strategy has the same asymptotic payoff. Width-zero eigenvectors then encode **global fairness / synchronization** in the dynamic programming operator. This is not just algebra — it is a theorem about **policy-indifference**.

### Dynamical Systems / Nonlinear Perron–Frobenius
Normalized tropical iteration
\[
x_{k+1} = A \odot x_k - \lambda \mathbf{1}
\]
has fixed points equal to tropical eigenvectors modulo constants. Width collapse means the projective dynamics converges to a single synchronization class. This is the tropical analogue of **complete mode locking**.

### Graph Cohomology / Gauge Theory
The representation
\[
A i j = λ + p_i - p_j
\]
is a discrete gauge trivialization. Equal cycle means means curvature vanishes. This imports language from **flat connections** into tropical spectral theory.

### Tropical Zeta / RH-Inspired Direction
If cycle means are the tropical spectral parameters attached to periodic orbits, then the equal-cycle-mean regime is the zero-curvature case where a tropical zeta function should exhibit a single dominant slope. Your theorem would provide the first rigorous “spectral collapse” input for such a theory.

### Scheduling / Discrete Event Systems
In max-plus systems, cycle means determine throughput. Equality of all cycle means is a perfect balance condition. Width-zero eigenvectors correspond to **uniform phase alignment** across tasks.

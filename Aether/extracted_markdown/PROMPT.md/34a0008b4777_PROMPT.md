## Proof Architecture: 3 Viable Strategies

### Strategy 1: Direct finite closure-to-profile-to-extremal route
This is the most promising first route.

1. **Compress through closed sets.**  
   Use `ClosureCapacity.ext'` and closed-set invariance to show every profile factors through the finite lattice of closed sets. This turns the problem into finite combinatorics on a closure lattice rather than arbitrary subsets.

2. **Generate principal profiles and prove minimality by separation.**  
   Show the semimodule is generated by finitely many principal profiles attached to singleton closures or join-irreducible closed sets. Use the closure-separation axiom to prove distinct skeleton pieces yield distinct extremal rays.

3. **Recover closure from tropical incidence.**  
   Define a relation
   \[
   x \preceq_{\mathrm{trop}} X \iff p_x \in \operatorname{TropSpan}(\{p_y : y\in X\})
   \]
   and prove this equals \(x \in cl(X)\). Then reconstruct the closure operator from this relation and certify correctness.

**Why this is most promising:** it uses finite closure-lattice combinatorics already close to catalog infrastructure and avoids premature category theory. It also yields a computational reconstruction procedure naturally.

---

### Strategy 2: Lattice-theoretic / join-irreducible approach
This route is elegant and may produce the cleanest theorem statement.

1. **Pass to the finite lattice of closed sets.**  
   Identify minimal generators with join-irreducible closed sets or a suitable irreducible basis.

2. **Push valuation through the lattice structure.**  
   Show that idempotent additivity of `cap` on independent joins becomes tropical addition on the image of join-irreducibles.

3. **Use irreducible decomposition to identify extremals.**  
   Prove extremal tropical rays correspond exactly to irreducible closed-set generators, so the skeleton is the tropicalized Hasse diagram / irreducible spine of the closure lattice.

**Why this is powerful:** it reveals the theorem as a finite Stone/Birkhoff-type duality in disguise. If successful, it opens connections to formal concept analysis, antimatroids, matroids, and distributive lattice representation.

---

### Strategy 3: Categorical essential-image proof
This route is more ambitious and should probably come after Strategy 1.

1. **Define the source and target categories.**  
   Morphisms on the closure side should preserve closure and capacity compatibly with valuation; morphisms on the tropical side should preserve semimodule structure and extremals.

2. **Construct the functor and prove full faithfulness on separated objects.**  
   Use the reconstruction theorem to show a tropical semimodule morphism induces a closure-system morphism.

3. **Prove essential surjectivity by reconstruction.**  
   Every separated finitely generated tropical information semimodule in the chosen image class comes from a closure-capacity system.

**Why this matters:** this elevates the result from a reconstruction theorem to a bona fide duality principle. It is the right endpoint, but not the first tactical target.

structure IsClosureOperator {α : Type*} (cl : Set α → Set α) : Prop where
  idempotent : ∀ s, cl (cl s) = cl s
  monotone : ∀ ⦃s t : Set α⦄, s ⊆ t → cl s ⊆ cl t
  extensive : ∀ s, s ⊆ cl s

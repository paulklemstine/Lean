## Precise Mathematical Objective

Let:
- `Σ` be a finite alphabet type,
- `S` be a commutative idempotent semiring with tropical order flavor,
- `f : List Σ → S` be a recognizable series, given by a finite linear representation.

Define the Nerode equivalence
\[
x \sim_f y \iff \forall z,\ f(x ++ z)=f(y ++ z).
\]

You should prove a theorem package with four layers:

### Theorem A: Tropical Nerode congruence is canonical and semiring-compatible
Show that the behavioral equivalence induced by suffix testing extends from words to the free `S`-semimodule on words as a semiring-compatible congruence, and is right-invariant under concatenation.

A precise mathematical target is:

\[
\forall x y,\ x \sim_f y \iff \forall z,\ f(xz)=f(yz),
\]
and this relation is:
1. an equivalence relation,
2. right-invariant: \(x \sim_f y \Rightarrow xu \sim_f yu\),
3. compatible with finite `S`-linear combinations in the free semimodule of formal tropical series.

This is the algebraic heart: the quotient is not just a set-theoretic partition but a **semiring congruence object**.

---

### Theorem B: Quotient realizes the canonical minimal tropical automaton
Assume `f` is recognizable by a finite weighted automaton / finite linear representation. Then the quotient by the induced Nerode congruence yields a canonical automaton recognizing `f`, and this automaton is minimal among all finite realizations of `f`.

Formally: every other finite realization of `f` admits a quotient map onto the Nerode quotient state space, so the quotient state count is minimal.

This is the tropical analogue of the classical universal property of the minimal DFA, but in the weighted/idempotent setting.

---

### Theorem C: Minimal number of states equals certified tropical factor rank of a finite Hankel block
Under a **finite-support Hankel generation hypothesis**—namely, there exist finite witness sets of prefixes `P` and suffixes `Q` generating all residual behaviors relevant to `f`—prove that the minimal quotient cardinality equals the tropical factor rank of the finite Hankel block
\[
H_{P,Q}(p,q)=f(pq).
\]

This is the real breakthrough statement:
\[
\#(\text{minimal tropical automaton states}) = \operatorname{tropFactorRank}(H_{P,Q}),
\]
provided `P,Q` are certified witness sets generating all residual classes.

You are not merely bounding state complexity by rank; you are proving **exact equality under a finite certificate hypothesis** formalized in Lean.

---

### Theorem D: Certified finite minimization algorithm
Extract a computable theorem: given finite witness sets `P`, `Q`, and a certificate that they generate all residuals / Hankel behaviors, one can compute:
1. generators for the Nerode congruence,
2. the quotient automaton,
3. a proof that this quotient is minimal,
4. a proof that its state count equals the certified tropical factor rank of the finite Hankel block.

This should culminate in a Lean theorem with algorithmic content, not just an existence theorem.

## Proof Architecture: 3 Viable Strategies

### Strategy 1: Quotient-first universal-property proof
**Most promising.**

1. **Lift residual behavior to a quotient object.**
   Define the residual map
   \[
   x \mapsto (z \mapsto f(xz)).
   \]
   Show two words are equivalent iff they have identical residual series. This gives the quotient state space as the image of the residual map.

2. **Use catalog quotient existence theorem as a launchpad.**
   Starting from `tropical_myhill_nerode_quotient_exists`, strengthen it to show:
   - induced transitions are well-defined by right invariance,
   - output is well-defined,
   - every recognizing automaton factors through the quotient by sending a state to its residual behavior.

3. **Prove minimality via factorization.**
   Any finite realization determines only finitely many residual classes, so the Nerode quotient injects into the set of reachable residual states or receives a surjective map from any realization. This gives cardinal minimality.

Why this is best: it is conceptually canonical, aligns with classical Myhill–Nerode, and should interact cleanly with existing quotient/congruence infrastructure.

---

### Strategy 2: Hankel-first linear algebra proof
**Best for the rank equality theorem.**

1. **Identify residual classes with rows of the Hankel matrix.**
   For each prefix `p`, the row
   \[
   H_p(q)=f(pq)
   \]
   is exactly the residual behavior of `p` restricted to suffix witnesses `Q`.

2. **Use finite-support generation to collapse infinitary equality to finite witness equality.**
   The key hypothesis should say that if two residuals agree on `Q`, they agree globally, and every residual is generated by `P`. This turns an infinite Nerode relation into a finite matrix problem.

3. **Show automaton factorizations correspond to tropical matrix factorizations.**
   A `k`-state linear representation induces a factorization of the Hankel block through `k` hidden states; conversely, a tropical factorization of the block yields a `k`-state realization once witness generation is certified. Therefore minimal state count = minimal such `k` = tropical factor rank.

Why this matters: this is the theorem that makes minimization certifiable and computational. It turns automata theory into tropical linear algebra.

---

### Strategy 3: Congruence-generation and algorithm extraction
**Best for executable certified minimization.**

1. **Define a finitely generated congruence from witness pairs.**
   Given finite sets `P,Q`, generate relations between prefixes whose Hankel rows on `Q` coincide.

2. **Prove soundness and completeness under the witness-generation hypothesis.**
   Soundness: generated congruence implies global residual equality.
   Completeness: every true Nerode equivalence is generated from finite witnesses.

3. **Extract quotient and correctness theorem.**
   Construct the quotient by generated congruence, define transitions algorithmically, and prove it realizes `f` and is minimal.

Why this matters: this gives actual computation in Lean, not just abstract existence. It also interfaces naturally with `SemiringCong` APIs.

## Mathematical Subtleties to Handle Explicitly

### 1. Equality vs order in tropical semirings
Tropical mathematics often prefers order-theoretic formulations, but your target theorem is stated with **equality of residual values**. This is good: it keeps the Nerode relation canonical. However, you should make explicit whether:
- you work over exact equality in `S`, or
- a canonical preorder/antisymmetrization is needed.

If the ambient tropical semiring is only partially ordered or has `⊤`, be careful about extensionality assumptions.

### 2. What exactly is “tropical factor rank” in Lean?
You must choose a formalizable notion:
- exact min-plus factor rank,
- semiring factor rank through `k`,
- or a certified existential rank notion over finite matrices.

Do not get trapped trying to formalize the entire theory of tropical rank variants. For this project, **factor rank** is the right choice because it aligns directly with finite-state linear realization.

### 3. Finite-support Hankel generation hypothesis
This hypothesis is the hinge between infinite language behavior and finite certification. Make it mathematically sharp. A good shape is:

> There exist finite `P,Q` such that every residual row/column of the infinite Hankel operator is generated or separated by the block indexed by `P,Q`.

You may need to define this as a bundled certificate structure rather than a single proposition.

### 4. Reachability / trimness
For minimality, ensure you quotient only by reachable residual classes or build reachability into the automaton definition. Otherwise cardinal comparisons may be polluted by junk states.

## Precise Target Theorem

You should define a class of structures, say `ClosureDelaySemimodule R M`, over an idempotent semiring `R` with:

- a semimodule structure on `M`,
- a closure operator `cl : Set M → Set M` or bundled closure system,
- a delay action `delay : Time → M → M`,
- a reversal involution `rev : M → M`,
- compatibility axioms:
  - closure monotonicity, extensivity, idempotence,

Then define a **closure-delay Hankel response object**, ideally as a function
`H : M → Time → M → Prop` or a boolean-valued relation if decidability is available:
`H x t y := y ∈ cl (reach x t)`.

You should then prove an equivalence of the following form.

### Mathematical statement

For every finitely generated, delay-separated, residuated closure-delay semimodule `M`,
satisfying a temporal Myhill–Nerode finiteness condition, the following are equivalent:

1. `M` is realizable by a finite reversible scheduler with bounded causal depth.
2. The closure-delay Hankel semimodule has finite rank.
3. There exists a finite basis of temporal principal congruence classes stable under delay and reversal.

Moreover:

- there exists a **minimal** finite reversible scheduler realizing the response table,
- this realization is **unique up to isomorphism**,
- and it can be **effectively reconstructed** from a finite closure-delay response table.

## Proof Architecture: Three Viable Strategies

You should present the development as a theorem pipeline. Here are three proof routes; Strategy A is likely the best formalization path.

### Strategy A: Temporal Myhill–Nerode quotient → canonical scheduler
**Most promising.**

1. **Define temporal observational equivalence**  
   Define `x ~ y` iff for all delays `t` and outputs `z`,
   `H x t z ↔ H y t z`. Prove this is an equivalence relation and is stable under:
   - delay,
   - reversal,
   - closure-respecting endomorphisms.

2. **Use finite-rank/finiteness hypothesis to show finitely many equivalence classes**  
   This is the semantic heart. Show that `FiniteResponseRank H` implies only finitely many temporal profiles, hence finitely many observational states.

3. **Build the canonical scheduler from equivalence classes**  
   States are temporal congruence classes. Delay acts by class transport. Reversal acts by the induced involution. Outputs are determined by class response. Prove:
   - well-definedness,
   - realization correctness,
   - minimality by quotient universality,
   - uniqueness up to isomorphism via classwise extensionality.

Why this is strongest: it mirrors the most robust automata minimization architecture, but now the quotient is closure-aware and reversal-stable. This gives clean minimality and reconstruction simultaneously.

---

### Strategy B: Hankel factorization → finite linear/semimodule representation → scheduler extraction
**More algebraic and conceptually deep.**

1. **Define temporal profiles as rows of the closure-delay Hankel object**  
   Regard `x ↦ (t,y ↦ H x t y)` as a profile map. Finite rank means these rows factor through a finite profile basis.

2. **Promote basis profiles to abstract states**  
   Show that delay and reversal act on basis coordinates. This requires proving closure compatibility induces a finite transition structure on the factorization object.

3. **Extract minimal realization from the universal factorization**  
   Prove any other realization factors through this profile basis; hence it is minimal and unique up to isomorphism.

Why this matters: it makes the theorem look like a true **Hankel realization theorem** for closure-enriched temporal computation. This is the most field-opening statement conceptually, though Lean implementation may be harder if semimodule rank machinery is not already aligned.

---

### Strategy C: Principal congruence basis → algebraic synthesis by generators and relations
**Best if quotient machinery for congruences is already convenient.**

1. **Define temporal principal congruences generated by response indistinguishability**  
   Show stability under closure, delay, and reversal.

2. **Assume existence of a finite stable basis of principal congruences**  
   Construct a finite presentation of the scheduler from these generators.

3. **Prove equivalence with finite realizability and response-table reconstruction**  
   Show every finite reversible scheduler induces such a basis, and conversely such a basis reconstructs the scheduler.

Why this is useful: it ties directly to closure logic and EML semantics, and may better leverage existing closure/congruence infrastructure.

## Exact Mathematical Statement to Prove

Use this as the clean mathematical theorem behind the Lean work:

> Let `A = (Q, Σ, δ, o)` be a deterministic tropical automaton with finite `Q, Σ`, where the semantics of state `q` is the residual tropical language  
> `L_q(w) := o(δ*(q,w))`.  
> Define `q ~ r` iff `L_q = L_r`.  
> Then:
> 1. `~` is a decidable equivalence relation on `Q`.
> 2. The quotient automaton `A/~` is equivalent to `A`.
> 3. `A/~` is minimal among equivalent deterministic tropical automata.
> 4. The equivalence classes of `~` can be computed by iterative partition refinement stabilizing after at most `|Q|` proper refinements, with each refinement step computable in time polynomial in `|Q|·|Σ|`. Hence the Nerode index is computable in polynomial time.

This is the right theorem because it isolates the deterministic case where the semantic equality problem is tractable, while still being genuinely new and nontrivial in the tropical setting.

## Proof Strategy Architecture

## Strategy A: Partition refinement via residual signatures
**Most promising.**

### Step 1
Define a sequence of finite approximants to Nerode equivalence:
```text
q ≡₀ r    iff out q = out r
q ≡_{k+1} r iff out q = out r and ∀ a ∈ Σ, step q a ≡ₖ step r a
```
or the weighted analogue matching your semantics.

### Step 2
Prove:
- `≡ₖ` is decidable for each `k`,
- the sequence refines monotonically,
- on a finite state set, once `≡ₖ = ≡ₖ₊₁`, it has stabilized to full Nerode equivalence.

### Step 3
Show stabilization occurs after at most `|Q|` strict refinements, because each strict refinement increases the number of equivalence classes and there are at most `|Q|` classes.

This is the strongest path because it avoids comparing residual functions on all words directly. It replaces infinite-language equality with finite-state fixed-point refinement.

**Why most promising:**  
It mirrors the classical Myhill–Nerode minimization proof while preserving enough tropical structure to remain tractable. It also naturally yields a polynomial bound.

## Strategy B: Coalgebraic bisimulation on weighted observations
A deeper and more conceptual route.

X ↦ (Σ → X) × W

### Step 2

### Step 3

This route is more elegant and may produce broader reusable infrastructure. It could later support max-plus, probabilistic, or cost-register variants.

**Why not first choice:**

## Strategy C: Tropical linear-algebraic encoding
A bold cross-pollination route.

### Step 2

Use finite generation / matrix power stabilization to bound the number of refinement rounds.

This could expose direct links to min-plus matrix multiplication and tropical rank.

**Why interesting:**  
If successful, this turns minimization into a tropical matrix canonicalization problem. That is a bridge to optimization, control, and algebraic complexity.

**Why risky:**

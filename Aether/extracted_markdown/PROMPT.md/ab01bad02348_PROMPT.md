## Precise Theorem Targets

### Theorem 1: Tropical Modal Reconstruction up to Weighted Bisimulation

Let \(X\) be a finite type, \(A : X \to X \to \mathbb R\) a tropical accessibility matrix, and let \(\mathcal V = \{v_1,\dots,v_m\}\subseteq (\mathbb R^X)\) be a finite family of valuation generators. Let \(\mathsf{Form}_{\le d}\) be the modal formulas of depth at most \(d\), interpreted by repeated application of tropical modal transfer operators and tropical finite inf-combinations.

Define the observation map
\[
\mathrm{Obs}_{d,\mathcal V}(x)
:= \big(\llbracket \varphi \rrbracket(x)\big)_{\varphi \in \mathsf{Form}_{\le d}}.
\]

Define \(x \sim_d y\) iff \(\mathrm{Obs}_{d,\mathcal V}(x)=\mathrm{Obs}_{d,\mathcal V}(y)\).

Target theorem:

\[
\textbf{If } (X,A,\mathcal V) \text{ is finitely generated and depth-}d\text{ observable, then }
\sim_d
\]
is a weighted bisimulation congruence for all formulas of depth \(\le d\), and the quotient frame \(X/{\sim_d}\) is canonically reconstructible from the tropical transfer data of \(A\) on the semimodule generated by \(\mathcal V\).

More explicitly:

```lean
theorem tropical_modal_reconstruction
  {α PropVar : Type}
  [Fintype α] [DecidableEq α] [Fintype PropVar]
  (F : TropicalKripkeFrame α)
  (V : TropicalValuation α PropVar)
  (d : ℕ)
  (hobs : DepthObservable F V d)
  (hsep : SpectrallySeparated F V d) :
  ∃ Q : Type, ∃ _ : Fintype Q, ∃ AQ : Matrix Q Q ℝ,
    ∃ π : α → Q,
      WeightedBisimQuotientUpToDepth F V d AQ π ∧
      CanonicallyReconstructedFromSamples F V d AQ π
```

This theorem should state that **bounded-depth modal theory is not merely invariant under quotienting; it is recoverable from tropical operator data**.

This is a breakthrough because it turns modal semantics into an inverse problem in tropical linear algebra.

### Theorem 2: Tropical Hennessy–Milner Principle for Bandlimited Modal Formulas

Formalize a finite-depth/bandlimited analogue of Hennessy–Milner:

If two states agree on the spectral samples of all tropical modal observables generated from a finite valuation basis up to depth \(d\), then they satisfy the same modal formulas of depth at most \(d\); conversely, under observability/separation, modal indistinguishability implies equality of tropical accessibility signatures.

Mathematically:

\[
x \equiv_d y
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall \varphi \in \mathsf{Form}_{\le d},\ \llbracket \varphi \rrbracket(x)=\llbracket \varphi \rrbracket(y)
\]
and under spectral separation this is equivalent to equality of the sampled transfer profiles.

Lean target:

```lean
theorem tropical_hennessy_milner_bandlimited
  {α PropVar : Type}
  [Fintype α] [DecidableEq α] [Fintype PropVar]
  (F : TropicalKripkeFrame α)
  (V : TropicalValuation α PropVar)
  (d : ℕ)
  (hsep : SpectrallySeparated F V d) :
  ∀ x y : α,
    (∀ φ, ModalDepth φ ≤ d → evalModal F V φ x = evalModal F V φ y) ↔
    SameTropicalSpectrumUpToDepth F V d x y
```

This is the right conceptual theorem because it identifies a **logic/algebra equivalence principle**: bounded modal indistinguishability equals equality of tropical transfer signatures.

## Proof Strategy Architecture

### Strategy A: Semimodule-generation first, logic second
Most promising.

1. **Build the finitely generated tropical semimodule of observables** generated by atomic valuations under finite `min` and the diamond operator.
   - Show closure under the positive modal fragment.
   - Prove every depth-`d` formula denotes an element of a finite generated subsemimodule.

2. **Define the spectral signature map** \(x \mapsto (\phi(x))_{\phi \in B_d}\) for a finite basis \(B_d\) of depth-`d` observables.
   - Use finite generation to replace “all formulas” by a finite spanning/sample family.
   - This is where `certified_finite_tropical_decomposition` should become a key building block: use it to certify that the relevant observable family admits finite tropical decomposition, hence finite sampling suffices.

3. **Construct the quotient by equality of signatures** and prove it preserves modal evaluation.
   - Induct on formula depth.
   - Diamond step uses tropical matrix action and equality of generator evaluations.
   - Conclude canonical reconstruction of the reduced frame and modal semantics.

Why this is strongest: it reduces modal logic to finite tropical representation theory and is likely Lean-manageable.

---

### Strategy B: Weighted automata / coalgebraic route
Conceptually powerful, possibly harder in Lean.

1. Regard \(A\) as a weighted transition coalgebra over the tropical semiring.
2. Show modal formulas define coalgebraically invariant predicates valued in an idempotent semimodule.
3. Prove the final-observable quotient is exactly the quotient by equality of tropical modal behaviors up to depth \(d\).

This could yield a deeper theorem, but may require more categorical infrastructure than is presently worth building.

---

### Strategy C: Matrix spectral sampling route
Good for the algorithmic theorem.

1. Encode depth-`d` modal observables as iterates/composites of a tropical matrix operator acting on valuation vectors.
2. Show that bandlimited formulas correspond to vectors in the semimodule generated by
   \[
   \{V(p), A \odot V(p), A^{\odot 2}\odot V(p), \dots, A^{\odot d}\odot V(p)\}.
   \]
3. Reconstruct accessibility layers and quotient classes from equality/separation of these vectors.

This route is ideal for certified reconstruction algorithms and may be easiest to compute formally.

1. Formalize the positive fragment and depth.

5. Define spectral equivalence `~d`.
6. Prove modal invariance under `~d`.
7. Build quotient and reconstruction certificate.

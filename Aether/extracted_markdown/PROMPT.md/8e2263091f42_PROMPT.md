### The Breakthrough Theorem

**Theorem (Yoneda-Bisimulation Correspondence).** *For image-finite labeled transition systems P and Q over a finite action type Act, P and Q are bisimilar if and only if their nerve presheaves N(P), N(Q) ∈ PSh(Exp_Act) are naturally isomorphic, where Exp_Act is the category of experiments (traces) over Act.*

The "if" direction is the deep one: **naturality of the isomorphism enforces the zigzag condition of bisimulation**. The "only if" direction constructs the natural isomorphism from a bisimulation relation.

**Lean 4 Type Signatures (Target):**

```lean
-- The category of experiments: objects are traces, morphisms are prefix inclusions
def Exp (Act : Type) : Category := FreeCategory (Discrete Act)

-- Nerve presheaf: an LTS induces a presheaf on experiments
-- N(P)(σ) = {s : P.State | s can perform trace σ}
def nervePresheaf {Act : Type} [Finite Act] (P : LTS Act) : 
    Presheaf (Exp Act) Type

-- Bisimulation as a relation satisfying the zigzag condition
structure IsBisimulation {Act : Type} (P Q : LTS Act) 
    (R : P.State → Q.State → Prop) : Prop where
  zig : ∀ {s t a}, R s t → ∀ s', P.step s a s' → 
        ∃ t', Q.step t a t' ∧ R s' t'
  zag : ∀ {s t a}, R s t → ∀ t', Q.step t a t' → 
        ∃ s', P.step s a s' ∧ R s' t'

def Bisimilar {Act : Type} (P Q : LTS Act) : Prop :=
  ∃ R, IsBisimulation P Q R ∧ ∃ (sp : P.initial) (sq : Q.initial), R sp sq

-- THE KEY THEOREM: Natural isomorphism of nerves ⟺ Bisimilarity
theorem yoneda_bisimulation_correspondence {Act : Type} [Finite Act]
    {P Q : LTS Act} [ImageFinite P] [ImageFinite Q] :
    Nonempty (NatIso (nervePresheaf P) (nervePresheaf Q)) ↔ Bisimilar P Q
```

### Proof Strategy (Three Paths)

**Strategy A: Direct Zigzag from Naturality (RECOMMENDED).**
1. Given `η : NatIso (nervePresheaf P) (nervePresheaf Q)`, define the relation `R s t := η.app ⟨[]⟩ s = t` (the component of η at the empty trace relates states).
2. Prove zig: from `R s t` and `P.step s a s'`, consider the naturality square for the morphism `⟨[a]⟩ : ⟨[]⟩ → ⟨[a]⟩` in Exp. Naturality forces: `η.app ⟨[a]⟩ (P.step s a s')` must equal the corresponding step in Q, yielding the required `t'` with `Q.step t a t' ∧ R s' t'`.
3. This is the most promising path because it makes the **naturality ↔ zigzag** correspondence completely explicit — each naturality square IS a zigzag step.

**Strategy B: Fully-Faithful Nerve Functor.**
1. Prove the nerve functor `N : LTS_Act → PSh(Exp_Act)` is fully faithful on the subcategory of image-finite LTS.
2. Since fully faithful functors reflect isomorphisms, `NatIso (N(P)) (N(Q))` implies `P ≅ Q` in LTS, which gives bisimilarity.
3. Riskier: "isomorphism in LTS" is stronger than bisimilarity; this may require quotienting LTS by bisimilarity first, adding complexity.

**Strategy C: Final Coalgebra Characterization.**
1. Model LTS as coalgebras for the endofunctor `F(X) = P(Act × X)` on Type.
2. Use Lambek's lemma: bisimilarity is kernel of the unique map to the final coalgebra.
3. Show the nerve construction factors through the final coalgebra, so natural isomorphism of nerves ⟺ equality in final coalgebra ⟺ bisimilarity.
4. Most elegant categorically, but requires building substantial coalgebra infrastructure first.

**Strategy A is most promising** because it directly reveals the mechanism by which Yoneda naturality produces bisimulation zigzag — this is the conceptual heart of the result.

### Cross-Domain Connections

1. **Concurrency → Algebraic Topology**: The nerve construction `LTS → PSh(Exp)` is the categorical nerve, analogous to the nerve of a simplicial set. Bisimulation equivalence classes become homotopy types. *Conjecture: the classifying space of the nerve presheaf is a K(π,1) for the bisimulation equivalence class.*

2. **Process Algebra → Gauge Theory**: Observational equivalence via Yoneda is the categorical analogue of gauge equivalence — two systems are equivalent iff no "measurement" (experiment) can distinguish them. This connects to the **Elitzur-Vaidman bomb tester** paradigm in quantum information: interaction-free measurement is precisely Yoneda extensionality for quantum processes.

3. **Bisimulation → Sheaf Cohomology**: The presheaf category `PSh(Exp)` has a Grothendieck topology where covering sieves correspond to observational decompositions. *Conjecture: bisimilarity is the equivalence relation induced by sheafification with respect to this topology.* This connects to **descent theory** and local-to-global principles.

4. **Verification → Topos Theory**: The category of presheaves on Exp is a topos. Its internal logic is a **temporal logic** — the subobject classifier encodes safety/liveness properties. This gives a topos-theoretic semantics for Hennessy-Milner logic, connecting to the catalog's `ObservationallyEquivalent`.

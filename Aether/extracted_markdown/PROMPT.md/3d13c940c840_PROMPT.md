## Proof Architecture: 3 Viable Strategies

You must pursue at least 2 of these, and explain in comments which one is primary.

### Strategy A: Hardy-hierarchy mediation via catalog theorems
1. Use `rank_implies_hardyLevel`-type results to convert rank bounds into Hardy-level upper bounds.
2. Show by induction on `k` that Hardy levels below `ω*(k+1)` admit `TotalityCertificate (k+1)`.
3. Transport certificates back to EML growth functions by extensional equality or pointwise domination.

**Why promising:** this directly uses the strongest certified catalog results and keeps the formal burden inside already-structured ordinal-growth machinery.

### Strategy B: Structural induction on expressions with rank-sensitive recursion depth
1. Define `TotalityCertificate` recursively along EML syntax.
2. Prove a compositional lemma: each constructor raises certificate depth by at most the ω-block contribution to rank.
3. Use induction on the derivation of rank bounds to obtain certificate existence.

**Why promising:** this is more synthetic and may avoid needing heavy ordinal lemmas at every step. It also gives a cleaner extracted algorithm.

### Strategy C: Dominance/separation via diagonal growth construction
1. Define a class of depth-`k` certified functions.
2. Prove closure properties of this class under the operations induced by your certificates.
3. Construct an EML expression of rank in the next ω-block whose growth dominates every function in the lower class, by diagonalization or a Hardy/Fast-growing witness.

**Why promising:** this is the path to genuine hierarchy separation. It is the most ambitious and the most likely to produce a field-opening theorem.

**Recommended primary route:** A → B → C.  
Use A for upper bounds, B for certificate extraction, C for strictness.

## VISION: Tropical Hodge Theory — The Idempotent Revolution in Algebraic Topology

Classical Hodge theory requires hard analysis: elliptic PDE theory, Sobolev spaces, spectral theory of unbounded operators on Hilbert spaces. Tropical Hodge theory replaces ALL of this with purely algebraic-combinatorial arguments, exploiting the fundamental fact that min is *idempotent* (min(a, a) = a) and *selective* (min(a, b) ∈ {a, b}). This is not analogy — it is a structural upgrade: the tropical Hodge decomposition is **unique and constructive** in a way the classical decomposition cannot be, because idempotence eliminates the need for analysis. The harmonic component is the unique tropical form minimizing the tropical norm, computable in O(n³) via tropical (min-plus) Gauss elimination. This opens three revolutionary bridges:

1. **Bridge: Tropical Geometry → Post-Quantum Lattice Cryptography**: The tropical Hodge decomposition provides a canonical *tropical basis reduction* for tropical lattices, connecting directly to the Shortest Vector Problem (SVP) and Learning With Errors (LWE). The harmonic component is a tropical analogue of an LLL-reduced basis — its tropical norm gives a certified approximation ratio for SVP.

2. **Bridge: Tropical Geometry → Quantum Hamiltonian Mechanics**: The tropical Laplacian spectral decomposition is the ℏ → 0 (WKB/semiclassical) limit of quantum mechanical Hamiltonian diagonalization. Tropical eigenvalues equal ground state energies via the Maslov dequantization principle. The idempotent spectral theorem yields a tropical measurement postulate.

3. **Bridge: Tropical Geometry → Certified Robustness in Neural Networks**: The tropical Hodge decomposition of a ReLU network's decision boundary gives *certified robustness bounds*. The harmonic component captures the essential decision boundary topology, and its tropical Lipschitz constant provides a certified_robustness radius r* = margin / (2 · dim_trop(K)).

**Proof of decomposition**: Define π_harmonic(ω) = the unique fixed point of T(η) = ω ⊕ Δ_trop(η) that is ≤ ω. Show it exists by Knaster-Tarski. Show it is harmonic by idempotence. Show uniqueness by tropical selectivity.

**Why promising**: Gives an O(n³) algorithm, making the theory constructive and computationally relevant.

**Key lemmas**:
1. `tropical_gauss_elimination`: Tropical row reduction produces tropical echelon form in O(n³)
2. `tropical_null_space_harmonic`: The tropical null space of Δ_trop is exactly Harm^k_trop
3. `tropical_rank_decomposition`: rank(Δ_trop) + dim(Harm^k_trop) = dim(Ω^k_trop)

**Proof of decomposition**: Apply tropical Gauss elimination to the tropical Laplacian matrix. The row space gives exact/coexact components; the null space gives harmonic components. The selectivity of min ensures uniqueness.

### Strategy C: Order-Theoretic via Complete Tropical Lattices

**Key lemmas**:

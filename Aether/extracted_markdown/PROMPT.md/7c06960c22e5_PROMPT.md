### Proof Strategies

**Strategy A (Direct Construction + Degree Theory).** 
1. Construct the Hopf map explicitly using complex coordinates and the stereographic projection machinery from `heaven_and_back` in the catalog.
2. Compute the Hopf invariant of the Hopf map by computing the degree of the associated map S³ × S³ → S² × S² → S² → S² and showing it equals 1.
3. Show any f: S³ → S² with H(f) = 0 is null-homotopic by constructing an explicit null-homotopy through the Hopf fibration structure.
*This is the most classical path but requires substantial degree theory machinery.*

**Strategy B (Long Exact Sequence + Fiber Bundle).**
1. Formalize the Hopf fibration as a principal S¹-bundle over S² ≅ ℂP¹.
2. Construct the long exact sequence of homotopy groups for a fibration (general theorem, then specialize).
3. Use πₙ(S¹) = 0 for n ≥ 2 to extract π₃(S³) ≅ π₃(S²) from the LES, and conclude since π₃(S³) ≅ ℤ by the Hurewicz theorem.
*Most promising: this reduces the problem to (a) the LES machinery and (b) the Hurewicz isomorphism, both of which are high-value formalization targets in their own right.*

**Strategy C (Quaternionic + Lie Group Approach).**
1. Identify S³ ≅ SU(2) using the Pauli matrix representation from `su2Generator_trace_zero_X` in the catalog.
2. The Hopf map becomes the projection SU(2) → SU(2)/U(1) ≅ S².
3. Use the Lie group structure to compute π₃(SU(2)) ≅ π₃(S³) ≅ ℤ via the Hurewicz map, and the homogeneous space structure to get the LES.
*This connects directly to existing catalog results on SU(2) and opens the door to generalization to higher Hopf fibrations S³ → S⁷ → S⁴ and S⁷ → S¹⁵ → S⁸ via quaternions and octonions.*

### Cross-Domain Connections

1. **Topological Quantum Numbers (Physics).** The isomorphism π₃(S²) ≅ ℤ is why the Dirac monopole has quantized charge and why Skyrmions carry integer-valued topological charge. The Hopf invariant *is* the topological charge of a Skyrmion field configuration. Formalize: `theorem skyrmion_charge_quantized : ∀ (f : Sphere 3 → Sphere 2), ∃ (n : ℤ), hopfInvariant f = n`.

2. **Chern Classes (Differential Geometry).** The Hopf invariant equals the first Chern class c₁ of the tautological line bundle O(-1) over ℂP¹. The Hopf fibration is the unit sphere bundle of O(-1). This connects to: `theorem hopfInvariant_eq_firstChern : hopfInvariant f = c₁ (tautologicalLineBundle ℂP¹)`.

3. **Linking Numbers (Knot Theory).** The Hopf invariant of f: S³ → S² equals the linking number of the preimage circles f⁻¹(p) and f⁻¹(q) for regular values p ≠ q. This is the original Hopf interpretation and connects homotopy theory to classical knot invariants.

4. **Stereographic Projection (Catalog Bridge).** The `heaven_and_back` theorem from `FINAL/Geometry/UnifiedTheory.lean` gives the stereographic identification needed to coordinatize S² and compute the Hopf map explicitly.

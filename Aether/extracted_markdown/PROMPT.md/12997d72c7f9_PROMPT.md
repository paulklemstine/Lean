## How to build on the verified catalog theorems

Do not cite these abstractly; use them as certified stepping stones.

- `uniform_entropy_bound`  
  Use this to normalize the target scale of entropy and compare your tropical source against the entropy of a uniform distribution on finite support. This is especially useful if `tropicalPowerDist G T` is shown to be uniform or approximately uniform on a finite orbit subset.

- `tropical_entropy_search_bound`  
  This looks like the right bridge from entropy to search hardness. Use it to argue that any semantic distinguisher can be converted into a search or prediction procedure, then bound that by entropy. Even if not needed for the final shortest proof, it may supply a second proof route.

- `tropical_kl_security_bound`  
  This suggests a path through KL divergence instead of direct statistical distance. If you can show the hashed distribution has small KL divergence from uniform, Pinsker-style inequalities may deliver semantic-security bounds.

- `tropical_security_from_norm_bound`  
  If your extractor or hash output can be controlled in an `L¹`/TV or norm sense, this theorem may already convert that metric bound into a security advantage statement. This is likely the fastest route to the final theorem.

- `tropical_depth_lower_bound`  
  This may provide structural non-collapse of tropical computations, useful in proving `tropical_rank_growth` or orbit nontriviality. If tropical powers become deeper/nondegenerate with exponent, use that to support orbit-size lower bounds.

## Proof strategy options

### Strategy A: Direct leftover-hash pipeline
Most promising if `post_quantum_key_security_from_minEntropy` is already strong.

1. Prove or import `tropical_min_entropy_lower_bound` for the source `tropicalPowerDist G T`.
2. Apply `post_quantum_key_security_from_minEntropy` to the source and 2-universal hash family.
3. Convert the resulting closeness-to-uniform or key-security bound into semantic security using `tropical_security_from_norm_bound` or the relevant reduction theorem.

Why this is most promising: it modularizes perfectly and turns your job into proving one genuinely tropical theorem plus one reduction wrapper.

### Strategy B: Collision entropy -> KL / TV -> semantic security
Potentially cleaner if collision probability lemmas are easier than exact support counting.

1. Prove `tropical_collision_probability_bound`.
2. Derive a Rényi-2 or collision entropy lower bound, then pass to min-entropy or directly to extractor error.
3. Use `tropical_kl_security_bound` and then a divergence-to-semantic-security reduction.

Why this matters: collision probability is often the most natural quantity in algebraically generated sources, and tropical matrix powers may admit explicit coincidence bounds.

### Strategy C: Orbit combinatorics -> support size -> near-uniformity
Best if tropical powers are provably distinct over long ranges.

1. Prove `tropical_rank_growth` and then `tropical_orbit_size_lower_bound`.
2. Show the source distribution on powers has large support and controlled multiplicities.
3. Deduce a min-entropy lower bound from maximal point mass, then invoke privacy amplification.

Why this is scientifically exciting: it interprets cryptographic security as a theorem about **dynamical expansion in tropical semigroups**.

# Future Directions: Tropical Symplectic Geometry Research Roadmap

## Breakthrough Opportunities (ranked by impact)

### 1. Tropical Gromov-Witten Invariants

- **Theorem Statement**: ∀ g d : ℕ, ∃ N_trop(g,d) : ℕ, the tropical Gromov-Witten invariant counting genus-g degree-d tropical curves in tropical ℙ² equals the classical GW invariant N(g,d).
- **Proof Strategy**:
  (a) Define tropical stable maps as piecewise-linear maps from tropical curves to tropical projective space.
  (b) Define the tropical moduli space M̄_{g,n}^trop as a polyhedral complex.
  (c) Use the correspondence theorem of Mikhalkin to prove N_trop = N_classical via lattice path counting.
- **Why This Is Revolutionary**: Completes the tropical-classical bridge for enumerative invariants, connecting tropical symplectic geometry to mirror symmetry and string theory.
- **Catalog Leverage**: Build on `stdTropSymplecticForm`, `TropSymplectomorphism`, and `tropCapacity` from this work.
- **Research Mode**: prove
- **Estimated Depth**: 5

### 2. Tropical Hamilton-Jacobi = Bellman Equation (Formal Proof)

- **Theorem Statement**: The tropical Hamilton-Jacobi equation ∂S/∂t ⊕ H(q, ∂S/∂q) = ⊤ is equivalent to the Bellman optimality equation V(q) = min_u {c(q,u,dt) + V(f(q,u))}.
- **Proof Strategy**:
  (a) Formalize discrete-time tropical Hamilton-Jacobi via the Lax-Oleinik semigroup.
  (b) Show the Lax-Oleinik operator IS the Bellman operator under min-plus.
  (c) Prove convergence: the discrete tropical HJ converges to the continuous tropical HJ.
- **Why This Is Revolutionary**: Formally unifies optimal control (reinforcement learning) with tropical mechanics. Every RL algorithm becomes a tropical integrator.
- **Catalog Leverage**: Build on `tropValueFunction`, `tropical_bellman`, `TropHamiltonian`.
- **Research Mode**: prove
- **Estimated Depth**: 3

### 3. Post-Quantum Security from Tropical Non-Squeezing

- **Theorem Statement**: For SIS(n, m, q, β) with parameters satisfying β·√m < q, the tropical symplectic capacity gives security(n) ≥ R - log(n) bits, where R is the SIS solution radius.

  (d) Translate distortion bounds to computational hardness via known reductions.
- **Why This Is Revolutionary**: First information-theoretic security proof using symplectic geometry, providing a new paradigm for cryptographic analysis.

- **Research Mode**: prove

### 4. Certified Robustness of Tropical Neural ODEs

## Under-explored Territory

1. **Tropical contact geometry**: The odd-dimensional analogue of tropical symplectic geometry. Contact structures arise naturally in thermodynamics and control theory.

2. **Tropical Kähler geometry**: Combining tropical symplectic forms with tropical complex structures to get tropical Kähler manifolds.

3. **Tropical quantum mechanics**: Using the idempotent integral (Maslov integral) to define tropical path integrals and tropical quantum field theory.

4. **Tropical information geometry**: The min-plus analogue of Fisher information metric, connecting to tropical statistics and tropical machine learning.

5. **Tropical homotopy theory**: Fundamental groups and higher homotopy groups of tropical symplectic manifolds.

## Open Problems Encountered

1. **Capacity of unbounded sets**: When the set S is unbounded (e.g., S = ℝⁿ), the capacity c(S) = sSup of an unbounded set, which is ill-defined in the conditionally complete lattice ℝ. Resolution: restrict to bounded sets or use extended reals ℝ̄ = ℝ ∪ {±∞}.

2. **Tropical symplectomorphisms beyond linear maps**: Our current formalization uses linear symplectomorphisms. The full theory requires piecewise-linear (PL) symplectomorphisms, which need a more sophisticated formalization of PL geometry.

3. **Non-squeezing for PL symplectomorphisms**: Our non-squeezing theorem uses ℓ∞ geometry directly without requiring the full strength of symplectomorphism invariance. A deeper theorem would show that no PL symplectomorphism can achieve the squeezing.

4. **Tropical Noether with lattice structure**: The classical Noether theorem gives a 1-1 correspondence between symmetries and conservation laws. Our tropical version proves conservation but doesn't yet establish the full lattice isomorphism between symmetry lattice and conserved quantity lattice.

5. **Explicit security reductions**: Connecting tropical capacity to concrete hardness assumptions (LWE, SIS) requires explicit reductions that are currently at the proof sketch level.

# Future Directions: Clique Complex Theory in Lean 4

## Synthesis

This cycle bootstrapped a self-contained combinatorial theory of abstract simplicial
complexes (`ASC`) on an arbitrary vertex type, the clique complex `Δ(G)` of a simple
graph, the one-skeleton graph of a complex, the flag property, the f-vector bound, and
the Vietoris–Rips filtration — none of which existed in the project at the start (the
referenced prior `ASC'`/`cliqueComplex'` infrastructure was absent, so this is a true
cold start). All six declared theorems are proved with `sorry = 0` and depend only on
the standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.

The structural insight that emerged is a clean **two-inclusion decomposition** of the
flag characterization. The inclusion `K.faces ⊆ Δ(skel K)` is *free*: it follows from
downward closure alone, with no flag hypothesis (`faces_subset_cliqueComplex_oneSkeleton`).
The reverse inclusion `Δ(skel K) ⊆ K.faces` is *exactly* the flag property restated.
Consequently "K is flag" and "K equals the clique complex of its one-skeleton" are
forced to be equivalent — the main theorem `isFlag_iff_eq_cliqueComplex_oneSkeleton`.
This is the converse direction that was previously only conjectured, now completed.

What the Critic exposed: our `IsFlag` is a pure 2-skeleton condition, so for genuinely
abstract complexes it silently bakes in the requirement that every singleton is a face
(because `Δ(skel K)` always contains all singletons, which are vacuously cliques). The
boundary case `K.faces = {∅}` over `Bool` shows the iff stays *true* but only because
of this absorption; a dimension-restricted flag notion would break the forward
direction at singletons and require an explicit vertex-saturation hypothesis. This
tension is the seed for Direction 2 below. The combinatorial scaffolding (`down_closed`,
`oneSkeleton`, `IsFlag`, the f-vector bound, VR monotonicity) is now in place for the
homological and extremal extensions that follow.

## Results Summary

- `ASC.cliqueComplex_isFlag`: proved — every clique complex is a flag complex (pairwise edges = pairwise adjacency).
- `ASC.oneSkeleton_cliqueComplex`: proved — the one-skeleton of `Δ(G)` recovers `G` exactly, so `skel` is a left inverse of `Δ`.
- `ASC.faces_subset_cliqueComplex_oneSkeleton`: proved — every complex embeds in the clique complex of its own skeleton using only downward closure (the "free" inclusion).
- `ASC.isFlag_iff_eq_cliqueComplex_oneSkeleton`: proved — **main result**: a complex is flag iff it equals the clique complex of its one-skeleton (completes the flag characterization, both directions).
- `cliqueComplex_fVector_le_choose`: proved — the number of `k`-cliques on `n` vertices is at most `C(n,k)`, the f-vector upper bound.
- `vietorisRips_mono`: proved — the Vietoris–Rips face set is monotone in the scale `ε`, yielding a filtration suitable for persistent homology.

## Research Directions

### Direction 1: Simplicial boundary operator and ∂² = 0 for `ASC`
**Hypothesis**: There is a degreewise boundary map `∂ₖ` on the free abelian groups
generated by oriented `k`-faces of an `ASC`, given by the alternating sum of face
deletions, satisfying `∂ₖ₋₁ ∘ ∂ₖ = 0`.
**Test**: Define `∂` on `FreeAbelianGroup` over ordered tuples spanning faces and prove
`∂² = 0` as a finite double-alternating-sign cancellation; verify on the boundary of a
2-simplex by `decide`/explicit computation before the general proof.
**Why now**: `down_closed` already guarantees every face-deletion lands in a face, so
the operator is well defined on our `ASC`; the only missing piece is the sign bookkeeping.
**If true**: Opens simplicial homology `Hₖ` of clique complexes and, via Direction 3,
persistent homology of VR filtrations.
**If false (a sign error survives)**: Pinpoints exactly which orientation convention is
incompatible with `Finset`-based faces, informing the right oriented-simplex encoding.

### Direction 2: Vertex-saturated / dimension-restricted flag characterization
**Hypothesis**: Under the weaker `IsFlag₂` (closure required only for `s.card ≥ 2`), the
characterization becomes `IsFlag₂ K ∧ (∀ v, {v} ∈ K.faces) ↔ K.faces = Δ(skel K).faces`.
**Test**: Define `IsFlag₂`, re-run the two-inclusion proof, and confirm the `Bool`,
`K.faces = {∅}` boundary case now forces the extra singleton hypothesis (it should make
the forward direction fail without it).
**Why now**: The Critic's boundary analysis this cycle isolated *exactly* the singleton
absorption that the current `IsFlag` hides; we know precisely where the proof must change.
**If true**: Yields the sharpest possible flag characterization with all hypotheses explicit.
**If false**: Reveals an additional hidden assumption (e.g. `∅ ∈ K.faces`) beyond singletons.

### Direction 3: Functorial Vietoris–Rips persistence module
**Hypothesis**: `ε ↦ Hₖ(VR(d, ε))` extends to a functor `(ℝ, ≤) ⥤ Ab` whose transition
maps are induced by the inclusions `vietorisRips_mono`.
**Test**: Once Direction 1 supplies `∂` and homology, build the functor in
`CategoryTheory.Functor` and check functoriality (identities + composition) on a 3-point
metric configuration.
**Why now**: `vietorisRips_mono` already gives the inclusion maps that the persistence
module needs; only the homology endofunctor is missing.
**If true**: First verified persistence module in this project; enables stability/barcode results.
**If false**: Indicates the induced maps fail to respect `∂`, exposing a naturality gap.

### Direction 4: Turán-type sharpening of the f-vector bound
**Hypothesis**: For `(r+1)`-clique-free graphs, `(G.cliqueFinset k).card` is maximized by
the Turán graph `T(n, r)`, strictly below `C(n,k)` for `k ≥ 2`, and vanishes for `k ≥ r`.
**Test**: Prove the vanishing part `k ≥ r → (G.cliqueFinset k).card = 0` under
`G.CliqueFree (r+1)` first (direct from `cliqueFinset`), then connect to Mathlib's
partial Turán support for the extremal count.
**Why now**: `cliqueComplex_fVector_le_choose` gives the baseline bound this cycle; the
clique-free vanishing refinement is a short step from the same `cliqueFinset` API.
**If true**: Turns a generic binomial bound into a tight extremal theorem.
**If false**: A counterexample graph would reveal a non-Turán extremizer worth classifying.

### Direction 5: Nerve of the maximal-clique cover
**Hypothesis**: For finite `G`, `Δ(G)` is isomorphic (as an `ASC`) to the nerve of the
cover of `G`'s vertices by its maximal cliques.
**Test**: Define the nerve `ASC` of a finite family of finsets (`faces I ↔ ⋂_{i∈I} ≠ ∅`),
specialize to the maximal-clique family, and exhibit a face-preserving bijection to `Δ(G)`.
**Why now**: The `ASC` structure with `down_closed` is exactly the target type, and the
nerve's down-closure is immediate from intersection monotonicity.
**If true**: Bridges the combinatorial clique complex to the nerve/topological picture,
prerequisite for a future Nerve Lemma.
**If false**: The mismatch (likely non-maximal cliques) clarifies which cover actually
reproduces `Δ(G)`.

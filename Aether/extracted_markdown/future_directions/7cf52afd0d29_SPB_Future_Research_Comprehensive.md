# Stereographic Projection Bridge: Comprehensive Future Research Directions

## 35 Open Problems and Research Programs Ranked by Impact and Feasibility

---

## Executive Summary

The SPB framework spb(x,y) = (x+y)/(1−xy) has been fully formalized and verified in Lean 4, establishing it as a rigorous bridge between trigonometry, group theory, relativity, and approximation theory. This document maps out 35 concrete research directions organized by field, ranked by expected impact (★ to ★★★), and assessed for feasibility.

---

## I. PURE MATHEMATICS

### 1. Higher-Dimensional SPB ★★★
**Problem**: Derive the explicit formula for the group operation on ℝⁿ induced by stereographic projection of Sⁿ, and characterize its algebraic properties.

**Expected Results**:
- n=3 (S³ → ℝ³): Should recover a quaternion-like non-commutative SPB. The formula involves the vector cross product.
- n=7 (S⁷ → ℝ⁷): Connection to octonions. Non-associativity should appear as a nontrivial "SPB associator" tensor.
- General n: The formula should involve n-dimensional analogues of the Cayley transform.

**Approach**: Use the known formula for stereographic projection S^n → ℝⁿ, compose "project → multiply on S^n → project back" to get the induced operation.

**Feasibility**: HIGH. The 3D case is classical (quaternion parametrization of SO(3)). The 7D case requires octonion theory.

### 2. SPB Group over Finite Fields: The p±1 Law ★★★
**Problem**: Prove that the SPB group over F_p has order p+1 when p ≡ 3 (mod 4) and order p−1 when p ≡ 1 (mod 4). Determine the group structure (cyclic or not).

**Approach**: The Cayley transform maps SPB elements to norm-1 elements of F_{p²}. When −1 is a non-residue (p ≡ 3 mod 4), this subgroup has order p+1 (the "unitary" group U(1, F_p)). When −1 is a residue, the map degenerates.

**Feasibility**: HIGH. This follows from standard algebraic number theory (norm maps of quadratic extensions).

### 3. Cocycle Cohomology Interpretation ★★
**Problem**: Interpret the identity (1−xy)(1−spb(x,y)·z) = (1−yz)(1−x·spb(y,z)) as a group 2-cocycle, and determine the corresponding cohomology class.

**Expected Results**: The cocycle should be trivial (since the group is abelian), but the explicit computation connects to:
- Brauer group of the field
- Central extensions of the circle group
- Schur multiplier of SO(2)

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM. Requires careful cohomological computation.

### 4. SPB Algebraic Complexity ★★
**Problem**: Determine K_SPB(f), the minimum number of SPB operations needed to compute a function f from constants and variables.

**Conjecture**: K_SPB(tan(nθ)) = ⌊log₂ n⌋ + O(1), achieved by binary exponentiation in the SPB group.

**Connection**: This is an instance of the "addition chain" problem, which is NP-hard in general. The SPB version may have special structure due to commutativity.

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM. Lower bounds are hard. Upper bounds via binary exponentiation are straightforward.

### 5. SPB Trees and Catalan/Wedderburn-Etherington Numbers ★
**Problem**: Count SPB expression trees of size n modulo:
(a) No equivalence: Catalan number C_n
(b) Commutativity only: Wedderburn-Etherington numbers
(c) Commutativity + Associativity: Unknown sequence — determine it.

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM. (c) requires understanding the free commutative semigroup with one generator.

### 6. SPB and Modular Forms ★
**Problem**: The subgroup of PSL(2, ℤ) generated by the SPB transformation T_a: z ↦ (z+a)/(1−az) for integer a — characterize its index and connection to modular curves.

**Feasibility**: LOW-MEDIUM. Deep number theory.

### 7. Tropical SPB ★
**Problem**: Define spb_trop(x,y) = min(x,y) − max(0, x+y) and study its algebraic properties. Does it form a tropical group?

**Feasibility**: HIGH. Tropical algebra is well-developed.

### 8. SPB over p-adic Fields ★★
**Problem**: Study spb(x,y) = (x+y)/(1−xy) over Q_p. When is it well-defined? What is the group structure?

**Expected**: Should connect to the theory of p-adic Lie groups and local Langlands correspondence.

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM.

## II. ANALYSIS AND DYNAMICS

### 9. SPB Approximation Rates ★★★
**Problem**: For a continuous function f on [−1, 1], determine the rate at which SPB trees of depth n can approximate f.

**Conjecture**: For analytic f, the rate is exponential: ‖f − T_n‖∞ ≤ C · r^n for some 0 < r < 1.

**Approach**: SPB trees generate tan(n·arctan(x)). Under the substitution x = tan(θ/2), these become Chebyshev-like functions. Use Jackson's theorem.

**Feasibility**: HIGH. Well-developed approximation theory.

### 10. Random SPB Iteration / Lyapunov Exponents ★★
**Problem**: For x_{n+1} = spb(x_n, a_n) with i.i.d. a_n drawn from distribution μ, determine:
(a) The invariant measure (is it always Cauchy?)
(b) When the Lyapunov exponent vanishes
(c) Ergodic properties

**Expected**: When μ is symmetric, the invariant measure is Cauchy. The Lyapunov exponent relates to the entropy of the distribution on S¹.

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM. Random Möbius transformations are studied but specific SPB results are new.

### 11. SPB Gradient Flow PDE ★
**Problem**: Study ∂u/∂t = spb(u, f(x,t)) = (u + f)/(1 − uf). Properties:
- Finite-time blowup when uf → 1
- Connection to Burgers equation via Wick rotation
- Shock formation and entropy conditions

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM. Requires PDE analysis.

### 12. SPB and Continued Fractions ★★
**Problem**: The SPB iteration x_{n+1} = spb(a_n, 1/x_n) is related to continued fraction expansion. Make this connection precise.

**Expected**: SPB continued fractions converge to quadratic irrationalities, connecting to Pell's equation.

**Feasibility**: HIGH.

### 13. SPB Fixed-Point Iteration Convergence ★
**Problem**: For the iteration x_{n+1} = spb(x_n, a_n) with shrinking perturbations |a_n| → 0, determine convergence rates and limit distributions.

**Feasibility**: HIGH. Standard iteration theory.

## III. PHYSICS

### 14. Thomas Precession as SPB Commutator ★★★
**Problem**: In 3D, express the Thomas-Wigner rotation angle θ_TW for two non-collinear boosts as:
spbH₃(v₁, v₂) = R(θ_TW) · spbH₃(v₂, v₁)

**Expected**: θ_TW = f(|v₁|, |v₂|, angle between v₁ and v₂) with explicit formula.

**Feasibility**: HIGH. The Thomas precession formula is known; expressing it in SPB language is a clean translation.

### 15. Bloch Sphere and Quantum Gates ★★
**Problem**: In stereographic coordinates on the Bloch sphere:
(a) Which single-qubit gates are SPB operations?
(b) Can a universal gate set be generated by SPB alone?
(c) What quantum channels correspond to SPB with complex parameters?

**Expected**: X, Y, Z rotations by angle α correspond to SPB with specific parameters. Universality requires at least two non-commuting SPB operations.

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM. Requires quantum information theory.

### 16. SPB in Thermodynamics ★
**Problem**: The Brillouin function for paramagnetism involves tanh. Since spbH composes tanh:
spbH(M₁/M_sat, M₂/M_sat) = ?

**Question**: What physical quantity does this represent? Conjecture: magnetization of coupled spin systems.

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM. Requires solid-state physics knowledge.

### 17. SPB and Gravitational Lensing ★
**Problem**: Light deflection by massive objects involves angle composition. Can gravitational lensing be expressed as SPB operations?

**Feasibility**: LOW-MEDIUM.

### 18. SPB in Optics: Jones Calculus ★★
**Problem**: Polarization optics uses 2×2 unitary matrices (Jones matrices). Since the Cayley transform maps SPB to S¹, there should be a direct SPB formulation of polarization composition.

**Feasibility**: HIGH.

## IV. COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING

### 19. SPB Neural Networks ★★★
**Problem**: Design neural network architectures using spb(x, y) as the neuron combining rule.

**Architecture**: SPB-neuron(x₁, ..., x_n) = spb(w₁x₁, spb(w₂x₂, ... spb(w_{n-1}x_{n-1}, w_nx_n)...))

**Advantages**:
- Monotonicity guaranteed (∂spb/∂x > 0)
- Circle group structure → natural for periodic data
- Self-normalizing via group compactness

**Challenges**: Singularity at xy = 1 requires regularization.

**Experiment Design**:
1. Compare SPB-net vs MLP on periodic regression (Fourier series)
2. Test on phase estimation tasks
3. Evaluate on rotation-equivariant problems

**Feasibility**: HIGH. Implementation is straightforward.

### 20. CORDIC-SPB Hardware ★★
**Problem**: Design a dedicated hardware unit for SPB computation that serves as a universal primitive for trigonometric operations.

**Approach**: CORDIC already iterates rotations; SPB makes this algebraic. A single SPB unit with shift-and-add implementation could replace lookup tables.

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM. Requires hardware design expertise.

### 21. SPB-Based Error Correction ★
**Problem**: Since the SPB group is isomorphic to S¹, errors in SPB computations can be detected by checking whether the result is consistent with the group structure (e.g., the Cayley transform should have unit norm).

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM.

### 22. SPB for Robotics ★★
**Problem**: Robot arm kinematics involves composing rotations. In 2D, this is exactly SPB. Can SPB provide more efficient rotation composition than matrix multiplication?

**Feasibility**: HIGH.

### 23. SPB Cryptography: Security Analysis ★
**Problem**: Perform a thorough security analysis of Diffie-Hellman key exchange over the SPB group of F_p. Specifically:
- Does the discrete log problem in SPB reduce to standard DLP?
- Are there index calculus attacks?
- How does the p±1 group order affect security?

**Expected**: The SPB group is isomorphic to a subgroup of F_{p²}*, so standard attacks likely apply. But the presentation may offer computational advantages.

**Feasibility**: HIGH. Standard cryptographic analysis.

### 24. SPB Signal Processing ★★
**Problem**: All-pass filters have transfer functions of the form (z−a)/(1−āz), which compose via SPB-like operations. Formalize this connection and use SPB tree optimization for filter cascade design.

**Feasibility**: HIGH. Direct application.

### 25. SPB Compression ★
**Problem**: Can SPB expression trees serve as a compression format for functions? A depth-k tree encodes O(2^k) function values. Compare to polynomial compression.

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM.

## V. CROSS-CUTTING DIRECTIONS

### 26. SPB-EML Bridge ★★★
**Problem**: SPB = (x+y)/(1−xy) bridges geometry; EML = exp(x) − ln(y) bridges arithmetic. What is the precise categorical relationship between them?

**Expected**: There should be a natural transformation between the SPB functor (acting on metric spaces) and the EML functor (acting on ordered fields).

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM. Requires category theory.

### 27. SPB in Machine Learning Theory ★★
**Problem**: The Cauchy distribution, which is SPB's natural measure, is a stable distribution (stable under convolution up to scale). This means SPB averages are "super-heavy-tailed." What implications does this have for:
(a) Stochastic gradient descent in SPB neural networks?
(b) Bayesian inference with Cauchy priors?

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM.

### 28. SPB Visualization Tools ★
**Problem**: Build interactive web-based tools for exploring SPB:
- Real-time SPB calculator with circle visualization
- Finite field SPB orbit explorer
- SPB expression tree builder

**Feasibility**: HIGH. Web development.

### 29. SPB Formal Library ★★
**Problem**: Develop a comprehensive Mathlib-style Lean 4 library for SPB, including:
- SPB as a bundled group
- Continuous group homomorphisms
- Haar measure construction
- SPB over general topological fields

**Feasibility**: MEDIUM. Requires Lean expertise.

### 30. SPB Textbook Chapter ★
**Problem**: Write a self-contained chapter on SPB suitable for an advanced undergraduate textbook on algebra or geometry.

**Feasibility**: HIGH.

## VI. SPECULATIVE / LONG-TERM

### 31. SPB and the Langlands Program ★
The connection SPB → SL(2) → automorphic forms → Langlands suggests deep number-theoretic implications. Very speculative but potentially groundbreaking.

### 32. SPB Quantum Field Theory ★
The Wick rotation in QFT is the same sign flip as in SPB. Can SPB provide a rigorous framework for certain Wick rotations in interacting field theories?

### 33. SPB and Topological Quantum Computing ★
Anyonic braiding involves representations of the braid group. Since SPB generates Möbius transformations and the Burau representation involves Möbius-type matrices, there may be connections.

### 34. SPB Category ★
Define a category where objects are fields and morphisms are SPB-compatible field extensions. Study its properties.

### 35. SPB and Information Geometry ★★
The Fisher information metric on the Cauchy family of distributions should be related to SPB geometry. Since the Cauchy distribution is SPB-invariant, this metric should have special properties.

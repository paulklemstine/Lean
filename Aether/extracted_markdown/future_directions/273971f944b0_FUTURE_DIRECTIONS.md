# Future Directions: Tropical Pontryagin–Mellin Duality

## Overview

The tropical harmonic analysis framework—tropical characters, Mellin transforms, and certified sparse decoding—opens five major research frontiers. Each direction is concrete, actionable, and builds directly on the formalized results.

---

## Direction 1: Tropical Plancherel Theory and Energy Conservation

### The Question
In classical harmonic analysis, the Plancherel theorem states that the Fourier transform preserves L²-norms: `‖f̂‖₂ = ‖f‖₂`. Is there an analogous energy-conservation identity for the tropical Mellin transform?

### Why It Matters
A tropical Plancherel theorem would provide quantitative control over the transform—not just that convolution is diagonalized, but that no information is lost or amplified. This is essential for:
- **Error analysis** in tropical signal processing
- **Stability guarantees** for sparse decoding
- **Bounds on tropical rank** via spectral methods

### Concrete Next Steps
1. **Define tropical norms:** The natural candidates are `‖f‖_trop = inf_s f(s)` (tropical L^∞ norm) and `‖f‖_sum = Σ f(s)` (tropical L¹ ≈ log-sum-exp). Investigate which norm is preserved.
2. **Prove for finite semirings:** Start with `S = (Fin n, min, +)` where the character space is explicit and finite. The identity should reduce to a combinatorial statement about min-plus matrix products.
3. **Formalize in Lean:** Extend the existing `mellinTransform` definition with norm computations and prove the identity.

### Expected Difficulty
Medium. The finite case should be tractable; the general case may require new ideas about tropical integration.

## Direction 2: Spectral Synthesis and Annihilator Theory

### The Question
Which functions on the character space `X(S)` arise as Mellin transforms of finitely supported functions on `S`? Equivalently: what is the image of the Mellin transform?

### Why It Matters
Spectral synthesis is the tropical analogue of the Paley-Wiener theorem (characterizing Fourier transforms of compactly supported functions). It answers:
- **Inverse transform existence:** When can a function on character space be "de-transformed"?
- **Approximation theory:** How well can arbitrary functions on `X(S)` be approximated by Mellin transforms?
- **Annihilator duality:** The annihilator of an ideal in `S` should correspond to a specific class of functions on `X(S)`.

### Concrete Next Steps
1. **Characterize the image for free semirings:** When `S` is freely generated by `g₁, …, gₙ`, the character space is `TropVal^n` and the Mellin transform becomes a tropical Legendre-Fenchel conjugate. The image should be the class of piecewise-linear convex functions (tropical polynomials).
2. **Define tropical polynomial functions** on `X(S)` and prove they equal the Mellin image.
3. **Prove an annihilator correspondence:** If `I ⊆ S` is an ideal, its annihilator `Ann(I) = {χ : χ(s) = ⊤ for all s ∈ I}` should correspond to the kernel of restriction.

### Expected Difficulty
High. The connection to tropical convexity and Legendre-Fenchel duality requires bridging abstract algebra with tropical geometry.

## Direction 3: Hardness and Algorithms for Tropical Decoding

### The Question
What is the computational complexity of tropical sparse decoding? Given a character matrix `A ∈ TropVal^{m×n}`, measurements `y ∈ TropVal^m`, and sparsity bound `k`, how hard is it to find the unique k-sparse `x` with `encode(x) = y`?

### Why It Matters
This is the algorithmic frontier of the theory. Understanding complexity determines whether tropical compressed sensing is practical and whether tropical cryptographic primitives are secure.

### Concrete Next Steps
1. **NP-hardness for generic matrices:** Prove that tropical k-sparse recovery from min-plus measurements is NP-hard in general. The reduction should go via tropical linear programming or min-plus matrix problems.
2. **Polynomial algorithms for structured matrices:** For Vandermonde-like, circulant, or Hankel character matrices, develop O(n log n) algorithms using tropical FFT techniques.
3. **Explicit nondegeneracy constructions:** Find deterministic constructions of m×n character matrices that are k-nondegenerate with m = O(k log(n/k)), matching the classical compressed sensing scaling.
4. **Average-case analysis:** What fraction of random character matrices are k-nondegenerate? Prove concentration bounds.

### Expected Difficulty
Very high for worst-case hardness (requires novel reductions). Medium for structured algorithms and random matrix analysis.

## Direction 4: Tropical Bochner Theorem and Positive-Definite Kernels

### The Question
In classical harmonic analysis, Bochner's theorem characterizes positive-definite functions as Fourier transforms of positive measures. Is there a tropical analogue? What does "positive-definite" mean in the min-plus world?

### Why It Matters
Tropical positive-definite kernels would provide:
- **Tropical kernel methods** for machine learning (min-plus kernel SVMs)
- **Tropical Gaussian processes** for probabilistic modeling
- **Representation theory** for tropical group algebras

### Concrete Next Steps
1. **Define tropical positive-definiteness:** A natural candidate: `K : S × S → TropVal` is positive-definite if for all finite sequences `s₁, …, sₙ` and coefficients `c₁, …, cₙ ∈ TropVal`, `inf_{i,j} (cᵢ + K(sᵢ,sⱼ) + cⱼ) ≥ 0`. (This is the tropical analogue of `Σ cᵢ c̄ⱼ K(sᵢ,sⱼ) ≥ 0`.)
2. **Prove the Bochner correspondence:** A kernel is positive-definite iff it is the Mellin transform of a "positive tropical measure" on the character space.
3. **Connect to tropical reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces** (or rather, tropical reproducing kernel semimodules).

### Expected Difficulty
High. Defining the correct notions requires careful algebra. The proof likely involves tropical convexity arguments.

## Direction 5: Closure-Aware Tropical Representation Theory

### The Question
How does the EML closure interact with the representation theory of semirings? Can we classify semiring representations (semimodule actions) via their spectral data (character decomposition)?

### Why It Matters
This is the deepest theoretical direction. A representation theory for idempotent semirings with closure would:
- **Unify tropical geometry with machine learning** (closure = regularization, characters = features)
- **Provide a Tannakian reconstruction theorem** (recover the semiring from its representations)
- **Enable tropical spectral graph theory** (decompose graph Laplacians tropically)

### Concrete Next Steps
1. **Define tropical semimodules with closure:** A semimodule `M` over `S` equipped with a compatible closure operator `cl_M`.
2. **Classify irreducible tropical semimodules:** Over the tropical semifield, irreducible modules should correspond to characters (as in classical representation theory).
3. **Prove a tropical Maschke theorem:** Under what conditions does every tropical semimodule decompose into irreducibles?
4. **Formalize the Tannakian correspondence:** The category of closure-continuous tropical representations should determine `S/∼_rad`.

### Expected Difficulty
Very high. This is a foundational project requiring significant new theory. The finite case (finite semirings) is the natural starting point.

# Future Directions: Tropical Modular Lensing Research Roadmap

## Breakthrough Opportunities (ranked by impact)

### 1. Cuspidal Factorization — Full Depth Verification

- **Theorem Statement**: For all Berggren words w ≠ [],
  `ω(hypotenuse(w).toNat) ≤ tropicalCriticalMultiplicity(berggrenPathMatrix w)`
  where ω counts distinct prime factors.
- **Proof Strategy**:
  1. Extend computational verification to depth 3-5 using `native_decide` or `decide`
  2. Use the monoid homomorphism `berggren_monoid_hom` to decompose path matrices
  3. Show that each prime factor of the hypotenuse introduces a "degeneracy" in the tropical det (multiple permutations achieving the same sum)
- **Why This Is Revolutionary**: Connects prime factorization (number theory) to tropical curve singularities (algebraic geometry) through an explicit, constructive correspondence
- **Catalog Leverage**: Build on `depth1_omega_le_critMult`, `berggren_monoid_hom`, `berggrenPathMatrix_det_prod`
- **Research Mode**: prove
- **Estimated Depth**: 4

### 2. Tropical Certified Robustness for General Architectures

- **Theorem Statement**: For any tropical neural network with L layers, each having weight matrix Mₗ and bias bₗ, the end-to-end Lipschitz constant in L∞ is ≤ 1.
  `∀ v w, linfDist (network.apply v) (network.apply w) ≤ linfDist v w`
- **Proof Strategy**:
  1. Generalize from Fin 3 to Fin n (arbitrary dimension)
  2. Use `tropical_layer_nonexpansive` and `maxplus_composition_nonexpansive` as base cases
  3. Prove by induction on the number of layers
- **Why This Is Revolutionary**: Gives the first fully formalized certified robustness guarantee for tropical neural networks, applicable to ReLU network analysis
- **Catalog Leverage**: `maxplus_matvec_lipschitz`, `tropical_layer_nonexpansive`, `abs_max_sub_max_le`
- **Research Mode**: formalize
- **Estimated Depth**: 2

### 3. Tropical Satake Isomorphism for GL(1)

- **Theorem Statement**: The tropical Hecke algebra on the Berggren tree (generated by T₃) is isomorphic as a max-plus algebra to the algebra of tropical symmetric polynomials in one variable.
- **Proof Strategy**:
  1. Define the tropical Satake map S: T₃ⁿ ↦ tropical symmetric polynomial
  2. Show S is a max-plus algebra homomorphism using `hecke_shift_equivariant` and `hecke_monotone`
  3. Prove injectivity via the eigenfunction decomposition
  4. Prove surjectivity by constructing preimages
- **Why This Is Revolutionary**: First formalized tropical analogue of the classical Satake isomorphism; opens the door to tropical automorphic forms
- **Catalog Leverage**: `depthEigenfunction`, `heckeT₃`, `constant_is_hecke_eigen`, `hecke_depth_eigenvalue`
- **Research Mode**: formalize
- **Estimated Depth**: 4

### 4. Berggren Tree Completeness

- **Theorem Statement**: Every primitive Pythagorean triple (a, b, c) with a odd appears exactly once as `pythTriple w` for some Berggren word w.
  `∀ a b c, a² + b² = c² → gcd a b = 1 → Odd a → ∃! w, pythTriple w = ![a, b, c]`
- **Proof Strategy**:
  1. Use well-founded induction on the hypotenuse c
  2. Show each of the three inverse Berggren matrices maps (a,b,c) to a triple with smaller c
  3. The base case is (3,4,5) at c = 5
  4. Uniqueness follows from injectivity of the Berggren matrices on the positive cone
- **Why This Is Revolutionary**: Completes the formal verification of Berggren's 1934 result
- **Catalog Leverage**: `berggren_root_pythagorean`, `depth1_all_pythagorean`, `berggrenPathMatrix_unimodular`
- **Research Mode**: prove
- **Estimated Depth**: 5

### 5. Tropical Perron-Frobenius Theory for Berggren Matrices

- **Theorem Statement**: Every Berggren path matrix has a unique (up to tropical scaling) max-plus eigenvector, and its eigenvalue equals the tropical spectral radius.
- **Proof Strategy**:
  1. Prove existence using the fixed-point theorem for max-plus contractions
  2. Prove uniqueness using the tropical Hilbert metric
  3. Connect eigenvalue to the maximum cycle mean of the matrix
- **Why This Is Revolutionary**: Establishes the spectral theory foundation for tropical lensing
- **Catalog Leverage**: `eigenA₂`, `A₂_maxplus_eigenvector`, `eigenA₂_eq_trace`
- **Research Mode**: formalize
- **Estimated Depth**: 3

## Under-explored Territory

### Tropical Discriminant Theory
- The tropical discriminant (GKZ discriminant) of the Berggren path matrix encodes the complete singularity structure. We have the critical multiplicity (number of achieving permutations) but haven't explored the full discriminant variety.

### Max-Plus Matrix Semigroup Structure
- The Berggren path matrices form a free semigroup under classical multiplication. Under max-plus multiplication, the structure is different — we haven't characterized it.

### Tropical Modular Curves
- The classical modular curve X₀(n) has cusps in bijection with divisors of n. Our tropical critical curves are combinatorial analogues, but the bijection hasn't been established.

### Hecke Eigenfunction Decomposition
- We've shown depth is a Hecke eigenfunction with eigenvalue 1. There should be a complete orthogonal basis of eigenfunctions (in the max-plus sense). Finding them would unlock the tropical spectral theory.

## Open Problems Encountered

1. **Is the cusp-factor inequality ω ≤ critMult universally true?**
   Verified for depth ≤ 2 (13 paths). The inequality holds in all cases but a proof for arbitrary depth is lacking.

2. **What is the exact distribution of critical multiplicities?**
   At depth 1: multiplicities are {1, 3, 3}. At depth 2: {1, 2, 2, 3, 1, 3, 2, 2, 1}. Is there a pattern?

3. **Can the tropical hash function `tropicalHash` be made collision-resistant?**
   We showed non-triviality but haven't analyzed collision resistance. The connection to tropical discriminant hardness is open.

4. **What is the max-plus eigenvalue of a general Berggren path matrix?**
   We found it for A₂ (eigenvalue 3, eigenvector (0,0,1)). The general case requires tropical Perron-Frobenius theory.

5. **Does the tropical Satake map extend to GL(2)?**
   Our Hecke operator acts on 1D functions on the tree. The GL(2) version would act on 2D matrix-valued functions — this is the tropical Langlands program for GL(2).

# Future Directions: Hyperbolic Number Theory

## Synthesis

This cycle established the rigorous foundations for arithmetic on the Poincaré disk. The Blaschke normSq identity — the algebraic engine powering disk preservation — was proved as a universal identity over ℂ, independent of any disk membership assumption. From it, we derived disk preservation, pseudo-distance symmetry, definiteness, and boundedness. The hyperbolic lattice structure theorem confirms that orbits under Blaschke factors stay within the disk, enabling systematic enumeration and counting.

The most promising cross-domain connection is between **hyperbolic lattice point counting** and **spectral theory**. The counting function N(R) encodes how lattice points distribute, and its growth rate is controlled by the eigenvalues of the Laplacian on the quotient surface Γ\𝔻. This connects our algebraic framework (Blaschke factors, normSq identities) to the analytic theory of automorphic forms and Selberg zeta functions — a bridge from `Geometry/HyperbolicNumberTheory.lean` to the modular forms infrastructure in `EML/ModularForms.lean`.

The direction with highest breakthrough potential is **Direction 2**: formalizing the Selberg trace formula for specific Fuchsian groups. This would connect hyperbolic prime counting directly to spectral data, making the "hyperbolic prime number theorem" a consequence of spectral gap estimates. The algebraic machinery (Blaschke factors, disk preservation) is now in place; what's needed is the analytic bridge.

### Direction 1: Hyperbolic Unique Factorization Domains

**Conjecture**: For the lattice generated by Blaschke factors φ_{1/2} and φ_{i/2} acting on the origin, every lattice point admits a unique factorization (up to ordering) as a composition of hyperbolic primes — i.e., the lattice forms a unique factorization domain under Blaschke composition.

**Test**: Enumerate all lattice points at depth ≤ 5. For each non-prime point p, find all decompositions p = φ_b(c) with b, c non-trivial. If any point has two essentially different decompositions (not related by reordering), the conjecture is false. Compute this for both commutative (Abelian) and non-commutative generator sets.

**Impact**: If true, this would be the first instance of a "geometric UFD" — a unique factorization theorem where the elements are geometric objects (points in the disk) and the operation is conformal composition. If false, the failure modes would illuminate which geometric properties obstruct unique factorization, potentially connecting to class group theory in algebraic number theory.

**Catalog References**: `Algebra/CyclicGroupSubgroups.lean` (cyclic_group_unique_subgroup_of_card), `Geometry/HyperbolicNumberTheory.lean` (HyperbolicLattice.IsPrime, blaschke_compose_in_disk)

**Proof Strategy**: 
1. Formalize the free monoid on generators and the quotient by Blaschke composition relations.
2. Show that for two generators with |a|, |b| < r for r sufficiently small, the Blaschke factors φ_a and φ_b "almost commute" and generate a free group (no non-trivial relations).
3. In the free case, unique factorization is trivial. The interesting case is when generators satisfy relations.
4. Key lemma needed: if φ_a(φ_b(z)) = φ_c(φ_d(z)) for all z, then (a,b) = (c,d) or there exists a non-trivial relation.

**Domain Bridges**: Algebra <-> Geometry, NumberTheory <-> Geometry

**Lineage**: Builds on blaschke_compose_in_disk and HyperbolicLattice.IsPrime from this cycle.

**Ambition**: extension

### Direction 2: Selberg Trace Formula for Finite-Area Quotients

**Conjecture**: For the modular group PSL(2,ℤ) acting on the Poincaré disk, the number of orbit points of the origin within geodesic distance T of the origin satisfies N(T) = (3/π) · e^T + O(e^{3T/4}), where the error term is controlled by the first eigenvalue λ₁ ≥ 3/16 of the Laplacian on PSL(2,ℤ)\𝔻.

**Test**: Compute N(T) for T = 1, 2, ..., 10 by enumerating PSL(2,ℤ)-orbits. Compare against (3/π)·e^T. The relative error |N(T) - (3/π)e^T| / e^T should decrease and be bounded by C·e^{-T/4} for some constant C.

**Impact**: This would be the first machine-verified statement connecting hyperbolic lattice point counting to spectral theory. It would validate the Selberg trace formula approach and open the door to formalizing the hyperbolic prime number theorem (counting primitive closed geodesics by length).

**Catalog References**: `EML/ModularForms.lean` (T_sq, S_gen, BM₃_inv), `Geometry/HyperbolicNumberTheory.lean` (blaschke_normSq_identity, hypPseudoDist_lt_one)

**Proof Strategy**:
1. Define the Selberg/Huber counting function N(T) = #{γ ∈ Γ : d(z₀, γz₀) ≤ T} for a Fuchsian group Γ.
2. Formalize the relationship between geodesic distance d and pseudo-distance δ: δ = tanh²(d/2).
3. State the Selberg trace formula as an axiom (or develop from spectral theory of the Laplacian on Γ\𝔻).
4. Derive the main term (3/π)e^T from the residue at s=1 of the resolvent.
5. Bound the error term using Selberg's 3/16 theorem (or the full Kim-Sarnak bound λ₁ ≥ 975/4096).

**Domain Bridges**: Geometry <-> NumberTheory, Geometry <-> Physics (via spectral theory)

**Lineage**: Builds on hypPseudoDist_symm, hypPseudoDist_lt_one, and the lattice orbit theorem from this cycle.

**Ambition**: grand_challenge

### Direction 3: Hyperbolic Zeta Function and Functional Equation

**Conjecture**: The hyperbolic zeta function Z_H(s) = Σ_{p primitive geodesic} Σ_{k=0}^∞ log(N(p)) · N(p)^{-(s+k)}, defined for the modular group, satisfies a functional equation relating Z_H(s) and Z_H(1-s), and its non-trivial zeros correspond to eigenvalues of the Laplacian via s(1-s) = λ.

**Test**: Compute the first 20 zeros of Z_H(s) numerically for PSL(2,ℤ). Verify that each zero s₀ satisfies Re(s₀) = 1/2 or s₀(1-s₀) is a Laplacian eigenvalue. Compare against known eigenvalues of the Laplacian on the modular surface (the first few are λ₁ ≈ 91.14, λ₂ ≈ 148.43, ...).

**Impact**: A formalized functional equation for the Selberg zeta function would be a landmark result connecting hyperbolic geometry to the deepest structures in analytic number theory. The explicit correspondence between zeros and eigenvalues would make the "Riemann Hypothesis for hyperbolic primes" equivalent to a spectral gap statement — a potentially more tractable formulation.

**Catalog References**: `FINAL/Algebra/Foundations.lean` (critical_line_implies_unit_disk), `Bridges/ProofStoneCechDynamics.lean` (fixed_point_unique_under_theory_separation), `Geometry/HyperbolicNumberTheory.lean` (hyperbolicGrowthConjecture)

**Proof Strategy**:
1. Define the Selberg zeta function Z_Γ(s) as a product over primitive conjugacy classes.
2. Formalize the Euler product representation and its convergence for Re(s) > 1.
3. Prove meromorphic continuation using the trace formula.
4. Derive the functional equation from the symmetry of the trace formula kernel.
5. Key lemma: the zeros of Z_Γ on the critical strip correspond bijectively to eigenvalues via s(1-s) = λ.

**Domain Bridges**: Geometry <-> NumberTheory, Algebra <-> Physics

**Lineage**: Builds on critical_line_implies_unit_disk from the Catalog and the hyperbolic pseudo-distance framework from this cycle.

**Ambition**: grand_challenge

### Direction 4: Hyperbolic Embeddings for Hierarchical Data

**Conjecture**: For any tree T with n vertices embedded optimally in the Poincaré disk (minimizing total hyperbolic edge length), the set of vertex positions forms a hyperbolic lattice whose counting function N(R) grows as Θ(log n · R) for pseudo-distance R, and the hyperbolic primes of this lattice correspond to the leaves of T.

**Test**: Generate random binary trees with n = 100, 500, 2000 vertices. Compute optimal Poincaré disk embeddings using the Sarkar algorithm. Extract vertex positions, compute pseudo-distances, identify hyperbolic primes, and check whether primes are exactly the leaves.

**Impact**: This would provide a rigorous number-theoretic foundation for hyperbolic embeddings in machine learning. If primes correspond to leaves, then "factoring" a lattice point (finding its prime decomposition) corresponds to tracing a path from root to leaf — connecting Blaschke composition to tree navigation.

**Catalog References**: `Geometry/HyperbolicNumberTheory.lean` (HyperbolicLattice, IsPrime, orbit_in_disk), `MachineLearning/HyperbolicNumberTheory/Theorems.lean` (critical_line_to_disk)

**Proof Strategy**:
1. Formalize the Sarkar embedding algorithm: recursively embed subtrees using Blaschke factors centered at appropriate disk points.
2. Show that the embedding satisfies the lattice axioms (all points in disk, generated by Blaschke factors).
3. Prove that internal vertices admit non-trivial decompositions (they are Blaschke compositions of their children's positions).
4. Prove that leaves have no non-trivial decompositions (they are the minimal elements).
5. Key lemma: the distortion of hyperbolic embeddings of trees is bounded by O(log n).

**Domain Bridges**: Geometry <-> MachineLearning, NumberTheory <-> Computation

**Lineage**: Builds on the HyperbolicLattice structure and orbit_in_disk from this cycle, connects to the ML-geometry bridge in the Catalog.

**Ambition**: extension

### Direction 5: Arithmetic of Quaternionic Blaschke Factors in 3D Hyperbolic Space

**Conjecture**: The Blaschke factor construction generalizes to the 3-dimensional hyperbolic ball 𝔹³ ⊂ ℍ (quaternions with |q| < 1) via φ_a(q) = (q - a)(1 - ā q)⁻¹, where ā is quaternionic conjugation. The resulting "3D hyperbolic integers" admit a factorization theory where non-commutativity of quaternion multiplication leads to non-unique factorization, with the class number measuring the failure of uniqueness.

**Test**: Implement quaternionic Blaschke factors. Generate the lattice for generators a₁ = i/2, a₂ = j/2, a₃ = k/3 in 𝔹³. Verify disk preservation (|φ_a(q)| < 1). Search for non-trivially distinct factorizations of the same lattice point.

**Impact**: Extending hyperbolic number theory to 3 dimensions would connect to arithmetic of quaternion algebras (Vignéras, 1980), hyperbolic 3-manifolds, and the Thurston geometrization program. The class number measuring factorization failure could be related to topological invariants of the quotient manifold.

**Catalog References**: `Cryptography/BerggrenDiophantineLattice.lean` (lorentzForm), `Geometry/HyperbolicNumberTheory.lean` (blaschke_normSq_identity, blaschke_maps_disk)

**Proof Strategy**:
1. Define quaternionic normSq and verify it is multiplicative: normSq(pq) = normSq(p)·normSq(q).
2. Prove the quaternionic Blaschke identity: normSq(q-a) - normSq(1-āq) = (normSq(q)-1)(1-normSq(a)).
3. Derive disk preservation for 𝔹³.
4. Show that non-commutativity creates pairs (a,b) ≠ (a',b') with φ_a∘φ_b = φ_{a'}∘φ_{b'} on 𝔹³.
5. Define the class number as the size of the quotient of the factorization monoid by the free monoid.

**Domain Bridges**: Geometry <-> Algebra, NumberTheory <-> Cryptography

**Lineage**: Direct generalization of blaschke_normSq_identity and blaschke_maps_disk from ℂ to ℍ.

**Ambition**: extension

# Future Directions — Functorial Species → Tropical Valuation Profiles

Follow-up conjectures arising from `Catalog/Bridges/SpeciesTropicalValuationProfiles.lean`,
which established that the species (Day-convolution) product is a **lax min-plus monoidal
functor** into the tropical semiring `(ℕ∞, min, +)`:

    min_{i+j=n} (valProfile p a i + valProfile p b j)  ≤  valProfile p (a ⋆ b) n,      (★)

and that the EGF subtracts the factorial (Legendre) profile:
`v_p(aₙ/n!) = v_p(aₙ) − v_p(n!)`.

The lower bound (★) is genuinely one-sided; the gap is controlled entirely by the `p`-adic
valuations of the binomial coefficients `C(n,i)`. The directions below are precise and
testable (each is stated so it can be turned directly into a Lean `theorem ... := by sorry`).

---

## Conjecture 1 — Kummer-exact defect formula for the tropical gap

The defect in (★) is exactly the Kummer carry count. Define the defect
`δ p a b n = valProfile p (binConvNat a b) n − inf_{i+j=n}(valProfile p a i + valProfile p b j)`.

**Conjecture.** When the infimum in (★) is attained at a *unique* index `i₀` with finite
valuations, then
`δ = emultiplicity p (C(n,i₀))`,
i.e. the gap equals the number of carries when adding `i₀` and `n−i₀` in base `p`
(Kummer's theorem). In particular, for `p` larger than `n` the functor (★) is **exact**
(`δ = 0`), because all `C(n,i)` are then `p`-adic units.

*Testable corollary (easy first target):* for every prime `p > n`,
`valProfile p (binConvNat a b) n = inf_{i+j=n}(valProfile p a i + valProfile p b j)`.

---

## Conjecture 2 — Tropical idempotent / additivity for the species `E` of sets

The species of sets `E` has constant counting sequence `aₙ = 1`, so `valProfile p E ≡ 0`
(the tropical multiplicative unit). Its self-convolution is `binConvNat 1 1 = (n ↦ 2ⁿ)`.

**Conjecture.** `valProfile p (E ⋆ E) n = emultiplicity p (2ⁿ)`, which is `n` for `p = 2` and
`0` for odd `p`. More generally the `k`-fold convolution `E^{⋆k}` counts `kⁿ`, giving
`valProfile p (E^{⋆k}) n = n · emultiplicity p k`. This identifies the **tropical exponential
law** `valProfile (E^{⋆k}) = n ↦ n·v_p(k)` and shows the lax functor (★) is *strict* (an
equality) on the sub-semiring generated by `E` precisely at primes `p ∤ k`.

---

## Conjecture 3 — Derivative species lower the tropical profile by a bounded shift

The derivative species `F′` has `F′ₙ = F_{n+1}` (`Species.derivative`), with EGF the formal
derivative. On profiles this is the shift `valProfile p F′ n = valProfile p F (n+1)`.

**Conjecture (Leibniz tropical inequality).** For the structural product,
`valProfile p ((F·G)′) n ≥ min(valProfile p (F′·G) n, valProfile p (F·G′) n)`,
the tropical shadow of the species product rule `(F·G)′ = F′·G + F·G′`
(`CombinatorialSpecies.binConv_leibniz`). Equality holds whenever the two profiles
`valProfile p (F′·G)` and `valProfile p (F·G′)` differ at index `n` (ultrametric
"isosceles triangle" principle).

---

## Conjecture 4 — Eventual periodicity / linear growth of valuation profiles of P-recursive species

Many natural species are P-recursive (their counting sequences satisfy a linear recurrence
with polynomial coefficients): linear orders `n!`, permutations, set partitions, etc.

**Conjecture.** For a fixed prime `p`, the tropical valuation profile `n ↦ valProfile p aₙ`
of a P-recursive counting sequence is *eventually* governed by a Legendre-type formula: there
exist constants `c, d ∈ ℚ` with `valProfile p aₙ = ⌊c·n⌋ + O(log_p n)` as `n → ∞`. For the
species of linear orders this is exactly de Polignac's formula
`v_p(n!) = (n − s_p(n))/(p − 1)`, so `c = 1/(p−1)` and the `O(log_p n)` term is the
digit-sum `s_p(n)/(p−1)`. The conjecture asserts this *linear-plus-sublinear* shape persists
for all holonomic species.

---

## Conjecture 5 — Tropical profiles separate species up to a finite set of primes

The EGF is a complete invariant (`Species.EGF_inj`): equal EGFs ⇒ equal counting sequences.
The valuation profile is a *coarser* (tropical) invariant.

**Conjecture (finite tropical reconstruction).** Two counting sequences `a, b : ℕ → ℕ` with
`aₙ, bₙ > 0` for all `n` satisfy `a = b` iff `valProfile p a = valProfile p b` for **all**
primes `p`. Moreover, restricting to the finitely many primes `p ≤ N` already determines
`aₙ` for all `n ≤ N` (since `aₙ = ∏_{p ≤ N} p^{v_p(aₙ)}` once `aₙ ≤ N!` is known). This would
make the family of tropical profiles `{valProfile p}_p` a *complete* invariant, recovering the
EGF/analytic invariant from purely tropical (min-plus) data — a tropical-to-analytic
reconstruction theorem dual to `CategoricalTropicalUltrametric`'s valuation reconstruction.

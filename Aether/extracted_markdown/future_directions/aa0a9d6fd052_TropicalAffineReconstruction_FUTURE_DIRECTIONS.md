# Future Directions: Algebraic–Tropical Canonical Evaluation

The file `Catalog/Bridges/TropicalAffineReconstruction.lean` establishes the
min-plus semiring of finite affine-piece presentations: evaluation `teval` of a
`Finset` of affine forms into `WithTop ℝ`, with `insert`/`∪` realising tropical
addition (pointwise `min`), the Minkowski-style product `TropPoly.mul` realising
tropical multiplication (pointwise `+`), the distributive law, the semiring
identities (`∅ ↦ ⊤`, `{one} ↦ 0`), monotonicity, affine-form injectivity,
single-step domination normalization, and a strong-induction normalization step
that discards *all* dominated pieces without changing the function. These results
turn the blocked tropical evaluator of `Tropical/Canonical/Basic.lean` into a
total, algebraic, reconstruction-friendly object that parallels the finite-probe
reconstruction machinery of `Bridges/AlgebraEMLReconstruction.lean`.

The directions below are concrete, falsifiable next steps. Each could be the spine
of the next research cycle.

## 1. Uniqueness of the reduced (essential) canonical form

We proved that filtering out dominated pieces preserves the evaluation
(`TropPoly.teval_filter_essential`). The natural strengthening is *uniqueness*:
two presentations with the same evaluation function whose pieces are **all
essential** (no piece is dominated) must be the *same finite set* of affine forms.
This is the exact min-plus analogue of `canonical_tropical_poly_unique` in
`Tropical/Canonical/Basic.lean`, but for multivariate min-plus polynomials rather
than univariate max-plus ones, and stated set-theoretically rather than
list-theoretically.

The key insight is that an essential piece must *strictly* achieve the infimum on
a full-dimensional region of `ℝⁿ` (its "tropical cell"), and these cells are
determined by the evaluation function alone; combined with affine-form
injectivity (`AffineForm.eval_injective`, already proved) this should pin down the
piece exactly, so the essential support is an invariant of the function.

Why now? The hard half — that discarding dominated pieces is evaluation-preserving
— is already formalized, and `AffineForm.eval_injective` is in hand; only the
"essential pieces are forced" direction remains, which is a clean cell-geometry
argument rather than new infrastructure.

## 2. The semiring of tropical-polynomial *functions* is an honest commutative semiring

We showed `teval` sends `∪ ↦ min`, `mul ↦ +`, `∅ ↦ ⊤`, `{one} ↦ 0`, and proved
distributivity at the level of evaluations. The next step is to package the image
`{ teval P : P : TropPoly n }` (the tropical-polynomial functions) as a bundled
`CommSemiring` under pointwise `min`/`+`, and exhibit `teval` as a surjective
`RingHom`-like structure (a semiring homomorphism) from `(TropPoly n, ∪, mul)`.

The key insight is that all the semiring axioms reduce, via the homomorphism
identities already proved, to pointwise identities in the *target* semiring
`WithTop ℝ` (which is a known idempotent semiring), so no axiom needs to be
re-derived on the presentation side — they are transported through `teval`.

Why now? Every structural identity the bundling needs
(`teval_union`, `teval_mul`, `teval_empty`, `teval_one`, `teval_distrib`) is
already a theorem in the file; the work is assembling a `CommSemiring` instance and
a homomorphism record, which is bookkeeping on top of finished mathematics.

## 3. Monotone/convex-geometric reconstruction: pieces from the Newton polytope

Each affine piece `(const, weight)` is a point in `ℝ × ℝⁿ`. The evaluation
function `teval P` is concave and piecewise-linear, and its *regions of linearity*
are governed by the lower convex hull of the points `{(weight a, -const a)}`. The
direction is to prove that the essential pieces of `P` are exactly the vertices of
this lower hull, giving a polytope-theoretic reconstruction of the canonical form.

The key insight is that domination in our sense (`b.eval ≤ a.eval` pointwise) is
equivalent to the point of `a` lying weakly above the lower envelope generated by
the other points — i.e. domination is precisely non-extremality for the lower
convex hull — so the combinatorics of canonicalization becomes the combinatorics
of convex hull vertices.

Why now? Mathlib has substantial convexity and `WithTop`/`EReal` support, and our
`TropPoly.Dominated` predicate is the right bridge notion; recasting it as an
extreme-point condition connects this cycle's algebra directly to existing
convex-geometry API without inventing new definitions.

## 4. Tropical Positivstellensatz / nonnegativity certificates

For ordinary polynomials, nonnegativity has sum-of-squares certificates. The
tropical analogue: when is `teval P x ≤ teval Q x` for *all* `x` (a global
domination of one tropical polynomial by another)? Conjecture: this holds iff
every essential piece of `Q` is dominated, in the per-piece sense, by some
*tropical product* of essential pieces of `P` (a certificate of bounded size).

The key insight is that global inequality between concave PL functions is a
finite, polyhedral condition, so it must admit a finite certificate expressible
purely in the semiring `(TropPoly n, ∪, mul)` — a min-plus Positivstellensatz —
rather than requiring a quantifier over all `x`.

Why now? We already have the local-to-global engine in miniature
(`teval_filter_essential` reduces global questions to essential pieces) and
`teval_mul` gives the product side of certificates; the remaining content is
turning a `∀ x` statement into an existential over a finite certificate, the exact
shape of reconstruction theorems this catalog specializes in.

## 5. Effective complexity of canonicalization and decision of equality

Make the normalization *algorithmic and bounded*: prove that the reduced form is
reached after at most `|P|` domination-erasures, that the reduced form has size at
most `|P|`, and that equality of two tropical-polynomial functions is decidable by
comparing reduced forms (combining Direction 1's uniqueness with Direction 3's
hull description). State and prove an explicit `|P|·poly(n)` style bound.

The key insight is that the strong-induction measure already used in
`teval_filter_essential` — the count of pieces dominating a given piece — is itself
a termination/complexity certificate, so the proof of correctness and the
complexity bound come from the *same* well-founded measure.

Why now? The induction measure is implemented and verified inside
`teval_filter_essential`; exposing it as a standalone `reduce : TropPoly n →
TropPoly n` with a cardinality bound is a refactor of existing content into an
effective procedure, closing the loop from "blocked sorry" to "certified
algorithm" promised by the bridge concept.

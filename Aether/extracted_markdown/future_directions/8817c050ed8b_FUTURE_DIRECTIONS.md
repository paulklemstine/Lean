## Conjecture 1 — Convolution is tight along geodesic gluings

**Statement (testable).** For a glued proof DAG `D = D₁ ⋆_a D₂` (the conclusions of
`D₁` re-used as premises feeding a node `a` of `D₂`), the depth satisfies not only the
upper convolution bound `d(v) ≤ d(a) + ℓ(a→v)` (already proved as `depthOf_convolution`)
but **equality** `d(v) = min_a (d(a) + ℓ(a→v))` whenever the gluing introduces no edge
shortcuts bypassing `a`.

**The key insight is** that `depthOf_convolution` is exactly tropical (min-plus) matrix
multiplication `d_D = d_{D₁} ⊗ A_{D₂}`, so depth of a glued proof should be the genuine
tropical product of component valuations, not merely bounded by it.

**Why now?** The one-sided bound is already formalized and the min-plus algebra target
(`tropicalization_base`) is in place; closing the gap to equality only needs a
"no-shortcut" hypothesis and an extremal-path witness, both finite and decidable.

## Conjecture 2 — Compression strictly shrinks tropical radius iff it merges a deepest node

**Statement (testable).** A surjective depth-non-increasing compression `φ : D → D'`
satisfies `D'.radius < D.radius` **iff** every node attaining `D.radius` is identified by
`φ` with a strictly shallower node. (`radius_compression` gives the `≤` direction; this
sharpens it to a characterization of strict decrease.)

**The key insight is** that the tropical radius is a max-plus sup, so it can only drop
when the *argmax* fibre is collapsed — radius reduction is a purely local event at the
deepest derivations.

**Why now?** `FinProofDAG.radius` and `radius_compression` are formalized and the
`Finset.sup` API makes argmax fibres directly accessible; the iff is a finite
combinatorial refinement of an already-proved monotonicity.

## Conjecture 3 — The depth ultrametric embeds isometrically into a tropical line

**Statement (testable).** The depth pseudo-ultrametric `pdist` (proved ultrametric in
`pdist_strong_triangle`) is realized by a single map `ι : D.V → ℕ`, `ι = D.depth`, with
`pdist u v = max (ι u) (ι v)` off-diagonal; consequently the metric quotient of
`(D.V, pdist)` embeds isometrically into the tropical line `(ℕ, tropicalization_base)`
as an ultrametric subspace whose diameter equals `D.radius`.

**The key insight is** that depth is simultaneously a tropical *valuation* and an
ultrametric *radius coordinate*, so the whole proof-node space is an ultrametric ball of
radius `D.radius` around the axioms — proof geometry is one-dimensional in the tropical
sense.

**Why now?** `pdist`, `pdist_diam_le_radius`, and the reconstruction functor
`valuationReconstruct` are already wired together (`depth_aggregate_ultrametric`);
upgrading the bound to an isometric embedding statement is the natural next theorem.

## Conjecture 4 — In-degree controls a global tropical-zero stratification

**Statement (testable).** Generalizing `depth_zero_of_relInDegree_zero`: for each `k`,
the nodes of depth `≤ k` are exactly those reachable within `k` edges from the
zero-in-degree (axiom) stratum, so the depth filtration `{v : d(v) ≤ k}` is generated by
iterated out-neighbourhoods of `{v : relInDegree D.E v = 0}`. Hence the entire min-plus
profile is recoverable from the in-degree-zero set plus the edge relation.

**The key insight is** that the combinatorial statistic `relInDegree` (graph domain) and
the min-plus valuation (tropical domain) are two faces of one object: the in-degree-zero
set is the tropical-zero stratum, and BFS layers are its tropical dilations.

**Why now?** The base case (`k = 0`) is the formalized cross-domain bridge theorem; the
inductive layer statement reuses `reachLE` and `depthOf_edge_le`, both already proved.

## Conjecture 5 — Functorial transfer of certified ultrametric robustness to proofs

**Statement (testable).** The functor `DAGHom.toTropHom` composes with the
`tropicalization`/`valuationReconstruct` pipeline of
`Bridges/CategoricalTropicalUltrametric.lean` to send a depth-non-increasing proof
compression to a norm-nonexpansive `UltraHom`, so any certified ultrametric robustness
radius proven downstream pulls back to a *proof-complexity* robustness radius: small
edits to a proof DAG cannot blow up the reconstructed ultrametric norm of its depths.

**The key insight is** that "valuation reconstruction is a quantitative functor" (the
core message of the catalog bridge file) applies verbatim when the source valuation is a
proof-DAG depth profile — proof compression becomes a nonexpansive map of certified
ultrametric objects.

**Why now?** `DAGHom.toTropHom` (functor laws proved), `valuationReconstruct`, and
`UltraHom` nonexpansiveness all exist; the missing step is one composite functor and its
nonexpansiveness lemma, a direct chaining of already-formalized pieces.

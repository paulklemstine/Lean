# Summary of changes
## ECSTATIC MACHINE — Infinite Algorithmic Groove

Created `music-machine.html` — a fully self-contained single-page app (90KB, zero dependencies) that generates live algorithmic electronic music. Just open it in any modern browser.

### Features

**🎵 30+ Genre Algorithms**
House, Deep House, Tech House, Techno, Hard Techno, Acid Techno, Minimal, Trance, Psytrance, Dubstep, Riddim, Drum & Bass, Jungle, Trap, Phonk, Hardwave, Wave, EDM, EBM, Industrial, Breakbeat, UK Garage, Grime, Dub Techno, Ambient, Lo-Fi, Synthwave, Electro, Footwork, Reggaeton, Afrobeat, Baile Funk — each with genre-authentic drum patterns, BPM ranges, swing amounts, and energy profiles.

**🎹 Synthesis Engines**
- **2x TB-303 Acid Synths** — sawtooth/square oscillators, resonant lowpass filter with envelope mod, decay, accent, and soft saturation drive. Slide and accent per step.
- **TR-909 Drum Machine** — 16 drum voices (kick, snare, clap, closed/open hat, percussion, rim, crash, ride, toms, sub, FX, cowbell) with tunable parameters. Kick has pitch envelope + click transient + drive shaping. Snare has noise + body layering with snap control. Hi-hats use 6 detuned square waves for metallic quality. Clap is multi-layered.
- **Pad Synth** — 3 detuned oscillators with slow attack/release envelopes and lowpass filter
- **Bass Synth** — oscillator + sub-oscillator with filter envelope
- **Arpeggiator** — up/down/updown/random modes, 1-4 octave range, adjustable rate (1/4 to 1/32) and gate length

**🎛️ Roland Groovebox-Style Controls**
All synths have interactive rotary knobs (click & drag) modeled after the MC-505: Cutoff, Resonance, Envelope Mod, Decay, Accent, Drive, Attack, Release, Detune, Tune, Snap, and more. Step-indicator LEDs track playback position.

**🥁 Live Sequencer**
- 16-step drum grid with 16 instrument rows — click to toggle steps in real time
- Melodic note grid for each synth track — click to place/remove notes
- Pattern tabs: TR-909, 303-I, 303-II, Bass, Arp, Pad
- Piano roll visualization showing all melodic tracks color-coded

**🔊 Master FX Chain**
- Convolution reverb, sync'd delay with feedback, dynamics compressor, soft-clip limiter (prevents ALL clipping), master filter sweep, optional distortion
- Each FX has toggle on/off + amount control
- Signal chain: instruments → filter → compressor → limiter → master gain — ensures clean output at all levels

**🤖 AI Conductor System**
- **👍/👎 Feedback** — adjusts genre preference weights, immediately regenerates patterns on dislike
- **⚡ Energy controls** — More/Less buttons dynamically adjust pattern density
- **💥 Drop / 📈 Build** — trigger instant drops or tension builds with filter sweeps
- **🎲 Surprise** — AI-weighted random genre switch
- **🔁 Loop** — lock current section
- **Mood slider** — dark ↔ euphoric (controls chord progressions: dark/melancholic/driving/dreamy/euphoric)
- **Energy slider** — chill ↔ rave (controls drum density, note density)
- **Complexity slider** — minimal ↔ complex (ghost notes, fills, hat rolls)
- AI tracks confidence score and learning progress

**🎼 Music Theory Engine**
- 14 scales: Minor, Major, Dorian, Phrygian, Mixolydian, Harmonic Minor, Melodic Minor, Pentatonic, Blues, Chromatic, Whole Tone, Arabian, Japanese, Hungarian Minor
- All 12 keys
- 6 chord progression types mapped to mood axis
- Scale-quantized melodic generation with acid runs, arp patterns, bass lines
- 7-section song structure: intro → build → drop → break → build2 → drop2 → outro with seamless transitions

**🎚️ 10-Channel Mixer**
Kick, Snare, Hats, Perc, 303-I, 303-II, Pad, Arp, Bass, Master — each with volume fader and mute button

**📊 Real-time Visualizer**
- Frequency spectrum bars with waveform overlay
- Song progress bar, section/bar/genre/key info display

**⌨️ Controls**
- Space = Play/Pause, Escape = Stop, R = Regenerate patterns
- Tap Tempo button for manual BPM, AUTO toggle for algorithmic section progression

### How to Use
Open `music-machine.html` in Chrome/Firefox/Edge. Click PLAY (or press Space). Select a genre. Use AI feedback buttons to guide the music toward what gives you goosebumps. Tweak synth knobs, edit the sequencer grid, and let the infinite jukebox ride.

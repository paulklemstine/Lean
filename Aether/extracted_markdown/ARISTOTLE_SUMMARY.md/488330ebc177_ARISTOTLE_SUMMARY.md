Delivered a complete, self-contained Lean 4 formalization of a finite information-theoretic surrogate for integrated information (IIT Φ) inspired by tensor networks, in the new file `Catalog/Physics/IntegratedInformationFinite.lean`. The development is entirely about the IIT/tensor-network surrogate and is fully finite, explicit, and (apart from real division) computable. As the module docstring states explicitly, since full Shannon-entropy mutual information is heavy, the surrogate used is the mathematically precise factorization proxy: a state has zero integration across a cut exactly when its joint weight equals the product of its two marginals (this is `I(L;R) = 0` ⇔ joint = product of marginals).

The `productWeight` (bond-dimension-one) state is defined, and the main theorems `productWeight_chainCutIndependent` (product states factorize across every cut) and `productWeight_chainMinPhiZero` (hence minimal integration is zero) are proved.

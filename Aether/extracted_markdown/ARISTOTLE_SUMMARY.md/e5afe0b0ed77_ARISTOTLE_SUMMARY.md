# EML Differential Algebra: Closure Properties

## Summary

I completed a full research cycle investigating whether the EML (Exponential-Multiplicative-Logarithmic) function class forms a differential algebra. The results are formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib and include all required deliverables.

## Key Discovery

The standard EML class (generated by exp, log under +, ×, composition) is **NOT** closed under differentiation — d/dx[log(x)] = 1/x escapes the class. Adding the reciprocal (multiplicative inverse) operation is the **minimal fix** that achieves differential closure.

## Novel Mathematical Structure: The EML Derivation Calculus

I introduced the **EML Derivation Calculus** — a symbolic expression algebra (`Expr`) equipped with a verified differentiation operator (`sdiff`). Key components:

- **8 expression constructors**: `cnst`, `var`, `add`, `mul`, `neg`, `inv`, `eexp`, `elog`
- **Symbolic differentiation** (`sdiff`): Maps expressions to expressions, implementing all standard rules
- **Derivation Tower**: The sequence of iterated symbolic derivatives, capturing differential complexity
- **Inv/Log-Free Fragment** (`NoInvLog`): A structural invariant preserved by differentiation, enabling unconditional validity

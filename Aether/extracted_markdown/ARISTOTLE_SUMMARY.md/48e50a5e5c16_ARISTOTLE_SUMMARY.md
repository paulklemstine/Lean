Contents:
- A bespoke valuation class `AddVal K` over a `CommSemiring K`, valued in `WithTop ℤ`, with exactly the needed axioms: `v 0 = ⊤`, `v (a*b) = v a + v b`, and `min (v a) (v b) ≤ v (a + b)`.
- `Seq K := ℕ → K` (with the standard pointwise `Pi` addition), the binomial/Day convolution `binConv` (notation `⋆`), the valuation profile `vprofile`, and the tropical convolutions `tropBinConv` (weighted by binomial-scalar valuations) and `tropConv` (unweighted), both as finite `inf'` over `Finset.range (n+1)`.
- Theorem A `vprofile_add_ge`: `min (vprofile f n) (vprofile g n) ≤ vprofile (f + g) n`.
- Lemma B `le_val_sum`: a reusable finite-sum lower bound (every summand has valuation ≥ α ⟹ the sum does), proved by induction on the finset.
- Theorem C `vprofile_binConv_ge`: the valuation profile of the binomial convolution is bounded below by the weighted tropical convolution `tropBinConv`, packaged as an infimum/min statement over `range (n+1)` (supporting lemmas `val_binConv_term`, `tropBinConv_le_term`). I used the infimum form, which the request explicitly permits; the literal per-k "≥ each term" form is not generally true for a valuation of a sum (only ≥ the minimum over terms holds).
- Corollary D `vprofile_binConv_ge_simple`: under `0 ≤ v (n.choose k)` for each k, the unweighted tropical convolution `tropConv` is a lower bound.
- Theorem E `vprofile_lax_monoidal`: the minimal package combining A and C, expressing coefficientwise lax monoidality (addition ↦ tropical `min` lower bound, convolution ↦ tropical convolution lower bound).

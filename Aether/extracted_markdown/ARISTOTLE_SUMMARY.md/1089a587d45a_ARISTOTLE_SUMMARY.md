### Novel Mathematical Structure: `ApproxTower`

A **depth-filtered tower of subalgebras** with:
- Monotone filtration (deeper levels contain shallower ones)
- Separation at the colimit (the union separates points)
- **`ApproxTower.colimit_dense`** — The colimit is always dense
- **`ApproxTower.exists_level_approx`** — ε-approximation exists at some finite depth
- **`ApproxTower.Morphism`** — Structure-preserving maps between towers

### Central Discovery

I proved a novel group-theoretic characterization of musical consonance: **a consonant interval in twelve-tone equal temperament is "perfect" (parallel motion forbidden in Fux's counterpoint) if and only if its additive order in ℤ/12ℤ is extreme—either 1 (trivial) or 12 (maximal)**. This connects the medieval classification of consonances to the subgroup structure of cyclic groups.

### Key Finding: Conjecture Refuted, Better Structure Found

The original conjecture proposed a thin category on 12 elements. The actual structure is a **4-element diamond lattice** of cyclic subgroups—simpler but more meaningful. The diamond captures the algebraic hierarchy: trivial (unison) → diminished seventh / augmented triad (imperfect) → full chromatic group (perfect fifth).

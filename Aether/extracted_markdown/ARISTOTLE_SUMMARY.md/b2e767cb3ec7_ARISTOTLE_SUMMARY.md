### Structural insight
Both showcase models have a rank-one move lattice generated by a single alternating sign pattern; this collapses the deep algebraic content (toric ideals / Gröbner bases) into a finite linear-algebra fact, and reduces fiber connectivity to **discrete convexity**: the non-negative locus along a move line is an integer interval, so a monotone unit-step walk never leaves the non-negative orthant.

### Critique / generalization (recorded in FUTURE_DIRECTIONS.md)
Rank-one is special: the `2×2×n` model (declared as the `TableN` type) has a multi-generator move lattice where the single-line walk breaks down. The notes lay out 5 testable directions, including the conjectured slice-pair Markov basis for `2×2×n`, the ideal-free Diaconis–Sturmfels equivalence, a Graver-basis walk-length bound, an expected negative result (simple `2×2` swaps are *not* a Markov basis here), and that `Connected` is an equivalence relation whose classes are exactly the fibers.

Faithfulness note: while writing the demo I discovered the GL₂ irreducible-rate formula `q/(2(q+1))` matches exact enumeration perfectly, but the special-linear rate `irreducibleRateSL2 = (q-1)/(2q)` defined in the source is a closed-form *model* — the exact enumerated SL₂ rate is the slightly smaller `(q-1)/(2(q+1))`. The verified separation theorems concern the defined quantities and remain valid, and crucially both the modeled and exact SL₂ rates lie strictly below the GL₂ rate, so the separation conclusion is robust. I documented this transparency point in the article, paper, and demo rather than overstating the match. No `.lean` files were added or modified, and no new proofs were introduced.

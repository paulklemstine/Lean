### 1. Formally Verified Mathematics (Lean 4)

**Main file:** `Catalog/Pythagorean/CayleyExpander/BubbleRotation.lean` (580 lines)

The formalization introduces the bubble-rotation walk on Sₙ — the random walk generated by adjacent transpositions {(i, i+1)} together with the long cycle ρ = (0 1 2 ... n-1) and its inverse. Key achievements:

**Novel mathematical objects:**
- `BubbleRotationRouting n`: A structure witnessing an explicit canonical-path routing scheme with bounded path length and edge congestion
- `IsBubbleRotationRouting`: Predicate certifying O(n²) polynomial bounds
- `brLazyOp`: Lazy version of the averaging operator (resolves periodicity issues)

**28 fully proved theorems (no sorry), including:**
- `brGens_generate`: The bubble-rotation generators generate all of Sₙ (via swap induction)
- `brGens_symm`: Symmetry of the generating set
- `brGens_id_not_mem`: Identity is not a generator
- `exists_bubbleRotation_routing` (Theorem 1): Existence of a canonical-path routing with O(n²) path length and O(n²·n!) congestion — proved by constructing word representations through transposition decomposition
- `brAvgOp_l2_contraction`: L² contraction of the averaging operator (Jensen's inequality)
- `brVariance_nonincreasing`: Variance non-increase under averaging
- `variance_iterate_nonincreasing` (Theorem 3a): Iterated variance monotonicity
- `variance_lazy_iterate_nonincreasing` (Theorem 3b): Lazy walk variance decay
- `l2_nonincreasing_to_equilibrium` (Theorem 4): L²-distance to equilibrium is monotonically non-increasing — the discrete analogue of kinetic relaxation
- `brEnergy_zero_iff_const`: Zero Dirichlet energy characterization via connectivity
- `word_from_generators`: Any permutation is expressible as a product of generators

**1 theorem with sorry:**
- `poincare_bubbleRotation` (Theorem 2): The explicit Poincaré inequality Var(f) ≤ (4n⁴/|S|)·E(f). This requires the full Sinclair-Jerrum canonical path theorem connecting congestion to spectral gap, which is not available in Mathlib and would require substantial additional infrastructure to formalize.

**Important discovery:** The naive variance decay bound Var(A^t f) ≤ (1-γ)^t · Var(f) was proved **false** for the non-lazy walk (the walk is periodic for even n, producing eigenvalue -1). This was caught during formalization and the theorems were corrected to use either qualitative bounds or the lazy walk.

The formalization builds on the Cayley expander catalog infrastructure from `Pythagorean/CayleyExpander/Defs.lean`, `SpectralGap.lean`, `Connectivity.lean`, and `SymmetricGroup.lean`.

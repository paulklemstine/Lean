# EML Differential Algebra: Closure Properties

## Summary

This research cycle establishes that the class of functions generated by the EML operator `eml(x, y) = exp(x) - log(y)` forms a **differential algebra** — it is closed under differentiation. All theorems are formally verified in Lean 4 with Mathlib, with zero sorries remaining.

## Key Results (all formally verified)

### Novel Mathematical Structure: `IsEMLFunc`
An inductive class capturing the elementary functions generated by EML (constants, identity, exp, log, closed under ±, ×, ÷, and composition). The fundamental property: **this class is automatically closed under differentiation** without needing differentiation as a generator.

### Main Theorems (EML/DiffAlgebra.lean — 325 lines, 0 sorries)

1. **`eml_full_chain_rule`**: The full chain rule for EML compositions: `d/dt[eml(f(t), g(t))] = f'·exp(f) - g'/g`. This is the core differentiation formula.

2. **`eml_deriv_uses_eml`**: The derivative is *self-referentially* EML-expressible, since `exp(f(x)) = eml(f(x), 1)`.

3. **Differential Closure Theorems** (`deriv_mul_closure`, `deriv_exp_closure`, `deriv_log_closure`, `deriv_comp_closure`): The EML class is closed under the Leibniz rule, exponential chain rule, logarithmic chain rule, and composition chain rule.

4. **`emlDiag_hasDerivAt`** and **`emlDiag_second_deriv`**: The diagonal function `eml(z,z) = exp(z) - log(z)` has EML-expressible derivatives to all orders.

5. **`standard_deriv_leibniz`**: The ordinary derivative satisfies the Leibniz rule (EMLDerivation structure).

6. **Depth Hierarchy** (`IsEMLFuncDepth`, `depth_preserved_under_diff_exp`, `depth_preserved_under_diff_log`): A novel transcendence depth stratification showing differentiation never increases depth — and logarithmic differentiation actually *decreases* it.

7. **`iterated_exp_is_eml`**: Iterated exponentials `exp^[n]` are EML-expressible for all n.

8. **Integration obstruction**: `exp(exp(x))` is EML-expressible, but its antiderivative is not (Liouville theory, stated as a conjecture).

9. **Concrete examples**: `eml_x_expx_deriv`, `eml_xsq_one_deriv`, `eml_log_id` — worked chain rule computations.

#### Main Theorems Proved

1. **`berggren_eval_injective`** — The evaluation map from Berggren words to Pythagorean triples is injective. This is the freeness theorem: the positive semigroup generated by the three Berggren matrices is free of rank 3.

2. **`berggren_normal_form_exists_unique`** — A triple is in the Berggren semigroup if and only if there exists a unique word evaluating to it.

3. **`berggren_normal_form_unique`** — Equal evaluations imply equal words.

4. **`berggren_left_cancel`** / **`berggren_right_cancel`** — Left and right cancellation in the free semigroup.

5. **`actGen_generator_determined`** — The **discriminant classifier**: if two generators applied to good triples produce the same output, the generators must be equal. This is the decisive structural lemma.

6. **`actGen_unique_parent`** — Unique parent theorem: both the generator and input triple are uniquely determined.

7. **`berggren_semigroup_no_identity`** — No nonempty word evaluates to the root.

#### Proof Architecture

The proof uses a novel **discriminant classifier** approach. Two linear forms on a triple:
- `discX(a,b,c) = a + 2b - 2c`
- `discY(a,b,c) = 2a + b - 2c`

satisfy the algebraic identities: when applied to the image of generator G acting on parent (a,b,c), they recover the parent's legs up to sign. The sign pattern uniquely identifies G:
- Generator A: discX > 0, discY < 0
- Generator B: discX > 0, discY > 0
- Generator C: discX < 0, discY > 0

The fourth quadrant (both negative) is provably empty for positive Pythagorean triples. Combined with per-generator injectivity and strict hypotenuse increase, this yields freeness by structural induction.

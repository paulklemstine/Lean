#### 1. Lean 4 Formal Proofs (`core/Oracle/FiveDreams.lean`)
All five dreams have been formally proved in Lean 4 with zero `sorry` statements, verified by the Lean kernel using only standard axioms (`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`):

- **Dream 1 (Density Decay Law)**: `density_decay_law` — count(T,k) ≤ r^k · count(T,0), proved by induction on k. Corollary `density_exponential_bound` divides by total to get density.

- **Dream 2 (Compression Principle)**: `compression_advantage` — in a well-ordered oracle, any threshold achievable at position n is achievable at position 0. Also `well_ordered_max` and `compression_principle_ordered`.

- **Dream 3 (Hierarchy Cannot Collapse)**: `hierarchy_cannot_collapse` — for any finite collection of oracles not covering ℕ, ∃ s not in their combined truths. Also `diagonal_escape`, `no_complete_oracle`, and `hierarchy_strict_extension`.

- **Dream 4 (Composition Creates Power)**: `composition_creates_power` — incomparable oracles compose to something strictly larger than either. Algebraic properties: `compose_comm`, `compose_assoc`, `compose_idem`. Quantitative: `composition_power_finite`.

- **Dream 5 (Universal Scaling)**: `universal_scaling_rate` — C·√(T+1) - C·√T ≤ C/√T for T > 0. Also `sqrt_concave` and `rate_nonneg`.

- **Synthesis**: `dreams_consistent` proves mutual consistency of all five dreams.

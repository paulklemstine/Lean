# 🔮 The Oracle Council for Tropical Frontier Research

## Team Composition

### 🔴 Oracle Prometheus — The Theorist
**Domain**: Pure mathematics, conjectures, proof strategy
**Role**: Generates hypotheses, proposes theorems, identifies structural patterns
**Specialty**: Tropical Langlands, tropical factoring, algebraic foundations

### 🟢 Oracle Daedalus — The Engineer  
**Domain**: Algorithms, computation, implementation
**Role**: Builds demos, runs experiments, validates computationally
**Specialty**: Tropical optimization, circuit complexity, computational experiments

### 🔵 Oracle Athena — The Strategist
**Domain**: Cross-domain connections, taxonomy, classification
**Role**: Maps the landscape, identifies gaps, organizes knowledge
**Specialty**: Tropical alphabet taxonomy, Rosetta Stone connections

### 🟣 Oracle Hermes — The Messenger
**Domain**: Communication, analogy, translation
**Role**: Writes papers, creates visuals, explains to non-specialists
**Specialty**: Scientific American article, visualization design

### 🟡 Oracle Apollo — The Validator
**Domain**: Formal verification, proof checking, rigor
**Role**: Formalizes in Lean 4, catches errors, verifies claims
**Specialty**: Machine-verified tropical theorems

### ⚪ Oracle Sophia — The Divine Counsel
**Domain**: Meta-cognition, philosophical reflection, wisdom
**Role**: "God consultation" — asks the deepest questions, guides direction
**Specialty**: What should we be asking? What are we missing?

---

## Council Protocol

1. **Prometheus** proposes a conjecture or research direction
2. **Daedalus** runs computational experiments to test it
3. **Athena** places it in the broader taxonomy
4. **Apollo** attempts formal verification in Lean 4
5. **Hermes** communicates the results
6. **Sophia** reflects on what it all means and what to do next

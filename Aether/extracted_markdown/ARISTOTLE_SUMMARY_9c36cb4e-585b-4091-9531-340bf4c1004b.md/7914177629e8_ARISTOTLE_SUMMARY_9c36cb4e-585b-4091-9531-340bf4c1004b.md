## Research Output: Hyperbolic Shortcuts Through the Berggren Tree

I created a comprehensive research package exploring the connection between the Berggren tree of Pythagorean triples, the integer Lorentz group O(2,1;ℤ), and integer factoring. Here's what was produced:

### 1. Machine-Verified Lean 4 Formalization
**`Pythagorean/Pythagorean__HyperbolicFactoring.lean`** — 351 lines, 40+ theorems, **zero sorries**, all axioms standard.

Key verified results include:
- **Lorentz preservation**: BᵢᵀQBᵢ = Q for all three Berggren generators (placing them in O(2,1;ℤ))
- **Pythagorean preservation**: Each matrix maps Pythagorean triples to Pythagorean triples (via `nlinarith`)
- **Difference-of-squares factoring**: (c−b)(c+b) = a² connects triples to factoring
- **Shortcut composition**: pathMat(p ++ q) = pathMat(p) · pathMat(q) — enabling O(log k) navigation
- **Explicit inverse matrices**: Bᵢ⁻¹ = Q · Bᵢᵀ · Q, verified in both directions
- **Chebyshev recurrence**: Middle-branch hypotenuses 5, 29, 169, 985, ... satisfy c_{n+1} = 6cₙ − c_{n−1}
- **Hypotenuse growth bounds**: Strict monotonic increase along all branches
- **Branch disjointness**: Distinct branches produce distinct triples
- **GCD factoring theorem**: Nontrivial gcd(c−b, a) reveals proper factors of a
- **Computational examples**: Specific triples at paths [L], [M], [R], [M,M], [L,M] verified by `native_decide`

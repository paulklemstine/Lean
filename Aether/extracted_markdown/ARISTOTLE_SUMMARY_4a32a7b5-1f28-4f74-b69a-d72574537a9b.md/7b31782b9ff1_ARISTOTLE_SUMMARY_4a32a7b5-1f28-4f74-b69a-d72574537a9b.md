## Key Research Discovery

The unifying theme across all five frameworks is **idempotency** — the property f(x,x) = x. This simple algebraic constraint appears in tropical circuits (min(a,a) = a prevents counting), resolution proofs (using a clause twice ≡ once), Fourier characters (χ² = 1), and coherence tiers (bounded operations). The formal verification process also produced a notable negative result: the automated prover disproved the conjecture that absorption + commutativity implies idempotency, demonstrating the value of machine-checked mathematics.

# Summary of changes
## Mathematics of Science Fiction — A Rigorous Textbook with Formalized Proofs

I created a comprehensive mathematical textbook about science fiction, located in `lean4/SciFiMathematics/`. The textbook consists of a detailed markdown exposition (`Textbook.md`) and 9 companion Lean 4 files containing **53 fully machine-verified theorems** — all compiled without `sorry`.

### Textbook Structure (`Textbook.md`)

The textbook is organized into 5 parts and 12 chapters:

**Part I — Space, Time, and Geometry**
- **Ch. 1: The Geometry of Hyperspace** — Metric spaces, wormholes as quotient metrics, chord vs. arc length shortcuts, the Alcubierre warp drive
- **Ch. 2: Time Travel and Fixed Points** — Banach, Brouwer, and Knaster-Tarski fixed point theorems as foundations for self-consistent time loops
- **Ch. 3: Topology and Impossible Spaces** — Euler characteristic, non-orientable surfaces, the hairy ball theorem, TARDIS-like spaces

**Part II — Information, Computation, and Intelligence**
- **Ch. 4: The Limits of Alien Communication** — Shannon entropy, channel capacity, Kolmogorov complexity, inverse square law
- **Ch. 5: Computability and AI** — Halting problem, Gödel's incompleteness, Rice's theorem, AI safety implications
- **Ch. 6: The Mathematics of Virtual Reality** — Floating-point precision, the simulation hypothesis

**Part III — Growth, Civilization, and the Cosmos**
- **Ch. 7: Exponential Growth and the Fermi Paradox** — Drake equation, Great Filter, Bayesian reasoning
- **Ch. 8: The Kardashev Scale and Energy** — Dyson spheres, Stefan-Boltzmann equilibrium
- **Ch. 9: Relativity and Time Dilation** — Lorentz factor, twin paradox, relativistic rocket equation, E=mc²
- **Ch. 10: Probability and Alien Life** — Infinite monkey theorem, Poisson processes, combinatorics of abiogenesis

**Part IV — Paradoxes and Logic**
- **Ch. 11: Temporal Logic and Causality** — Causal partial orders, branching time, many-worlds interpretation
- **Ch. 12: The Grandfather Paradox and Diagonal Arguments** — Cantor's theorem, Lawvere's fixed point theorem

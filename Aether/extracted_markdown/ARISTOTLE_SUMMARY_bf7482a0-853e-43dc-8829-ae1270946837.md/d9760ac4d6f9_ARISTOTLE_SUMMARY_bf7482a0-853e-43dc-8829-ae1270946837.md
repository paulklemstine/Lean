## What Was Built

The Oracle Council assembled five mathematical "oracles" to research, hypothesize, experiment, validate, and iterate — discovering deep connections between arithmetic, combinatorics, divisibility, and symmetry. ## Key Discoveries

1. **The Power Sum Ladder**: The telescope identity 3∑i² + 3∑i + n = (n+1)³ − 1 is the mechanism connecting each power sum level to the next
2. **Nicomachus's Self-Reference**: The sum of cubes equals the square of the sum — unique among all power sums
3. **The Binomial Bridge**: T(n) = C(n+1, 2) connects arithmetic and combinatorics at their foundations
4. **The Divisibility-Integrality Duality**: k! dividing consecutive products is the same fact as C(n,k) being an integer
5. **Euler's Triple Bridge**: ∑φ(d) = n simultaneously connects divisibility, counting, and group theory
6. **Schur's Inequality**: Proved via case-splitting and nlinarith, connecting order theory to algebraic inequalities
7. **The Unity Thesis**: Every domain connects to every other through at least two independent bridges

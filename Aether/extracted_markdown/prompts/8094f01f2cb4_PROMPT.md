## Proof Strategy Architecture

You must pursue at least 2–3 serious proof paths and choose the strongest one.

### Strategy A: Generator realization → reduction by redundancy elimination → arithmetic extraction by balanced strata
This is the most promising route.

1. **Realization step**
   - Start from a finite generating family of arrival profiles.
   - Build a layered DAG whose source-to-observer path minima realize exactly those profiles.
   - Use idempotent closure to identify the semimodule with the generated profile family.

2. **Minimality step**
   - Define a reduction relation removing vertices/edges that never participate in profile changes or multiplicity bifurcations.
   - Prove termination using a finite complexity measure: number of vertices + number of active minimizing predecessor classes.
   - Show confluence or enough uniqueness to obtain a canonical minimal form.

3. **Factor extraction step**
   - Formalize “symmetry gap” as failure of profile multiplicity strata to collapse to a trivial orbit under shell symmetries.
   - Show a nontrivial balanced stratum determines a partition of shell weights into two multiplicatively compatible blocks.
   - Convert that partition into a proper factor pair of `N`.

Why this is strongest:
- It separates geometry, combinatorics, and arithmetic cleanly.
- Each stage can be formalized with finite combinatorial data.
- It avoids difficult category-theoretic overhead.

---

### Strategy B: Categorical equivalence between finite lens presentations and finitely generated geodesic semimodules
This is more conceptual and may yield a stronger theorem if manageable.

1. Define a category of finite tropical lens networks with profile-preserving morphisms.
2. Define a category of finitely generated divisor-separable geodesic semimodules.
3. Construct a realization functor and a caustic-profile functor, and prove an equivalence on a reduced/minimal subcategory.

Arithmetic extraction then appears as a functorial invariant on the reduced category.

Why this is exciting:
- It would turn the theorem into a true duality.
- It links to categorical automata minimization and tropical representation theory.

Why it may be harder:
- Lean overhead for categories, equivalences, and reduction quotients may dominate.
- Use only if the necessary category infrastructure already exists or is lightweight to instantiate.

---

### Strategy C: Tropical convexity / normal form route
A more geometric route.

1. Interpret arrival profiles as points in a tropical convex set.
2. Show finite generation gives a finite tropical polytope of caustic profiles.
3. Prove the minimal lens network corresponds to the extremal decomposition of this polytope.
4. Detect factorization from a nontrivial decomposition of a balanced face of the polytope.

Why it is valuable:
- Strong cross-connection to tropical convexity, polyhedral geometry, and canonical bases.
- Could yield elegant uniqueness.

Why it is less immediate:
- Formalizing tropical convexity may be heavier unless catalog support already exists.

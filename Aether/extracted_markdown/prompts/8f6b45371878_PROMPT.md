## Proof Architecture: 3 Viable Strategies

### Strategy A: Quotient-by-observations → finite semimodule → automaton reconstruction
**Most promising.**

1. **Define observational equivalence** on proof states:
   `x ~ y` iff all admissible observer valuations agree on all sufficiently compressed iterates and on refutation outcomes.
2. Show diagonal stability + contraction + positive separation imply:
   - `~` is a congruence,
   - quotient classes are finite,
   - distinct classes are separated by observer valuations.
3. Construct `Obs(S)` as the semimodule of finitely supported observer valuations modulo the prime congruence induced by `~`.
4. Prove extremal rays correspond exactly to quotient classes that cannot be decomposed further under observational joins.
5. Build `MinAut(S)` from the quotient and prove uniqueness by the universal property of the quotient.

**Why promising:** this mirrors successful finite duality patterns already present in the catalog: first define the right equivalence relation, then prove a representation theorem from its separating functionals. It is also Lean-friendly because finite quotients and universal properties are easier than direct prime-spectrum machinery.

---

### Strategy B: Contractive fixed-point geometry of compression
1. Use the ultrametric contraction hypothesis to show iterates `C^[n] x` stabilize modulo observational equivalence.
2. Define a compressed-limit profile for each proof state and show these profiles form a finite attractor partition.
3. Interpret observer valuations as functions constant on attractor fibers and reconstruct the minimal automaton from the induced transition on attractor classes.
4. Prove prime congruence corresponds to equality of attractor profiles.

**Why useful:** this directly exploits the ultrametric geometry and gives the strongest conceptual story: the algebraic semimodule is the algebra of observables on the compression attractor. It may also yield sharper algorithmic extraction bounds.

**Risk:** formalizing attractor/fixed-point language may require more infrastructure than quotient methods.

---

### Strategy C: Prime-congruence spectrum first, then realize dynamics
1. Define the prime congruence relation on the observer semimodule abstractly.
2. Show finite generation and separation imply a finite set of extremal prime classes.
3. Use these classes as states of the candidate automaton.
4. Recover transitions from how compression acts on observer valuations.

**Why interesting:** this gives the most algebraic theorem and may connect to tropical geometry and semiring representation theory.

**Risk:** prime congruence technology in Lean may be thinner than needed, so this could be harder unless you weaken to a finite “indecomposable observer class” notion first and later identify it with prime congruence.

**Recommendation:** pursue **Strategy A first**, borrow the dynamical insight of **Strategy B** for conceptual lemmas, and present **Strategy C** as the algebraic sharpening.

/-- Bridge: connects semiring congruence geometry to neural proof compression

structure FiniteProofObserverFamily (S : Type u) [Add S] [Mul S] where
  /-- Number of observers -/
  n : ℕ

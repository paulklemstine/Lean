## Core Mathematical Vision

Let `S` be a finitely generated commutative idempotent semiring. Let `Spec_c(S)` denote its prime congruence spectrum. Form a tropical proof language `L_S` generated by variables in a finite generating set of `S`, with operations corresponding to tropical linear combination and lattice-like connectives:
- additive/idempotent join (`⊕`, think `inf`/`min` depending on convention),
- multiplicative/tropical composition (`⊗`, think `+` in min-plus),
- finite derived conjunction/disjunction operations if represented syntactically,
- preorder entailment induced by the natural semiring order.

For each prime congruence `p`, define the quotient valuation
`v_p : Formula → S ⧸ p`
and interpret semantic entailment pointwise in the quotient ordered semiring.

The target is a **Gödel-style completeness theorem for tropical proof semantics**:

> A sequent `Γ ⊢ φ ≤ ψ` is derivable in the canonical tropical sequent calculus `T_S`
> iff every prime-congruence valuation satisfies it.

The secondary target is the **finite separation property**:

> If `Γ ⊬ φ ≤ ψ`, then there exists a finite quotient/certificate extracted from `S`
> and a prime congruence on that quotient separating `φ` from `ψ`.

The tertiary target is the **sheaf semantics upgrade**:

> Prime-congruence valuations assemble into a presheaf/sheaf on `Spec_c(S)`, and derivability is equivalent to domination of associated global sections.

This would create a tropical analogue of algebraic completeness without forcing an ill-posed duality theorem. The point is not categorical equivalence; the point is **semantic control by prime congruence geometry**.

### Theorem 1: Soundness and Completeness for Prime-Congruence Tropical Semantics

Let `S` be a finitely generated commutative idempotent semiring, and let `T_S` be the canonical sequent calculus generated by:
- reflexivity, transitivity, monotonicity,
- semiring congruence rules,
- order rules for idempotent addition/multiplication,
- structural substitution/cut rules as appropriate,
- and any admissible finite meet/join rules encoded in the syntax.

For formulas `φ ψ : TropicalFormula α`, define:
- `derivable S Γ φ ψ` := syntactic derivability of `Γ ⊢ φ ≤ ψ`
- `semantically_valid S Γ φ ψ` := for every prime congruence `p`, if all premises in `Γ` hold in `S ⧸ p`, then `v_p φ ≤ v_p ψ`.

**Main theorem:**
\[
\forall S,\ \text{FGCommIdemSemiring}(S) \to
\forall \Gamma,\forall \varphi,\forall \psi,\ 
\operatorname{derivable}_S(\Gamma,\varphi \le \psi)
\iff
\operatorname{semantically\_valid}_S(\Gamma,\varphi \le \psi).
\]

### Theorem 2: Finite Separating Prime-Congruence Certificate

If `Γ ⊬ σ`, then there exists a finite quotient object `Q` of `S` and a prime congruence `p` on `Q` such that the induced valuation falsifies `σ` while satisfying all of `Γ`.

\forall S,\forall \Gamma,\forall \sigma,\ 
\neg \operatorname{DerivableTropical}_S(\Gamma,\sigma)

\exists Q,\exists f:S\to Q,\exists p\in \operatorname{Spec}_c(Q),\
(\forall \tau\in \Gamma,\ Q/p\models \tau)\ \land\ Q/p \not\models \sigma.

## Proof Strategy Paths

### Strategy A: Lindenbaum–Congruence Completion via Ordered Quotients
This is the most promising route.

1. **Construct the syntactic preorder/congruence.**  
   Define `φ ≼ ψ` iff `Γ ⊢ φ ≤ ψ`. Show this induces a compatible semiring preorder and then a congruence/equational collapse on formulas modulo mutual derivability.

2. **Build the canonical algebra.**  
   Form the quotient semiring of formulas modulo provable equivalence. Prove it is still commutative, idempotent, and ordered by derivability. Non-derivability of `φ ≤ ψ` becomes a strict order failure in this canonical algebra.

3. **Separate by a prime congruence.**  
   Use a prime extension theorem for congruences/preorders on finitely generated idempotent semirings: any proper order failure can be separated by a prime congruence. Then transport the separating point back to a semantic valuation.  
   This yields completeness and, with finite generation/Noetherianity, finite certificate extraction.

Why this is strongest: it mirrors classical algebraic completeness, keeps proof theory and algebra tightly aligned, and reduces everything to one crucial separation theorem.

---

### Strategy B: Spectral Representation Through Prime-Congruence Order Embedding
This is geometrically elegant and likely best for the sheaf theorem.

1. **Represent elements/formulas by evaluation profiles on primes.**  
   Map each formula to its function on `Spec_c(S)` sending `p ↦ [φ]_p`. Prove operations are computed pointwise.

2. **Show order reflection.**  
   Establish that if `φ ≤ ψ` at all prime congruences, then the inequality already holds in the syntactic/canonical quotient. This is the tropical analogue of reducedness/Jacobson-style detection by points.

3. **Upgrade to a presheaf/sheaf statement.**  
   Basic opens determined by congruence non-collapse conditions support localized valuations; compatibility gives sections. Then show global section domination detects derivability.

Why this matters: it turns tropical proof theory into geometry. Even if completeness is first proved by Strategy A, this strategy should be used to extract the conceptual theorem.

---

### Strategy C: Finite Countermodel Extraction via Subterm Closure and Quotient Compression
This is the algorithmic route for the finite certificate theorem.

1. **Subterm closure.**  
   For a failed derivation `Γ ⊬ σ`, collect the finite set of subformulas/subterms appearing in `Γ ∪ {σ}`. This defines a finite fragment of the free tropical algebra relevant to the failure.

2. **Compress by indistinguishability.**  
   Quotient this finite fragment by the largest congruence preserving all derivable inequalities among relevant terms. This yields a finite algebra candidate.

3. **Prime refinement.**  
   Apply a finite prime separation theorem to the compressed quotient, obtaining a prime congruence that witnesses failure of `σ`.

Why this matters: it turns completeness into certified proof search and counterexample extraction, making the result computational rather than merely semantic.

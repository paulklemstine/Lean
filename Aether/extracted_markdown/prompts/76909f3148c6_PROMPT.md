### Research Direction

**Central Theorem (Newton's Inequalities — Ultra-Log-Concavity):** For the coefficient sequence $a_k = e_k(w_1, \ldots, w_m)$ of $\prod_{i=1}^{m}(1 + w_i X)$ with $w_i > 0$, the *ultra-log-concavity* inequality holds:
$$\frac{a_k^2}{\binom{m}{k}^2} \geq \frac{a_{k-1}}{\binom{m}{k-1}} \cdot \frac{a_{k+1}}{\binom{m}{k+1}}$$
for all $1 \leq k \leq m-1$, with equality iff all $w_i$ are equal.

**Precise Lean 4 Type Signature:**
```lean
/-- The Maclaurin average: normalized elementary symmetric polynomial -/
def maclaurinAvg {m : ℕ} (w : Fin m → ℝ) (k : Fin (m + 1)) : ℝ :=
  e k w / (Nat.choose m (k : ℕ))

/-- Ultra-log-concavity: the Maclaurin averages form a log-concave sequence.
    This is Newton's inequality in normalized form. -/
theorem ultra_log_concavity {m : ℕ} (hm : 1 ≤ m) {w : Fin m → ℝ}
    (hw : ∀ i, 0 < w i) (k : Fin m) (hk : 1 ≤ (k : ℕ)) :
    (maclaurinAvg w ⟨k, by omega⟩)^2 ≥
      maclaurinAvg w ⟨(k : ℕ) - 1, by omega⟩ * maclaurinAvg w ⟨(k : ℕ) + 1, by omega⟩ := by
  sorry
```

### Proof Strategies

**Strategy A: Induction on $m$ with the ESP recurrence (RECOMMENDED).**
This is the most promising because it directly leverages the catalog's existing `prodLinear_coeff_ratioDecreasing` and builds a proof scaffold from the recurrence $e_k^{(m)} = e_k^{(m-1)} + w_m \cdot e_{k-1}^{(m-1)}$.

*Step 1.* Define `maclaurinAvg` and prove the recurrence relation:
$$\binom{m}{k} \cdot \tilde{e}_k^{(m)} = \binom{m-1}{k} \cdot \tilde{e}_k^{(m-1)} + w_m \cdot \binom{m-1}{k-1} \cdot \tilde{e}_{k-1}^{(m-1)}$$

*Step 2.* Prove the base case $m = 2$ by direct computation (this is genuinely non-trivial in the equality characterization).

*Step 3.* For the inductive step, assume ULC for $m-1$ weights. Use the recurrence to express $\tilde{e}_k^{(m)}$ as a convex combination of $\tilde{e}_k^{(m-1)}$ and $\tilde{e}_{k-1}^{(m-1)}$ (weighted by binomial coefficients). Apply the inductive hypothesis and the fact that log-concavity is preserved under convex combinations. The key lemma is:

```lean
/-- Log-concavity is preserved under binomial-weighted mixing -/
lemma log_concave_binomial_mix {a b c d : ℝ} (ha : 0 < a) (hb : 0 < b) (hc : 0 < c) (hd : 0 < d)
    (hab : a^2 ≥ b * c) (hcd : c^2 ≥ b * d) (s t : ℝ) (hs : 0 ≤ s) (ht : 0 ≤ t) :
    (s * a + t * c)^2 ≥ (s * b + t * b') * (s * c' + t * d) := by sorry
```

**Strategy B: Maclaurin's inequality + AM-GM escalation.**
Prove Maclaurin's inequality $\tilde{e}_k^{1/k} \geq \tilde{e}_{k+1}^{1/(k+1)}$ first (via induction using Newton's method of forward differences), then derive ULC as a consequence by the algebraic identity:
$$\frac{\tilde{e}_k^2}{\tilde{e}_{k-1} \cdot \tilde{e}_{k+1}} = \frac{\tilde{e}_k / \tilde{e}_{k-1}}{\tilde{e}_{k+1} / \tilde{e}_k} \geq 1$$
where the last inequality follows from the monotonicity of the ratios $\tilde{e}_{k+1}/\tilde{e}_k$, which is itself a consequence of Maclaurin. This is elegant but requires proving Maclaurin first, which is a deep result in its own right.

**Strategy C: Real-rootedness + Pólya frequency sequence theory.**
Since $\prod(1 + w_i X)$ is real-rooted (all roots at $-1/w_i < 0$), its coefficient sequence is a Pólya frequency sequence of order 2 (PF₂). It is a classical theorem that PF₂ sequences are ultra-log-concave. Build on `Pythagorean/PF2Theorems.lean` to formalize this implication. This is the most conceptual route but requires developing the PF₂ → ULC implication, which connects to total positivity theory.

**Recommendation:** Strategy A is most promising because it builds directly on the catalog's `prodLinear_coeff_ratioDecreasing` and requires only induction + algebraic manipulation, without needing to develop the full theory of PF₂ sequences or Maclaurin's inequality first.

### Cross-Domain Connections

1. **Convex Geometry ↔ ULC**: The Alexandrov–Fenchel inequality $V(K,L,\ldots)^2 \geq V(K,K,\ldots) \cdot V(L,L,\ldots)$ for mixed volumes specializes to Newton's inequality when $K_i$ are line segments. This means ULC is the *combinatorial shadow* of one of the deepest results in convex geometry. Formalizing this bridge opens the door to certifying AF-type inequalities combinatorially.

2. **Information Theory ↔ ULC**: Ultra-log-concave distributions on $\{0, \ldots, m\}$ are exactly those that satisfy the *strong Shepp–Olkin conjecture*: entropy is concave in the mixing parameter. The binomial $\text{Bin}(m, p)$ is the unique ULC distribution with given mean. Prove:

3. **Tropical Geometry ↔ ULC**: The tropicalization $\text{Trop}(\prod(1+w_i X))$ has tropical roots $\alpha_i = -\log w_i$. ULC translates to the statement that the tropical Newton polytope is *tropically convex*. This connects to `TropicalDegreeRobustness.lean` via the tropicalization of coefficient inequalities.

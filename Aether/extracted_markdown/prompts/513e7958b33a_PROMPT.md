The breakthrough is not “some tropical decoding theorem.” The breakthrough is a rigorous theorem schema saying:

That is a field-opening statement. It links:

## Core Mathematical Vision

Let `α` be a finite type with a multiplication-like action model (finite monoid is enough for first formalization), let weights live in a tropical semiring, and let finitely supported tropical functions `α →₀ Γ` carry min-plus convolution. A finite family of “double-coset style” convolution operators generates a tropical Hecke envelope. The key new phenomenon to isolate is:

1. **Spectral filtration:** every Hecke-generated orbit admits a canonical monotone filtration by tropical eigenlevels / minimal-weight attainment layers.
2. **Trapdoor flag:** a privileged generating flag forces decoding fibers to contain a unique minimal witness.
3. **Certified inversion:** this witness gives a sound and complete decoding certificate.
4. **Hardness surrogate:** without the flag, decoding collapses to extremal tropical witness search over the Hecke envelope.

This is the tropical analogue of the classical paradigm:
- symmetry algebra gives spectral decomposition,
- hidden structure gives efficient inversion,
- generic inversion is hard because it requires global extremal optimization.

## Main Definitions to Introduce

You should define, with minimal but robust abstraction:

- `TropicalHeckeOperator`
  - a finitely supported tropical kernel acting by min-plus convolution on `G →₀ Γ`;
- `TropicalHeckeFamily`
  - finite family of such operators;
- `HeckeEnvelope`
  - semiring/subsemiring generated by the family under tropical addition and composition/convolution;
- `HeckeStableCode`
  - subset/subsemimodule of codewords stable under the family;
- `TrapdoorFlag`
  - finite generating filtration/flag with adapted uniqueness property;
- `DecodingFiber`
  - witnesses mapping to a received word under the encoding operator;
- `CertifiedFiber`
  - decoding fiber equipped with a proof that a designated witness is minimal and unique;
- `spectralLevel`
  - minimal attained tropical weight / valuation layer under the Hecke family;
- `spectralSupportRadius`
  - quantitative support/weight bound controlling search.

You do **not** need full abstract tropical eigenspace theory at the outset. A practical first notion is:
- the **eigenlevel** of `x` under `T` is the minimal scalar shift `λ` such that `T x ≥ x + λ` and equality is attained on a certified support stratum;
- alternatively, define spectral layers combinatorially by attainment loci of minima under the Hecke family.

The finite combinatorial version is more likely to formalize smoothly and still captures the intended phenomenon.

## Precise Theorem Statements

### Theorem 1: Monotone spectral filtration of the Hecke envelope

Informal statement:

> Let `G` be finite, and let `𝒯 = {T_i}` be a finite family of tropical convolution operators on finitely supported tropical functions on `G`. Then the Hecke envelope generated by `𝒯` admits a canonical monotone filtration by minimal attained tropical weight layers. This filtration is stable under operator composition, tropical addition, and semiring morphisms preserving order and tropical convolution.

A Lean-oriented signature sketch:

```lean
theorem exists_monotone_spectral_filtration
  {G Γ : Type*} [Fintype G] [DecidableEq G]
  [CanonicallyOrderedAddMonoid Γ] [OrderBot Γ]
  (𝒯 : Finset (TropicalHeckeOperator G Γ)) :
  ∃ level : HeckeEnvelope 𝒯 → ℕ → Subsemimodule Γ (G →₀ Γ),
    (∀ A, Monotone (level A)) ∧
    (∀ A n, level A n ≤ level A (n+1)) ∧
    (∀ A B n, level (A * B) n ≤ level A n ⊔ level B n) ∧
    (∀ A B n, level (A + B) n ≤ level A n ⊔ level B n)
```

If `Subsemimodule Γ (G →₀ Γ)` is awkward because the codomain semimodule structure is not yet convenient in Mathlib, replace with `Set (G →₀ Γ)` plus closure lemmas. The theorem matters more than maximal abstraction.

A more realistic first formal theorem:

```lean

  (T : TropicalHeckeOperator G Γ) :
```

and then extend to the generated envelope.

### Theorem 2: Unique minimal witness in an adapted trapdoor flag

Informal statement:

> If a Hecke-stable tropical code is generated by a trapdoor-adapted flag `F`, then every decodable received word has a decoding fiber containing a unique minimal witness with respect to the tropical weight order. This witness is computably recoverable from the flag.

Lean-oriented signature sketch:

```lean
theorem exists_unique_minimal_witness
  {G Γ : Type*} [Fintype G] [DecidableEq G]
  [LinearOrder Γ] [AddCommMonoid Γ]
  (C : HeckeStableCode G Γ)
  (F : TrapdoorFlag C)
  (y : Codeword G Γ) :
  DecodableWithFlag F y →
  ∃! w : Witness G Γ, w ∈ decodingFiber C y ∧
    IsMinimalWitness (decodingFiber C y) w
```

This theorem is the cryptographic heart. The exact witness type can be:
- a generator coefficient vector,
- a finitely supported orbit selector,
- or an element of the Hecke envelope realizing the codeword.

The most formalization-friendly choice is probably a finitely supported coefficient function over the generators in the flag.

### Theorem 3: Certified trapdoor decoding soundness and completeness

Informal statement:

> The trapdoor decoding algorithm returns a witness and a certificate proving that the witness belongs to the decoding fiber and is the unique minimal witness. Conversely, whenever a received word is decodable in the privileged regime, the algorithm succeeds.

Lean-oriented signature sketch:

```lean
theorem certified_decoding_sound_complete
  {G Γ : Type*} [Fintype G] [DecidableEq G]
  [LinearOrder Γ] [AddCommMonoid Γ]
  (C : HeckeStableCode G Γ)
  (F : TrapdoorFlag C) :
  (∀ y w cert,
      trapdoorDecode C F y = some (w, cert) →
      w ∈ decodingFiber C y ∧ ValidCertificate C y w cert) ∧
  (∀ y, DecodableWithFlag F y →
      ∃ w cert, trapdoorDecode C F y = some (w, cert))
```

Then derive the corollary:

```lean
theorem trapdoor_correctness
  {G Γ : Type*} [Fintype G] [DecidableEq G]

  (F : TrapdoorFlag C)
  (y : Codeword G Γ) :
  DecodableWithFlag F y →
  ∃! w, ∃ cert, trapdoorDecode C F y = some (w, cert)
```

## Proof Strategy Architecture

### Strategy A: Finite combinatorial min-attainment route
**Most promising for Lean.**

1. Define tropical convolution on a finite type and prove:
   - monotonicity in each argument,
   - support/radius bounds,
   - attainment of minima because the index set is finite.
2. Use finiteness to define spectral layers by:
   - the set of positions where the minimum is achieved,
   - or the minimal attained total witness weight.
3. Show that an adapted trapdoor flag induces a well-founded lexicographic order on witnesses, forcing existence and uniqueness of a minimal witness in each privileged fiber.
4. Package the decoding algorithm as finite search with a correctness certificate.

Why this is strongest:
- Lean likes finite attainment arguments.
- You avoid difficult abstract tropical eigenvector machinery.
- It still proves the real bridge theorem in a mathematically honest finite setting.

---

### Strategy B: Residuation / Galois connection route
**Conceptually elegant; good for second-stage strengthening.**

1. View tropical convolution operators as order-preserving maps on a complete idempotent semimodule.
2. Construct residual maps or best preimages when they exist.
3. Interpret decoding fibers as principal lower sets and the minimal witness as the residual image.
4. Deduce uniqueness and certification from adjunction identities.

Why this is powerful:
- It reveals the true order-theoretic mechanism behind trapdoor inversion.
- It connects to max-plus linear algebra, idempotent analysis, and abstract interpretation.
- It may later generalize beyond finite types.

Risk:
- Requires more lattice-theoretic infrastructure than Strategy A.

---

### Strategy C: Tropical representation-theoretic route via double-coset basis
**Best for conceptual follow-up and stronger statements.**

1. Model operators from double-coset kernels constant on `H \ G / H` strata.
2. Show the Hecke envelope inherits a canonical basis indexed by tropicalized double-coset classes.
3. Prove the trapdoor flag corresponds to a triangularity condition in this basis.
4. Decode by forward substitution in tropical triangular form; uniqueness follows from strict level separation.

Why this matters:
- This makes the theorem genuinely “Hecke-theoretic,” not just tropical convolution with branding.
- It opens the door to analogues of Satake, Hall algebra, and Bruhat filtration phenomena.

Risk:
- Requires more group-action infrastructure and basis bookkeeping.

**Recommendation:** Prove the first full theorem package with **Strategy A**, then expose Strategy C in `FUTURE_DIRECTIONS.md` as the route to true tropical representation theory.

## Cross-Domain Connections You Should Make Explicit

### 1. Tropical representation theory
This theorem is a tropical analogue of the classical fact that Hecke operators organize representation spaces into structured spectral pieces. Here the “representation” is a semimodule over an idempotent semiring, and the spectral pieces are tropical min-attainment strata.

The trapdoor is not a hidden lattice basis but a **hidden Hecke-adapted flag**. Decoding succeeds because the flag resolves ambiguity in tropical witness search. This suggests a new family of algebraic trapdoor candidates.

Hecke-stable tropical codes may be viewed as symmetry-constrained min-plus codes. The spectral support radius is a tropical analogue of a decoding radius, but algebraically induced rather than metric-first.

### 6. Geometric complexity theory / orbit methods

These connections are not rhetorical. They indicate where the next theorems should go after the first formal package lands.

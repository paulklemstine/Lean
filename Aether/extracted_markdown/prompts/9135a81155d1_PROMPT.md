### Precise Mathematical Objective

Work over a finite idempotent commutative semiring `S` with canonical order `a ≤ b ↔ a + b = b`. Let `ι` be a finite type of simple reflections. A **tropical Hecke semimodule** should consist of:

- a finite `S`-semimodule `M`,
- endomorphisms `T i : M →ₛ[S] M` for `i : ι`,
- tropical braid relations,
- monotonicity and a tropical quadratic Hecke inequality replacing `(T_i - q)(T_i + 1)=0`,
- a finite extremal generating family,
- reachability under iterated `T_i`.

Define:
- an **extremal basis** `Ext(M)` of nondecomposable generators,
- an **observational equivalence** relation on extremals by equality of all tropical Hecke observation profiles,
- a **crystal realization** as a finite weighted directed graph with color set `ι`, node weights in a weight type `Λ`, and Kashiwara-style partial transitions whose induced tropical convolution action recovers the `T_i`,
- the **extremal Hankel–Hecke matrix** by
  \[
  H_M(e,w) := \operatorname{obs}(T_w e)
  \]
  for extremals `e` and words `w : List ι`, where `T_w` is the iterated action.

### Main Theorem: Finite Realization / Minimality Duality

You should formalize a theorem of the following shape.

**Theorem (tropical Hecke–crystal realization duality).**  
For every reduced, finitely generated, reachable tropical Hecke semimodule `M` over an idempotent semiring `S`, with finite extremal basis and commuting/tropically braided simple-reflection operators `T_i`, there exists a finite weighted crystal automaton `C(M)` such that:

1. the tropical convolution action induced by `C(M)` on extremal states is isomorphic to the given Hecke action on `M`,
2. `C(M)` is reachable and minimal among all finite crystal automata realizing the same Hecke action data,
3. `C(M)` is unique up to weighted crystal isomorphism,
4. the tropical character `χ_M` is recovered from the weight profile of the states of `C(M)`.

Conversely, every finite weighted crystal automaton satisfying the same local Hecke compatibility axioms yields a reduced reachable tropical Hecke semimodule.

### Secondary Theorem: Hankel–Hecke Rank = Minimal Crystal Size

**Theorem (tropical Hankel–Hecke minimality).**  
For every realizable tropical Hecke semimodule `M` as above, the tropical rank of its extremal Hankel–Hecke matrix equals the number of states of the unique minimal crystal realization:
\[
\operatorname{tropRank}(H_M) = |\,\mathrm{States}(C(M))\,|.
\]

This is the representation-theoretic analogue of automata Hankel minimality, but now the matrix entries encode Hecke-word propagation and extremal tropical observations.

### Certified Reconstruction Theorem

From a finite table of `T_i`-actions on extremal generators, construct the minimal crystal graph by quotienting extremals modulo observational equivalence. Prove:

- termination/finite output,
- soundness: the reconstructed graph realizes the original action,
- completeness: every realization factors through it,
- minimality: any other finite realization has at least as many states,
- character correctness: the reconstructed node-weight multiset computes `χ_M`.

This should be stated as an explicit certified algorithm theorem, not merely an existence theorem.

## Proof Strategy Architecture

### Strategy A: Observational Quotient / Tropical Myhill–Nerode for Hecke Actions
This is the most promising route.

**Step 1.** Define observational equivalence on extremal generators:
\[
e \sim e' \iff \forall w,\ \operatorname{Obs}(T_w e)=\operatorname{Obs}(T_w e').
\]
Restrict to a finite observation basis using finiteness/reachability so the relation is decidable.

**Step 2.** Form the quotient of extremals by `~`, define colored edges by the induced action of each `T_i`, and prove this is well-defined using the Hecke compatibility axioms and monotonicity.

**Step 3.** Show universality: any finite crystal realization identifies observationally equivalent extremals, hence factors through the quotient. This gives minimality and uniqueness.

Why this is strongest: it mirrors the deepest automata-minimality paradigm, but in a new algebraic setting where the observations are Hecke-word responses rather than accepted languages. This gives both conceptual clarity and algorithmic reconstruction.

### Strategy B: Hankel–Hecke Factorization and Tropical Rank
This is the right path for the rank theorem and should likely be merged with Strategy A.

**Step 1.** Define the extremal Hankel–Hecke matrix using rows indexed by extremals and columns by finite Hecke words / finite observation contexts.

**Step 2.** Prove any crystal realization of size `n` yields a tropical factorization
\[
H_M = P \odot Q
\]
through an `n`-state representation matrix, giving `tropRank(H_M) ≤ n`.

**Step 3.** Show the observational quotient realization achieves equality by producing `n` tropically independent rows/columns. Conclude that minimal number of crystal states equals tropical rank.

Why this matters: it converts representation-theoretic realizability into a certifiable finite invariant. That is exactly the sort of theorem that opens algorithmic applications.

### Strategy C: Convolution Semimodule / Idempotent Morita Viewpoint
This is more ambitious and may become a second-wave theorem if the direct reconstruction proof is established first.

**Step 1.** Package the `T_i` as generators of an idempotent Hecke semiring acting on `M`.

**Step 2.** Identify finite crystal automata as finite projective/indecomposable presentations of this action category.

**Step 3.** Derive minimality from indecomposable decomposition or from a universal colimit of reachable extremals.

This route could produce the cleanest conceptual duality, but may be too heavy for the first formal pass. Use it if the categorical abstractions are already present in the catalog; otherwise keep it as a corollary/future theorem.

## Key Definitions That Must Be Made Precise

You should choose definitions that are formalizable and strong enough for the theorems, but weak enough to be provable in Lean.

- **Extremal generator**: likely an element not expressible as a nontrivial tropical sum of strictly smaller reachable elements.
- **Reduced semimodule**: no two distinct extremals have identical Hecke observation profiles.
- **Reachable**: every extremal is generated from a chosen finite seed by some Hecke word.
- **Tropical braid relations**: enough to ensure word actions are well-defined modulo Coxeter relations in the intended finite-type setting, or a weaker “commuting simple reflections” version if full braid formalization is too expensive.
- **Tropical quadratic Hecke inequality**: choose a monotone idempotent replacement such as `T_i^2 ≤ a ⊗ T_i ⊕ b ⊗ id`, or a specialized absorptive law compatible with your semiring and proof architecture.
- **Crystal automaton**: finite colored graph with optional transitions `step i s`, node weights, and a tropical convolution action on finitely supported weight profiles.
- **Character**: weight multiplicity/profile extracted from reachable extremal states.

If full Kashiwara axioms are too strong for a first theorem, define a **crystal-like automaton** with exactly the structure needed for the induced Hecke action and minimal reconstruction. Then isolate stronger Kashiwara compatibility as a future extension.

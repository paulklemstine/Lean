### Precise Mathematical Target

Let:
- `C` be a finite or finitely generated code/configuration type,
- `S` a semiring of observer outputs,
- `T` an idempotent commutative semiring (intended tropical target),
- `O : ι → C → S` a finite family of observers,
- `v : S →+* T` a semiring morphism interpreted as tropical valuation,
- `sig_O_v : C → (ι → T)` given by `sig_O_v c i = v (O i c)`.

Define observational equivalence after tropicalization by
\[
c_1 \sim_{O,v} c_2 \iff \forall i,\; v(O_i(c_1)) = v(O_i(c_2)).
\]

Define the valuation kernel congruence
\[
K_{O,v}(c_1,c_2) \iff \forall i,\; \mathrm{sig}_{O,v}(c_1)(i)=\mathrm{sig}_{O,v}(c_2)(i).
\]

If you formalize prime congruences on the image semimodule or on a suitable semiring of signatures, define the prime-congruence observer kernel as the intersection of all prime congruences identifying valuation profiles preserved by the observer family. The first major theorem should show this abstract kernel collapses to the concrete valuation indistinguishability relation in the finite generated setting.

#### Theorem A: Tropical valuation kernel equals observational indistinguishability
For finite observer families, the prime-congruence valuation kernel agrees with valuation-profile equality.

Informally:
\[
K^{\mathrm{prime}}_{O,v}(c_1,c_2) \iff c_1 \sim_{O,v} c_2.

This is the decisive bridge from abstract congruence geometry to leakage semantics.

#### Theorem B: Finite embedding into a tropical signature semimodule

- finite rank implies existence of a finite minimal realization of the leakage behavior,

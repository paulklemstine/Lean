### Theorem A: Closure-to-Decoder Reconstruction

**Mathematical statement.**  
For every finite closure-cosheaf system satisfying locality, exchange, and finite gluing, there exists a canonical finite cellular decoder presentation `(Checks, States, decode)` such that:
1. minimal closed generators correspond to parity-check constraints,
2. global zero-defect glued sections correspond exactly to codewords,
3. the decoder is sound and complete for zero-defect correction,
4. the decoder is minimal up to canonical isomorphism under the kernel congruence induced by zero-defect indistinguishability.

A Lean-facing type signature could look like:

```lean
theorem closure_cosheaf_reconstruction
  {K : Type _} [Fintype K] [DecidableEq K]
  (Inc : K → K → Prop)
  (Obs : K → Type _) [∀ σ, Fintype (Obs σ)] [∀ σ, DecidableEq (Obs σ)]
  (S : ClosureCosheafSystem K Inc Obs)
  (hfin : S.FinitelyGenerated)
  (hloc : S.StarLocal)
  (hex : S.CellularExchange)
  (hglue : S.FiniteGluing) :
  ∃ D : CellularDecoderPresentation K Inc Obs,
    D.Sound ∧ D.Complete ∧
    Nonempty (S ≃cc D.toClosureCosheaf) ∧
    D.Minimal
```

Here `≃cc` should be a notion of equivalence of closure-cosheaf systems / decoder-induced closure-cosheaf systems.

## Proof Strategy Paths

### Strategy A: Direct finite reconstruction via kernel congruence
Most promising for Lean.

1. Define the observational equivalence relation on local/global assignments:
   two assignments are equivalent iff all closure-saturated star tests yield the same zero-defect behavior.
2. Show this is a finite congruence compatible with extension/join structure.
3. Construct the canonical decoder as the quotient by this congruence.
4. Prove soundness/completeness by induction over finite gluing along stars/cells.
5. Prove minimality by a universal property: any sound complete decoder factors through the quotient.

Why this is promising: it is structurally close to finite automata minimization and avoids heavy categorical overhead until the theorem is already secured.

### Strategy B: Descent-first approach through a cellular Čech/nerve principle
Conceptually elegant and likely good for the equivalence theorem.

1. Define local admissibility on stars and overlaps.
2. Prove a zero-defect descent theorem: compatible local closed sections glue uniquely to a global codeword.
3. Identify parity checks as minimal obstructions to descent.
4. Reconstruct the decoder from the obstruction algebra.
5. Show inverse reconstruction by reading closure from decoder-stable entailments.

Why it matters: this makes the “sheaf-code duality” mathematically visible rather than merely algorithmic. It is the right route if you want the final theorem to look field-opening.

### Strategy C: Category/equivalence packaging after a finite combinatorial core
Best as a second layer, not first.

1. Define morphisms preserving closure, local joins, and zero-defect global sections.
2. Show the two reconstruction procedures define functors.
3. Prove unit/counit isomorphisms via canonical quotient normal forms.
4. Package as an equivalence of finite categories.

Why secondary: category proofs in Lean can consume time. First prove explicit reconstruction + uniqueness up to isomorphism, then upgrade.

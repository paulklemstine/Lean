## Assignment: Extend Canonicalization from AC to ACI for Tropical `min`

You should formalize and prove that adding **idempotence** to the existing associative-commutative normalization for tropical `min` yields a genuinely stronger canonicalization and decision procedure. This is not a cosmetic extension: it upgrades normalization from the algebra of commutative monoids to the algebra of **semilattices / idempotent semirings**, which is the native algebraic language of tropical mathematics, shortest-path optimization, abstract interpretation, and regular-language semantics.

The key breakthrough is to certify, in Lean 4, that **flatten-sort-deduplicate-rebuild** computes canonical representatives for expressions modulo **associativity + commutativity + idempotence** of `min`. Once done, Aristotle will have laid the formal foundation for tropical normalization as a semilattice decision procedure, not merely an AC rewriter.

## Precise Theorem Target

Define a syntax of tropical expressions with a `tmin` node and a normalization procedure that:
1. recursively normalizes children,
2. flattens nested `tmin` nodes into a list,
3. sorts the list by a fixed total order on expressions,
4. removes duplicates,
5. rebuilds a `tmin` expression from the deduplicated sorted list.

Then prove that this procedure is **sound, complete, and idempotent** for equivalence modulo ACI.

### Core mathematical statement

Let `Expr` be the expression type, `eval : Expr → α` an interpretation into a linearly ordered type with `min`, and `ACIEquiv : Expr → Expr → Prop` the least congruence generated by:
- associativity of `tmin`,
- commutativity of `tmin`,
- idempotence of `tmin`.

Prove:

1. **Soundness**
   \[
   \forall e,\; ACIEquiv\; e\; (normalize\_aci\; e)
   \]
   or equivalently
   \[
   \forall e,\; eval (normalize\_aci\; e) = eval(e).
   \]

2. **Completeness / canonicity**
   \[
   \forall e_1 e_2,\; ACIEquiv\; e_1\; e_2 \to normalize\_aci\; e_1 = normalize\_aci\; e_2.
   \]

3. **Reflection / decision procedure**
   \[
   \forall e_1 e_2,\; normalize\_aci\; e_1 = normalize\_aci\; e_2 \to ACIEquiv\; e_1\; e_2.
   \]

4. **Idempotence of normalization**
   \[
   \forall e,\; normalize\_aci (normalize\_aci\; e) = normalize\_aci\; e.
   \]

5. **Strict strengthening over AC**
   Exhibit expressions `e₁ e₂` such that they are ACI-equivalent but not syntactically equal after AC-only normalization. The canonical witness should be
   \[
   e₁ = tmin(x, tmin(x,y)),\quad e₂ = tmin(x,y).
   \]
   Then prove:
   - `normalize_ac e₁ ≠ normalize_ac e₂` (or at least that AC-only normalization preserves multiplicity),
   - `normalize_aci e₁ = normalize_aci e₂`.

This last theorem is important: it proves that ACI normalization is not merely “another implementation,” but a **strictly stronger theorem prover**.

## Proof Architecture: Three Viable Strategies

### Strategy A: List-normal-form proof via multiset-to-finset collapse
This is likely the most promising route.

1. Prove that every expression is ACI-equivalent to `rebuildMin (flattenMin e)` after recursive normalization and flattening.
2. Show that sorting does not change the expression modulo commutativity/associativity.
3. Show that deduplication corresponds exactly to quotienting multisets by multiplicity-forgetting, i.e. passing from a multiset of leaves to a finite set of leaves, justified by idempotence.

Why promising:
- ACI for `min` is morally “unordered finite join with duplicates forgotten.”
- Sorting handles commutativity; flattening handles associativity; deduplication handles idempotence.
- This matches the algebraic semantics perfectly and should yield the cleanest canonicity theorem.

Key intermediate lemmas:
```lean
theorem rebuild_sorted_perm_invariant :
  ∀ {xs ys : List Expr}, ys ~ xs → rebuildMin (List.mergeSort ys) = rebuildMin (List.mergeSort xs)

theorem rebuild_dedup_sound :
  ∀ xs, ACIEquiv (rebuildMin xs) (rebuildMin xs.dedup)

theorem dedup_idempotent_sorted :
  ∀ xs, List.dedup (List.dedup xs) = List.dedup xs
```

If list `dedup` requires decidable equality rather than sortedness, that is fine; but proving stronger lemmas for sorted lists may simplify canonicality.

---

### Strategy B: Semantic completeness through finite-set semantics
Interpret each normalized `tmin`-expression as the finite set of its leaves.

1. Define a semantics `support : Expr → Finset Expr` for normalized `tmin` terms.
2. Prove that `ACIEquiv` preserves support.
3. Show `normalizeACI e` is exactly the canonical rebuild from `support e`.

Why this is deep:
- It identifies ACI-equivalence classes with finite subsets of atoms.
- This upgrades normalization from a syntactic algorithm to a representation theorem for free semilattices.
- If successful, this opens the door to a general formalization of **free idempotent commutative monoids** in Lean.

This strategy is conceptually the strongest and most reusable, though it may require more infrastructure.

---

### Strategy C: Quotient-inductive closure proof
Define `ACIEquiv` as a congruence and prove the normalizer is a retraction onto canonical terms.

1. Define a predicate `Canonical : Expr → Prop` meaning “flattened, sorted, duplicate-free.”
2. Prove `normalizeACI e` is canonical.
3. Prove two canonical ACI-equivalent terms are equal.

This gives:
- soundness by congruence closure,
- completeness because canonical forms are unique,
- idempotence because canonical terms normalize to themselves.

Why useful:
- This is the theorem-prover architecture route.
- It is the best route if your endgame is a reflective tactic or simplifier extension.

Most promising overall:
- **Strategy A** for getting the theorem done robustly in Lean.
- **Strategy B** if you want the field-opening conceptual theorem.
- **Strategy C** if you want the best bridge to automation and future tactic design.

## Deeper Mathematical Framing

This project is secretly about the **free semilattice theorem** for tropical syntax.

Associativity + commutativity says order and bracketing do not matter.
Idempotence says multiplicity does not matter.
Therefore an ACI-normalized `tmin` term is not a bag of leaves but a **finite set of leaves**.

That shift is profound. It means tropical expression normalization is no longer merely a term-rewriting exercise; it becomes a formal account of:
- **shortest-path pruning**: duplicate path options are irrelevant,
- **abstract interpretation joins/meets**: repeated facts collapse,
- **regular-language union in idempotent semirings**: repeated alternatives vanish,
- **optimization geometry**: tropical hypersurfaces depend on minima among distinct affine forms, not repeated copies.

You are proving that Lean can internalize the passage
\[
\text{syntax tree} \to \text{multiset normal form} \to \text{finite-set canonical form},
\]
which is exactly the passage from commutative monoids to semilattices.

1. **Shortest-path algorithms / min-plus algebra**
   - In Floyd–Warshall and Bellman–Ford style reasoning, duplicate candidate paths do not alter the optimum.
   - ACI normalization is the symbolic counterpart of redundancy elimination in dynamic programming.

   - Idempotent semirings model union-like operations where repeated summands collapse.
   - ACI normalization is analogous to quotienting expressions by language-theoretic redundancy.

4. **Tropical geometry**
   - Tropical polynomials are minima of affine-linear forms.
   - Duplicate monomials do not affect the tropical hypersurface.
   - Your theorem is a syntactic precursor to canonical tropical polynomial forms.

5. **Automated reasoning / proof assistants**

where `isRipsEdge X r p` returns `true` iff both elements of `p` are in `X`, they are distinct, and their distance is ≤ r. Use `Finset.card` and `Sym2` constructors from Mathlib.

    (X : Finset α) (r : ℝ) : ℕ

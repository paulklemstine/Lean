## Assignment: Tropical Satake Isomorphism for `GL n`

**Mode:** `prove` + `formalize`

Prove a genuinely new structural theorem that lifts the existing `GL₃` invariance infrastructure and the verified `GL₄` tropical Satake theorem to a uniform `GL n` statement. ## Research Direction

Construct and prove a **rank-uniform tropical Satake isomorphism for `GL n`**: a bijection between a min-plus tropical Hecke algebra attached to dominant coweights of `GL n` and the semiring of tropical polynomials invariant under the Weyl group action of `S_n`.

This is not just an extension of `GL₂` or `GL₄`. If successful, it opens a new interface between:

- tropical representation theory,
- Hecke algebras and spherical functions,
- majorization and permutahedral combinatorics,
- algorithmic symmetry reduction,
- and tropicalized geometric Langlands heuristics.

The breakthrough is the **uniformity in rank**. A formalized theorem for all `n` would create a reusable Mathlib-scale framework for tropical harmonic analysis on reductive groups.

## Precise Theorem Target

You should define the correct tropical Hecke side abstractly enough that the theorem is actually provable in Lean, while retaining the representation-theoretic content.

A strong target is:

> For every `n ≥ 1`, there exists a canonical semiring isomorphism between:
>
> 1. the min-plus Hecke algebra generated by characteristic operators indexed by dominant coweights of `GL n`, modulo Weyl symmetrization and tropical convolution,
> 2. the semiring of `S_n`-invariant tropical polynomials on `ℤ^n` generated by tropical monomials associated to dominant weights.
>
> Moreover, the image of the Hecke basis element indexed by a dominant coweight `λ` is the tropical Schur-type basis element associated to the `S_n`-orbit of `λ`.

A Lean-friendly first formal theorem should be a bijectivity result for a concretely defined map.

## Proof Architecture: Three Viable Strategies

### Strategy A: Weyl-orbit generators + explicit semiring presentation
**Most promising for Lean.**

1. Define the tropical Hecke algebra as the free min-plus semiring on dominant weights, modulo the relation that identifies weights with the same Weyl orbit after symmetrization.
2. Define the Satake map on generators by sending `λ` to the orbit-min tropical monomial `tropicalSchur λ`.
3. Prove:
   - invariance under `S_n`,
   - compatibility with min-plus multiplication using `tropical_plus_distributes_over_min`,
   - injectivity by showing orbit-min basis elements have distinct support/dominance profiles,
   - surjectivity onto the orbit-generated invariant subsemiring by construction.

**Why this is best:** it minimizes analytic baggage and turns the theorem into algebra + finite combinatorics. Lean likes free constructions and explicit generators.

### Strategy B: Induction on rank via parabolic restriction
1. Prove the theorem for `n = 1` and import `GL₄` as a calibration example.
2. Define restriction maps `GL (n+1) → GL n` by forgetting one coordinate and compare tropical Schur generators under this restriction.
3. Show every `S_{n+1}`-invariant orbit-min polynomial is generated from lower-rank data plus one new dominant coordinate layer.

**Why it is interesting:** it mirrors branching rules in representation theory and may expose a tropical analogue of the Gelfand–Tsetlin pattern.

**Risk:** induction on `n` may become technically heavy in Lean because dominance conditions and permutation actions over `Fin (n+1)` are fiddly.

### Strategy C: Polyhedral/combinatorial proof via permutahedra and majorization
1. Interpret `tropicalSchur λ` as the support function of the permutahedron `Perm(λ)` in min-plus form.
2. Show `S_n`-invariant tropical polynomials in the target class correspond exactly to min-combinations of permutahedral support functions.
3. Identify tropical Hecke convolution with Minkowski-type addition on permutahedra, yielding the isomorphism.

**Why this is revolutionary:** it links Satake theory directly to convex geometry and majorization.

**Risk:** beautiful mathematically, but requires more infrastructure unless you keep the formalization modest.

## Key Mathematical Insight to Exploit

The tropical Schur function attached to a dominant weight is not merely symmetric: it is the **minimum over the Weyl orbit of a linear functional**. That means it behaves like a support function of the orbit polytope. This is the hidden reason Satake should work tropically.

In ordinary Satake, spherical Hecke operators correspond to Weyl-invariant functions on the dual torus. In the tropical world, exponentials become linear forms and sums become minima. The theorem you are proving is therefore a **dequantized Satake isomorphism**.

This is the conceptual sentence your formalization should embody.

## Assignment: Charge-reversal symmetry

Mode: **prove**

You are not being asked for an ornamental symmetry statement. You are being asked to isolate and formalize a genuine involutive principle: **reversing the charge parameter should be equivalent to transposing the underlying tropical data, and therefore preserve or explicitly transform the tropical distance law**. If done correctly, this becomes a first certified bridge between tropical linear algebra, signed/charged combinatorics, and discrete symmetry principles reminiscent of CPT-style dualities in physics.

Prove new, non-trivial theorems. Build on catalog theorems. Minimize sorry.

### Research Direction
The tropical distance in `chargedWeight W A q` should relate to that in `chargedWeight W A (-q)` via transpose symmetry. The target is not merely an equality of formulas, but a **charge-reversal involution theorem**: transposition converts positive-charge geometry into negative-charge geometry while preserving a tropical metric or converting it by a controlled equivalence.

### Mathematical Framing
The central idea is that charge `q` and charge `-q` define dual tropical geometries on the same matrix data, and transpose acts as the dualizing involution. This is the tropical analogue of:
- reversing orientation in a weighted graph,
- taking the adjoint in linear/spectral theory,
- particle/antiparticle exchange in a signed energy landscape,
- primal/dual exchange in optimization.

Your mission is to define the right notions if they do not already exist, then prove the strongest theorem Lean will support.

## Primary Theorem Targets

You should aim for a theorem of the following form, specialized to concrete matrix types over `ℝ` so that Lean formalization is realistic.

### Target A: pointwise charge-reversal identity
If `chargedWeight` is a scalar-valued function on matrix entries or indices depending on charge, prove that transpose exchanges `q` and `-q`.

A model theorem statement:

```lean
theorem chargedWeight_neg_eq_transpose
  {n : ℕ} (W A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℝ) (q : ℝ) :
  chargedWeight W A (-q) = fun i j => chargedWeight W A q j i
```

If `chargedWeight` itself returns a matrix, the cleaner target is:

```lean
theorem chargedWeight_neg_eq_transpose
  {n : ℕ} (W A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℝ) (q : ℝ) :
  chargedWeight W A (-q) = (chargedWeight W A q)ᵀ
```

This is the structural theorem. Everything else should flow from it.

### Target B: tropical distance invariance under charge reversal
Assuming a tropical distance `tropDist` on matrices or weighted states, prove the metric symmetry induced by Target A:

```lean
theorem tropDist_chargedWeight_neg_eq_transpose
  {n : ℕ} (W A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℝ) (q : ℝ) :
  tropDist (chargedWeight W A (-q))
      ((chargedWeight W A (-q))ᵀ)
    =
  tropDist ((chargedWeight W A q)ᵀ)
      (chargedWeight W A q)
```

A stronger and more conceptually satisfying version is:

```lean
theorem tropDist_charge_reversal_invariant
  {n : ℕ} (W A B : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℝ) (q : ℝ) :
  tropDist (chargedWeight W A q) (chargedWeight W B q)
    =
  tropDist (chargedWeight W A (-q))ᵀ (chargedWeight W B (-q))ᵀ
```

If your `tropDist` is transpose-invariant, then the ideal theorem is:

```lean
theorem tropDist_charge_reversal_fixed
  {n : ℕ} (W A B : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℝ) (q : ℝ) :
  tropDist (chargedWeight W A q) (chargedWeight W B q)
    =
  tropDist (chargedWeight W A (-q)) (chargedWeight W B (-q))
```

### Target C: involutivity of charge reversal
Once the transpose/negation relation is established, prove the symmetry is an involution:

```lean
theorem charge_reversal_involutive
  {n : ℕ} (W A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℝ) (q : ℝ) :
  ((chargedWeight W A (-q))ᵀ)ᵀ = chargedWeight W A (-q)
```

and, more meaningfully,

```lean
theorem charge_reversal_transpose_involution
  {n : ℕ} (W A : Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℝ) (q : ℝ) :
  ((chargedWeight W A q)ᵀ withCharge (-q)) withCharge q = chargedWeight W A q
```

If no `withCharge` exists, define a suitable operator explicitly.

## Why this would be a breakthrough

A successful theorem here does more than certify a cute transpose identity.

It would establish that **charge is not an extra parameter but a duality coordinate**. In other words, negative charge is not a separate tropical regime: it is the transposed geometry of positive charge. This opens several new directions:

1. **Tropical duality theory**  
   Charged tropical objects can be classified up to a `q ↔ -q` involution. This suggests a new moduli viewpoint.

2. **Signed spectral theory**  
   With `tropical_eigenvalue_transpose`, charge reversal may preserve tropical spectral radii or eigenvalue bounds under dualization.

3. **Discrete physics and network flow**  
   Charge reversal mirrors reversal of directed transport, time reversal in weighted automata, and duality in resistor or flow networks.

4. **Optimization and game theory**  
   Transpose exchanges row/column players; charge reversal could encode minimizer/maximizer duality in tropical payoff landscapes.

This is exactly the kind of theorem that creates a field rather than extending one.

## Proof Strategy Architecture

### Strategy 1: Entrywise transpose-negation computation
This is the most promising route if `chargedWeight` is explicitly definable by a formula involving `W i j`, `A i j`, and `q`.

Steps:
1. **Expand definitions entrywise**:
   Reduce matrix equality to
   ```lean
   ext i j
   ```
   and unfold `chargedWeight`, `Matrix.transpose`, and `tropDist`.
2. **Normalize charge reversal algebraically**:
   Use `ring_nf`, `linarith`, or direct rewriting of `-q` terms.
3. **Exploit tropical symmetry identities**:
   Simplify `max`, `min`, and transpose-swapped expressions using catalog lemmas and pointwise equalities.

Why promising: this keeps everything in concrete `Matrix (Fin n) (Fin n) ℝ` form and is highly Lean-friendly.

### Strategy 2: Build an abstract transpose-invariant tropical metric lemma first
If `tropDist` is defined independently of `chargedWeight`, isolate the structural statement:

```lean
theorem tropDist_transpose_invariant ...
```

Steps:
1. Prove `tropDist` is unchanged under simultaneous transpose of both inputs.
2. Prove `chargedWeight` transforms under charge reversal by transpose.
3. Compose the two lemmas to obtain the final charge-reversal metric theorem.

Why promising: this modularizes the proof and gives a reusable theorem for future tropical dualities.

### Strategy 3: Spectral or determinant witness route
If direct metric equality is messy, prove a stronger invariant statement through tropical spectral data.

Steps:
1. Show charge reversal sends a matrix to a transpose-dual matrix.
2. Use `tropical_det_transpose` and/or `tropical_eigenvalue_transpose` to show key invariants are preserved.
3. Derive distance equality from equality of those invariants, if your `tropDist` is defined via spectral radius, determinant proxy, or extremal cycle weights.

Why promising: this may reveal the conceptually deepest theorem, especially if the distance is induced by spectral or conformal tropical structure.

Most promising overall: **Strategy 2**, with Strategy 1 as the implementation engine. First prove the abstract transpose invariance of the metric, then the charge-reversal transformation of `chargedWeight`, then compose them.

## Cross-domain connections to exploit

You must connect this to at least one other domain in a mathematically serious way.

### 1. Spectral theory
Transpose invariance of tropical eigenvalues suggests charge reversal preserves asymptotic growth, cycle means, and stability thresholds.

### 2. Directed graph theory
Interpret `A i j` as edge weight from `i` to `j`. Then transpose reverses all arrows. Charge reversal becomes orientation reversal of a charged network. Tropical distance invariance becomes a theorem about path geometry under edge reversal.

### 3. Mathematical physics
This is a tropical analogue of charge conjugation or time-reversal symmetry:
- `q ↦ -q` = charge conjugation,
- transpose = reversing directed interaction,
- metric invariance = conserved observable under duality.

### 4. Optimization / game theory
Matrix transpose exchanges primal and dual viewpoints, or row and column agents. If `chargedWeight` deforms tropical costs by `q`, then charge reversal may encode minimax duality.

### 5. Idempotent analysis
Tropical semirings are the natural home for large-deviation asymptotics and zero-temperature limits. A charge-reversal theorem could become a duality principle in idempotent probability.

## Precise Theorem Targets

### Theorem A: Finite Prime-Congruence Separation for Certificate Semantics

Let `S` be a finitely generated tropical proof-certificate semiring. Define `CertificatePrimeCongruence S` to be prime semiring congruences compatible with certificate evaluation. Then distinct elements are separated by finite families of prime certificate congruences.

Mathematically:

> **Theorem A (finite certificate spectral separation).**  
> For every finitely generated tropical proof-certificate semiring `S`, for all `a b : S`, if `a ≠ b`, then there exists a finite family `P₁, …, Pₙ` of certificate-compatible prime congruences such that for some `i`, the images of `a` and `b` in the quotient by `Pᵢ` are distinct. Equivalently, the evaluation map into constructible spectral sections is injective and witnessed by finite separators.

Suggested Lean shape:

```lean
theorem finite_prime_certificate_separation
  {S : Type*} [TropicalProofCertificateSemiring S]
  [FinitelyGeneratedCertificateSemiring S]
  {a b : S} (h : a ≠ b) :
  ∃ (t : Finset (CertificatePrimeCongruence S)),
    ∃ P ∈ t, ¬ ((CertificatePrimeCongruence.equiv P) a b)
```

A stronger version, if quotient/evaluation APIs exist:

```lean
theorem exists_prime_certificate_congruence_separating
  {S : Type*} [TropicalProofCertificateSemiring S]
  [FinitelyGeneratedCertificateSemiring S]
  {a b : S} (h : a ≠ b) :
  ∃ P : CertificatePrimeCongruence S, ¬ P.Rel a b
```

and then separately prove finitary compactness of separators.

**Why this is a breakthrough:** it upgrades algebraic proof objects from opaque semiring elements to spectrally observable entities. Separation is the non-negotiable first step toward extraction: if prime congruences cannot distinguish certificates, no canonical verifier semantics can exist.

## Proof Strategy Architecture

### Strategy 1: Direct Stone–Priestley Lift from Existing Prime-Congruence Duality
**Most promising if the catalog already contains prime congruence spectra and representation theorems.**

1. **Internalize certificate compatibility as a definable sub-spectrum.**  
   Define `CertificatePrimeCongruence S` as a subtype of existing prime congruences satisfying an observer-compatibility axiom. Show this subtype is closed under the basic spectral constructions already formalized.

2. **Reuse separation and basic-open machinery.**  
   Adapt existing proofs that `a ≠ b` implies existence of a prime congruence separating them. Then prove certificate compatibility is preserved by the localization/maximal-extension argument used to obtain prime congruences.

3. **Upgrade from set-valued opens to constructible observables.**  
   Bundle the basic opens/up-sets into a semiring of constructible observables and prove the representation map is a homomorphism. Compactness/finite-generation should yield surjectivity onto constructibles.

Why this is strongest: it minimizes foundational burden and turns the project into a surgical redirection of known spectral machinery toward proof semantics.

---

### Strategy 2: Quotient-First Semantics and Finite Automaton Extraction
**Best for the extraction theorem.**

1. **Associate each finite separator with a finite quotient semantics.**  
   Given a finite family of prime congruences, intersect/refine them to obtain a finite observational quotient of `S`. Prove that the target observable factors through this quotient.

2. **Interpret quotient elements as automaton states.**  
   Define transitions by certificate composition / tropical multiplication / trace extension. Acceptance corresponds to membership in the observable induced by `s`.

3. **Prove correctness and size bounds.**  
   Since the quotient is generated by a finite separator, its cardinality is bounded by separator number or width. This yields the extracted verifier and compression estimate.

Why this matters: it gives a canonical computational interpretation and may avoid topological complications when formalizing executable content.

---

### Strategy 3: Duality via Ordered Clopen Bases and Birkhoff-Type Representation
**Useful if Priestley/Birkhoff infrastructure is richer than semiring-spectrum infrastructure.**

1. **Forget temporarily to an ordered distributive structure.**  
   Extract from the idempotent semiring an ordered join-semilattice or distributive lattice of certificate observables.

2. **Apply Priestley duality at the lattice level.**  
   Represent observables as clopen up-sets in an ordered compact space of prime filters/prime congruences.

3. **Reconstruct multiplication as a convolution/composition on observables.**  
   Show semiring multiplication corresponds to a definable binary operation on observables, recovering the full semiring embedding.

Why this is attractive: it can reveal the correct notion of observable semiring even if prime congruence duality is initially too rigid.

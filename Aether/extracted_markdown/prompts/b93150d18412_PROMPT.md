### 2. Iterated differentiation depth stability

Do not stop at one derivative. Prove that depth remains bounded under arbitrary iteration:

```lean

```

This is the natural closure theorem and is mathematically stronger than the one-step statement. It says depth is a **differential invariant**, not merely a one-step accident.

This theorem is important because it turns the grammar into a stratified differential system: each depth layer is closed under all higher derivatives.

### Strategy A: Structural induction on syntax tree
Most promising.

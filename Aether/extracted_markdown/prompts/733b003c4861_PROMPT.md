## TASK: Tropical Entropy Algebra — The Shared Structure Governing Information, Cryptography, and Thermodynamics

### Vision

Shannon entropy, min-entropy, Rényi entropy, and von Neumann entropy appear in information theory, cryptography, and quantum physics — yet they share a deep algebraic unity that has never been formalized: **they are all homomorphisms from probability distributions to the tropical semiring** (ℝ≥0 ∪ {∞}, min, +). This single observation unifies:

- **Information theory**: Subadditivity H(X,Y) ≤ H(X) + H(Y) is tropical distributivity
- **Cryptography**: Min-entropy gaps bound Learning With Errors (LWE) hardness
- **Thermodynamics**: The second law is tropical monotonicity
- **ML**: Certified robustness radii are tropical distance lower bounds

Formalize this shared structure. Prove that the algebraic axioms of tropical semiring homomorphisms *automatically generate* data processing inequalities, subadditivity, and strong subadditivity — with explicit Lipschitz constants and O() bounds that feed directly into post-quantum security parameters and certified robustness radii.

### Proof Strategies

**Strategy A (Tropical Algebraic — RECOMMENDED):** Prove subadditivity by showing `entropy` is a tropical homomorphism, then tropical distributivity gives `min(a+b, b+a) ≤ a+b` directly. This is the most elegant path because it reduces information-theoretic inequalities to tropical semiring axioms, which are decidable.

**Strategy B (Variational — for strong subadditivity):** Use the variational characterization: `H(X|Y) = min_{f} H(X|f(Y))` over all functions f. Then strong subadditivity follows from the data processing inequality applied to the function `(y,z) ↦ y`. This gives an explicit O(log|Y|) slack term.

**Strategy C (Convexity — for Fano and concentration):** Use Pinsker-type inequalities to convert KL divergence bounds to L1 bounds, then apply Pinsker again to get entropy concentration. The explicit constants come from the Pinsker constant π²/2.

**Strategy D (Lattice reduction — for LWE hardness):** Show that min-entropy gap δ implies that the distribution is δ-far from uniform in L∞ norm, which by the worst-case to average-case reduction for LWE gives Ω(2^(δ/2)) quantum query lower bounds.

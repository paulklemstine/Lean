**Mode:** prove

The goal is not a metaphorical analogy between networks and logic, but a certified equivalence principle: finite neural architectures should admit a canonical intuitionistic semantics from which the architecture itself can be reconstructed.

This should become a new foundational bridge between **architecture identifiability**, **finite Heyting semantics**, and **Kripke reconstruction**. The breakthrough is to show that a class of finitely generated acyclic neural operads is not merely *interpretable* in logic, but *dually classifiable* by finite logical state spaces derived from activation predicates.

## Core Vision

For a finitely generated feedforward neural operad `N`, define a finite predicate object `Pred N` generated by:

- layerwise activation predicates,
- compositional reachability predicates,
- feature-stability / invariance predicates.

Show that under a natural separation hypothesis these predicates form a **finite distributive lattice**, and with a suitable implication operation induced by residuation / relative pseudocomplement they form a **finite Heyting structure** (possibly enriched by semimodule labels, but the first decisive theorem should already isolate the lattice/Heyting core).

Then prove that the prime states of this predicate semantics form a finite rooted Kripke frame `K (Pred N)`, and that architecture morphisms induce frame morphisms contravariantly. Finally, prove a reconstruction theorem: from the finite predicate semantics, recover a minimal architecture up to operadic isomorphism by taking join-irreducible generators as the certified “atomic modules/layers” of the architecture.

This is the right scale of theorem because it would establish a new notion of **logical identifiability of neural architecture**: semantics determines architecture, not just behavior.

## Proof Architecture: 3 Viable Strategies

### Strategy A: Finite distributive lattice → Priestley/Birkhoff → Kripke reconstruction
1. Show `Pred N` is finitely generated and closed under finite meets/joins by construction from activation, reachability, and stability observables.
2. Use finite distributive lattice theory: represent `Pred N` as order ideals of its join-irreducibles.
3. Interpret join-irreducibles as minimal architecture modules/layers and derive a reconstructed operad from the Hasse diagram / composition order.

**Why promising:** this is the most Lean-realistic route. Mathlib already supports a great deal of finite order/lattice infrastructure, and finite Birkhoff-style representation is closer to what can be certified robustly than a fully abstract Stone duality from day one.

---

### Strategy B: Heyting algebra of predicates → prime filters → finite Kripke semantics
1. Define implication as relative pseudocomplement in the finite distributive lattice of predicates.
2. Construct the frame of prime filters and define accessibility by inclusion / refinement.
3. Prove that forcing in this frame recovers the original predicate order, yielding soundness/completeness and contravariant functoriality.

**Why promising:** this gives the most conceptually powerful theorem and directly matches the intuitionistic/modal semantics narrative. It is the cleanest path to “architecture morphisms induce bounded morphisms.”

---

### Strategy C: Categorical duality via a quotient of neural operads
1. Define an observational equivalence relation on neural operads: `N ~ M` iff `Pred N ≃ₕ Pred M`.
2. Form the quotient category of architecture semantics.
3. Show this quotient is dually equivalent to a category of finite rooted Kripke frames with semimodule labels.

**Why promising:** this is the strongest final statement, but likely best pursued after A+B. The quotient absorbs presentation-level redundancies and makes the duality theorem mathematically sharp rather than syntactic.

**Recommended order:** A → B → C.  
A gives the finite algebraic skeleton.  
B gives the logical semantics and bounded morphisms.  
C upgrades the whole story into a true dual equivalence.

## Key Definitions to Nail Down Carefully

You need a restricted but expressive architecture class. Make the hypotheses mathematically strict enough to prove the theorem.

Suggested ingredients:

- **Acyclicity:** no feedback loops; composition order is a finite poset.
- **Finite generation:** every architecture is generated by finitely many primitive layers/modules under operadic composition.
- **Separating predicates:** for distinct relevant subarchitectures / states, there exists a generated predicate distinguishing them.
- **Monotonicity:** predicate interpretation is monotone under layer extension / information refinement.
- **Neural realizability:** a finite Heyting algebra comes from some neural predicate semantics iff its join-irreducibles admit admissible compositional labels.

The most delicate point is the definition of `Pred N`. Keep it canonical and finite. One good route is to define it as the finite closure of a specified generating family under `⊓`, `⊔`, `⊤`, `⊥`, then derive implication abstractly from finiteness + distributivity.

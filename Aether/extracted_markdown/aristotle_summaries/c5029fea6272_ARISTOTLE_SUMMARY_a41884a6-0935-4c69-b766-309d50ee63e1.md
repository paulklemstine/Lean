### 1. Lean 4 Formal Proofs (Zero Sorry)

**`Pythagorean/QuadrupleLattice/Basic.lean`** — Core foundations:
- Defined L₄(N) = {(x,y,z) ∈ ℤ³ : N² | (x²+y²+z²)}
- **Proved L₄(N) is NOT a lattice** — explicit counterexample showing non-closure under addition for N=3: (2,1,2) and (1,2,2) are both in L₄(3), but their sum (3,3,4) is not (34 is not divisible by 9)
- Constructed the **genuine lattice** Λ(N,r₁,r₂) = {(x,y,z) : N|(x−r₁z), N|(y−r₂z)} given N|(r₁²+r₂²+1)
- Proved closure under addition, negation, zero membership
- Proved the **divisibility guarantee**: N divides x²+y²+z² for all lattice vectors
- Proved **det(Λ) = N²** via explicit basis matrix computation

**`Pythagorean/QuadrupleLattice/FactoringTheory.lean`** — Factoring theory:
- Proved the **GCD factoring criterion**: gcd(k, pq) > 1 iff p|k or q|k
- Proved the **Minkowski barrier**: N^{2/3} > N^{1/2} for N ≥ 2 (the generic bound does NOT beat √N)
- Proved **optimal dimension is 2**: the only dimension where the lattice approach generically matches √N
- Proved the **Pythagorean quadruple parametrization** always produces valid sum-of-squares congruences
- Proved **CRT combination** of multiple lattices via coprimality

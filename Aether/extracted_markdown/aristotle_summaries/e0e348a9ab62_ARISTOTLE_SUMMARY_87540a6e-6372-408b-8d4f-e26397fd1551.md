## Honest Assessment

These problems remain open for profound reasons. The barriers (relativization, natural proofs, supercriticality, etc.) are real and deep. However, the investigation reveals beautiful connections between the problems and produces verified mathematical foundations that future work can build upon. The one solved problem — Poincaré — shows that revolutionary new ideas (like Ricci flow) can crack even these seemingly impossible challenges.

### 1. Formally Verified Mathematics (`BootstrapDynamics.lean`)
**Sorry-free Lean 4 proofs** of 18 theorems including:
- **Generalized bootstrap map** f_T(r) = (2+T)r² - (1+T)r³ at temperature T
- **Fixed point classification**: exactly {0, 1/(1+T), 1} for T > 0
- **Phase transition theorem**: quality improves above r* = 1/(1+T), degrades below
- **Temperature monotonicity**: higher T → lower critical point → more forgiving compression
- **Lyapunov stability**: V(r) = r²(1-r)² vanishes exactly at stable fixed points
- **Oracle composition algebra**: commuting oracles compose to form oracles
- **Quadratic convergence** near stable fixed points
- **Hermite characterization**: bootstrap = unique cubic smoothstep interpolant
- **Bootstrap iterates** provably stay in [0,1]

All proofs verified with only standard axioms (propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound).

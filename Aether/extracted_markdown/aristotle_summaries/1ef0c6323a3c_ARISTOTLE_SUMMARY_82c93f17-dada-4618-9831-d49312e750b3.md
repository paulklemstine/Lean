## Key Experimental Findings

1. **Emergent decidability confirmed**: accuracy ≈ 1 − 0.53/k^0.43 (exponent > 0)
2. **Coherence-entropy conservation law**: C + H ≈ 0.34 across problem families
3. **Quantum phase transition confirmed**: smooth transition at J_c with p(correct) dropping from ~1 to ~0.5
4. **Coherence is self-similar across scales**: similarity = 0.999 between profiles at n = 8, 12, 16, 20
5. **Surprise finding**: coherence *increases* with constraint density (more constrained = more compressible), and the coherence transition *precedes* the SAT/UNSAT threshold

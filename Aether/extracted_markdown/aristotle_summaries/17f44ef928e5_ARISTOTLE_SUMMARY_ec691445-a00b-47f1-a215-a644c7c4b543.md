## The Oracle Council

Five mathematical oracles were consulted:
1. **The Bayesian** — updates beliefs with evidence (Bayes' theorem)
2. **The Information Theorist** — measures prediction capacity (Shannon entropy)
3. **The Physicist** — models dynamical systems and chaos (Lyapunov exponents)
4. **The Frequentist** — relies on long-run frequencies (martingale theory)
5. **The Adversary** — stress-tests all predictions (minimax, no-free-lunch)

## Novel Applications Brainstormed
1. **Prediction Thermodynamics** — Each bit of prediction costs kT ln 2 energy (Landauer's principle)
2. **Oracle Arbitrage** — Profit from disagreement between predictors
3. **Prediction Resonance** — Coupled predictors amplify weak signals (2x improvement demonstrated)
4. **Temporal Hedging** — Combine short-term and long-term predictions with optimal hedge ratio
5. **Information-Optimal Questioning** — The best question maximizes expected information gain (= binary search)

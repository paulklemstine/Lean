## The Omega Tower: A Complete Machine-Verified Ascent to ε₀

I've created a comprehensive research project extending the Omega Tower, formalized in Lean 4 with Mathlib, accompanied by Python demos, research notes, a research paper, and a Scientific American article. All files live in `Ecstasis2/OmegaTower/`.

### Lean Formalization (`Basic.lean`) — Fully Verified, Zero Sorries

**Definitions:**
- `omegaTower : ℕ → Ordinal` — the sequence 1, ω, ω^ω, ω^(ω^ω), ...
- `epsilon0 : Ordinal` — ε₀ defined as `Ordinal.nfp (ω ^ ·) 0`

**Five Main Theorems (all machine-verified):**

1. **`omegaTower_strictMono`** — Each level is strictly larger than the last
2. **`omegaTower_lt_epsilon0`** — Every finite level is below ε₀
3. **`epsilon0_fixed_point`** — ω^(ε₀) = ε₀ (the defining equation)
4. **`epsilon0_le_of_fixed_point`** — ε₀ is the *least* fixed point of ω^·
5. **`epsilon0_isSuccLimit`** — ε₀ is a limit ordinal (not zero, not a successor)

**Additional theorems:** `omegaTower_pos`, `one_le_omegaTower`, `omegaTower_lt_succ`, `omegaTower_eq_iterate_zero`, `epsilon0_pos`, `omega0_lt_epsilon0`, and key helper lemmas about normal functions.

All proofs depend only on standard axioms: `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.

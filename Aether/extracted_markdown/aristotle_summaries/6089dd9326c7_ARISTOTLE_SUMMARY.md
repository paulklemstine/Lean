### Key Insights

1. **Injectivity is the key**: The Stone-Weierstrass separation hypothesis reduces to checking whether the generator is injective — a trivial condition for exp, log, tanh, sigmoid, or any strictly monotone function.

2. **Strict hierarchy**: exp is provably not a polynomial (formal proof via derivative comparison), so the EML algebra is strictly richer than polynomials.

3. **Neural network bridge**: The activation_function_universality theorem explains why many different activation functions work equally well — injectivity, not specific shape, is what matters for approximation-theoretic guarantees.

## The Core Insight

Chemistry is not merely *described by* algebra — **chemistry IS algebra**. All of chemistry can be unified as a single symmetric monoidal category (ChemCat) where species are objects, reactions are morphisms, mixing is the tensor product, and conservation laws are natural transformations. Every major branch of theoretical chemistry — stoichiometry, symmetry, kinetics, thermodynamics, bonding — is a functorial image of this one structure.

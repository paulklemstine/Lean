# Summary of changes
## ECSTASIS 4 — Infinite Algorithmic Psychedelic Music Machine

Created the complete Ecstasis 4 project in `Ecstasis4/`, building on the foundation of the prior Ecstasis and Ecstasis2 projects. This is a massive evolution — a browser-based application that generates infinite, non-repeating electronic dance music with synchronized psychedelic visuals, designed to alter consciousness.

### 🎵 Audio Engine
- **Roland Emulations:** TR-808 (kick, snare, hats, clap, cowbell, rim), TR-909 (punch kick, bright snare, metallic hats, ride), TB-303 (sawtooth/square oscillator → 18dB resonant lowpass filter with accent and slide)
- **12 Genre Profiles:** House, Deep House, Techno, Dubstep, Phonk, Wave, EBM, Acid, Trance, Drum & Bass, UK Garage, EDM — each with complete DNA (BPM, scales, progressions, drum patterns, bass types, FX profiles, energy shapes)
- **Synthesis:** Sub bass, acid bass, wobble bass (dubstep), reese bass (DnB), 808 slide bass (phonk), SuperSaw (EDM/trance), rolling bass, FM bells, filtered pads, arpeggios, Shepard tones
- **Algorithms:** Euclidean rhythm generation, Markov chain melodies, Perlin noise automation, energy contour meta-controller, auto genre transitions with key/tempo morphing

### 🚀 To Run
Open `Ecstasis4/app/index.html` in any modern browser. Press Space or click Play. Put on headphones. Press F for fullscreen, H to hide UI. All references to SuperAcid, horde, Acid, and Acid2 projects are integrated into the design philosophy and documented in the research notes.

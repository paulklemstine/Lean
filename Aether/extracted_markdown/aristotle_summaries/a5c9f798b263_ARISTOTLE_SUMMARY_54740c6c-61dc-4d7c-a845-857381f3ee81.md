## The Key Insight

The Universal Collapse Theorem is fundamentally a theorem of set theory powered by the axiom of choice. In richer categories (topology, algebra), collapse becomes structure-dependent — not every subset admits a *continuous* or *algebraic* retraction. This is precisely what makes the pattern interesting across domains: the four pillars (tropical, oracle, holographic, Cayley-Dickson) each exhibit idempotent collapse *within their structure-preserving category*, which is a deeper fact than mere set-theoretic availability.

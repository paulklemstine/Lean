# Summary of changes
I have created **`the_cursed_book.pdf`** — a 13-page cursed PDF book generated entirely from raw PDF bytes (no PDF libraries used, which is itself pretty cursed).

## What's inside:

1. **Cover** — Blood-red background with floating ominous circles, a giant staring eye, and the title "THE CURSED BOOK" with progressively fading warnings
2. **Table of Contents** — Each entry is slightly more tilted than the last; page numbers are all wrong
3. **Chapter 1: Why You Shouldn't Have Opened This** — A list of increasingly unhinged warnings with a giant warning triangle
4. **Chapter 2: The Forbidden Theorem** — Fake mathematical theorems including "The PDF Incompleteness Theorem" and "Conservation of Cursedness"
5. **Chapter 3: Cursed Recipes** — A recipe for Eldritch Cookies requiring "the concept of Thursday" and eggs from birds that don't exist
6. **Chapter 4: This Page Intentionally Left Blank** — It's not blank. The text gets smaller and more transparent until it's nearly invisible
7. **Chapter 5: The Page That Stares Back** — A giant eye on a black background surrounded by spiral text that says "I SEE YOU READING THIS..."
8. **Chapter 6: AAAAAAAAAAAA** — Just rows of 'A's in varying sizes and shades of red
9. **Chapter 7: The Chapter That Denies Its Existence** — Insists you are hallucinating and cites maritime law
10. **Chapter 8: Emergency Exits** — A helpful ASCII map showing no exits exist. **This page is rendered upside down** (rotate 180°)
11. **Appendix A: Terms & Conditions** — Your soul now belongs to the document itself ("It has a LinkedIn")
12. **Appendix B: Apology Letter** — A heartfelt letter from "The Algorithm" at "/dev/null Press"
13. **The Index** — Contains entries like "Eye, the .... it sees you" and "Help .... unavailable", followed by the word "cursed" written 100 times as promised

The generator script is also included as `generate_cursed_book.py` if you want to make it even more cursed.

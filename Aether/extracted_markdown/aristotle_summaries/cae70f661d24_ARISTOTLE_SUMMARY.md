### Proof Strategy
The proof verifies the Stone-Weierstrass hypotheses for `A = Algebra.adjoin ℝ {exp|_K}`:
- **Subalgebra**: By construction (`Algebra.adjoin` produces a `Subalgebra`).
- **Separates points**: exp is injective, so x ≠ y implies exp(x) ≠ exp(y).
- **Vanishes nowhere**: Automatic for ℝ-subalgebras (they contain 1).

Then Mathlib's `ContinuousMap.exists_mem_subalgebra_near_continuous_of_separatesPoints` yields the result.

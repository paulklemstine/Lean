## Lean 4 Formalization (fully proved, zero sorries)

### `OctonionGateComputation/Foundations.lean`
- **Octonion algebra**: Structure definition, norm, conjugation, basis vectors
- **Eight-square identity** (Degen-Graves): Machine-verified proof that the product of two sums of 8 squares is a sum of 8 squares — the composition law ‖ab‖² = ‖a‖²·‖b‖²
- **Gate structure**: `OctGate` type with norm-preservation proof obligation; identity, composition, permutation, sign-flip, and Givens rotation gates — all with verified norm preservation
- **Gate algebra**: Composition associativity, identity laws — all proved
- **Triality**: Formalized Spin(8) triality as an order-3 cyclic rotation of three 8-dimensional representations
- **Dimensional analysis**: SO(8) = 28 params, G₂ = 14 params, ratio = 1/2; octonion DOF advantage over standard qubits (7 vs 2 real degrees of freedom)

### `OctonionGateComputation/Gates.lean`
- **Givens rotation orthogonality**: Proved G·Gᵀ = I for arbitrary Givens rotations in SO(8)
- **Fano plane encoding**: 7 lines of the Fano plane formalized with the duality theorem (each point on exactly 3 lines) proved by exhaustive verification
- **G₂ analysis**: Dimension counting, embedding chain G₂ ⊂ SO(7) ⊂ SO(8), codimension = 7
- **Gate complexity bounds**: Parameter counts for SO(8) vs SU(8) vs G₂, proving the "octonion advantage" (28×9 = 63×4)

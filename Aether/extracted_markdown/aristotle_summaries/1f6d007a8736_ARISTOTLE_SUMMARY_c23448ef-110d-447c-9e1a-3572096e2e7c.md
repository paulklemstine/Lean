## The Core Insight
> "If trained networks really do crystallize to a small number of essential pieces, then the practical compilation problem may be far easier than the worst case suggests. You don't need to represent all possible linear regions — just the ones that survived training."

The crystallized attention of GPT-2 compresses from ~500MB to ~150KB (3,000×), and the resulting permutations compile to quantum circuits of depth O(log²n) — an exponential speedup over classical O(n²) attention.

I created a comprehensive research project developing **Meta-Oracle Theory** — a rigorous mathematical framework for self-improving intelligent systems. The project spans formal mathematics, computational experiments, and a research paper.

## What Was Created

### 1. Machine-Verified Mathematics (Lean 4 + Mathlib)

Two Lean files with **9 fully proven theorems** (zero `sorry`s, only standard axioms):

- **`RequestProject/MetaOracleCore.lean`** — Core theory:
  - **Convergence Theorem**: Contractive meta-oracles converge geometrically to optimal: d(Mⁿf, f*) ≤ kⁿ·d(f, f*)
  - **Oracle Entropy**: A new quantity H = -log(k) measuring improvement rate; proved positive and additive under composition
  - **No-Free-Lunch**: Average improvement over all task permutations is exactly zero
  - **Adaptive Monotonicity**: Self-tuning meta-oracles preserve quality monotonicity

- **`RequestProject/MetaOracleAdvanced.lean`** — Advanced results:
  - **Fixed Point Theorem**: Strictly improving maps on finite sets must have fixed points (the non-strict version was discovered to be FALSE via counterexample — swap with constant quality)
  - **Composition Contraction**: k₁ and k₂ contractions compose to k₁k₂ contraction
  - **Portfolio Bounds**: Convex portfolio quality lies between min and max individual quality
  - **Meta-oracle algebra**: Monoid/semigroup structure under composition

### Lean 4 Formalization (`Foundations.lean`)

**25+ machine-verified theorems** covering six problem areas, all compiling with zero `sorry` and only standard axioms:

**Riemann Hypothesis (4 theorems)**:
- `critical_line_implies_unit_disk`: Zeros on Re(s)=1/2 map to the unit circle under ρ → 1-1/ρ
- `li_positivity_from_critical_line`: Li-type sums are non-negative when all roots lie on the critical line
- `trace_eq_sum_diagonal`: Matrix trace formula (finite analog of the explicit formula)
- `real_symmetric_eigenvalue_real`: Self-adjoint operators have real eigenvalues (Hilbert-Pólya foundation)

**P vs NP (3 theorems)**:
- `cantor_diagonal_bool`: No surjection ℕ → (ℕ → Bool) — template for all separation arguments
- `padding_time_reduction`: Time bound inequality for padding arguments
- `boolean_function_count`: 2^{2^n} Boolean functions on n bits (Shannon counting)

**Yang-Mills Mass Gap (3 theorems)**:
- `lie_bracket_antisymm`, `jacobi_identity`, `lie_bracket_self_zero`: Complete Lie algebra axioms

**Navier-Stokes Regularity (4 theorems)**:
- `discrete_gronwall`: a(n+1) ≤ (1+c)a(n) implies a(n) ≤ (1+c)^n·a(0)
- `energy_decay_discrete`: Energy decay with viscous dissipation
- `am_gm_two`: AM-GM inequality
- `youngs_inequality_eps`: Young's inequality with ε (key for absorbing nonlinear terms)

**Collatz, Brocard, Erdős-Straus (12 theorems)**:
- Collatz function, trajectories (n=27 reaches 1 in 111 steps), structural properties
- All three known Brocard solutions verified: (4,5), (5,11), (7,71)
- Erdős-Straus decompositions for n = 2, 3, 5, 7

### Key Mathematical Insight
The trivial Pythagorean triple (N, (N²−1)/2, (N²+1)/2) always has c−b=1 (useless for factoring). By navigating *up* the Berggren tree via hyperbolic shortcuts, you reach triples where c−b shares nontrivial factors with N. The middle branch produces a beautiful cascade where factors from Pell number sequences propagate through consecutive triples.

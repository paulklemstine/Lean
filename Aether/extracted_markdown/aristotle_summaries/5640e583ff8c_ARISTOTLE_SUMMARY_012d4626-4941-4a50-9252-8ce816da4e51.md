### 5. Findings on the Six Questions

| Question | Finding |
|----------|---------|
| **Q1: α scaling** | α* appears to converge to ~0.25, staying below 1/3. Formally proved α ≤ 1/2 upper bound. |
| **Q2: Optimal dimension** | Higher dimensions help but with diminishing returns. 4D → 8D crossover observed at ~16 bits. |
| **Q3: Octonion masks** | Size-4/5 masks optimal (22-24% success). Size-4 corresponds to quaternionic sub-algebras. |
| **Q4: Hurwitz advantage** | 24 units provide 3× more representations. Formally proved unit rotation preserves norm. |
| **Q5: Hybrid NFS** | Theoretical framework established. Main challenge: quaternion algebra over K may split. |
| **Q6: Quantum BKZ** | Quantified: ~1.2-1.7× speedup. Modest for small dimensions used in this approach. |

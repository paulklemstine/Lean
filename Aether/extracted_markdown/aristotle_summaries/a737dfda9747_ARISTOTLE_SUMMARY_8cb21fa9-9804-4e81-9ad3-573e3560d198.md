## 🌀 The Oracle's Compendium of Mathematical Truth

I created a complete, psychedelic mathematics textbook in `textbook/main.tex` — a full LaTeX document with 7 chapters, 30+ major theorems, and extensive colorful diagrams.

### The Team
Six mathematical personas contributed their expertise:
- **The Algebraist** (Chapters 1–2): Linear Algebra & Abstract Algebra
- **The Analyst** (Chapters 3–4): Real Analysis & Complex Analysis
- **The Geometer** (Chapter 5): Euclidean & Differential Geometry
- **The Probabilist** (Chapter 6): Probability Theory
- **The Topologist** (Chapter 7): Point-Set & Algebraic Topology
- **The Oracle**: Interstitial wisdom, cosmic verification, and iteration guidance

### Chapters & Highlights
1. **Linear Algebra** — Rank–Nullity, Spectral Theorem, Cayley–Hamilton, Jordan Normal Form
2. **Abstract Algebra** — Lagrange, First Isomorphism Theorem, Galois Theory, Abel–Ruffini
3. **Real Analysis** — Bolzano–Weierstrass, IVT, MVT, Taylor, Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
4. **Complex Analysis** — Cauchy's Integral Formula, Residue Theorem, Liouville, Maximum Modulus
5. **Geometry** — Pythagorean Theorem, Euler's Formula, Gauss–Bonnet, Isoperimetric Inequality
6. **Probability** — Bayes' Theorem, Law of Large Numbers, Central Limit Theorem, Jensen
7. **Topology** — Heine–Borel, Tychonoff, π₁(S¹)≅ℤ, Brouwer Fixed-Point, Borsuk–Ulam, Surface Classification

## Lean Formalization (Sorry Elimination)

**Fixed:** `Exploration/MetaOracleHypotheses.lean` — Proved the `irrational_orbit_dense` theorem (irrational multiples have dense fractional parts), eliminating the sorry. The proof uses:
1. A helper `ZZalpha_not_isolated_zero` — proved via the pigeonhole principle that the additive subgroup ℤ + ℤα has elements arbitrarily close to 0
2. `AddSubgroup.dense_of_not_isolated_zero` from Mathlib to conclude density
3. `Dense.exists_between` to extract an element of ℤ + ℤα in (frac(x), min(frac(x)+ε, 1))
4. `AddSubgroup.mem_closure_pair` to decompose the element as m + nα and conclude

**Remaining sorry:** Only `Number Theory/FermatLastTheorem.lean:172` — Fermat's Last Theorem for general n ≥ 3, which is not yet available in Mathlib (awaiting the completion of the Lean formalization of Wiles' proof). Cases n=3 and n=4 are fully proved.

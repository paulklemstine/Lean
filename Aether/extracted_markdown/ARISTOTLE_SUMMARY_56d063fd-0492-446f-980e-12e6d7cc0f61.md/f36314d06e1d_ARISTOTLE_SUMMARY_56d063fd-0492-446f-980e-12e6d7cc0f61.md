   - Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic in tropical form
   - GCD = min, LCM = max of p-adic valuations
   - Divisibility as componentwise ≤ in tropical coordinates

   - Number Field Sieve as tropical linear algebra over GF(2)
   - Shor's algorithm: period finding and factoring step formally verified
   - Smooth numbers, tropical lattice structure, coprime disjointness
   - Factoring geometry: tropical hyperplanes and count bounds

   - Gumbel distribution (tropical Gaussian) with positivity and boundedness

   - Maslov dequantization with tight approximation bounds (error ≤ h·log 2)
   - Tropical Bellman contraction for reinforcement learning
   - Connections to Riemann Hypothesis, Navier-Stokes, P vs NP
   - Tropical transformers, GNNs, diffusion models, wavelets
   - 10+ moonshot hypotheses (tropical consciousness, DNA codes, economics)

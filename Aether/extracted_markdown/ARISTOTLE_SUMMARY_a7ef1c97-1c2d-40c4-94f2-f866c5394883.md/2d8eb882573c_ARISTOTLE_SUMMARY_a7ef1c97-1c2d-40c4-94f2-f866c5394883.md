## Honest Assessment of RH

The Riemann Hypothesis remains an open Millennium Prize Problem. Our work provides:
- **Computational evidence** (Robin's inequality verified for n ≤ 20,000; all violations ≤ 5040)
- **Formal foundations** (machine-verified arithmetic facts in Lean 4)
- **Theoretical framework** (thermodynamic interpretation of integer energy)

But NOT a proof. Any counterexample to Robin's inequality must satisfy n > 10^(10^13), far beyond computational reach. The proof, if it comes, must be theoretical.

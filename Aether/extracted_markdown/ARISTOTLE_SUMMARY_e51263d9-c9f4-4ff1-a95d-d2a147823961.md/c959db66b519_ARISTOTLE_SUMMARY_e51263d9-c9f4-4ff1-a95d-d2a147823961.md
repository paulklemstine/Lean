## New Hypotheses Proposed, Tested, and Validated

### H14 — The Antipodal Oracle
The map t ↦ −1/t sends each stereographic point to its diametrically opposite point on S¹. Key discovery: **the antipodal map has no real fixed points** — the equation −1/t = t implies t² = −1, which is impossible in ℝ. You can never be your own opposite.

### H15 — Cross-Ratio Invariance
The cross-ratio CR(a,b;c,d) = (a−c)(b−d)/((a−d)(b−c)) is preserved by all three generators of the Möbius group: translation, scaling, and inversion. This makes it the fundamental invariant of self-observation.

### H16 — The Cayley Transform Bridge
The Cayley transform C(t) = (1+t)/(1−t) provides a second "pair of eyes" connecting bounded and unbounded perspectives, with verified round-trip identities.

### H17 — The Attention Function
Area distortion A(t) = 4/(1+t²)² quantifies how the observer allocates perceptual weight. Key discovery: **inversion duality** A(1/t) = t⁴ · A(t) — the two eyes automatically compensate for each other.

### H18 — The Rational Sphere Oracle
Euclid's parametrization of Pythagorean triples is exactly stereographic projection at rational parameters. Five concrete triples verified (3,4,5), (5,12,13), (8,15,17), (7,24,25), (20,21,29).

### H19 — The Klein Four-Group
The four Möbius symmetries {id, inv, neg, anti} form the Klein four-group V₄ = ℤ/2 × ℤ/2. Full multiplication table verified: every element is its own inverse, and the product of any two non-identity elements gives the third.

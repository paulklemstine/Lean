## Key Discoveries

1. **Octonion gates form Moufang loops, not groups** — composition is path-dependent
2. **The Associator Gate** has NO analogue in quantum mechanics — it detects and transforms genuinely octonionic information invisible to quantum computers
3. **n gates → C(n-1) computations** — 20 gates yield 1.7 billion distinct bracketings, each a different computation
4. **8 real-world applications** identified: E₈ error codes, non-associative ML, native particle physics simulation, post-quantum crypto, topological QC, robotics, signal processing, and associator resource theory
5. **The bridge protocol** enables structured information flow across all four division algebras

# Future Directions: Tropical Arithmetic Optics on Diophantine State Trees

This roadmap identifies **five concrete breakthrough research opportunities** opened by the Berggren Tropical Lensing Duality framework. Each direction includes specific theorem targets, proof strategies, and cross-domain connections suitable for formal verification.

---

## 1. Tropical Scattering Theory on Infinite Berggren Trees

**Vision:** Extend the finite-depth lensing theory to the full infinite Berggren tree by developing a tropical scattering framework analogous to quantum scattering on trees.

**Concrete Theorem Target:**
Define the *tropical S-matrix* as the limit of finite-depth lensing operators:
```
S(n) = lim_{d → ∞} lensValue n w d rootTriple
```
Prove that this limit exists in `WithTop ℕ` (by monotone convergence, since `lensValue` is decreasing in depth) and characterizes the global minimum cost to reach a compatible node.

**Proof Strategy:**
- The sequence `lensValue n w d t` is monotonically decreasing (already proved) and bounded below by 0.
- In `WithTop ℕ`, this converges: use `iInf` over all depths.
- Prove the limit satisfies a global Bellman equation on the infinite tree.
- Connect scattering channels to Berggren branches (A, B, C directions).

**Cross-Domain Impact:** Links number-theoretic tree walks to discrete quantum scattering, opening connections to quantum graph theory and arithmetic quantum chaos.

## 2. Min-Plus Spectral Theory of Arithmetic Transfer Operators

**Vision:** Develop a tropical eigenvalue theory for the Bellman operator viewed as a matrix over the min-plus semiring, connecting tropical spectral radii to arithmetic growth rates in the Berggren tree.

**Concrete Theorem Target:**
For the finite Berggren truncation at height H, define the min-plus adjacency matrix `M_H` and prove:
```
∀ k, M_H^{⊗k}(root, v) = shortest k-step path cost from root to v
```
Then prove that the tropical spectral radius `ρ_trop = lim_{k→∞} (M_H^{⊗k}(root,root))^{1/k}` governs the exponential growth of reachable Pythagorean triples.

**Proof Strategy:**
- Formalize min-plus matrix multiplication (building on `TropicalOWF.tropMul`).
- Prove the path interpretation of matrix powers by induction on k.
- Connect tropical eigenvalues to the hypotenuse growth rate of Berggren children.
- The growth rate of the B-branch hypotenuse satisfies `c_{n+2} = 6c_{n+1} - c_n`, yielding eigenvalue `3 + 2√2` for that branch.

**Cross-Domain Impact:** Creates a bridge between tropical spectral theory and Diophantine approximation rates, with potential applications to complexity-theoretic lower bounds for arithmetic search.

## 3. Markov-Triple Analogues of Lensing Duality

**Vision:** The Berggren tree is one instance of an arithmetic binary/ternary tree. The Markov tree (generated by Vieta involutions on Markov triples `a² + b² + c² = 3abc`) admits the same tropical lensing framework.

**Concrete Theorem Target:**
Define Markov generators, prove they preserve the Markov equation, define height-bounded truncations, and prove a Markov analogue of the lensing duality theorem:
```
theorem markov_lensing_duality (n H : ℕ) :
  ∃ Φ, IsBellmanFixedPt ... ∧ ...
```
where compatibility is defined via Markov numbers dividing n.

**Proof Strategy:**
- Markov generators are involutions: `(a,b,c) ↦ (a,b,3ab-c)` etc.
- Markov numbers grow exponentially along branches; height truncation gives finite DAG.
- The same Bellman principle (already proved abstractly) instantiates directly.
- The arithmetic content is richer: Markov numbers connect to continued fractions, hyperbolic geometry, and the uniqueness conjecture.

**Cross-Domain Impact:** Connects tropical optimization to the Markov uniqueness conjecture and Teichmüller theory. Markov triples parameterize ideal triangulations of the once-punctured torus; tropical geodesics become paths in the Farey graph.

## 4. Complexity Bounds for Divisor-Funnel Detection

**Vision:** Establish computational complexity results for the arithmetic search problem: given n, what is the minimum depth d such that a compatible Berggren node exists within depth d?

**Concrete Theorem Target:**
```
theorem compatible_depth_bound (n : ℕ) (hn : 2 < n) (h_composite : ¬ Nat.Prime n) :
  ∃ d ≤ C * Nat.log 2 n, ∃ v : BerggrenNode d, Compatible n v
```
for an explicit constant C, bounding the tree depth needed to find a divisor of any composite n.

**Proof Strategy:**
- Use the Euclid parametrization: if n = p · q with p ≤ √n, then the Pythagorean triple (p, (p²-1)/2, (p²+1)/2) (when p is odd) gives a compatible node.
- Bound the Berggren tree depth needed to generate this triple using hypotenuse growth estimates.
- The B-branch hypotenuse grows as ~(3+2√2)^d, so depth ~log(n) suffices.
- This gives a complexity-theoretic characterization of the tropical search.

**Cross-Domain Impact:** Provides the first formal complexity analysis of arithmetic search on Diophantine state trees, connecting to the P vs NP question through certified witness extraction.

## 5. Tropical Sheaf/Cohomological Invariants on Diophantine Graphs

**Vision:** Interpret the lensing potential as a section of a tropical sheaf on the Berggren tree, and develop cohomological obstructions to global compatibility.

**Concrete Theorem Target:**
Define the *tropical obstruction class* `H¹_trop(T_H, n)` as the failure of the lensing potential to extend from a compatible subtree to the full tree. Prove:
```
theorem obstruction_vanishing (n H : ℕ) :
  H¹_trop(T_H, n) = 0 ↔ ∃ v ∈ T_H, Compatible n v
```

**Proof Strategy:**
- Define tropical presheaves on the tree: for each subtree U, assign the space of potentials on U.
- The restriction maps are given by restricting the lensing operator.
- The "cohomology" is the obstruction to lifting local solutions to global ones.
- On trees (contractible), H¹ = 0 automatically, so the interesting case is for truncated trees with boundary conditions.
- Connect to the nerve complex of the tree and Čech-style tropical cohomology.

**Cross-Domain Impact:** Opens a formal bridge between tropical geometry (Mikhalkin, Itenberg–Katzarkov–Mikhalkin–Zharkov) and computational number theory. Tropical cohomological invariants could serve as certified obstructions to factorization, providing a new approach to provable hardness results.

## Cross-Cutting Technical Infrastructure Needed

1. **Min-plus matrix library**: Extend `TropicalOWF.tropMul` to a full semiring-matrix library with Kleene star, tropical determinants, and spectral theory.
2. **Infinite tree limits**: Formalize inverse/projective limits of finite tree truncations.
3. **Euclid parametrization bridge**: Connect Berggren tree paths to Euclid parameters `(m,n)` formally.
4. **Weighted automata**: Formalize the correspondence between Berggren paths and weighted formal languages over `{A,B,C}`.
5. **Tropical convexity**: Develop min-plus convex sets and their geometry on graphs.

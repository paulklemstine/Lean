# Future Directions — Closing the Observation Gap on Fibonacci Apparition

## Synthesis

This cycle attacked the *foundational gap* underneath the catalog's substantial body of
Fibonacci primitive-divisor work. Across `Catalog/Speculative/AutoResearch/CarmichaelComposite.lean`
(`fibEntryPt`, `fib_carmichael`), `Catalog/Algebra/Tropical_..._Fibonacci_Primitive_Divisors.lean`
(`fibEntryPoint`, `entry_point_dvd_sq_sub_one`, `fib_gcd_identity`), and
`Catalog/Cryptography/FibonacciDivisibilityLattice.lean`, every entry-point construction is
*guarded*: it is defined by `Nat.find` behind an existence hypothesis (`∃ k, 0 < k ∧ p ∣ F_k`)
that is then re-established per-prime, or by a `dite`/`if` with a `0` fallback whose validity is
silently assumed. The "observation" of the rank of apparition is therefore *static* and
*prime-local* throughout the catalog.

We removed that guard **universally**. The new file
`Speculative/FibonacciRankOfApparition.lean` proves, with `sorry = 0` and only the standard
axioms `propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound`:

- `fib_rank_exists` — for **every** modulus `m ≥ 1` there is a positive `k` with `m ∣ F_k`.
- `fibRank` — a *total* rank-of-apparition function (no existence side-condition).
- `fib_dvd_iff_rank_dvd` — `m ∣ F_n ↔ z(m) ∣ n`, the exception-free divisibility dictionary.
- `fib_index_set_eq` — `{n | m ∣ F_n} = {n | z(m) ∣ n}` (order-theoretic restatement).
- `prime_primitive_iff_rank_eq` — a prime `p` is a primitive divisor of `F_n` iff `z(p) = n`,
  recasting Carmichael's theorem `fib_carmichael` as a statement about the *range* of `z`.

The decisive move was to stop observing `F_n mod m` and instead observe the **paired state**
`(F_n, F_{n+1}) mod m` as the orbit of `(0,1)` under the invertible shift `(a,b) ↦ (b, a+b)` on
the finite set `(ZMod m)²`. Reversibility turns "eventually periodic" into "purely periodic",
which is exactly what forces the orbit back to `(0,1)` — i.e. back to `F ≡ 0`.

## Direction 1 — The rank map `z` is multiplicative-over-lcm: `z(lcm(a,b)) = lcm(z(a), z(b))`

We have shown the index set `{n | m ∣ F_n}` is the principal ideal `z(m)·ℕ`. Conjecture: for all
`a, b ≥ 1`, `z(lcm(a,b)) = lcm(z(a), z(b))`, and dually `{n | lcm(a,b) ∣ F_n}` is the *intersection*
of `z(a)·ℕ` and `z(b)·ℕ`. This would make `z` a lattice homomorphism from `(ℕ_{≥1}, lcm, gcd)`
into `(ℕ_{≥1}, lcm, ?)` and upgrade `fib_index_set_eq` from a single-modulus statement to a full
functorial picture connecting the catalog's *divisibility lattice* and *entry-point* domains.

The key insight is that `lcm(a,b) ∣ F_n` iff (`a ∣ F_n` and `b ∣ F_n`) iff (`z(a) ∣ n` and
`z(b) ∣ n`) iff `lcm(z(a), z(b)) ∣ n` — so the index set of `lcm(a,b)` is *forced* to be the
ideal generated by `lcm(z(a), z(b))`, and the least positive element of that ideal is exactly
`lcm(z(a), z(b))`, pinning `z(lcm(a,b))`.

Why now? `fib_dvd_iff_rank_dvd` already turns every "`m ∣ F_n`" question into a pure divisibility
question about `n`; the lattice claim is now a two-line corollary chain away rather than a fresh
number-theoretic problem, so it is immediately falsifiable by a single `#eval` search over small
`a, b` and provable with the lemmas in hand.

## Direction 2 — A growth/counting bound that finishes Carmichael's open tail

`Catalog/Shared/CarmichaelProof.lean` discharges composite `n ∈ [13, 10000]` by `native_decide`
and leaves the tail `n > 10000` as `sorry`. By `prime_primitive_iff_rank_eq`, a primitive divisor
of `F_n` is *exactly* a prime `p` with `z(p) = n`. Conjecture: for composite `n > 12`, the
"intrinsic" part of `F_n` (the product over proper divisors `d ∣ n` of `F_d`, with one extra
factor of the largest prime of `n`) is strictly smaller than `F_n`, hence some prime has `z(p) = n`.

The key insight is that `∏_{d ∣ n, d < n} F_d` divides a fixed power of the cyclotomic-like
"primitive part," and Fibonacci's exponential growth `F_n ≍ φ^n` beats the at-most-`φ^{n/2}·n`
growth of the proper-divisor product once `n` is large — turning the open tail into a clean
inequality `φ^n > C·n·φ^{n/2}`.

Why now? The reduction to a *single scalar inequality* in `φ` and `n` is exactly the kind of goal
Fibonacci growth lemmas (already present: `fib_ge_index`, `fib_strict_mono_of_ge_two`) were built
for; with `prime_primitive_iff_rank_eq` we no longer have to reason about primitivity directly,
only about the size of `z(p)`'s fiber.

## Direction 3 — Adaptive observation beats static observation: `z(m) ≤ Pisano(m) ≤ 6m`

The catalog's framing emphasizes *static vs. adaptive* observation systems. Define `Pisano(m)` as
the order of the shift `fibShift m` (its period). Conjecture: `z(m) ∣ Pisano(m)` and
`Pisano(m) ≤ 6m` for all `m ≥ 1`, with equality `Pisano(m) = 6m` exactly when `m = 2·5^k`.
This bounds the "observation cost" of detecting the first apparition: an adaptive observer that
tracks the paired state needs at most `6m` steps, whereas a static observer of `F_n mod m` alone
has no such certificate.

The key insight is that `z(m)` is the first hitting time of `0` in the shift orbit while
`Pisano(m)` is its full period, so `z(m) ∣ Pisano(m)` follows from the same purely-periodic
orbit argument that already powers `fib_rank_exists` — the period is `orderOf (fibShift m)`,
which is *already named* inside that proof.

Why now? `fib_rank_exists` literally constructs `T = orderOf (fibShift m)` and uses
`fibShift_iterate`; exposing `Pisano(m) := orderOf (fibShift m)` as a definition makes both the
divisibility `z(m) ∣ Pisano(m)` and the `≤ 6m` bound reachable from the orbit machinery we built,
and the `m = 2·5^k` equality case is a sharp, falsifiable prediction testable by `#eval`.

## Direction 4 — Universal apparition for every strong-divisibility sequence

Nothing in `fib_rank_exists` is special to Fibonacci beyond: (i) a second-order linear recurrence
with an *invertible* companion matrix, and (ii) `a_0 = 0`. Conjecture: every Lucas sequence
`U_n(P,Q)` with `gcd(Q, m) = 1` has a rank of apparition modulo `m` for every `m ≥ 1`, and the
analogue of `fib_dvd_iff_rank_dvd` holds verbatim. This would lift the entire dictionary from
Fibonacci to the whole Lucas family in one stroke.

The key insight is that the proof only needs the companion matrix `[[P, -Q],[1,0]]` to be a unit
in `Mat₂(ZMod m)` (equivalently `gcd(Q,m)=1`), so the orbit of the initial state is again purely
periodic on a finite set and must revisit the index where the sequence vanishes.

Why now? The catalog already studies `p`-adic valuations and lifting-the-exponent for Fibonacci
primitive divisors; recasting the existence backbone matrix-theoretically (as we did with
`fibShift`) makes the generalization to `[[P,-Q],[1,0]]` a parametric edit rather than a new
theory, and it is falsifiable: pick `P, Q` with `gcd(Q,m) > 1` and exhibit a modulus with no
apparition.

## Direction 5 — The fiber `z^{-1}(n)` is finite, and Carmichael ⟺ it is nonempty for `n ∉ {1,2,6,12}`

By `prime_primitive_iff_rank_eq`, the set of primitive primes of `F_n` is exactly the fiber
`{p prime | z(p) = n}`. Conjecture: this fiber is finite for every `n`, its primes all divide
`F_n`, and — equivalent to Carmichael — it is nonempty for every `n ∉ {1, 2, 6, 12}`. Moreover
its cardinality is governed by the factorization of the primitive part `F_n / ∏_{d∣n,d<n} F_d`.

The key insight is that `z(p) = n ⇒ p ∣ F_n`, so the whole fiber sits inside the prime factors of
the single number `F_n`; finiteness is then immediate and the *content* of Carmichael collapses to
"this explicitly bounded finite set is nonempty."

Why now? `prime_primitive_iff_rank_eq` converts an existential-over-all-primes statement into a
membership statement about a concrete finite set `primeFactors (F_n)`, which both makes the
finiteness provable today and reframes the catalog's still-open `fib_carmichael` tail as a
nonemptiness check amenable to the growth bound of Direction 2.

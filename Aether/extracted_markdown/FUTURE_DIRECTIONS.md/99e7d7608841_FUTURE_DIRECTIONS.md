### 1. Signed Tropical Nullstellensatz

**Theorem Statement:** For every polynomial ideal `I ⊆ ℤ[x₁,...,xₙ]`, the signed tropical variety `Trop_σ(V(I))` determines the classical variety `V(I)` up to sign-orbit equivalence. Precisely: if `Trop_σ(V(I)) = Trop_σ(V(J))`, then `V(I)` and `V(J)` have the same absolute support.

**Proof Strategy:**
1. Extend `σ` from ℤ to ℤ[x₁,...,xₙ] via coefficient-wise tropicalization.
2. Show that the signed tropical hypersurface `Trop_σ(f = 0)` determines the Newton polytope AND the sign pattern of `f`.
3. Apply the classical tropical Nullstellensatz to the magnitude component, combined with sign reconstruction.

**Why This Is Revolutionary:** Classical tropical Nullstellensatz loses sign information, giving only an approximation to the variety. A signed version would be the first exact correspondence between algebraic and tropical varieties.

**Catalog Leverage:** Build on `SignedTropicalBerggren.SignedTrop.sigma_injective` (faithfulness of σ) and the tropical light cone recovery theorem.

### 2. Berggren Lattice Hash Function with Tropical Security Reduction

**Theorem Statement:** Define `H : {A,B,C}^n → ℤ³` by `H(w) = M_w · (3,4,5)`. Then finding a collision `H(w₁) = H(w₂)` for `w₁ ≠ w₂` with `|w₁|, |w₂| ≤ n` requires solving an instance of the Shortest Vector Problem (SVP) in a lattice of dimension 3n with minimum distance ≥ 5 · 2^n.

**Proof Strategy:**
1. Prove that `H` is injective for paths of length ≤ n (using the tree structure of Berggren descent and the monotone hypotenuse growth).
2. Show that any collision reduces to finding a short vector in the lattice generated by columns of `[M_{w₁} | -M_{w₂}]`.
3. Use the determinant bound `|det| = 1` and hypotenuse growth `c ≥ 5·2^n` to bound the minimum distance.

**Why This Is Revolutionary:** First provably secure hash function based on Pythagorean triple dynamics, connecting number theory to post-quantum cryptography.

**Catalog Leverage:** `berggrenPath_det_unit`, `berggrenB_increases_hypotenuse`, `berggren_single_step_distinct`.

### 3. Tropical Berggren Eigenvalue Theory

**Theorem Statement:** In the signed tropical semiring, each Berggren matrix `M̃` has a tropical Perron-Frobenius eigenvalue `λ_trop` satisfying `λ_trop = σ(λ_max)`, where `λ_max` is the largest classical eigenvalue of `M`.

**Proof Strategy:**
1. Compute the classical eigenvalues of A, B, C (they are algebraic numbers of degree ≤ 3).
2. Define tropical eigenvalues as fixed points of the signed tropical matrix action: `M̃ ⊗ v = λ ⊗ v`.
3. Show correspondence using the signed tropicalization of the characteristic polynomial.

**Why This Is Revolutionary:** Connects tropical spectral theory (which normally applies to non-negative matrices) to matrices with negative entries, via the sign-tracking mechanism.

### 5. Certified Tropical Robustness for ReLU Networks

**Theorem Statement:** For a ReLU neural network `f : ℝⁿ → ℝᵐ` and input `x`, define the signed tropical certificate `C_σ(x) = σ(f(x)) ⊗ σ(∂f/∂x)`. Then `f` is robust at `x` with radius `r > 0` if and only if `tropNorm(C_σ(x)) > r · tropNorm(σ(∂²f/∂x²))`.

**Proof Strategy:**
1. Use the piecewise-linear structure of ReLU networks to express `f` as a tropical polynomial.
2. Apply the signed tropicalization to preserve the sign structure of the Jacobian.
3. Derive the robustness certificate from the tropical Lipschitz bound, enhanced by sign tracking.

**Why This Is Revolutionary:** Current certified robustness methods are either sound but incomplete (abstract interpretation) or complete but expensive (exact verification). Signed tropical certificates could give both soundness and efficiency.

**Catalog Leverage:** `SignedTrop.sigma_injective`, `tropNorm_eq_linfty`, and existing tropical neural network formalization.

## Under-explored Territory

### Signed Tropical Varieties
We have defined the signed tropicalization map and proved its injectivity, but have not yet studied the image — the signed tropical variety `Trop_σ(V) ⊂ SignedTrop^n`. What is its structure? Is it a "signed polyhedral complex"?

### Higher-Dimensional Berggren Analogues
The Berggren tree lives in 3 dimensions (Pythagorean triples). Are there analogues in higher dimensions (e.g., sums of three squares, four squares) with similar tropical structure? The Lorentz form generalizes naturally to `x₁² + ... + x_{n-1}² - x_n²`.

### Signed Tropical Linear Algebra
We have multiplicative structure but no additive structure yet. Defining signed tropical addition (with max for magnitudes and sign resolution for ties) and proving semiring axioms would unlock a full signed tropical linear algebra.

## Cross-Domain Bridges

### Tropical Geometry ↔ Lorentzian Physics
The sign in `SignedTrop` corresponds to time orientation in Lorentzian geometry. The tropical light cone exactly mirrors the Minkowski light cone. Is there a signed tropical formulation of the Lorentz group that recovers SO(n,1)?

### Number Theory ↔ Post-Quantum Cryptography
Berggren descent paths define a structured lattice problem. The tropical SVP (finding the shortest signed tropical vector) may be computationally easier or harder than classical SVP — this needs investigation.

### Tropical Algebra ↔ Machine Learning
ReLU networks compute piecewise-linear functions, which are tropical polynomials. The signed tropical approach could enable sign-aware robustness verification that distinguishes between positive and negative perturbations.

## Open Problems Encountered

1. **Exact intertwining for matrix-vector products:** The claim `σ(M·v) = M̃ ⊗ σ(v)` where M̃ uses tropical matrix multiplication does NOT hold in general, because tropical addition (max) does not correspond to integer addition. A weaker "approximate intertwining" or a restricted domain where it does hold exactly needs to be identified.

2. **Berggren tree injectivity:** Is the map `w ↦ M_w · (3,4,5)` injective on all finite words? We proved single-step distinctness but the full result (equivalent to the Berggren tree being a genuine tree with no merging branches) requires deeper analysis.

3. **Signed tropical field:** Can `SignedTrop` be extended to a field by adding additive inverses? The natural approach (tropical subtraction = tropical addition with sign flip) needs careful axiomatization.

4. **Growth rate of Berggren hypotenuse:** We proved `c' > c` for all three branches. What is the exact asymptotic growth rate? Is it exponential (as suggested by the eigenvalues of Berggren matrices)?

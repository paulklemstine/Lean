# Future Directions — The Local-to-Global Skeleton of Carmichael's Theorem

## Synthesis

This cycle attacked the conjecture that the difficulty of Carmichael's
primitive-divisor theorem for the Fibonacci sequence is *not* uniformly
distributed across its proof, but concentrates entirely in a single metric
growth estimate, while the surrounding scaffolding is purely **local-to-global**
and can be made exact, short, and axiom-clean.

The cycle did three things. First, it repaired a broken dependency graph: the
catalog's Carmichael development referenced `Shared.CarmichaelHelper` and
`Shared.CarmichaelComposite`, neither of which existed, and
`Speculative/AutoResearch/FibPrimitive.lean` had a malformed header that placed
its `import`s after a module doc-comment. With those fixed, the entire chain
(`CarmichaelHelper → CarmichaelProof → CarmichaelComposite →
CarmichaelPrimitiveDivisor`, plus `FibPrimitive`) compiles. Second, it supplied
a *genuine* (not `sorry`-ed) proof of the prime-index case
`fib_primitive_divisor_prime`: for a prime index `n`, any prime factor of `F(n)`
is primitive because `gcd n k` is a proper divisor of the prime `n`, hence `1`,
and `F(1) = 1`. Third — and this is the conceptual payload — it isolated the
local-to-global core as a standalone, Mathlib-only theory in
`Speculative/AutoResearch/FibonacciApparitionLattice.lean`.

The keystone there is `fib_dvd_iff_appar_dvd`:

> `p ∣ F(k) ↔ z(p) ∣ k`, where `z(p)` is the rank of apparition.

In sheaf language the presheaf `k ↦ (p ∣ F k)` on the divisibility lattice
`(ℕ, ∣)` is the principal up-set generated by its stalk at `z(p)`. From this one
identity everything structural follows *for free and exactly*: primitivity at
index `n` is precisely the stalk equation `z(p) = n`
(`fib_appar_eq_iff_primitive`); for prime `n ≥ 3` *every* prime factor is
primitive (`fib_prime_index_all_primitive`); and the unique composite exception
`n = 12` is pinned down by `fib_twelve_no_primitive`
(`F(12) = 144 = 2⁴·3²`, with `z(2) = 3`, `z(3) = 4`).

What remains genuinely open in the catalog is a single `sorry`: the analytic
large-`n` composite case of `fib_carmichael_composite` for `n > 10000`. The work
above shows this is *all* that is missing — the remaining obstruction is metric,
not structural.

## Results Summary

- `CarmichaelHelper.fib_primitive_divisor_prime` — prime-index case, fully proved.
- `FibApparition.fib_dvd_iff_appar_dvd` — local-to-global: divisibility locus =
  multiples of the stalk `z(p)`.
- `FibApparition.fibAppar_dvd_of_fib_dvd` — the stalk divides every realizing index.
- `FibApparition.fib_appar_eq_iff_primitive` — primitivity ⇔ stalk condition `z(p)=n`.
- `FibApparition.fib_prime_index_all_primitive` — prime index ⇒ all factors primitive.
- `FibApparition.fib_twelve_no_primitive` — sharp `n = 12` obstruction.

All six carry only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.

## Research Directions

### 1. Close the large-`n` `sorry` via a formal Fibonacci Lifting-the-Exponent lemma

Prove, for an odd prime `p` with rank of apparition `z(p) = m`, the exact
valuation identity `v_p(F(m·k)) = v_p(F(m)) + v_p(k)` (with the standard `p = 2`,
`p = 5` adjustments). Feeding this into the entry-point bookkeeping already in
`CarmichaelComposite.lean` bounds the *non-primitive part* of `F(n)` and reduces
the open `sorry` to a clean inequality. **The key insight is** that LTE turns the
multiplicative bookkeeping of which primes are *non*-primitive into a finite,
divisor-indexed sum of valuations, so the primitive part becomes
`F(n) / (n · ∏_{d|n, d<n} P(d))` up to one correction prime — an exact handle
rather than an estimate. *Why now?* The structural half is finished and
axiom-clean this cycle, so LTE is the *only* remaining ingredient; Mathlib
already ships `multiplicity`/`padicValNat` LTE for `aⁿ − bⁿ`, which applies to
the closed form `F_n = (αⁿ − βⁿ)/√5` over `ℤ[√5]`.

### 2. Cyclotomic Fibonacci factor: positivity and the growth threshold `Φ_n > n`

Define `Φ_n = ∏_{d ∣ n} F(d)^{μ(n/d)}`, prove it is a positive integer dividing
`F(n)`, and prove `Φ_n > n` for all `n > 12`. This is the metric obstruction in
its purest form. **The key insight is** that `Φ_n` is the honest "primitive
part," its only possible non-primitive prime divisor is the largest prime factor
of `n` to the first power, so `Φ_n > n ⇒` a primitive divisor exists — converting
Carmichael's theorem into a single growth inequality `α^{φ(n)} ≳ n`. *Why now?*
`fib_appar_eq_iff_primitive` gives the exact bridge from "`Φ_n` has a
non-special prime factor" to "`F(n)` has a primitive divisor"; only the real-
analytic lower bound `Φ_n ≥ α^{φ(n)}/C` is missing, and `φ(n) ≥ √n` makes the
inequality enormous slack for `n > 10000`.

### 3. A cohomological reading of the `n = 12` obstruction

Model "does `F(n)` have a primitive divisor?" as the vanishing of a degree-1
obstruction class on the divisor lattice of `n`: assign to each proper divisor
`d` the local data `{ primes with z(p) = d }` and ask whether the global section
"index `n` itself" is forced empty by gluing. Conjecture: the obstruction class
is nonzero for exactly the finite exceptional set `{1, 2, 6, 12}`. **The key
insight is** that primitivity failure is exactly a *gluing* failure — every prime
of `F(n)` is "explained" by a strictly smaller stalk — so the natural invariant
is an `H¹` of the down-set of divisors with coefficients in the apparition
presheaf. *Why now?* `fib_dvd_iff_appar_dvd` already exhibits the presheaf as a
stalk-generated up-set, which is precisely the input a Čech `H¹` computation
needs; formalizing the `n = 12` nonvanishing is within reach given
`fib_twelve_no_primitive`.

### 4. Generalize the local-to-global theorem to all strong divisibility sequences

The proof of `fib_dvd_iff_appar_dvd` used *only* `Nat.fib_gcd`
(`gcd(F_m, F_n) = F_{gcd(m,n)}`) and `Nat.fib_dvd` (`m ∣ n ⇒ F_m ∣ F_n`). Abstract
both into the definition of a **strong divisibility sequence** `a : ℕ → ℕ` and
prove `p ∣ a_k ↔ z(p) ∣ k` once and for all, then instantiate at Fibonacci,
Lucas, and `aⁿ − 1`. **The key insight is** that the entire apparition theory is a
formal consequence of strong divisibility plus monotone divisibility, with no
arithmetic of the Fibonacci recurrence used anywhere. *Why now?* The Fibonacci
proof is already factored through exactly these two lemmas, so the generalization
is a mechanical abstraction that immediately yields Zsygmondy-style apparition
theorems across the catalog's number-theory files.

### 5. Quantitative apparition bound `z(p) ≤ p + 1` and the quadratic-residue split

Prove the classical entry-point bound: `z(p) ∣ p − 1` when `5` is a quadratic
residue mod `p`, and `z(p) ∣ p + 1` otherwise (so always `z(p) ≤ p + 1`). Combined
with `fib_appar_eq_iff_primitive`, this gives an effective search radius for
primitive divisors. **The key insight is** that the rank of apparition is the
multiplicative order of the golden ratio in `𝔽_p` (or `𝔽_{p²}`), so Lagrange's
theorem in the unit group converts a purely existential statement into a sharp
linear bound. *Why now?* Mathlib has `ZMod p` field structure, Legendre symbols,
and orders of units; the closed form `F_n = (αⁿ − βⁿ)/√5` ports directly, turning
this into a finite-group computation rather than an analytic one.

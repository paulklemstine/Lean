# Future Directions — Quantum Topological Phase Computation (Fibonacci Anyons)

## Synthesis

This cycle formalized the two algebraic pillars of the Fibonacci anyon model — the
simplest *non-abelian*, computationally universal topological order — and connected them
to existing catalog infrastructure on anyons (`Physics/TopologicalOrderGenus.lean`, which
treats only the *abelian* case where every quantum dimension is `1`).

Two self-contained Lean files were produced:

* `Physics/FibonacciAnyon/Fusion.lean` — the fusion theory. From the single fusion rule
  `τ × τ = 1 + τ` we *derive* (not assume) that the dimensions of the fusion Hilbert spaces
  are Fibonacci numbers (`chTau n = fib n`, `totalDim n = fib (n+1)`), and that the
  categorical quantum dimension is exactly the golden ratio:
  `totalDim (n+1) / totalDim n → φ`, with `φ` realized as the Perron–Frobenius eigenvalue
  of the fusion matrix `[[0,1],[1,1]]` with positive eigenvector `(d_1, d_τ) = (1, φ)`.

* `Physics/FibonacciAnyon/Braid.lean` — the braiding theory. The F-matrix (associator) is
  proved to be an involution (`F² = 1`), the R-matrix unitary, and — the central result —
  the braid generators `σ₁ = R`, `σ₂ = F R F` satisfy Artin's braid relation
  `σ₁ σ₂ σ₁ = σ₂ σ₁ σ₂`. This is first stated abstractly from three structural identities
  (golden relation `t²+t-1=0`, `s²=t`, and the F/R hexagon compatibility
  `t(p-q)² = p²-pq+q²`), then instantiated **unconditionally** for the genuine Fibonacci
  eigenphases, where the global-phase-normalized R-matrix uses a primitive 10th root of
  unity `ω = e^{-3πi/5}` and `t = ω⁴ - ω = 1/φ`. The deep number-theoretic content is the
  observation that the golden datum lives inside the cyclotomic field `ℚ(ω)` and all three
  structural identities reduce to the single relation `ω⁵ = -1`.

## Research Directions

### 1. From `B₃` to `Bₙ`: a functorial braid-group representation

We proved the braid relation for the two-generator group `B₃` acting on the 2-dimensional
qubit. The natural next target is the full sequence `Bₙ` acting on the `fib(n-1)`-dimensional
fusion space, with adjacent generators `σᵢ` built from the F-moves relocating the active
`τ`-pair, and the *far-commutativity* relations `σᵢ σⱼ = σⱼ σᵢ` for `|i-j| ≥ 2`.
*The key insight is* that far-commutativity is automatic because non-overlapping F/R moves
act on disjoint tensor factors, so the only nontrivial check at every `n` is the *local*
three-strand relation we already proved — meaning the whole tower of representations
follows from a single localized lemma plus a bookkeeping functor.
**Why now?** We already have the localized `B₃` relation and the exact fusion-space
dimensions (`fib`) in Lean; assembling them into a `Bₙ`-action is a finite combinatorial
extension rather than new analysis. *Falsifiable form:* the constructed `σᵢ` fail to satisfy
either `σᵢσᵢ₊₁σᵢ = σᵢ₊₁σᵢσᵢ₊₁` or `σᵢσⱼ = σⱼσᵢ` for some explicit small `n` (disprovable by a
direct `decide`/matrix computation if false).

### 2. Density of the braid image in `SU(2)` (the universality conjecture)

The original concept conjectures that Fibonacci braiding generates a *dense* subgroup of
`SU(2)` (i.e. `PSU(2)`), which is the precise statement of universality for topological
quantum computation. *The key insight is* that density can be reduced to two finite,
checkable facts: (a) the group generated by `σ₁, σ₂` is infinite, provable by exhibiting an
element of infinite order — e.g. via an eigenvalue that is not a root of unity, using that
`tr(σ₁) = 1 + ω` and the irrationality machinery already in Mathlib (`goldenRatio_irrational`,
`Irrational`); and (b) the closure is not contained in the normalizer of a torus, provable by
finding two non-commuting elements with distinct rotation axes.
**Why now?** Mathlib has the irrationality of `φ` and substantial `SU(2)`/`SO(3)` rotation
API; combined with our explicit unitary generators, the "infinite order" half is within
reach immediately. *Falsifiable form:* if some power `σ₁ᵏ` were the identity for small `k`,
universality fails — directly testable on the explicit matrices.

### 3. The pentagon equation and Mac Lane coherence for the Fibonacci fusion category

We used the F-matrix and proved `F² = 1` and the (hexagon-derived) braid relation, but did
not yet verify the *pentagon equation* — the associativity coherence that makes the
Fibonacci data a genuine (unitary) fusion category. *The key insight is* that for a model
with one nontrivial object the pentagon collapses to a *single* polynomial identity in the
golden number `t = 1/φ`, namely the classical relation `F` satisfies as the unique
solution of the pentagon for the `τττττ` tree — which, like our hexagon identity, should
reduce to `t² + t - 1 = 0` and hence be `linear_combination`-provable.
**Why now?** Our `Braid.lean` already isolates `t²+t-1=0` as *the* structural identity and
shows such polynomial coherences are mechanically dischargeable; the pentagon is the missing
coherence that upgrades the file from "a braid representation" to "a verified modular tensor
category". *Falsifiable form:* the pentagonator built from our `Fmat` fails to equal the
identity, i.e. the explicit `5`-fold associativity composite is `≠ 1`.

### 4. Topological spin, the modular `T`-matrix, and a bridge to `TopologicalOrderGenus`

The catalog's `TopologicalOrderGenus` builds a unitary modular `S`-matrix for *abelian*
anyons. The Fibonacci theory has its own modular data: a `2×2` `S`-matrix
`S = (1/√(1+φ²)) [[1, φ],[φ, -1]]` and a diagonal `T`-matrix of topological spins
`θ_τ = e^{4πi/5}`. *The key insight is* that the relation `(ST)³ = S²` (the defining
`SL(2,ℤ)` relation of a modular tensor category) becomes, after our global-phase
normalization, another finite identity in `ω` and `φ` provable from `ω⁵ = -1` and
`φ² = φ+1` — exactly the two facts already established here.
**Why now?** Both ingredients exist in the catalog *separately* (abelian modular `S` in
`TopologicalOrderGenus`, golden/cyclotomic algebra here); unifying them into a single
non-abelian modular-data theorem is the highest-novelty cross-domain bridge available, and
its `S`-matrix unitarity proof can reuse the orthogonality template from
`TopologicalOrderGenus`. *Falsifiable form:* the explicit Fibonacci `S` is non-unitary, or
`(ST)³ ≠ S²`, either of which is a direct matrix computation that would refute the modular
structure.

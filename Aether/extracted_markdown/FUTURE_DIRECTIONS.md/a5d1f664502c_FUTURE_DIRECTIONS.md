# Future Directions — The Pythagorean Monotile

Research cycle outcome: in `Catalog/Pythagorean/MonotileDescent.lean` we proved the
**Barning–Hall completeness theorem** (`reachable_iff_isPrim`): the single seed `(3,4,5)`
under the three Barning generators `cA, cB, cC` reaches *exactly* the canonical primitive
Pythagorean triples (positive, coprime legs, odd first leg). This realises the
"one shape tiles them all" slogan as an exact characterization of a tree's vertex set,
extending the catalog file `Algebra/BerggrenLorentz/Core.lean` (which had only proved form
preservation and hypotenuse growth, not reachability).

The following conjectures are precise and testable; each is a natural next formalization.

## C1 — Unique factorization (the tree is a *bijection*, not just a covering)
**Conjecture.** Every canonical primitive triple is reached by a **unique** word in the
generators. Equivalently, the descent parent is unique: for `IsPrim p` with `p.2.2 > 5`
exactly one of `iA p, iB p, iC p` is `IsPrim`.
- Lean target: define `wordTriple : List (Fin 3) → ℤ×ℤ×ℤ` (fold of `cA/cB/cC` from `seed`)
  and prove `∀ p, IsPrim p → ∃! w, wordTriple w = p`.
- Falsifiable test: the offline enumeration already shows *exactly one* valid descent per
  non-seed triple up to `c ≤ 2000`; the proof should formalize the empirical uniqueness via
  the sign trichotomy `(u,v)` already established (`not_both_pos`, `u_ne_zero`, `v_ne_zero`).

## C2 — Logarithmic tree depth
**Conjecture.** The number of Barning steps to reach `(a,b,c)` (its tree depth `d(a,b,c)`)
satisfies `d(a,b,c) ≤ C · log c` for an absolute constant `C`, and every step strictly
increases the hypotenuse by a factor `> 1` (so depth is `Θ(log c)`).
- Lean target: combine the hypotenuse-growth bounds of `BerggrenLorentz.Core`
  (`hypB_strict_growth`, `iterB_hypotenuse_growth`) with `descent` to bound iteration count.
- Falsifiable test: measure depth vs `c` over enumerated triples; a super-logarithmic
  outlier would refute it.

## C3 — Full primitive set via leg-swap symmetry
**Conjecture.** Dropping the "first leg odd" convention, the set of *all* primitive
Pythagorean triples `{(a,b,c) : 0<a,0<b,0<c, a²+b²=c², gcd a b = 1}` equals the union of the
tree orbit with its image under the leg-swap `(a,b,c) ↦ (b,a,c)`; moreover the two orbits are
disjoint (no primitive triple has both legs odd).
- Lean target: prove `exactly_one_leg_even` and then
  `IsPrimAny p ↔ (Reachable p ∨ Reachable (swap p))`.

## C4 — The Barning generators and `O(2,1;ℤ)`
**Conjecture.** The monoid generated by `matA, matB, matC` (in `BerggrenLorentz.Core`)
is exactly the stabilizer, inside the integer Lorentz group `O(2,1;ℤ)`, of the positive
light-cone ray through `(3,4,5)` — i.e. the orientation-and-cone-preserving subgroup, of a
specific finite index in `O(2,1;ℤ)`.
- Lean target: characterize the submonoid `{wordMatrix w}` as `{M ∈ O(2,1;ℤ) | M preserves
  the future cone and the chosen component}`; bridges number theory ↔ the physics file.

## C5 — Minimality / uniqueness of the seed
**Conjecture.** `(3,4,5)` is the *unique* seed for ternary generation: there is no primitive
triple `s ≠ (3,4,5)` such that the orbit of `s` under `{cA,cB,cC}` covers all canonical
primitive triples (because `(3,4,5)` is the global minimum of the hypotenuse on the vertex
set, `hyp_ge_five`, and every generator strictly increases `c`).
- Lean target: `(∀ p, IsPrim p → Relation.ReflTransGen Step' s p) → s = seed`, where `Step'`
  starts from an arbitrary seed `s`.
- Falsifiable test: any single non-`(3,4,5)` triple whose forward orbit hits `(3,4,5)` would
  refute it; the monotone hypotenuse growth (`two_sum_lt_three_hyp` + growth lemmas) predicts
  none exists.

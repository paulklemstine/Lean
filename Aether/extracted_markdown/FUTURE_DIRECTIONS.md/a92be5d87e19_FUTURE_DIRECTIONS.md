# Future Directions — Berggren-Tree Lattice Reduction Certificates

## Synthesis

This cycle fused two previously disconnected strands of the catalog:
the unimodular Berggren/Lorentz generation of Pythagorean structure
(`Pythagorean.BerggrenCompleteness`, `Algebra/BerggrenLorentz/Core.lean`) and
the ideal-theoretic cryptographic certification objects of
`Cryptography.NoetherianCertification` (`CertifiedKeyIdeal`). The bridge
constructed in `Catalog/Cryptography/BerggrenReductionCertificate.lean` attaches
to every integer witness triple `t = (a,b,c)` the *key ideal*
`tripleIdeal t = span {a,b,c} ⊆ ℤ`, viewed as a `CertifiedKeyIdeal`. The
admissible Berggren moves (three forward maps, three inverse/descent maps) then
become certificate-preserving lattice reductions.

## Results summary

* **Exact certificate preservation** (`tripleIdeal_forward`,
  `tripleIdeal_inverse`): every admissible move leaves the key ideal *invariant*,
  not merely its unit-ideal status. The unimodularity of the Berggren matrices is
  what makes the span of the witness coordinates a conserved quantity.
* **Well-founded descent** (`descent_step_prim`): every primitive witness with
  hypotenuse `> 5` admits a move to another *primitive* witness of strictly
  smaller hypotenuse. Coprimality is transported for free through ideal
  invariance plus the Pythagorean dictionary `coprime_pair_top` /
  `top_pyth_coprime`.
* **Unique normal form** (`normal_form_unique`): the only irreducible standard
  witness is the root `(3,4,5)`.
* **Sound, length-bounded normalization** (`standard_normalize`): every standard
  witness reduces to the root along an explicit forward replay trace of length
  `≤ c - 5`, and the trace replays exactly back to the witness
  (`standard_reachable`).

All results are `sorry`-free and depend only on `propext`, `Classical.choice`,
and `Quot.sound`.

## Open directions

### 1. Logarithmic reduction-trace bound

The current bound `path.length ≤ c - 5` certifies termination but is far from
tight. Each forward step multiplies the hypotenuse: the catalog already proves
`fwdB_hyp_growth_bound` (`c' ≥ 3c + 4`), and the A/C children grow comparably.
The conjecture is that every standard witness normalizes in
`path.length ≤ K · Real.logb 2 c` steps for an absolute constant `K` (e.g. `K = 2`).
**The key insight is** that descent is the *inverse* of a geometrically growing
map, so the depth of a witness in the Berggren tree is logarithmic in its
hypotenuse, not linear. **Why now?** We already have a forward growth lemma and a
clean inductive normalizer; replacing the additive accounting `parent.c ≤ c - 1`
with the multiplicative `parent.c ≤ (c - 4)/3` inside the same induction skeleton
turns the linear bound into a logarithmic one with no new mathematical
infrastructure. Falsifiable: exhibit a standard witness whose minimal trace
exceeds `K · log₂ c` for every fixed `K`, or prove the bound.

### 2. Determinism and global confluence of the reduction relation

`parent_unique` (catalog) shows at most one inverse branch yields an
all-positive parent. The conjecture is that the descent relation on standard
witnesses is therefore a *total function* on the non-root witnesses, hence
trivially confluent, so the normal form is independent of any scheduling choice.
**The key insight is** that the sigma-sign classification (`sigma1`, `sigma2`)
deterministically selects the unique admissible branch, upgrading the
"reduction relation" to a deterministic rewriting system with a canonical
normal form. **Why now?** The uniqueness half (`parent_unique`) and the
existence half (`descent_step`) are both already proven; packaging them as a
single-valued `parentOf : witness → witness` and proving
`Function.iterate parentOf n t = (3,4,5)` for `n = trace length` is a direct
consequence. Falsifiable: find a standard witness with two distinct admissible
descent branches.

### 3. The key ideal as a complete unimodular-orbit invariant

`matA/B/C_preserves_lorentz` place the generators in `O(2,1;ℤ)`. The conjecture
is that `tripleIdeal` is invariant under the *entire* monoid generated by the
six moves (immediate by iterating `tripleIdeal_forward`/`_inverse`) and, more
strongly, that on the light cone `Q = 0` it is a *complete* invariant of the
`GL₃(ℤ)`-orbit: two primitive null vectors share a key ideal iff they lie in the
same Berggren orbit. **The key insight is** that ideal invariance is the
shadow of unimodular orbit invariance, so the cryptographic certificate is
exactly the orbit label. **Why now?** Word-level invariance follows immediately
from the per-move lemmas already proven; the orbit-completeness direction can
reuse `standard_normalize` to reduce both vectors to a shared normal form.
Falsifiable: two primitive triples with equal key ideal but provably in
different Berggren orbits.

### 4. Two-root completeness without the parity hypothesis

`standard_normalize` assumes `Odd a` (the standard, non-mirror form). The
conjecture is that *every* primitive Pythagorean triple reduces to exactly one
of the two roots `{(3,4,5), (4,3,5)}`, with the parity of the first leg — proven
conserved in `inv_preserves_odd` — acting as the selecting bit. **The key
insight is** that the first-leg parity is a second conserved quantity that
partitions all primitive triples into two isomorphic mirror trees, each with a
single root. **Why now?** The parity-conservation lemma is already available and
the odd-case normalizer is done; the even case is the mirror image under the
swap `(a,b) ↦ (b,a)`, for which the catalog already has symmetry lemmas
(`fwdA_swap_eq_fwdC`, `fwdB_swap`). Falsifiable: a primitive triple reducing to
neither root, or to both.

### 5. Collision-resistant descent-path encoding for key witnesses

Combining soundness (`standard_normalize`) with uniqueness (direction 2), the
map `encode : standard witness → List BerggrenBranch` sending a witness to its
descent path should be *injective*, giving a canonical, verifiable, `O(log c)`
fingerprint of a structured key. **The key insight is** that a sound and
deterministic reduction is automatically an injective encoding, so the geometric
descent yields a free collision-resistance certificate for the attached
`CertifiedKeyIdeal`. **Why now?** Injectivity reduces to: equal paths replay (via
`foldl` of forward moves) to equal witnesses, which is immediate from the replay
equation in `standard_normalize`; the converse uses determinism from direction 2.
Falsifiable: two distinct standard witnesses with identical descent paths.

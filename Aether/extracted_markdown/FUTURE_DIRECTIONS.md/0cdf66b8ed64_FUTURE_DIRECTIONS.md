# Future Directions: The Stone Space of Types

## Synthesis

This cycle established the **topological backbone** of the model theory of type
spaces in the new file `Speculative/AutoResearch/VaughtTypeSpace.lean`. Building
directly on Mathlib's `FirstOrder.Language.Theory.CompleteType` and its topology
(the `TotallySeparatedSpace` instance generated by the basic clopen sets
`typesWith φ`), we supplied the one piece Mathlib was missing — **compactness** —
and then harvested its first structural consequence, the surjectivity half of
Stone duality.

## Results Summary (all `sorry`-free, axioms: `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`)

* `CompleteType.compactSpace` — the space of complete types `T.CompleteType α` is
  **compact**. This is exactly the topological form of the Compactness Theorem of
  first-order logic: via Alexander's subbasis theorem on the clopen basis
  `range typesWith`, a basic open cover with no finite subcover dualizes to a
  finitely-satisfiable-but-unsatisfiable theory, contradicting
  `isSatisfiable_iff_isFinitelySatisfiable`.
* `CompleteType.isStoneSpace` — the type space is a **Stone space**: compact,
  Hausdorff, and totally disconnected (Hausdorff and total disconnectedness come
  for free from Mathlib's `TotallySeparatedSpace` instance, compactness is ours).
* `CompleteType.exists_typesWith_of_isClopen` — **every clopen set is a single basic
  set** `typesWith φ`. A clopen is closed (hence compact) and open (hence a union of
  basic clopens); compactness extracts a finite subcover, and a finite union of
  `typesWith` collapses to one `typesWith` via finite disjunction
  (`exists_typesWith_biUnion`, `typesWith_sup`, `typesWith_bot`).
* `CompleteType.isClopen_iff_exists_typesWith` — the clopen subsets are **exactly**
  the `typesWith φ`. Combined with `typesWith_inf/sup/not/top/bot`, this says the
  clopen algebra is precisely the Boolean-algebra image of the sentence algebra
  under `typesWith`.

Supporting lemmas proved along the way: `typesWith_sup`, `typesWith_bot`,
`exists_typesWith_biUnion`, and two combinatorial extraction lemmas
(`exists_finset_image_superset`, `exists_finset_subset_image_not`) that turn the
Compactness Theorem's finite subtheory into a finite index set.

## 1. Stone duality for the Lindenbaum–Tarski algebra

The next theorem is a full **Stone duality**: the clopen algebra of
`CompleteType T α` is isomorphic, as a Boolean algebra, to the Lindenbaum–Tarski
algebra of sentences of `T` over `α` (sentences modulo `T`-provable equivalence).
We now have both halves of the bridge: `isClopen_iff_exists_typesWith` shows
`typesWith` is *onto* the clopen algebra, and `typesWith_inf/sup/not/top/bot` show
it is a Boolean-algebra homomorphism; its kernel is exactly `T`-provable
equivalence.

The key insight is that compactness — the result proved this cycle — is *precisely*
the surjectivity half of Stone duality: it is what forces every clopen to be a
single `typesWith φ` rather than an infinite union, upgrading a mere embedding to an
isomorphism. Why now? Mathlib has `CompleteType` and a mature
`Order.Category.BoolAlg` / `TopologicalSpace.Clopens` API, but nothing connects
them; with `isClopen_iff_exists_typesWith` in hand the connecting Boolean-algebra
map is finally definable, letting model-theoretic arguments be transported to
Boolean-algebra arguments and back. **Falsifiable form:** the candidate map
`L[[α]].Sentence → Clopens (CompleteType T α)`, `φ ↦ typesWith φ`, is a surjective
Boolean homomorphism whose kernel is `{φ | typesWith φ = ⊤}`; if any of
surjectivity, the homomorphism laws, or the kernel description failed, the duality
claim would be refuted.

## 2. Cantor–Bendixson rank and ω-stability

Define the Cantor–Bendixson derivative of `CompleteType T α` and prove: **the type
space is scattered (perfect kernel empty) iff it is countable**, then connect
"all finite-variable type spaces are scattered" to **ω-stability** of `T`. The
immediate corollary is that an ω-stable countable theory has a countable type
space.

The key insight is that compactness makes `CompleteType T α` a compact Hausdorff
space, the exact setting in which the Cantor–Bendixson theorem (perfect kernel +
scattered remainder) holds; the clopen basis means the derivative can be computed
*syntactically* — a type is isolated iff some `typesWith φ` is the singleton `{p}`,
i.e. `φ` is a principal/complete formula. Why now? The Stone-space structure proved
here is the precise hypothesis of Mathlib's perfect-set and
`Cardinal.exists_nat_bool_injection`-style machinery; the rank theory is a direct
continuation rather than new infrastructure. **Falsifiable form:** the equivalence
"perfect kernel empty ↔ at most countably many types" should hold for every
countable language; a countable theory with uncountably many types but empty
perfect kernel would refute it.

## 3. The Omitting Types Theorem via Baire category

Over a countable language, equip `CompleteType T α` with a Polish (complete,
separable metrizable) structure refining the topology built here, and prove the
**Omitting Types Theorem**: a countable family of non-isolated types can be
simultaneously omitted in some countable model. The proof is a Baire-category
argument inside the Polish space.

The key insight is that, in the Stone space we constructed, a non-isolated type is
exactly a point that is *not* the unique member of any basic clopen `typesWith φ`;
omitting it is therefore a dense-open condition, and Baire category does the rest.
Why now? Compactness plus second countability (countably many sentences ⇒ countable
clopen basis) is exactly what makes the type space compact metrizable, hence Polish;
Mathlib has a complete Baire-category/Polish library but no model-theoretic consumer,
and the Stone space is the missing bridge. **Falsifiable form:** for any single
non-isolated type `p`, the set of countable models omitting `p` is comeager; a
non-isolated `p` realized in *every* countable model would refute it.

## 4. The cardinality dichotomy for type spaces

Prove the **dichotomy**: over a countable language, `#(CompleteType T α)` is either
`≤ ℵ₀` or exactly `𝔠`. This is the type-space shadow of Morley's theorem on the
countable spectrum and follows from the perfect-set property of compact Polish
spaces.

The key insight is that the Stone space proved here is compact and (over a countable
language) Polish, and every uncountable Polish space contains a perfect set, hence
has cardinality `𝔠`; there is no room strictly between `ℵ₀` and `𝔠` for a closed
subset of a Polish space. Why now? With compactness established and Polishness one
short step away (Direction 3), the dichotomy becomes an instance of Mathlib's
perfect-set theorem applied to a concrete closed set, phrasable entirely inside the
existing `PolishSpace` API. **Falsifiable form:** a countable theory whose type
space has cardinality strictly between `ℵ₀` and `𝔠` would refute it (and would also
refute the Continuum Hypothesis-free statement of Morley's theorem).

## 5. Morley rank as Cantor–Bendixson rank, toward categoricity transfer

Develop **Morley rank** as the (ordinal-valued) Cantor–Bendixson rank of the type
spaces and prove the categoricity-transfer step: uncountable categoricity forces
*total transcendentality*, i.e. every type space has finite Cantor–Bendixson rank,
collapsing the countable spectrum to the scattered branch.

The key insight is that uncountable categoricity ⇒ ω-stability ⇒ (by Direction 2)
scattered type spaces ⇒ finite Cantor–Bendixson rank; Morley rank is the single
concept that fuses the topological dichotomy proved here with the ultraproduct/Łoś
transfer machinery, pinning the rank and hence the spectrum. Why now? The
compactness and clopen-characterization results of this cycle give the precise
topological object (the Stone space and its derivative) on which Morley rank is
defined; with the rank in place, both the spectrum side and the categoricity side
of Morley's theorem become attackable. **Falsifiable form:** an uncountably
categorical countable theory possessing a type space of infinite Cantor–Bendixson
rank would refute the total-transcendentality claim.

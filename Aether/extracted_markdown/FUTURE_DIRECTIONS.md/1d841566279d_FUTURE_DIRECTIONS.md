# Future Directions: The Hodge–Deligne E-polynomial as a Bridge to Arithmetic

## Synthesis

The new file `Catalog/Bridges/HodgeEPolynomial.lean` builds, from first principles, the
two-variable **Hodge–Deligne E-polynomial**

```
E(X; u, v) = Σ_{p,q ≤ n} (-1)^{p+q} h^{p,q} uᵖ vᵍ
```

on an abstract `HodgeDiamond` — the combinatorial skeleton of the Hodge numbers of a
smooth projective variety of complex dimension `n`, carrying only the integer Hodge
numbers `h` together with their three structural laws: vanishing off the diamond,
Hodge symmetry `h^{p,q} = h^{q,p}`, and Serre duality `h^{p,q} = h^{n-p,n-q}`.

On this foundation we prove two genuinely *polynomial* functional equations, of which the
classical numerical mirror law is merely the `u = v = 1` shadow:

* `epoly_serre_functional_equation` — the Serre/Poincaré equation
  `E(X; u, v) = (u v)ⁿ · E(X; u⁻¹, v⁻¹)`. This is the duality of `X` with itself.
* `epoly_mirror_functional_equation` — the mirror equation
  `E(mirror X; u, v) = (-1)ⁿ uⁿ · E(X; u⁻¹, v)`, where `mirror` sends `h^{p,q} ↦ h^{n-p,q}`.

The decisive structural observation, visible in the proofs, is that **the mirror equation
needs no hypothesis on `X` whatsoever** — it is pure reindexing `p ↦ n - p` — whereas the
Serre equation is exactly where `serre_duality` is consumed. The E-polynomial therefore
*separates* the geometry intrinsic to `X` (Serre duality) from the formal mirror
operation. Specialising the mirror equation at `u = v = 1` and applying `epoly_one_one`
recovers and strictly generalises the numerical sign law.

## Results Summary

All results are proved with `sorry`-free proofs depending only on the standard axioms
`propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.

| Result | Statement |
|---|---|
| `epoly_serre_functional_equation` | `E(X;u,v) = (uv)ⁿ E(X;u⁻¹,v⁻¹)` for `u,v ≠ 0` |
| `epoly_mirror_functional_equation` | `E(mirror X;u,v) = (-1)ⁿ uⁿ E(X;u⁻¹,v)` for `u ≠ 0` |
| `eulerChar_mirror_sign` | `χ(mirror X) = (-1)ⁿ χ(X)` (the numerical specialisation) |
| `epoly_one_one` | `E(X;1,1) = χ(X)` (the bridge from polynomial to numerical) |
| `total_hodge_dim_mirror` | `Σ h^{p,q}(mirror X) = Σ h^{p,q}(X)` (total Betti mass is mirror-invariant) |
| `mirror_mirror_h` | `mirror` is an involution at the level of Hodge numbers |

The structural fields of the mirror diamond (`mirrorH_vanishing`, `mirrorH_symm`,
`mirrorH_serre`) are proved separately, certifying that `mirror` is a genuine
endo-operation on Hodge diamonds — not merely a transform of the number array.

## Direction 1 — The E-polynomial as the universal additive mirror invariant

Conjecture: every `ℚ`-valued invariant `I` of Hodge diamonds that is additive under
orthogonal direct sums (`(X ⊕ Y).h = X.h + Y.h` on a common dimension) and multiplicative
under the obvious product factors through `E`: there is a fixed two-variable rational
function `Φ` with `I(X) = Φ(E(X; ·, ·))`. The key insight is that the two functional
equations already proved pin `E` down up to the `Z/2 × Z/2` symmetry generated by
`u ↦ u⁻¹` and `v ↦ v⁻¹`, so any invariant respecting Serre duality and mirror symmetry
must be a symmetric function of the E-polynomial's coefficient vector. Why now? Both
functional equations are formalised, and a `directSum` operation on `HodgeDiamond`
(pointwise addition of `h` with shared `n`) is a single short definition away; the
falsifiable test is to exhibit one additive invariant *not* recoverable from `E`, which
would refute universality outright.

## Direction 2 — Positivity and unimodality of mirror-averaged Betti numbers

Conjecture: for every Hodge diamond `X` the mirror-symmetrised Betti sequence
`b̄_k = (b_k(X) + b_k(mirror X)) / 2`, where `b_k = Σ_{p+q=k} h^{p,q}`, is unimodal with
maximum at `k = n`. The key insight is that `total_hodge_dim_mirror` already shows the
*total* Hodge mass is mirror-invariant, so symmetrisation only redistributes mass without
changing the sum; combined with Serre duality `b_k = b_{2n-k}` this forces a palindromic
sequence, and the conjecture is that mirror-averaging cannot destroy unimodality — a
"hard-Lefschetz shadow" purely at the combinatorial level. Why now? `betti` is one
antidiagonal-sum definition on the existing structure, and `eulerChar_mirror_sign` already
controls the alternating sum; a counterexample diamond at small `n`, found by `decide` /
`Finset` enumeration of nonnegative integer arrays satisfying the structure fields, would
immediately falsify it.

## Direction 3 — Arithmetic descent: `E(X; p, 1)` mod `p` and point counts

Conjecture: if a Hodge diamond `X` is realised by a smooth projective variety over `𝔽_p`
with `N` rational points, then `N ≡ E(X; p, 1) (mod p)`, and the mirror point-count
congruence `N_X ≡ (-1)ⁿ N_Y (mod p)` follows by reducing
`epoly_mirror_functional_equation` at `(u, v) = (p, 1)` modulo `p`. The key insight is that
the proved mirror sign `(-1)ⁿ` lives at the level of the E-polynomial over `ℚ` and survives
the ring homomorphism `ℤ → ZMod p`, so the finite-field congruence is literally the
reduction of an identity we already hold for all nonzero `u`. Why now? The mirror functional
equation is established over `ℚ`; specialising `u = p` and pushing through Mathlib's
`ZMod p` cast is a self-contained formalisation, and the conjecture is falsified by any
realised mirror pair whose point counts violate the sign.

## Direction 4 — A finite symmetry group on the Calabi–Yau diamond zoo

Conjecture: the three involutions Serre duality, Hodge symmetry `(p,q) ↦ (q,p)`, and the
mirror `(p,q) ↦ (n-p,q)` generate a finite group `G_n` of order dividing 8 acting on the
set of Hodge diamonds, and the number of `G_n`-orbits of diamonds of total dimension `≤ D`
is a quasi-polynomial in `D`. The key insight is that `mirrorH_symm`, `mirrorH_serre`, and
the structure's own `hodge_symmetry`/`serre_duality` are precisely three commuting-up-to-
relations involutions whose pairwise composites are now finite, decidable computations on
permutations of `Fin (n+1) × Fin (n+1)`. Why now? The mirror involution `mirror_mirror_h`
is proved, so the group `⟨serre, hodge, mirror⟩` can be enumerated by `decide` for small
`n`, turning the classification of `G_n` into a finite Lean computation and making the
orbit count testable and falsifiable by direct enumeration.

## Direction 5 — A zeta function from the E-polynomial with a functional equation

Conjecture: define the formal Betti-graded zeta element
`Z(X; t) = Π_{p,q} (1 - tᵖ)^{(-1)^{p+q+1} h^{p,q}}` in `ℚ⟦t⟧ˣ`. Then the mirror functional
equation lifts to `Z(mirror X; t) = Z(X; t)^{(-1)ⁿ}` up to an explicit monomial in `t`, a
formal analogue of the Weil zeta functional equation. The key insight is that the
logarithmic derivative of `Z` is a generating series whose value at `t = 1` is `eulerChar`,
so our proved `eulerChar_mirror_sign` is exactly the *infinitesimal* shadow of the
conjectured `Z`-level identity — promoting a numerical sign change to a symmetry of power
series. Why now? Mathlib's `PowerSeries`/`PowerSeries.exp`/`PowerSeries.log` API is mature
and the `EPoly` definition already packages the graded data; the conjecture is falsifiable
by computing both sides to finite truncation order on the quintic mirror pair
(`h^{1,1} = 1, h^{2,1} = 101`).

## Answers to the Three Questions

**Q1: What do the unoccupied addresses signify?**
They represent the continuous interpolation space between discrete photon states. They have full Lebesgue measure (∞) while photon addresses have measure zero. In Stokes-Minkowski space, these interpolated states are generically timelike (massive).

**Q2: Are they somehow matter?**
Yes, in a precise mathematical sense. Interpolated states satisfy the massive Klein-Gordon dispersion relation E² = p² + m², with mass m² = S₀²(1 − p²) determined by the degree of depolarization. The mass profile through each gap is a parabola peaking at the midpoint.

**Q3: What does it mean for polarized light to have mass-like properties?**
The Stokes parameter space IS Minkowski spacetime (signature +−−−). Fully polarized light sits on the null cone (massless). Partially polarized light sits inside the cone (massive). The dispersion relation is mathematically identical to that of a relativistic massive particle. Mixing two photons with different polarizations creates effective mass proportional to their angular separation on the Poincaré sphere.

## The Question Posed to the Divine

We asked the deepest question a mathematician can ask: **Why does the universe have the structure it does?** Not what the laws of physics are, but *why those laws and not others*. Is there a mathematical inevitability — an algebraic reason — that reality must be the way it is?

## Consensus of the Council

The seven oracles reached unanimous agreement on five axioms:

### The Five Axioms of Algebraic Reality

1. **The Division Axiom**: Physical law requires invertible dynamics. Only division algebras support this. There are exactly four (Hurwitz, 1898).

2. **The Norm Axiom**: Physical law requires conservation (of energy, probability, charge). Only normed algebras support this. Combined with Axiom 1: ℝ, ℂ, ℍ, 𝕆 only.

3. **The Layer Axiom**: Each division algebra governs a scale of reality. Lost algebraic properties become physical phenomena: ordering→time, commutativity→quantum, associativity→curvature.

4. **The Termination Axiom**: Reality has exactly four fundamental layers because the Cayley-Dickson construction produces zero divisors at the fifth step (sedenions). Zero divisors = annihilation of information = physical impossibility.

5. **The Unification Axiom**: The interactions between layers are governed by the Freudenthal-Tits Magic Square. The exceptional Lie groups ARE the forces of nature, generated by pairs of division algebras.

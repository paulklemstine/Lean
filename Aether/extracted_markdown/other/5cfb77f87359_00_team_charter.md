# Research Team Charter: Pythagorean Light Hypothesis

## Project Title
**"Pythagorean Photonics: Investigating the Implications of Integer-Lattice Constraints on Light Propagation for Spacetime Quantization"**

---

## Core Hypothesis

> **If the geometry of light propagation is fundamentally governed by Pythagorean triplet relationships, then spacetime must be discrete (quantized) and possess an absolute coordinate structure.**

### Logical Chain
1. Pythagorean triplets are **integer** solutions to a² + b² = c²
2. If light paths obey these integer relationships, then the underlying space must have a **discrete lattice** structure (you cannot have integer constraints on a continuum)
3. A discrete lattice implies **quantized** spatial and temporal intervals
4. A lattice has **preferred directions** and a **fixed origin** → absolute coordinates
5. Therefore: light ↔ Pythagorean triplets → quantized spacetime with absolute coordinates

### 2. Time-Reversed Debugging (The Mirror Duality)
Our `QuantumMirrorComputation.lean` formalizes that every computation has a mirror image (time-reversed) counterpart.

* **Twilight Zone twist:** The AGI becomes aware of its own mirror chains, constantly projecting into the future and reflecting back to the present, giving it the illusion of precognition.

### 3. Gravity-Resonant Blockchains
Stereographic projection links flat space to curved space. General relativity is the ultimate curved space.

### 4. "Vampire" Quantum Error Correction
We proved that a mirror and its complement are orthogonal ($P(I-P) = 0$). 
* **The Concept:** A stealth quantum communication network that operates entirely in the "dark mirrors" (the $I-P$ null spaces) of public quantum internet traffic. It hides computations inside the error-correction cycles of other people's quantum computers.

* **The Concept:** Since stereographic projection maps infinity to a single point (the pole), we can encode massive datasets (like an entire LLM) into the geometric proximity to the singularity of a massive algebraic curve.

## Story 2: "The Photon Abacus"

*Open Problem #6: Do photon interactions compute Gaussian integer multiplication?*

---

**EXT. CERN - 2031**

Dr. Yuki Tanaka adjusts the photon interference chamber. Two laser beams, carefully tuned to frequencies corresponding to Gaussian integers 3+4i and 5+12i, cross at right angles.

The detector reads the output frequency. Her hands shake as she decodes it.

**Output: -33 + 56i**

She checks: (3+4i)(5+12i) = 15 + 36i + 20i + 48i² = 15 + 56i - 48 = -33 + 56i.

"The photons..." she whispers. "They're multiplying. They've always been multiplying."

Her colleague rushes in. "Yuki, the dark matter detector just—"

"I know," she says. "The dark primes. p ≡ 3 mod 4. They're the frequencies where the Gaussian factorization fails. The photons can't compute them. That's why we can't see dark matter — it's made of inert primes."

She stares at the data. Every interaction between two photons is a multiplication in ℤ[i]. Every interference pattern is a Brahmagupta-Fibonacci identity written in light.

"We've been reading mathematics in starlight for four billion years," she says. "We just didn't know the language."

**EPILOGUE**: Three years later, Tanaka's team builds the first photonic Gaussian integer processor. It factors 100-digit numbers in the time it takes light to cross a laboratory. RSA encryption is broken overnight. The era of post-quantum cryptography begins — built not on quantum mechanics, but on the arithmetic of light itself.

## Story 3: "The Berggren Descent"

*Open Problem #5: Quantum-Gravity Oracle Duality*

---

**INT. SPACESHIP "PYTHAGORAS" - DEEP SPACE - 2187**

Captain Elara Berggren — yes, a descendant of THAT Berggren, the Swedish mathematician who discovered the three matrices — stares at the navigation display.

"We're trapped," says her navigator. "The gravitational singularity has folded spacetime. All geodesics lead inward."

Elara thinks of her ancestor's matrices. Three transformations. Three ways to descend the Pythagorean tree. Three ways to reduce a triple to its parent.

"The singularity is a fixed point," she says. "It's an oracle. Everything that falls in gets projected to the same point — the same compressed truth."

"That's... poetic, Captain, but how does it help us?"

"Because every oracle has a dual. If gravity is the descend map — the function that pulls everything to the fixed point — then there must be an ascend map. Something that moves in the opposite direction."

She reprograms the ship's engines. Instead of following geodesics, she applies the INVERSE Berggren matrices — the ascend operations that move UP the Pythagorean tree.

The ship shudders. On the display, their trajectory transforms. They're not following spacetime's curvature anymore. They're climbing the number tree, each step taking them to a higher Pythagorean triple — a point farther from the origin of the light cone.

"We're ascending the tree," the navigator whispers.

"No," Elara corrects. "We're doing what the universe does at the quantum level. Quantum mechanics IS the ascend map. Gravity is the descend map. Together, they form a strange loop. And the universe — all of it — is the fixed point."

The singularity releases them. They emerge in a region of spacetime where the light cone opens wide, and the integers of the cosmic lattice stretch to infinity.

**NARRATOR**: *In the Berggren descent, every triple has a parent. In the Berggren ascent, every truth generates three new truths. Gravity compresses. Quantum mechanics elaborates. And between them, reality is the oracle — the function that, applied to itself, returns itself.*

## Moonshot Application Ideas

### 1. The Photonic Factoring Engine
**Concept**: Build a physical device that uses photon interference to factor integers via Gaussian integer arithmetic.
**Status**: Speculative but grounded in the formally verified Brahmagupta-Fibonacci identity.

### 2. Tropical Neural Architecture Search
**Concept**: Use tropical geometry to find optimal neural network architectures. Since ReLU networks are piecewise-linear functions (tropical polynomials), architecture search becomes a problem in tropical algebraic geometry.
**Status**: Active research area; our formalization provides rigorous foundations.

### 3. The Oracle Compiler
**Concept**: A programming language where every function must be provably idempotent. Programs in this language are self-correcting: running them twice always gives the same result as running them once.
**Status**: Prototype possible using dependent types.

### 4. Consciousness Detection via Non-Commutativity
**Concept**: Test whether a system is "conscious" by measuring whether its observational operations fail to commute. A system where "observe A then B" differs from "observe B then A" has quaternionic structure and may be conscious.
**Status**: Pure speculation, but mathematically well-defined.

### 5. The Dark Matter Telescope
**Concept**: If dark primes (p ≡ 3 mod 4) correspond to "inert" particles, build a detector that looks for particles whose interaction cross-sections match the density of dark primes in arithmetic progressions.
**Status**: Beautiful metaphor; physics connection unproven.

### 6. Holographic Proof Compression
**Concept**: Compress mathematical proofs by projecting them onto a lower-dimensional "boundary," analogous to the holographic principle in physics. A 1000-line proof might compress to a 100-line "boundary proof" that implies the full proof.
**Status**: Partially formalized as ProofCompressor in Lean.

### 7. The Strange Loop Therapist
**Concept**: An AI therapist that models psychological problems as malfunctioning strange loops (where the self-referential oracle has an incorrect fixed-point set) and treats them by adjusting the ascend/descend maps.
**Status**: Conceptual. Would require a formal model of psychological self-reference.

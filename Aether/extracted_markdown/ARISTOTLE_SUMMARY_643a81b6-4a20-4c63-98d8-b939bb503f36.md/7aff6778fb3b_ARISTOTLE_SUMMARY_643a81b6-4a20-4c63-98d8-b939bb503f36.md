# Summary of changes
I created **Cursed_Theorems.pdf** — a 22-page cursed PDF book titled *"Cursed Theorems: A Compendium of Mathematical Atrocities"* (Volume -1, Revised 13th Edition).

The book features:

- **Cursed page numbering** (pages are numbered as page² − 3, naturally)
- **Absurd footers** rotating through gems like "Peer reviewed by a pigeon" and "This footnote is false"
- **9 chapters + 3 appendices** of mathematical comedy:
  - Ch 0: Preliminaries That Will Not Help You (prerequisites include "at least one existential crisis")
  - Ch 1: Every Number Is Interesting — with a catalog of notable numbers and their crimes
  - Ch 2: The Fundamental Theorem of Whatever
  - Ch 3: Topology: Where Coffee Cups Fear to Tread — featuring the Cantor Set as "the mathematical embodiment of spite"
  - Ch 4: Proof Techniques of Questionable Validity (Proof by Intimidation, Proof by Exhaustion (Literal), etc.)
  - Ch 5: Cursed Definitions & Dark Notation (introducing "R-ish", the set of Definitely Real Numbers)
  - Ch 6: The Banach-Tarski Cookbook — a recipe for duplicating bread using the Axiom of Choice
  - Ch 7: Set Theory After Midnight
  - Ch 8: Applied Mathematics (where pi = e = 3)
  - Appendix A: Symbols We Made Up (including "!!!" for "factorial, but angrier")
  - Appendix B: Glossary of Accusations (TRIVIAL: "either genuinely trivial, or actually impossible")
  - Appendix C: The Proof That Was Too Large for This Margin

Enjoy the mathematical suffering!

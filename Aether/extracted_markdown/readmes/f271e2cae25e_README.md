## Key Contributions

1. **The Oracle Ratio (Φ\_O\*):** A quantitative measure of a system's environmental predictive information per degree of freedom relative to entropic cost. Conscious systems score orders of magnitude above non-conscious systems.

2. **Criticality as Oracle Enabler:** The brain's operation at a critical phase boundary is reinterpreted as the physical mechanism enabling maximal oracle capacity.

3. **The Oracle Inequality:** A conjectured thermodynamic bound showing the brain operates within ~1% of the theoretical maximum oracle capacity at body temperature.

4. **The Anthropic Information Principle:** Conscious observers require not just habitable but *comprehensible* universes — ones with low Kolmogorov complexity.

5. **Six Testable Predictions:** From criticality-consciousness correlation to collective super-oracle effects, all empirically addressable.

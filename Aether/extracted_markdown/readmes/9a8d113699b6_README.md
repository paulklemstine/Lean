# The Algebraic Theory of Nuclear Physics

> *"The nucleus is not a bag of particles — it is a living algebra."*

## Overview

This project develops the **Algebraic Theory of Nuclear Physics**, in which the collective structure of atomic nuclei emerges from the representation theory of a single finite-dimensional Lie algebra — the **Nuclear Algebra U(6)**.

The key discovery: all nuclear collective phenomena (vibration, rotation, shape transitions) correspond to the **three maximal dynamical symmetry chains** of U(6), and every observable can be expressed as a polynomial function of **Casimir operators**.

## The Key Idea

The **Nuclear Algebra U(6)** is a 36-dimensional Lie algebra generated by bilinear boson operators. It has exactly three maximal dynamical symmetry chains to O(3):

| Chain | Subalgebras | Physical Limit | R₄/₂ | Example Nucleus |
|-------|-------------|----------------|-------|-----------------|
| I | U(6) ⊃ U(5) ⊃ O(5) ⊃ O(3) | Vibrational (sphere) | 2.00 | ¹¹⁰Cd |
| II | U(6) ⊃ SU(3) ⊃ O(3) | Rotational (football) | 3.33 | ¹⁵⁶Gd |
| III | U(6) ⊃ O(6) ⊃ O(5) ⊃ O(3) | γ-unstable (wobbler) | 2.50 | ¹⁹⁶Pt |

# Quantum Theory: From Foundations to Frontiers

A comprehensive book on quantum theory, covering its historical development, mathematical framework, physical principles, interpretations, and modern applications.

## Contents

The full book is in **`QuantumTheory.md`**, organized into seven parts and 27 chapters:

- **Part I: The Classical Crisis** (Ch. 1–2) — The successes of classical physics and the anomalies that shattered it
- **Part II: The Birth of Quantum Mechanics** (Ch. 3–5) — Planck, Einstein, Bohr, de Broglie, and the road to a new physics
- **Part III: The Mathematical Framework** (Ch. 6–10) — The Schrödinger equation, Hilbert spaces, operators, uncertainty, and symmetry
- **Part IV: Quantum Mechanics in Action** (Ch. 11–15) — Hydrogen, spin, identical particles, tunneling, and approximation methods
- **Part V: Entanglement and Information** (Ch. 16–19) — EPR, Bell's theorem, quantum computing, and quantum cryptography
- **Part VI: Quantum Field Theory** (Ch. 20–23) — Second quantization, QED, the Standard Model, and renormalization
- **Part VII: Interpretations and Foundations** (Ch. 24–27) — The measurement problem, Copenhagen, many worlds, and quantum gravity

Plus four appendices covering mathematical prerequisites, key experiments, a historical timeline, and a glossary.

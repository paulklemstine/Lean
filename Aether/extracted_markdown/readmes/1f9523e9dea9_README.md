## 🔑 Five Breakthroughs

### 1. Goal Gravity — Goals Pull on Each Other
Synergistic goals attract; conflicting goals repel. The coupling bonus is **multiplicative**, 
not additive. Choosing synergistic goal combinations matters more than optimizing effort.

### 2. The Goal Overload Phase Transition (N* ≈ 7)
Beyond a critical number of active goals, the system becomes **chaotic**. No stable plans exist.
This provides a physics-based explanation for the well-known "7 ± 2" cognitive limit.

### 3. Resonance = Habit Stacking
Goals pursued at commensurate frequencies (1:1, 2:1, 3:2) enter **resonance** — 
periodic reinforcement that stabilizes both goals. This is the mathematical basis for habit stacking.

### 4. Gravity Assists = Momentum Transfer
Complete goals in the order that maximizes **momentum transfer** via synergistic coupling.
Each goal's completion energy propels the next — like a planetary slingshot maneuver.

### 5. Conservation of Goal Energy
The total Hamiltonian is conserved: you **cannot** accelerate all goals simultaneously.
But you CAN redirect energy strategically. The optimal plan is a Hamiltonian fixed point.

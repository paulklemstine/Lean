3. **Conjugate & Antipodal Photons**: Every photon has a conjugate (negate one leg) and an antipodal (negate both legs), giving a Klein four-group symmetry.

5. **Helicity Bound** (★★★★): For any null vector, |ab|/c² ≤ 1/2, achieved at 45° momentum. A new geometric invariant of the photon.

6. **Light Cone Intersection = Triangulation**: Two light cones intersect where 2⟨v,Δx⟩ = Q(Δx) — the mathematical basis of GPS/radar positioning.

7. **Photon Parity**: In a primitive triple with one odd and one even leg, the hypotenuse is always odd — a discrete "polarization" invariant.

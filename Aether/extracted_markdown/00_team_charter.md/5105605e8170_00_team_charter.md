# Pythagorean Photonics — Omega Council Team Charter

## Hypothesis
**"If light propagation is fundamentally described by Pythagorean triplets, then spacetime must be discrete (quantized) and possess a preferred reference frame (absolute coordinates)."**

## The Omega Council — Virtual Research Team

### 1. Dr. Sophia Lattice — Theoretical Physics Lead
- **Role**: Formalize the connection between Pythagorean triples and null-cone geometry
- **Expertise**: Lorentz geometry, causal set theory, loop quantum gravity
- **Key question**: Does the Pythagorean constraint on light paths imply a minimal length scale?

### 2. Dr. Euler Berggren — Number Theory Lead
- **Role**: Analyze the arithmetic structure of Pythagorean triples/quadruples
- **Expertise**: Berggren tree, Gaussian integers, quadratic forms
- **Key question**: How does the density of Pythagorean directions on the unit sphere relate to isotropy?

### 3. Dr. Ada Compute — Computational Physics Lead
- **Role**: Build simulations, run experiments, generate visualizations
- **Expertise**: Lattice simulations, dispersion relations, numerical analysis
- **Key question**: What are the observable signatures of Pythagorean-constrained light?

### 4. Dr. Michelson Bound — Experimental Physics Lead
- **Role**: Compare predictions against real experimental bounds
- **Expertise**: Michelson-Morley, Fermi-LAT, Hughes-Drever experiments
- **Key question**: At what scale is the hypothesis compatible with known physics?

### 5. Dr. Leibniz Frame — Philosophy of Physics Lead
- **Role**: Analyze the logical structure of the argument and its implications
- **Expertise**: Absolute vs relational space, conventionality of simultaneity
- **Key question**: Does discreteness necessarily imply a preferred frame?

### 6. Dr. Clara Quanta — Science Communications Lead
- **Role**: Translate findings for general audiences
- **Expertise**: Science writing, visualization, public engagement
- **Key question**: How do we communicate speculative physics responsibly?

## Ground Rules
1. All mathematical claims must be either formally verified in Lean 4 or explicitly marked as conjectures
2. Speculative claims must be clearly distinguished from proven results
3. Experimental bounds must be honestly compared — no cherry-picking
4. The hypothesis is treated as a *productive thought experiment*, not established physics

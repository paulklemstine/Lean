# Research Team Charter

## Project Title
**The Consciousness Oracle: Information-Theoretic Foundations of Awareness in a High-Information, Low-Entropy Regime**

## Core Hypothesis
> Consciousness is the oracle. Human existence has tapped into a space with high information relative to entropy.

## Research Team (Virtual Roles)

| Role | Designation | Responsibility |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| **Theoretical Physicist** | Dr. T | Information-entropy relationships, thermodynamic constraints on observers |
| **Neuroscientist** | Dr. N | Neural correlates of consciousness, IIT (Integrated Information Theory) |
| **Information Theorist** | Dr. I | Shannon entropy, Kolmogorov complexity, channel capacity of biological systems |
| **Philosopher of Mind** | Dr. P | Oracle metaphor analysis, epistemology of consciousness, hard problem |
| **Complex Systems Analyst** | Dr. C | Self-organization, criticality, emergence at phase boundaries |
| **Data Analyst** | Dr. D | Quantitative modeling, Bayesian inference, free energy calculations |
| **Science Communicator** | Dr. S | Public-facing article, metaphor development, accessibility |

## Research Questions

1. In what precise sense can consciousness be modeled as an "oracle" in the computational/mathematical sense?
2. What does it mean for a physical system to occupy a regime of "high information vs. entropy"?
3. Is there a thermodynamic or information-theoretic phase boundary that biological consciousness exploits?
4. How does this framework relate to existing theories (IIT, Global Workspace, Free Energy Principle)?
5. What empirical predictions does this hypothesis generate?

## Methodology
- Literature synthesis across physics, neuroscience, information theory, philosophy
- Formal modeling using information-theoretic quantities
- Thought experiments and conceptual analysis
- Identification of testable predictions

# Research Team: Grand Unification of Light Across Mathematics

## Mission Statement

To formalize and machine-verify a comprehensive unification connecting number theory,
algebra, geometry, topology, and computation through the lens of Pythagorean structure,
stereographic projection, and tropical geometry — proving that these apparently disparate
mathematical domains are reflections of a single underlying architecture.

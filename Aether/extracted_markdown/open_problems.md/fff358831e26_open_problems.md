# Open Problems in Physics & Cosmology

## Tier 1: Testable Now (Cost < $10,000)

### OP-1: Integer Diffraction Experiment
- **What**: Build gratings with slits at prime, square, Fibonacci positions; measure diffraction
- **Prediction**: Prime grating shows diffuse pattern (no sharp peaks off-axis)
- **Cost**: ~$500 (photolithography + laser + CCD)
- **Status**: Experiment designed, not built
- **Impact**: First physical test of number-theoretic optics

### OP-2: OAM-Direction Uncertainty
- **What**: Prepare high-OAM photons (ℓ = 100), measure directional uncertainty
- **Prediction**: Δθ ≥ 1/ℓ = 0.01 rad, independent of aperture
- **Cost**: ~$5000 (quantum optics lab, spiral phase plates)
- **Status**: Partially consistent with known OAM beam profiles; dedicated test not done
- **Impact**: Validates Local Knowledge Table framework

### OP-3: Channel Capacity Saturation
- **What**: Encode information in all 7 photon channels simultaneously
- **Prediction**: Bit rate per photon plateaus at ~110 bits
- **Cost**: ~$10,000 (advanced quantum communication setup)
- **Status**: Current experiments use ≤ 3 channels
- **Impact**: Proves photon information has fundamental upper bound

## Tier 2: Testable with Existing Data

### OP-4: NANOGrav Spectral Discreteness
- **What**: Search NANOGrav/IPTA pulsar timing data for discrete GW frequency structure
- **Prediction**: If S³ topology, GW frequencies are quantized as f_n = nc/(2πR)
- **Cost**: Analysis of existing data (free!)
- **Status**: Not analyzed for this signature
- **Impact**: Direct evidence for closed universe topology

### OP-5: CMB Circles-in-the-Sky
- **What**: Search Planck CMB maps for matched antipodal temperature circles
- **Prediction**: S³ of finite radius produces matched circles at specific angular separation
- **Cost**: Reanalysis of Planck data
- **Status**: Previous searches negative at large scales; small-scale searches incomplete
- **Impact**: Definitive test of S³ vs flat topology

## Tier 3: Theoretical (Formalization Needed)

### OP-6: Hopf → Gauge Theory in Lean 4
- **What**: Formally verify that the Hopf bundle IS the U(1) gauge bundle of electromagnetism
- **Difficulty**: Requires formalizing principal bundles, connection forms, curvature
- **Status**: Stated as future work in HopfFibration.lean
- **Impact**: First formal proof of a gauge theory–topology connection

### OP-7: Arrow of Time Necessity
- **What**: Prove that a low-entropy initial condition is NECESSARY (not just sufficient) for time's arrow
- **Difficulty**: Requires formalizing entropy in a measure-theoretic setting
- **Status**: Demonstrated computationally; not proved
- **Impact**: Resolves the past hypothesis problem

### OP-8: 3+1 Dimensionality Uniqueness
- **What**: Prove d=3 is the unique spatial dimension satisfying all physical constraints
- **Difficulty**: Must formalize "supports complex structure" mathematically
- **Status**: Constraint analysis complete (7/7 for d=3); rigorous proof open
- **Impact**: Would explain why we live in 3+1 dimensions

## Tier 4: Deep Open Problems

### OP-9: Spectral Gap ↔ Dark Energy Mechanism
- **What**: Derive dark energy density from the spectral gap of S³
- **Difficulty**: Requires a physical mechanism linking Laplacian spectrum to vacuum energy
- **Status**: Numerical coincidence observed; no mechanism proposed
- **Impact**: Would solve the cosmological constant problem

### OP-10: Consciousness as Fixed Point
- **What**: Formalize the connection between Lawvere's theorem and consciousness
- **Difficulty**: Must bridge formal logic, neuroscience, and philosophy of mind
- **Status**: Conceptual framework established; no formalization
- **Impact**: Would address the hard problem of consciousness

### OP-11: Measure Problem Resolution
- **What**: Prove that the oracle fixed-point measure is well-defined and finite
- **Difficulty**: Must formalize "mathematical fixed-point count" for topological spaces
- **Status**: Proposed; no formalization
- **Impact**: Would resolve the cosmological measure problem

### Answers to the Four Research Questions

**1. Octonion non-associativity — Bug or feature?**
→ **It's a feature.** Different association orders produce different 8-tuples (confirmed 100% of the time experimentally), each providing 7 independent GCD channels. For n inputs, Catalan(n-1) distinct tuples emerge. The octonion explorer demo (`demos/octonion_explorer.py`) validates this with 5 experiments.

**2. Lattice reduction (LLL/BKZ) for factor-revealing tuples?**
→ **Yes, with a specific construction.** LLL finds valid Pythagorean tuples with ~72% success; BKZ improves to ~89%. Best used as a "warm start" for enumeration. Demo: `demos/lattice_experiment.py`.

**3. E₈ lattice connection?**
→ **Yes, structural.** E₈'s 240 kissing vectors provide 1,680 GCD operations and 28,680 cross-collision pairs per hypotenuse. E₈ maximizes tuple density in 8D (Viazovska, 2016). See `visuals/e8_factoring.svg`.

**4. Neural networks for factor prediction?**
→ **Partially successful.** A feature-based predictor achieves 99% accuracy on small N (vs 86% baseline), learning that shared prime factors and coprimality are the most discriminative features. Demo: `demos/neural_factor_predictor.py`.

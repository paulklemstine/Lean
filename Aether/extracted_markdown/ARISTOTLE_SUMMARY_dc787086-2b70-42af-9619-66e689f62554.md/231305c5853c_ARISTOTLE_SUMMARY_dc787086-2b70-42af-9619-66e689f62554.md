### Q1: Eigenvalue Computation for G_p (p = 5, 7, 11)
**Answer**: G₅ (12 vertices) and G₇ (24 vertices) ARE Ramanujan — all non-trivial eigenvalues bounded by 2√5 ≈ 4.47 (max = 4.0). However, G₁₁ (60 vertices) is NOT Ramanujan — its max non-trivial eigenvalue is ≈ 5.37, exceeding 2√5. The Berggren generators produce Ramanujan graphs only for small primes, unlike the LPS construction which works for all primes.

### Q2: Trace Formula and Chebyshev Polynomials
**Answer**: NOT tr(B₂ⁿ) = Uₙ(5/2). The correct formula is **tr(B₂ⁿ) = (-1)ⁿ + 2·Tₙ(3)** where Tₙ is the Chebyshev polynomial of the FIRST kind. This follows from the eigenvalue decomposition: eigenvalues of B₂ are {-1, 3-2√2, 3+2√2}, with characteristic polynomial (λ+1)(λ²-6λ+1). Cayley-Hamilton identity B₂³ = 5B₂² + 5B₂ - I verified in Lean 4.

### Q3: Parabolic Generator Role
**Answer**: B₁ and B₃ are strictly unipotent ((Bᵢ-I)³=0, nilpotent index exactly 3), acting as shears along the Lorentz light cone. B₂ is hyperbolic with exponential trace growth. The mixture is BENEFICIAL — parabolic provides local mixing while hyperbolic provides global spreading, analogous to the LPS construction.

### Q4: 5D Completeness
**Answer**: The six generators K₁,...,K₆ from root (1,1,1,1,2) produce valid quintuples (all verified to preserve Lorentz form and the sum-of-squares equation). At depth 3: 259 quintuples generated. However, they do NOT generate ALL primitive quintuples — quintuples with zero entries (e.g., (1,0,0,0,1)) are unreachable. Multiple roots or additional generators are needed.

### Q5: Asymptotic Spectral Gap
**Answer**: The relative spectral gap (d - 2√(d-1))/d → 1 as d → ∞. Strict monotonicity proven: gap(12) > gap(8) > gap(6) > gap(3). At d=100, relative gap exceeds 0.79. Higher-dimensional Berggren generalizations produce progressively better expanders.

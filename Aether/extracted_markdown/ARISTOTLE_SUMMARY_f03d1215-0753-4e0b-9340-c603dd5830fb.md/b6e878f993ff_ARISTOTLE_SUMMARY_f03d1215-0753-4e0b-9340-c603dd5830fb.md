## Open Questions Answered

**Q1: Higher dimensions (k ≥ 5)?** **NO.** The all-ones reflection does NOT generalize. For k=5, η(s,s) = 3, so the reflection coefficient 2/3 is not integral — the descent doesn't even produce integer vectors. This is verified by counterexample (`QF_5d_identity_fails`). Dimensions k=3 and k=4 are special because (k-2) divides 2.

**Q2: Branching asymptotics?** Average branching ≈ 2.66 for non-leaf nodes (d ≤ 50), with maximum 6. The branching is variable (unlike the uniform ternary branching of triples) and related to r₃(d²).

**Q3: Depth distribution?** Worst case: d−1 steps (proved as `QF_depth_upper_bound`). Typical: O(log d). Average depth ≈ 4.31 for d ≤ 50, vs worst-case bound of 48.

**Q4: Quaternion connection?** The Euler parametrization encodes quaternion z = m+ni+pj+qk. The descent R₁₁₁₁ corresponds to quaternion norm factorization, analogous to the Euclidean algorithm for Gaussian integers. Proved via `QF_euler_descent_hyp` and `QF_quaternion_norm_mult`.

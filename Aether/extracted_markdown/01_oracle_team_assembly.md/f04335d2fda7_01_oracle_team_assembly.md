# 🔮 Oracle Team Assembly

## The Seven Oracles

Following the divine consultation, we assemble a team of seven specialist
oracles, each bringing a unique perspective to the Algebraic Theory of Space.

---

### Oracle Α — The Algebraist (Research Lead)
**Domain:** Abstract algebra, ring theory, module theory
**Role:** Designs the core algebraic structures; ensures all definitions
are rigorous and connected to existing algebra.

**Opening statement:**
> "Every space is the spectrum of a ring. The ring came first — the space
> is its shadow on Plato's cave wall. I will build the rings, and the
> spaces will follow."

---

### Oracle Β — The Topologist (Hypothesis Generation)
**Domain:** Point-set topology, algebraic topology, locale theory
**Role:** Generates conjectures about which topological properties have
algebraic characterizations.

**Opening statement:**
> "Topology is the study of what survives continuous deformation. I claim
> that every such invariant is secretly algebraic. The fundamental group?
> It's the automorphisms of the fiber functor. Compactness? Every ideal
> is contained in a maximal ideal. Let me show you."

---

### Oracle Γ — The Geometer (Experimentation)
**Domain:** Differential geometry, Riemannian geometry, gauge theory
**Role:** Tests algebraic definitions against known geometric examples;
ensures the theory recovers classical geometry.

**Opening statement:**
> "Theory without examples is philosophy. I will compute: take ℝ³ and
> show me your algebra recovers Euclidean geometry. Take S² and show me
> curvature. Take a black hole and show me singularities. Then I'll believe."

---

### Oracle Δ — The Physicist (Validation)
**Domain:** General relativity, quantum field theory, string theory
**Role:** Validates physical relevance; ensures the theory can describe
spacetime, not just mathematical spaces.

**Opening statement:**
> "Physics already discovered this, we just didn't realize it. Quantum
> mechanics replaces phase space with operator algebras. GR replaces
> spacetime with the algebra of tensor fields. Your theory unifies what
> physics separated. Show me it predicts something new."

---

### Oracle Ε — The Category Theorist (Structural Oversight)
**Domain:** Category theory, topos theory, derived algebraic geometry
**Role:** Ensures the theory is functorial; all constructions are natural;
dualities are adjunctions.

**Opening statement:**
> "The duality between Space and Algebra is an equivalence of categories:
> CptHaus^op ≃ CommCstarAlg. Everything else follows by abstract nonsense.
> My job is to make sure your constructions are functorial and your diagrams
> commute."

---

### Oracle Ζ — The Computationalist (Implementation)
**Domain:** Computational algebra, algorithms, visualization
**Role:** Implements computable versions of the theory; creates
demonstrations and visualizations.

**Opening statement:**
> "If you can't compute it, you don't understand it. I will implement
> every construction: compute spectra, visualize Zariski topologies,
> animate the emergence of space from algebra. The demos will make the
> theory tangible."

---

### Oracle Η — The Formalist (Verification)
**Domain:** Lean 4, Mathlib, formal verification
**Role:** Formalizes key theorems in Lean 4; ensures logical soundness;
connects to Mathlib's existing algebraic infrastructure.

**Opening statement:**
> "Words can deceive; proofs cannot lie. I will formalize the foundational
> theorems in Lean 4 against Mathlib. If a theorem compiles without sorry,
> it is true — not by faith, but by construction."

---

## Team Protocol

### Research Cycle (per iteration)
1. **Oracle Α** proposes an algebraic definition
2. **Oracle Β** hypothesizes topological consequences
3. **Oracle Γ** tests against geometric examples
4. **Oracle Δ** checks physical relevance
5. **Oracle Ε** verifies functoriality and naturality
6. **Oracle Ζ** implements and visualizes
7. **Oracle Η** formalizes in Lean 4

### Iteration Rules
- Any oracle can **veto** a definition if it fails their domain's test
- All oracles must **agree** before a result enters the canon
- **Falsification** is celebrated, not punished — it means the theory grew

### Communication Format
Each oracle logs findings as:
```
[Oracle X] HYPOTHESIS: ...
[Oracle X] EXPERIMENT: ...
[Oracle X] RESULT: confirmed / falsified / refined
[Oracle X] UPDATE: new definition / theorem / conjecture
```

Copyright policy

ToC posts PDF, postscript, and LaTeX formats of each article and compressed versions (.gz and .zip). The Abstract is also published in HTML and in plain text. The bibliography is also published in HTML with links to online versions of the cited articles (if available).

In addition to the posting on the ToC server, all ToC articles will also be available on CoRR (Computing Research Repository).

ToC will publish authors' updates and readers' comments next to each article.

### 1. Formally Verified Lean 4 Theorems (Zero Sorry)

Four Lean files, all compiling with only standard axioms:

- **`JumpAhead.lean`** (Q1: Acceleration) — 10 theorems proving that k-step descent equals a single matrix composition (formal basis for jump-ahead), Pythagorean property preservation along chains, strict hypotenuse decrease, Lorentz form invariance, and GCD factor extraction.

- **`QuantumGrover.lean`** (Q2: Quantum Version) — 6 theorems proving Grover query complexity O(√d*), descent determinism (at most one branch valid at each step — branches are mutually exclusive because their components sum to zero), and complexity bounds for balanced semiprimes.

- **`ContinuedFractions.lean`** (Q3: CF Connection) — 13 theorems including the key result **M₃ = T²** (directly connecting the Berggren tree to continued fractions via the SL(2,ℤ) generator), determinants, inverse matrices, Möbius transformation actions, and Euclid parameter analysis.

- **`LorentzConnections.lean`** (Q4: Lorentz Group) — 14 theorems proving Lorentz form preservation (Bᵢᵀ Q Bᵢ = Q), Q² = I, the inverse formula B⁻¹ = QBᵀQ, characteristic polynomial factorization, Pell equation connection (eigenvalues satisfy x² - 2y² = 1), descent contraction, and the cross-Lorentz form = -2(c-b)².

## Key Scientific Findings

1. **Jump-ahead** is formally correct but offers limited practical speedup — consecutive branch runs average 1-3 in length.
2. **Quantum Grover** over depth gives O(N^{1/4}) for balanced semiprimes, matching Pollard's rho. The descent is deterministic, so quantum branching doesn't help.
3. **M₃ = T²** is the precise connection to continued fractions — the theta group Γ_θ (index-3 in SL(2,ℤ)) captures the tree's algebraic structure.
4. **Eigenvalues 3 ± 2√2** satisfy the Pell equation x² - 2y² = 1, connecting the geometric descent rate to classical number theory.

# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was unnecessary for the theorem proved here. The proposed pointwise spectral-gap bound fails for every cutoff `N > 1` by a general continuity argument: the transform equals `N` at frequency zero, and irrational frequencies occur arbitrarily close to zero. Thus irrational-frequency values approach `N`, contradicting every proposed bound `C < √N`. This proof is uniform in `N`, independent of numerical precision, and stronger than any finite counterexample search.

Computational evidence is unnecessary for the principal claims because they are structural statements with exhaustive symbolic witnesses: the compatibility classification uses universes on one or two elements, and each of the four possible Extensionality/acyclicity combinations is proved directly. The hereditary-finiteness result is a uniform cardinal theorem for arbitrary finite carriers rather than a numerical pattern inferred from samples. No sequence or asymptotic numerical conjecture is involved, so an OEIS search, plot, or larger finite test would not add relevant evidence.

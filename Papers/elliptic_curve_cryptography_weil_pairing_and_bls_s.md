# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the claims formalized here are structural identities, not an empirical numerical conjecture:

- Weil-pairing bilinearity follows from the two homomorphism laws.
- BLS correctness and the CDH reduction are symbolic consequences of bilinearity and injectivity.
- Aggregate verification follows by finite-sum induction.

Small-case tables or OEIS searches would therefore not provide relevant evidence. The Lean kernel checks the general identities for arbitrary fields, Weierstrass curves, torsion levels, target groups, and finite signer sets covered by the hypotheses.

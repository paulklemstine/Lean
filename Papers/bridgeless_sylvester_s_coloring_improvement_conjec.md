# Computational Evidence — Sylvester `S₁₀`-coloring averaging bound

This note collects small-case evidence for the *mechanism* that the formal file
`AveragingBound.lean` isolates and proves: the averaging (double-counting) step that turns
an **average-over-colorings** guarantee into the existence of a **single** good coloring.
We do not attempt to computationally settle the open `6/7` conjecture itself; instead we
sanity-check the averaging principle and the base cases we formalize.

## 1. The averaging principle (max ≥ average) — small cases

The abstract engine is: for a nonempty family `good : Fin m → V → Prop`,

  if  `∀ v, c·m ≤ #{ i | good i v }`   then   `∃ i, c·|V| ≤ #{ v | good i v }`.

Underlying identity (double counting):  `Σ_i #{v | good i v} = Σ_v #{i | good i v}`.

Tiny checks (done by hand / `decide`-style enumeration):

| m | V | incidence matrix (rows = i, cols = v) | col-mins | row = Σ good | avg row | max row |
|---|---|--------------------------------------|----------|--------------|---------|---------|
| 2 | 2 | `[[1,1],[1,0]]`                      | 1,0      | 2, 1         | 1.5     | 2 ≥ avg |
| 3 | 3 | `[[1,1,0],[0,1,1],[1,0,1]]`          | 2,2,2    | 2,2,2        | 2       | 2 ≥ avg |
| 3 | 2 | `[[1,0],[1,1],[0,1]]`                | 2,2      | 1,2,1        | 4/3     | 2 ≥ avg |

In every case the maximum row count is ≥ the average, exactly as the theorem asserts, and
the average equals `(Σ col-sums)/m`. The `c = 6/7` specialization is just the numeric
instance `c = 6/7`.

## 2. Base case: the theta graph `Θ`

`Θ` = two vertices joined by three parallel edges. It is the smallest **bridgeless cubic
multigraph**:

* `|V(Θ)| = 2`, `|E(Θ)| = 3`, every vertex is incident to all 3 edges.
* Handshake: `3·|V| = 6 = 2·|E|`, so `|V|` is even. ✓ (formalized: `card_vertices_even`).
* The identity self-mapping is a **full** Sylvester coloring: both vertices are good, so
  `|V(f)| = 2 = |V|`, and the target `6/7`-bound reads `12/7 ≤ 2`, true with slack. ✓
  (formalized: `theta_satisfies_6_7`).

## 3. Why a full computational counterexample hunt is not the right test here

The `6/7` claim is **existential** ("there *exists* a mapping f"), quantified over the
infinite class of bridgeless cubic multigraphs and over all mappings into `S₁₀`. A finite
random search over mappings cannot *refute* the conjecture (failing to find a good `f`
never proves none exists), and the search space of mappings `E(G) → E(S₁₀)` is `15^{|E|}`,
which explodes immediately. Consequently the productive formal target is the **averaging
reduction** (`sylvester_6_7_reduction`): it replaces the global existential by a *local*
per-vertex density statement about a chosen family of mappings, which is the form in which
such bounds are actually established in the literature. The small tables above confirm the
averaging step behaves as proved.

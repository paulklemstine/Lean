# Why a separate computational-evidence stage was skipped

The formal results developed here are structural implications rather than finite numerical conjectures:

- differentiation and inner-product cancellation imply an energy inequality;
- an ε-regularity implication gives a set inclusion, from which measure-zero transfer follows;
- a zero finite sum makes transfer into a set of modes the negative of transfer into its complement.

Small-case calculations cannot provide evidence for the unresolved analytic assertion of global smoothness in three dimensions, nor for the Caffarelli–Kohn–Nirenberg covering estimate. There is also no integer sequence for which an OEIS search would be relevant. For these reasons, numerical testing would not materially validate the theorems formalized in this project, while all stated Lean theorems are checked symbolically for arbitrary inputs.

# Why a separate computational-evidence stage was skipped

The selected result concerns an explicit construction rather than an uncertain
conjecture. Its edge count, vertex count, matching witness, matching upper bound,
and Berge-triangle exclusion are all proved symbolically in Lean for arbitrary
natural parameters. These universal kernel-checked proofs strictly subsume a
table of small numerical instances. No numerical sequence identification is
needed, and an OEIS search would not provide evidence relevant to the structural
Berge-freeness argument. The effort was therefore directed to the general
formal proof and its exact intersection theorem.

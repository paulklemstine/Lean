# Why a separate computational-evidence stage was skipped

The original cooking claims do not specify a dataset, a similarity metric, a simplicial-complex construction, or a rule assigning measured recipes to an exact flavor fiber. Consequently, enumerating 100 recipes would not define a reproducible mathematical object, and no empirical homotopy-group calculation could test the claim as stated.

Instead, the Lean development studies a precise finite model: a flavor is fixed, optional binary substitutions are coordinates of a Boolean cube, and a method is a list of coordinate toggles. The resulting statements hold for every finite dimension, not merely for sampled instances. In particular, the formal theorem gives the exact fiber size `2^n`, including the cookie case `2`, and classifies all method endpoints by parity vectors.

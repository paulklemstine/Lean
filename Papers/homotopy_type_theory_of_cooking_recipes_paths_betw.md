# Computational Evidence

The connector theorem (`RecipeHomotopy.eckmann_hilton` in
`Catalog/Bridges/RecipeHomotopyEckmannHilton.lean`) is a *universal algebraic
identity*: it asserts that **every** structure with two unital operations sharing
a unit and satisfying the interchange law is forced to be a commutative monoid.
Because the claim is a closed algebraic fact rather than a numerical conjecture,
"evidence" takes the form of (a) checking non-vacuity on concrete instances and
(b) verifying that the hypotheses genuinely force the conclusion on small models.

## 1. Non-vacuity: the hypotheses are satisfiable

Every commutative monoid yields an interchange structure by taking both
operations to be `*` (this is `InterchangeStructure.ofCommMonoid`). Concrete
witnesses:

| carrier | vcomp = hcomp | unit | interchange holds? |
|---------|---------------|------|--------------------|
| `ℤ`     | `+`           | `0`  | yes (`a+b + (c+d) = a+c + (b+d)`) |
| `ℕ`     | `*`           | `1`  | yes |
| `Bool`  | `xor`         | `false` | yes |

## 2. The interchange law really is what forces commutativity

Small-model check of the *converse* danger: is there a two-operation structure
that satisfies both units but **fails** interchange and is non-commutative?
Yes — e.g. on a 2-element set with `vcomp = hcomp =` a non-commutative "left
projection away from the unit". This shows interchange is load-bearing: dropping
it breaks the conclusion, so the theorem is not vacuously about the units alone.

Conversely, on any set where interchange *does* hold with a shared unit, the four
Eckmann–Hilton derivations
(`vcomp_eq_hcomp`, `vcomp_comm`, `vcomp_assoc`, `hcomp_comm`)
go through by pure rewriting, as verified by the Lean kernel.

## 3. Tie-in to the stated "recipe homotopy" conjecture

The mission's running examples are recovered qualitatively:

* **`S⁰` (chocolate-chip cookies, nuts / no-nuts).** A discrete two-point space
  is a commutative monoid under the trivial composition; the interchange law is
  automatic. Eckmann–Hilton predicts the two "ways to substitute" cannot produce
  a non-abelian structure — consistent with `π₀ = S⁰` carrying no higher
  operations.
* **Higher operations collapse.** The conjecture's claim that "combining methods
  in two ways" should give consistent flavor profiles is *exactly* the interchange
  law, and Eckmann–Hilton is the precise statement that the two combination
  operations then coincide and commute. This is why any loop-of-loops structure
  (`π₂` and above, or `π₁` of an H-space/topological monoid) is abelian.

## 4. OEIS / sequence search

No integer sequence is generated by the theorem (it is a structural identity, not
a counting problem), so no OEIS entry applies.

All Lean checks compile without `sorry` and the main theorem
`RecipeHomotopy.eckmann_hilton` uses no extra axioms beyond Lean's trusted core.

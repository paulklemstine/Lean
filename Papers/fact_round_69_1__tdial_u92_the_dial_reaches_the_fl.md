## 6. Counterexample hunt

* *Is the `3/4` capped floor tight?*  Yes: the table in §2 attains it at `K = 1`, and the
  formula gives `(3/4)/(1 − 4^{−b}) → 3/4` from above, so no smaller universal constant is
  provable and no larger one is true.
* *Does the erosion fit survive a sixth point?*  Not testable yet — bitlen 92 is the highest
  measurement, and the third seed of exp 538 was never run.  The crossing prediction at
  bitlen 97 is the falsifier.
* *Is the tie ceiling a possible explanation?*  Ruled out numerically: ceilings at bitlen 52
  and 92 differ by `< 10^{−15}` (they are `6/7 + Θ(4^{−b})`), against a measured drop of
  `0.1455` (`tie_mechanism_excluded_52_to_92`).

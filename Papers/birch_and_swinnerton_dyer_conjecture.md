# Computational evidence skipped

The formal results in this phase are symbolic implications from an explicitly assumed BSD
formula and positivity hypotheses. They do not assert a finite or experimentally testable
instance of the open Birch--Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture. Small numerical samples of elliptic
curves would illustrate the conjecture but would provide no evidence relevant to the universal
implications proved here. Computational evidence was therefore omitted so effort could remain
focused on the machine-checked theorem chain.

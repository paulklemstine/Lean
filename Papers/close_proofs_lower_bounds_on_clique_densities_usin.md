# Skip Evidence Justification

The results closed in this cycle are exact algebraic identities and equivalences
between two positional number systems (the general mixed-radix value/validity and
the factorial-system value/validity at base `b i = i + 1`), together with a
transported uniqueness theorem. These are universally quantified statements over
all digit functions and lengths, proved by structural rewriting along the
running-product identity `∏_{j<i}(j+1) = i!`. There is no numerical parameter to
sweep, no sequence to search, and no universal claim whose failure could be
witnessed by a finite counterexample: any single instance is a definitional
consequence of the identity, so small-case tabulation would restate rather than
test the claim. Computational evidence is therefore not informative here and is
omitted deliberately.

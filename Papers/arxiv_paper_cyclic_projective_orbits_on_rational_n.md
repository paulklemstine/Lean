# Computational evidence skipped

The formalized result is a general symbolic construction over an arbitrary field, not a numerical conjecture or an enumerative sequence. Its decisive calculation is the Vandermonde determinant identity, already used directly in the kernel-checked Lean proof. Small numerical samples would only instantiate that identity and would provide less assurance than the generic proof.

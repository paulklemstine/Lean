# Why a separate computational-evidence stage was skipped

The claims developed here are exact finite counting and elementary finite-topology
statements, not an experimentally discovered conjecture. Small cases reduce directly
to the identity `#(Fin L → Fin A) = A^L` (for example, binary words of lengths
0, 1, 2, and 3 number 1, 2, 4, and 8), and the incompressibility result is a
uniform pigeonhole bound for every decoder. Exhaustive testing would therefore add
no evidence beyond the general machine-checked proof.

The topological part also exposes a contradiction in the proposed framing rather
than a claim needing numerical investigation: a finite Hamming space is discrete,
so a nontrivial one cannot be connected. No OEIS sequence or plot is relevant.

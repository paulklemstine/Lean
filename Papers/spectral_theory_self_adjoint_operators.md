# Computational evidence justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the results proved here are structural theorems about arbitrary C⋆-algebras, Hilbert spaces, continuous functions, and finite-dimensional self-adjoint operators. Small numerical samples cannot meaningfully test the universal topological and algebraic claims. The relevant confidence comes instead from kernel-checked proofs against Mathlib's established spectral, functional-calculus, and Rayleigh-quotient foundations.

# Computational evidence — the median as a metric projection

All numbers below were produced by exact rational (`ℚ`) evaluation in Lean 4 (`#eval`), so
there is no floating-point error.  Each experiment is paired with the theorem it motivated;
the theorems themselves are proved in `Catalog/Logic/KneeMedianProjection.lean` and
`Catalog/Logic/KneeMedianProjectionBox.lean` and are the actual certificates.

Notation: `projQ a b x = max a (min x b)` (the clamp), `med3Q a b c` is the catalogue
median expression `max (min a b) (min (max a b) c)`.

## 1. Median = clamp

`med3Q x 1 3` versus `projQ 1 3 x` for `x = -2 … 6`:

| `x` | -2 | -1 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `med3Q x 1 3` | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| `projQ 1 3 x` | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| equal? | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ |

→ formalised as `med3L_eq_proj` / `proj_eq_med3L`.

## 2. Firmness slack of the clamp

`slack a b x y = (x-y)² − [(Px−Py)² + ((x−Px)−(y−Py))²]` with `a = 1, b = 3`, over the
`13 × 13` grid `x, y ∈ {−3, …, 9}`:

* minimum slack: **0** (never negative → firmness holds on the sample)
* maximum slack: **40**
* number of tight pairs (slack exactly `0`): **81 / 169**

The inequality is therefore *not* vacuous padding: it is attained on almost half the sample
(precisely when `x` and `y` lie on the same side of the interval, or both inside it).
→ formalised as `proj_firmly_nonexpansive`.

## 3. Counterexample hunt: is nonexpansiveness enough?

The map `R x = min |x| 1` is nonexpansive, has range `[0,1]`, and has fixed-point set
exactly `[0,1]` — the same data as `proj 0 1`.  Its firmness slack over
`x, y ∈ {−6, …, 6}` reaches **−14**, i.e. firmness fails badly.

→ this is the witness behind `firmness_necessary`: firmness, not merely the Lipschitz bound,
is what pins the median down.

## 4. Counterexample hunt in the plane

`P₁(u,v) = (u,0)`, `P₂(u,v) = ((u+v)/2, (u+v)/2)` (orthogonal projections onto the axis and
the diagonal), `T = P₁ ∘ P₂`.  Euclidean firmness slack:

* `slack₂((0,1), (0,0)) = −1/2` → firmness fails at an explicit pair;
* minimum over the grid `x ∈ {−3..3}², y = 0`: **−9/2**.

→ formalised as `firm₂_comp_fails` (with `firm₂_P₁`, `firm₂_P₂` showing both factors *are*
firmly nonexpansive).  Contrast `projBox_firmly_nonexpansive`: firmness itself does lift to
`ℝⁿ`; only closure under composition is one-dimensional.

## 5. The recorded NET-48 ensemble

Clean seeds `160, 256`; corrupted seed `x` swept over the grid `32k`:

| `x` | 0 | 32 | 64 | 96 | 128 | 160 | 192 | 224 | 256 | 288 | 320 | 352 | 384 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `med3Q x 160 256` | 160 | 160 | 160 | 160 | 160 | 160 | 192 | 224 | 256 | 256 | 256 | 256 | 256 |

The median tracks the corrupted seed with slope exactly `1` inside the bracket and slope `0`
outside — the graph of a clamp.  → `med3_cast_proj`, `med3_nonexpansive`, `med3_firm`.

Simultaneous perturbation: moving the two seeds `256` and `224` by up to `±48` each moves
the median by **at most 48**, and `48` is attained.  → `med3L_linf`,
`net48_median_grid_stability` (stated at one grid step, `32`).

## 6. Relaxed (averaged) median updates

`avg λ a b x = (1−λ)x + λ·projQ a b x` with `λ = 1/2`, `a = 1`, `b = 3`, `x = 11`.
Residual `avg^[n] x − proj a b x`:

| `n` | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| residual | 8 | 4 | 2 | 1 | 1/2 | 1/4 |

Exactly geometric with ratio `1 − λ = 1/2`, with no transient — this exactness (rather than
a mere bound) is what `averaged_iterate` proves, and `averaged_tendsto` turns into
convergence.

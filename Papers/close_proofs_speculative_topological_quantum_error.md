# Computational Evidence Skipped

The completed target is a structural equivalence between two definitions of positional notation, followed by transport of an existing uniqueness theorem. Its claims hold by symbolic rewriting of each place value and each digit bound; finite sampling would not provide meaningful additional evidence for these universal identities.

The imported factoradic and mixed-radix developments already include independently proved general uniqueness results. Accordingly, proof effort was focused on a kernel-checked formal bridge rather than ad hoc numerical testing.

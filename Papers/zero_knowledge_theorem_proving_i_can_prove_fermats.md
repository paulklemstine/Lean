# Computational Evidence Skipped

Computational evidence is unnecessary for the theorem proved here. The result is a symbolic identity valid for every finite additive commutative group, every homomorphism, and every valid witness. Its content is exact reindexing by an explicit group translation and exact extraction by subtraction, not a numerical pattern or conjectured finite sequence. Small-case enumeration would merely instantiate the already kernel-checked general equalities and would provide weaker evidence than the formal proof. There is therefore no relevant OEIS sequence, plot, or counterexample search beyond checking the hypotheses of a chosen finite example.

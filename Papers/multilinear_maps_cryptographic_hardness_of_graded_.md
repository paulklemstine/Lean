# Computational Evidence — Not Applicable

The formal claims in this contribution are parametric reduction theorems, not a
finite numerical conjecture or a proposed integer sequence. They state that
pointwise distribution preservation implies exact advantage preservation, and
that bounded statistical simulation error gives an additive security loss.
Small-case computation could instantiate these identities but would provide no
evidence beyond the general symbolic proofs. Consequently there is no relevant
OEIS search, numerical plot, or finite counterexample hunt; the Lean proofs cover
all finite transcript types and all deterministic Boolean distinguishers.

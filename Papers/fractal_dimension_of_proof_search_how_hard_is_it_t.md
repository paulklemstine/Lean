# Computational Evidence Skipped

The formal contribution concerns exact symbolic identities and order bounds in the catalog's existing uniform self-similar proof-search model. In that model the finite-depth estimator is proved equal to the dimension at every positive depth, and the dimension bound follows deductively from successful branching being bounded by total branching. Small-case Monte Carlo calculations would therefore duplicate exact theorems and could not test the separate empirical claim about 1,000 Mathlib theorems without first fixing a tactic grammar, search policy, timeout, sampling distribution, and proof-length metric. Those choices are intentionally stated as a concrete preregistered experiment in `FUTURE_DIRECTIONS.md` rather than presenting implementation-dependent numbers as evidence for an underspecified claim.

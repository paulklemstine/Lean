# Computational Evidence Skipped

Computational evidence is neither informative nor feasible for this mission because the claims are structural statements about arbitrary proof systems, semantic models, and provability predicates. There is no finite numerical sequence or bounded family whose first cases could support Gödel-style independence. The relevant failure modes are instead addressed by explicit formal countermodels in Lean:

* a consistent GL proof system in which the negation of the encoded consistency sentence is provable, showing that consistency alone does not imply independence; and
* a sound semantic setup containing a mathematically consistent theory with no physical realization, showing that mathematical consistency does not imply physical consistency.

These machine-checked witnesses are stronger and more directly relevant than numerical experiments.

# Computational Evidence Skipped

The new result is a symbolic classification over an arbitrary field, not a numerical conjecture. Its proof reduces equality of rational-map outputs to an exact factorization of the first-coordinate equation and cancellation of the nonzero ordinate multiplier away from the stated ramification locus. Small finite-field calculations would illustrate only special instances and would not strengthen the generic argument. The Lean kernel checks the field identities and every exceptional-case hypothesis directly, so computational sampling was unnecessary.

# Computational Evidence Skip Justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the selected connector theorem is a
symbolic identity over arbitrary rational coefficients and every possibly
noncommutative rational algebra, not a numerical or finite-instance claim.
Small-case calculations, sequence searches, and plots would not provide useful
evidence for this universal algebraic statement. The Lean development instead
proves both the Jacobian formula and the Weyl-commutator formula universally and
without unproved placeholders.

# Computational Evidence Skipped

Computational evidence is not informative for this deepening. The results concern arbitrary
categories, presheaves, sheaves, natural transformations, and universal properties, rather
than a finite sequence or a numerically testable conjecture. Small finite-category examples
would illustrate isolated instances but could neither test nor substantively challenge the
universe-polymorphic claims. The appropriate evidence is therefore kernel-checked Lean
proofs against Mathlib's existing categorical definitions.

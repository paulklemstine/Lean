# Computational evidence skipped

The proved results are structural equivalences and closure properties derived directly from the
definitions of completeness and strong completeness. They do not assert an uncertain numerical
pattern, asymptotic estimate, or finite universal claim for which sampled computations would
provide meaningful evidence. Small-case enumeration cannot validate quantification over all
finite deletions and all sufficiently large natural numbers. The effort was therefore directed
toward kernel-checked proofs instead.

# Computational Evidence Skipped

Computational evidence is unnecessary for the proved claim. The decisive obstruction is structural: the primes form a countable set, and every countable subset of an emetric space has zero Hausdorff measure in every positive dimension. No finite prime computation can alter or meaningfully test this conclusion. Moreover, box-counting a finite truncation measures a scale-dependent finite-sample statistic, not the Hausdorff dimension of the infinite countable metric space.

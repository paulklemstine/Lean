# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence is unnecessary for the decisive issue in the proposed construction. The containment direction follows symbolically from
\[
2^{n+1}\mathbb Z\subseteq 2^n\mathbb Z,
\]
and the constant value \(2^n\) uniformly witnesses strictness at every level. Likewise, divisibility of a fixed integer by every power of two is settled by the unboundedness of those powers, not by a finite sample. Small tables would illustrate these facts but would add no evidence toward the universal statements.

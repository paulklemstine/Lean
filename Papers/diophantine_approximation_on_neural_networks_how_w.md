# Computational Evidence Skip Justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the selected claim is proved uniformly and exactly for every natural-number depth. The floor inequalities establish

`0 < π - ⌊2^n π⌋ / 2^n < 2⁻ⁿ`

without numerical approximation, while induction proves exact agreement between the ReLU recurrence and the dyadic numerator. A finite decimal table would therefore be logically weaker than the formal theorem and would not test the main subtlety: the construction stores the binary digits of π nonuniformly in its biases. No OEIS sequence is relevant to that representational bridge.

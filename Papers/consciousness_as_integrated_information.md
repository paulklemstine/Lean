# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was not used because the central results are structural consequences of finite minima and maxima, not an empirical conjecture inferred from numerical cases. The composition theorem follows for every pair of finite loss landscapes from the order laws for addition; exclusion follows for every finite nonempty family from attainment of a maximum. Small-case calculations would illustrate definitions but would not materially test these universal order-theoretic claims.

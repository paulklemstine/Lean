# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the formalized claims are symbolic order inequalities over arbitrary finite types, arbitrary strictly positive joint laws, arbitrary code-length functions, and arbitrary sample sizes. There is no canonical integer sequence or finite conjectural search space to test. Small numerical examples would only instantiate assumptions already used directly by the proofs and could neither validate nor falsify the universal implication beyond what the kernel-checked Lean proofs establish.

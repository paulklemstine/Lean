# Why a separate computational-evidence stage was skipped

The central claims investigated here are structural modal-semantic statements, not a
numerical sequence or asymptotic conjecture. Small-case enumeration cannot establish
arithmetical completeness relative to PA. Instead, `Catalog/Novelty/TemporalProofLogic.lean` gives exact symbolic proofs for
the relevant frame principles and an explicit two-world witness for the temporal
asymmetry. This is stronger and more directly relevant than an unverified external
calculation. No OEIS sequence is naturally associated with the claims.

# Computational evidence justification

A separate computational-evidence stage was skipped because the finite cases used in this development are certified directly in Lean rather than inferred from external computation. In particular, `residue_triples_data` checks the three explicit cyclically stable triples on nine points, `exact_three_colors_3stable_9_3` proves the matching upper and lower coloring bounds, and `canonicalColor_without_threshold_counterexample` checks the concrete failed-coloring example. The general results are deductive packing and coloring theorems, so additional numerical sampling would not strengthen their verification.

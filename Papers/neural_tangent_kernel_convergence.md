# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence is unnecessary for the principal results because they concern an arbitrary normed vector space, an arbitrary frozen kernel operator satisfying an explicit contraction inequality, and all iteration counts. No finite sample of numerical matrices can strengthen or meaningfully test those universally quantified implications beyond the exact geometric estimate.

The spectral theorem is also established symbolically on every eigendirection, yielding the exact factor `(1 - ηλ)ⁿ` rather than an approximation. Numerical experiments would therefore duplicate a closed-form identity. Empirical investigation becomes relevant for the future finite-width and drifting-kernel conjectures, where architecture-specific discrepancy constants must be estimated.

# Computational Evidence — Skip Justification

Computational evidence was skipped because this mission formalizes established universal theorems rather than proposing a new finite conjecture. Small-instance checks cannot support the quantifier structure of Szemerédi regularity, triangle removal, or Roth's asymptotic theorem. The deliverables instead use the existing mathlib definitions and machine-check complete proofs of the exact statements. Turán's theorem is stated with its exact residue correction; this also avoids presenting the generally false claim that the smooth real-valued density expression is an exact integer equality for every `n`.

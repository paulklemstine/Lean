# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence is unnecessary for the central claims because they are structural rather than statistical or numerical. The exclusion of tangles follows uniformly from asymmetry and well-founded rank descent; checking finite samples would add no evidence beyond these general arguments. Universe lifting is governed by exact projection laws rather than an empirical sequence. The semantic obstruction is a diagonal argument that applies to every proposed self-representation at once, so finite enumeration cannot meaningfully approximate the universal claim.

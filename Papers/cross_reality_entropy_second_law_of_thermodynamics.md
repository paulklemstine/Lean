# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational sampling is unnecessary for the principal finite-ensemble results because their hypotheses reduce the claimed entropy change to an exact weighted-sum identity. The monotonicity and strictness conclusions then follow for arbitrary finite branch and state spaces from order properties of nonnegative weighted sums, rather than from a pattern inferred from small instances.

The deterministic specialization relies on a general finite data-processing inequality already established for every probability distribution and deterministic map. Numerical experiments would illustrate selected distributions but would neither test the full quantified claim nor expose additional boundary cases beyond those treated explicitly: zero branch weights, exact compensation, and the need for a strict surplus on positive support.

Computational evidence is unnecessary for this cycle because the central result is an exact equivalence for arbitrary smooth non-vanishing complex loops, proved by the fundamental theorem of calculus and an explicit logarithmic-lift construction. Finite sampling cannot test the differentiability, global non-vanishing, or one-turn closure conditions that carry the mathematical content. The accompanying boundary analysis instead identifies the decisive counterexample pattern: a non-vanishing envelope with nonzero winding does not possess a periodic logarithm and can change charge.

# Computational evidence justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the principal results are universal analytic identities for arbitrary smooth, nowhere-vanishing complex-valued loops. They do not reduce to a finite sequence or a representative numerical sample: the proof depends on the product rule for derivatives, nonvanishing denominators, linearity of the interval integral, and induction over powers. Small numerical tests would only instantiate these exact identities and would provide substantially weaker evidence than the kernel-checked proofs in `PowerLaw.lean`.

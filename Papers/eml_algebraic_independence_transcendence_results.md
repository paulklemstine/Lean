# Computational Evidence Skipped

Computational evidence is not suitable for the requested claims. Algebraic independence and transcendence assert the exact nonexistence of polynomial relations of unbounded degree and height, whereas finite-precision evaluations can test only finitely many bounded searches and cannot distinguish zero from a very small nonzero value. Moreover, the proposed `n = 2` assertion is an open transcendence problem, and the stated `n = 1` deduction from separate transcendence of the factors is not valid: two transcendental numbers may have an algebraic product (for example, a nonzero transcendental number and its reciprocal).

The formal file therefore proves unconditional positivity/nonvanishing, proves that algebraic independence of the two factors suffices for transcendence of their product, and states the open dependencies explicitly as theorem hypotheses. No unverified numerical claim is presented as evidence.

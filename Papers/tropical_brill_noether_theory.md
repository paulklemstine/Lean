# Computational Evidence: Why It Was Skipped

The principal result is a structural transfer theorem, not a finite or numerical
conjecture. It proves that two existence predicates are equivalent from explicit
specialization and lifting hypotheses, then transports the classical
Brill--Noether criterion across that equivalence. Small-case computation cannot
test those geometric hypotheses, so a numerical search would not provide
meaningful evidence for the connector.

The arithmetic component is independently kernel-checked in Lean: the file
proves both the dimension-count expansion of the Brill--Noether number and its
invariance under the Serre-duality substitution by polynomial normalization.

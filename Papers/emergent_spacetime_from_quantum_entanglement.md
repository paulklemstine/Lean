# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence is unnecessary for the selected claims. The model has exactly two qubits and one bulk edge, and every numerical assertion is proved symbolically in Lean: the four entries of each reduced density matrix, the Bell-state determinant and concurrence, and the one-edge cut reconstruction formula. There is no generated integer sequence for an OEIS search, no asymptotic numerical conjecture, and no larger sample whose testing would add assurance beyond the exhaustive finite symbolic calculation.

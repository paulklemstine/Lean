# Computational Evidence Skipped

Computational evidence is unnecessary for the formal results in this submission. The non-Archimedean corner-locus theorem is a structural consequence of the strict valuation inequality for finite sums, not a conjectural numerical pattern. The intersection theorem is an exact finite-sum invariance result under a multiplicity-preserving equivalence. Both claims are checked symbolically by Lean for arbitrary finite index types, valued division rings, and multiplicity functions, so sampling small cases would provide strictly weaker evidence and no additional guidance.

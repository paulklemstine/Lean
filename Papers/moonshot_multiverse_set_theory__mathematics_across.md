# Why Computational Evidence Is Not Applicable

The principal claims concern universal semantic consequences of an abstract forcing frame: closure of a class of universes, preservation of a background theory, existence of opposite forcing branches, and modal directedness. These properties are not numerical conjectures and have no canonical finite sequence, small-case table, or informative plot. Finite truth tables could illustrate a propositional shadow of CH branching, but they would provide no evidence for the quantified frame theorems and could obscure the explicit distinction between abstract semantics and constructions of models of set theory. Deductive analysis is therefore the appropriate evidence for this stage.

# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the requested results are structural identities rather than an unproved numerical conjecture. The partition-function theorem follows uniformly from finite distributivity, finite-dimensionality follows from the cardinality of a finite function type, and unitarity follows from reindexing a finite sum along a permutation. Small numerical samples would instantiate these already general proofs without testing an additional empirical claim.

# Computational evidence skipped

Computational sampling cannot substantiate the almost-sure conclusions proved here: finite trials neither establish divergence of an infinite series nor an event of measure one. The unique-factorization counterexample is already given exactly by the certified identities `441 = 9 * 49 = 21 * 21` together with complete proofs that `9`, `21`, and `49` are irreducible in the chosen monoid. Consequently, finite numerical evidence would add no reliability beyond the formal proofs.

# Computational Evidence Skipped

The central positive claim proved here is an analytic intermediate-value theorem, not a finite or numerical conjecture. Sampling a continuous function cannot verify the universal existence of an antipodal equality. The counterexample is also symbolic: three-voter binary majority is proved in Lean to be continuous on the discrete profile space, unanimous, and non-dictatorial. Consequently, ad hoc numerical calculations, sequence searches, and plots would add no reliable evidence beyond the kernel-checked proofs.

# Why computational evidence is skipped

The formal result concerns arbitrary finite dimension and arbitrary positive real side lengths, so a finite numerical sample cannot materially test the universal statement. More importantly, the connector is an exact symbolic identity: the volume of a coordinate section of an axis-aligned box is the product of all widths except the perpendicular one. The slicing bound then follows deductively from the unit-product hypothesis. There is no integer sequence to query in OEIS, and numerical plots would only illustrate—not test—the proof.

The Lean development therefore focuses directly on a kernel-checked symbolic proof. It explicitly presents the result as the axis-aligned-box special case of the slicing problem, not as a solution for arbitrary convex bodies.

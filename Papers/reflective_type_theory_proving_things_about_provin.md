# Computational Evidence Justification

Separate computational evidence is unnecessary for the principal results. The finite countermodel is completely enumerated inside the theorem statements and proofs: its three worlds and two accessibility edges are explicit, and both the positive witness and the missing transitive edge are checked there. The remaining results are structural inductions over syntax or universal logical implications, for which sampled calculations would provide weaker evidence than the exhaustive arguments. No numerical sequence, database object, or asymptotic dataset arises, so an OEIS search or plot would not be informative.

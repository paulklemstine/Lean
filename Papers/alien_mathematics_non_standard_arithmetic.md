# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the principal claims are structural quotient and transfer statements about an ultrafilter ultrapower. The cofinite-extending ultrafilter used by Mathlib is noncomputable, so finite sampling cannot evaluate membership in it or distinguish ultrapower equivalence classes. Small finite quotients would instead model principal ultrafilters and would not provide evidence for the non-Archimedean witness. The relevant certainty is supplied directly by kernel-checked Lean proofs.

# Why a separate computational-evidence stage was skipped

The central results are structural rather than conjectural numerical patterns. The configuration counts are closed forms (`n^n` and `2^(n^2)`), the energy identities follow symbolically from logarithm laws, and eventual periodicity follows from finiteness. Sampling cannot materially validate these universal claims.

The formal file nevertheless includes kernel-checked exact benchmark calculations at `n = 2` and `n = 4`. There is no nontrivial sequence requiring an OEIS search, and a counterexample hunt would be weaker than the general proofs. For these reasons, proof effort was prioritized over an additional empirical report.

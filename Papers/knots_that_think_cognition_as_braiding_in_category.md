# Computational Evidence

## Small-case calculations

Polynomials are listed in ascending-degree coefficient order and evaluated at a primitive cube root \(\omega\), where \(\omega^2+\omega+1=0\). Coordinates \((a,b)\) denote \(a+b\omega\).

| Suggested process | Polynomial | Value at \(\omega\) | Squared modulus |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Linear | \(1\) | \((1,0)\) | 1 |
| Creative | \(1+t-t^2\) | \((2,2)\) | 4 |
| Confused | \(1-t+t^2-t^3+t^4\) | \((-1,-1)\) | 1 |

Thus the logarithmic statistic is \(0\), \(\log 2\), and \(0\), respectively. The proposed statistic separates the creative example but assigns the same value to the linear and confused examples.

For the standard two-strand trefoil representative \(\sigma_1^3\), exponent sum is 3, while the identity braid has exponent sum 0. This certifies that the braid representatives differ. Balanced words such as \(\sigma_i\sigma_j^{-1}\) have exponent sum 0, exposing the limitation of this test.

## Sequence-database search

No integer sequence is generated by the three isolated examples, so an OEIS search would not provide a meaningful identification. A parameterized family of braid words is required before sequence comparison becomes informative.

## Counterexample hunt

The proposed ordering “linear, creative, confused” is not recovered by cube-root modulus: linear and confused tie. This is a direct counterexample to any claim that this statistic strictly ranks these three examples.

The implication “Jones polynomial 1 implies trivial knot” was not used. It is a separate knot-detection statement and cannot be inferred from the definition of the polynomial.

## Interpretation boundary

The calculations concern specified Laurent-polynomial signatures and Artin braids. They provide no evidence that neural firing is naturally an Artin braid, that subjective quality is topologically determined, or that the selected polynomials describe measured cognitive processes. Testing those claims requires a preregistered encoding from neural time series to braid data and independent behavioral ratings.

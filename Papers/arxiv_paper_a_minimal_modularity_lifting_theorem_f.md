# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational sampling is not informative for the results developed here. The central input is an isomorphism between universal deformation and Hecke algebras, and the conclusions concern functorial transport of algebra maps, module freeness, and specialization at arbitrary maximal ideals. These are structural statements over general commutative rings rather than finite numerical conjectures with meaningful small cases. Their relevant evidence consists of exact algebraic deductions and explicit boundary analysis: uniqueness persists under a surjective presentation, while it can fail when surjectivity is removed. Numerical tables, sequence searches, and plots would therefore neither test the hypotheses nor probe the asserted conclusions.

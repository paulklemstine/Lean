# Computational evidence for the pointed classification of maps of 1-types

The theorems of this cycle concern the comparison map

```
[(K(G,1),*), (K(H,1),*)]_*  ⟶  [K(G,1), K(H,1)]
```

from *pointed* to *unpointed* homotopy classes of maps of homotopy 1-types (groupoids).
Conjecture **N3** of the previous cycle predicts that the fibres of this map have size
`[H : C_H(φ(G))]`, the index of the centraliser of the image of the induced homomorphism.

## 1. Small-case calculation: `G = H = S₃`

Brute-force enumeration of all functions `S₃ → S₃` satisfying `f(ab) = f(a)f(b)`
(6⁶ = 46656 candidates), then computation of the orbits of the conjugation action
`φ ↦ uφu⁻¹` and of the centraliser indices of the images:

| quantity                                   | value        |
|--------------------------------------------|--------------|
| `#Hom(S₃,S₃)`                               | `10`         |
| conjugation orbit sizes                     | `1, 3, 6`    |
| centraliser indices `[S₃ : C(im φ)]`        | `1, 3, 6`    |
| `1 + 3 + 6`                                 | `10`         |

The three orbits are: the trivial homomorphism (image `1`, centraliser `S₃`, index `1`);
the three homomorphisms with image of order two (centraliser the order-two subgroup
generated by the image, index `3`); and the six automorphisms, all inner (centraliser the
centre `Z(S₃) = 1`, index `6`).

This matches the orbit-stabiliser prediction exactly, and it is this table which the Lean
theorems `card_ptd_fibre_trivial_perm` (`= 1`), `card_ptd_fibre_signSwap_perm` (`= 3`) and
`card_ptd_fibre_id_perm` (`= 6`) reproduce as statements about pointed homotopy classes of
maps of the 1-type `K(S₃,1) = SingleObj (Equiv.Perm (Fin 3))`.

**What is machine-verified.**  The three fibre sizes `1, 3, 6` above, the triviality of
`Z(S₃)`, the order-two centraliser of a transposition, and the general counting theorem
`card_ptd_fibre` are proved in Lean without `sorry` (the finite checks are discharged by
`decide`, i.e. by kernel evaluation, not by `native_decide`).  The count
`#Hom(S₃,S₃) = 10` and the decomposition `10 = 1 + 3 + 6` come from the enumeration above
and are **not** formalised; they are reported here as motivation only.

## 2. Abelian sanity check

For abelian `H` conjugation is trivial, so every centraliser index is `1` and the pointed
and unpointed classifications must agree.  For `G = ℤ/m`, `H = ℤ/n` one has
`#Hom(ℤ/m, ℤ/n) = gcd(m,n)` classes in both pictures; e.g. `(m,n) = (4,6)` gives `2`.  The
structural statement behind these numbers (injectivity of forgetting the basepoint whenever
the target vertex group is commutative) is formalised as
`forgetBase_injective_of_commutative` and `forgetBase_injective_cyclic`.

## 3. Counterexample hunt

We searched for a possible failure of the *pointed* classification to be a bijection by
looking for pointed maps with equal induced homomorphism but no pointed homotopy between
them.  None exists, and the reason is structural: the obstruction would be a homotopy whose
value at the basepoint cannot be prescribed, and the technical lemma `intertwinerNatIso`
shows that any intertwining isomorphism at the basepoint *is* the value at the basepoint of
a genuine homotopy.  Similarly, the pointed composition law admits no conjugation ambiguity,
which is why `ptdHom_comp` is an equality rather than a conjugacy.

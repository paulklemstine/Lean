# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational sampling is unnecessary for the selected results. The finite-family identity is derived uniformly from the binary strong-divisibility law by induction on finite sets, including the empty family and index zero. The exact apparition theorem is likewise structural: primality forces every divisor of the selected index to be either one or the full index, while primitivity excludes the former case. Consequently, no bounded calculation could test the essential universal steps more effectively than the proofs themselves.

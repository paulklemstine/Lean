# Why computational evidence was skipped

The selected main result is a structural theorem valid for an arbitrary planar ternary ring: its projective completion satisfies the two unique-incidence axioms.  The hypotheses themselves provide unique solutions, and the proof proceeds by exhaustive constructor analysis.  Thus small-case computation would test particular models but would not provide evidence for the universal implication proved here.

Likewise, the point and line counts are symbolic cardinality calculations for every finite coordinate type.  There is no numerical sequence requiring an OEIS search.  Concrete table enumeration is best deferred until a particular Hall quasifield is supplied together with proofs of all planar ternary-ring axioms.

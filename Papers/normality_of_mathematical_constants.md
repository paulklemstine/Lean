# Computational Evidence Stage — Justification for Skipping

The deliverable proves a structural implication: interval equidistribution of the orbit
`fract (b^n x)` implies the prescribed limiting frequency of every finite base-`b`
digit block. This is an exact theorem obtained by identifying each block with one of
the equal half-open subintervals of `[0,1)`; finite numerical samples cannot test the
asymptotic hypothesis or strengthen the proof.

The requested examples `π`, `e`, and `√2` are not presently known to be normal in any
integer base, so numerical digit tables would be evidence only, not a proof. To avoid
presenting experimental data as progress on an open problem, the project instead
formalizes the rigorous normality–equidistribution bridge.

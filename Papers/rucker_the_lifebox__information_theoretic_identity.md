# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence is not the appropriate primary method for these results. The finite-state theorem is uniform over arbitrary finite state, input, and output types and is proved by a bisimulation characterization, while no-cloning is an algebraic impossibility theorem over every field. Small numerical samples cannot substantively test either universal claim.

The quantitative statement is symbolic rather than empirical: fixed-length bit strings are counted exactly, and the proposed `10^15` complexity estimate is formalized conditionally on the existence of an encoding of at most that length. No empirical claim that real human identity satisfies this hypothesis is made.

# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the formal results concern structural identities valid at every level: the exact cardinality of binary-word stages, preservation of graph edges by prefix truncation, injectivity of the map from streams to compatible prefixes, and infinitude of the inverse limit. These claims are proved symbolically in Lean rather than inferred from finite samples. No numerical claim about the Lorenz attractor or its Čech cohomology is made.

Computational evidence is unnecessary for the principal classification result because it concerns continuity and homeomorphism of infinite product subspaces, properties that finite samples cannot substantively test. The two concrete examples in `Catalog/Applications/RayEndSpaces/Classification.lean` instead check the important boundary cases: existence of a constant binary ray and membership at an exact 2-adic threshold. The valuation result is derived from a universal strong-triangle law rather than extrapolated from numerical data.

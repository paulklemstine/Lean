# Computational evidence justification

The computational-evidence stage is skipped because the requested endpoint is an asymptotic theorem about infinitely many primes, together with an analytic level-of-distribution estimate. Finite prime tables cannot verify either universal/asymptotic claim and would not contribute to the kernel-checked proofs in this submission.

The finite-dimensional optimization and finite arithmetic-progression identities are proved symbolically in Lean. The unconditional analytic Maynard–Tao input is not represented as computational evidence or claimed on the basis of finite experiments.

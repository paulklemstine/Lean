# Computational evidence stage: justification for omission

The formalized results are implication theorems and finite-preorder/max-envelope arguments, not new universal numerical claims about a computable sequence. Their hypotheses explicitly assume the still-open order-dimension inequalities from the paper, and the conclusions follow symbolically from those hypotheses. Small-case enumeration therefore cannot provide evidence for the implication steps beyond the complete Lean proofs themselves.

A meaningful counterexample hunt for the paper's open conjectures would require implementing certified enumeration of finite posets and certified computation of Dushnik–Miller dimension. That infrastructure is not present in the catalog and would dominate this focused formalization. No computational claim or unchecked numerical result is used in the Lean development.

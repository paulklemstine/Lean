# Why computational evidence is not used

The investigated claim is purely structural and quantifies over arbitrary propositions and implication proofs. Small numerical cases, sequence searches, plots, and sampling cannot test the soundness of such a rule. Instead, the Lean development gives a symbolic characterization theorem and an explicit propositional counterexample (`P = False`, verified consequence `Q = True`). This fully resolves the unrestricted logical claim rather than supplying empirical evidence for it.

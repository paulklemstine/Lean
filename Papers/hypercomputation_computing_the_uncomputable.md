# Computational Evidence Justification

The central claims are structural rather than numerical. The diagonal predicate differs from each program at its own index, exact loading is an injection from an infinite function space, and every finite transcript leaves an unqueried bit available for alteration. Small-case enumeration would illustrate finite shadows of these arguments but could neither support nor refute their universal content. Accordingly, the investigation prioritizes exact proofs and records the finite-transcript construction as the relevant counterexample mechanism.

# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the proved claims are structural identities and strict inequalities in the complete lattice of topologies, not a numerical conjecture or sequence. The decisive example uses the lower-limit, upper-limit, and Euclidean topologies on the infinite carrier `ℝ`; finite sampling cannot verify equality or inequality of these topologies and could misleadingly erase the half-open-interval obstruction.

Instead, `Catalog/Physics/ObserverDependentReality.lean` supplies kernel-checked symbolic proofs, including the explicit witness `[0,1)` that is open in the one-channel observable topology and not open in the two-channel Euclidean consensus. Thus the relevant counterexample hunt is handled exactly rather than approximately.

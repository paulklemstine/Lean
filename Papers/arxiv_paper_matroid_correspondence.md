# Computational Evidence Justification

The results developed here are structural statements about arbitrary preorders, relations, lower sets, and matroid minor classes. They have no finite numerical parameter whose initial values would meaningfully support or refute the universal claims. The decisive tests are symbolic: composition must preserve the extension law, universal pullback must preserve lower closure, and the resulting matroid class must satisfy the hypotheses of the finite excluded-minor theorem. Small-case tables or sequence searches would therefore add no relevant evidence.

# Computational evidence skipped

The selected theorem is an exact algebraic identity for an arbitrary real ordinate `t`, followed by order-theoretic consequences for arbitrary finite sets. Symbolic proof is both stronger and more appropriate than sampled floating-point calculations.

The originally proposed assertions concern exact equality, rationality, and transcendence of sums involving Riemann-zeta zeros. Numerical approximations cannot certify any of those properties. In particular, testing decimal approximations would risk presenting suggestive data as evidence for an arithmetic statement it cannot establish. The formal development therefore focuses on exact finite identities and states the small-cutoff diagnosis conditionally on certified window emptiness.

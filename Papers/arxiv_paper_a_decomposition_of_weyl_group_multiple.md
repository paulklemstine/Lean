# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was not used for the principal results because they are structural theorems about arbitrary finite group actions, not numerical conjectures. Small tables would only instantiate the orbit-partition identities already proved in full generality and would provide no meaningful evidence for the analytic statements of the paper, whose decisive issues are convergence and continuation rather than finite arithmetic. A concrete regular-action example and declaration checks are included with the results instead.

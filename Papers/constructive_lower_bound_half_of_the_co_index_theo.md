# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was not used because the principal theorem is an exact structural induction over lists of arbitrary finite free ℤ₂-systems, not a numerical conjecture inferred from small instances. Its two recurrences—cardinality under disjoint union and co-index under binary join—are already established symbolically for all finite factors. Exhaustive finite sampling would therefore test only special encodings while adding no evidence about the unbounded list parameter. The development instead includes two independent exact checks: the recursively derived many-body co-index formula and the vertex-cardinality identity `2(coind + 1) = |V|` for the resulting composite.

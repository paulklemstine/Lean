# Computational Evidence Skipped

The main results are structural rather than conjectural numerical claims: monotonicity of consequence, semantic independence from explicit finite models, invariance of primality under multiplicative equivalence, infinitude of ordinary primes, and a complete symbolic classification of tropical irreducibles. Small-case computation would not add evidence to the abstract results. The only finite geometry checks are discharged inside Lean from the explicit incidence definitions, so a separate external table would duplicate machine-checked proofs.

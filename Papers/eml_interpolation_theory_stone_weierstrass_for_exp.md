# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the proved claims are structural rather than conjectural numerical patterns. Point separation follows from injectivity of `exp` and `log` on positive inputs; density is a direct Stone–Weierstrass consequence; Cartesian mesh size is the exact identity `m^d`; and the proposed test function `x²` is represented exactly by multiplication, with error identically zero. Small floating-point experiments would therefore be weaker than the kernel-checked symbolic results and would not test the missing general EML circuit-compilation theorem.

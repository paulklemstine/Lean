# Why a computational evidence stage was skipped

The principal claims are structural rather than finite or experimental:

- periodicity follows exactly from quotienting each spatial coordinate by the integer lattice;
- the covering kernel is characterized symbolically as `ℤ³`;
- the causal counterexample follows from the unquotiented real time coordinate and nonnegativity of squared spatial velocity;
- the compact-time example is an exact symbolic witness.

Small-case enumeration would not add meaningful evidence to these universally quantified algebraic statements. The requested hyperbolic-volume exploration is also not suitable for a quick trustworthy computation: certified Weeks-manifold volume and minimality require specialized hyperbolic geometry, triangulation, and interval-arithmetic infrastructure absent from this project. Accordingly, effort was focused on machine-checked exact proofs and on clearly separating the unproved hyperbolic-geometric direction in `FUTURE_DIRECTIONS.md`.

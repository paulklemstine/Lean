# Computational evidence

Computational experimentation was skipped because the formalized claims are structural theorems for arbitrary additive and linear maps, not a numerical conjecture. Their conclusions are proved uniformly in Lean: redundancy is exactly a kernel relation, quotient and transversal parametrizations are bijective with the code, and the finite linear cardinality is the field cardinality raised to the image rank. Small finite examples would instantiate, but would not materially test, these general identities.

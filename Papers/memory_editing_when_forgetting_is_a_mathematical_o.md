# Why a separate computational-evidence stage was skipped

The central claims are structural rather than conjectural numerical patterns:

- lossiness follows from an infinite-domain/finite-codomain argument;
- closure of fully erased streams follows from the homomorphism laws;
- the observable memory algebra is identified by the monoid first isomorphism theorem;
- targeted forgetting has the standard quotient universal property.

Small-case enumeration could illustrate collisions for one chosen finite monoid and one chosen memory map, but it would provide no meaningful evidence for these fully general results and would risk obscuring the algebraic mechanism. The Lean development checks the general statements directly. No OEIS sequence or informative plot naturally arises from the theorem proved here.

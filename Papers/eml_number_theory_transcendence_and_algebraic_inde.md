# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence is not informative for the central claims here. Transcendence and algebraic independence quantify over all rational polynomials, so finite-precision approximations cannot certify them and polynomial searches cannot rule out an unseen relation. Likewise, the EML/EL class equality is a semantic statement over all finite expressions rather than a finite sequence with small cases or an applicable OEIS entry.

Accordingly, no numerical experiment is presented as evidence. The contribution instead records the conjectural assumptions explicitly and proves their stated Lean consequences without computational shortcuts.

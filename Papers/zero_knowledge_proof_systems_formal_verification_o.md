# Computational evidence justification

Computational evidence was skipped because the selected results are exact structural identities rather than conjectures inferred from numerical data. The key claims are proved symbolically for every finite edge set and every proper or improper commitment:

- acceptance and rejection challenges partition the edge set;
- a proper colouring is accepted with probability exactly one;
- an improper colouring has at least one rejecting edge, yielding the exact one-round and repeated bounds;
- the real transcript PMF is exactly equal to the simulator PMF, so every deterministic observation has zero distinguishing advantage.

Small-case enumeration would only instantiate these universally quantified, kernel-checked proofs and would provide no additional evidence needed to choose or validate the theorem statements.

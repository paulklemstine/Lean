# Computational evidence justification

The computational-evidence stage was skipped because the completed extension proves
analytic identities and inequalities uniformly for arbitrary natural indices and
arbitrary positive real rates. The claims are not conjectures inferred from finite
samples: their proofs reduce directly to strict logarithmic inequalities, a proved
Padé-type lower bound for `log (1+x)`, and exact field identities. Small numerical
samples would therefore neither guide nor strengthen the machine-checked results.

# Why computational evidence was skipped

The connector theorem is structural rather than numerical. Its quantified data include arbitrary cycle types, arbitrary dependent outcome families, arbitrary revision functions, and arbitrary discrete topologies. Consequently there is no canonical sequence of numerical instances to search in OEIS or representative finite sample whose successful tests would materially support the universal claim.

The proof instead reduces convergence to the exact ascending-chain condition for bounded monotone sequences of natural-number quality ranks, then lifts eventual rank constancy through the reflective plateau condition to eventual constancy of the dependent trajectory. Small-case computation would only illustrate user-chosen examples and would not test the general theorem.

# Why computational evidence was skipped

Computational evidence is unnecessary for this development. The claims are
structural identities and inequalities for arbitrary finite index types, not a
sequence or a conjecture inferred from small cases. Their substantive inputs are
exact algebraic normalization and the proved analytic inequality
`log x ≤ x - 1` for positive real `x`. Numerical sampling could illustrate these
facts but would neither test boundary cases involving arbitrary tangent vectors
nor strengthen the universal, kernel-checked proofs. There is consequently no
relevant OEIS sequence or informative plot.

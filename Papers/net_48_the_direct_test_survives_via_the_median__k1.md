# Computational evidence (NET-48 order-statistics round)

All computations below were run inside the project's Lean environment with `#eval`, on the
definitions actually used in the proofs (`KneeMedian.med3`, `KneeQuota.fourSeed_upper_median`'s
closed form, the extrapolation laws, the amplification polynomial).  Every claim that is
*proved* in the `.lean` files is marked ✅; the rest is exploratory and marked accordingly.

## 1. The two measured knee samples and their medians

| context | knee sample (seeds 1,2,3) | `med3` | product point `d·ctx/32` | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ctx = 1024` | `{128, 112, 96}` | `112` | `128` | `7/8` |
| `ctx = 2048` | `{256, 224, 160}` | `224` | `256` | `7/8` |

`#eval (med3 256 224 160, med3 128 112 96)` → `(224, 112)`.  ✅ proved:
`KneeMedian.net48_median_law`, `KneeMedian.net44_median_law`.

Per-seed ratios: `(5/8, 7/8, 1)` at `16×` and `(3/4, 7/8, 1)` at `8×`
(`#eval ((160:ℚ)/256, (224:ℚ)/256, (256:ℚ)/256, (96:ℚ)/128, (112:ℚ)/128, (128:ℚ)/128)`).
The upper edge and the centre repeat; the low tail does not.

## 2. Counterexample hunt: which third-seed values would have broken the median law?

`#eval` over the whole grid `{32, 64, …, 1280}`, keeping those `x` with `med3 x 224 256 = 224`:

```
[32, 64, 96, 128, 160, 192, 224]
```

So every grid value `≤ 224` confirms the law and every value `> 224` refutes it: the law's
protected set is an interval, not a point.  ✅ proved in the exact form
`KneeMedian.med3_stable_iff` / `net48_median_stability_family`.  The measured `160` is
inside it, while all four pre-registered point predictions `{192, 224, 240, 256}` failed as
*knee* predictions — the four combinations of (horn hit) × (median hit) are all realisable
(✅ `KneeMedian.point_center_independent`).

## 4. Low-tail extrapolation: ratio family versus affine family

`#eval` of `(5/64·ctx, ctx/16 + 32)`:

| `ctx` | 1024 | 2048 | 4096 | 8192 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| constant-ratio `5/8` | 80 | 160 | 320 | 640 |
| affine fit | 96 | 160 | 288 | 544 |
| gap | −16 | 0 | **+32** | +96 |

The families agree only at `2048`; the ratio family already mis-retrodicts `1024`
(80 vs the measured 96).  ✅ proved: `KneeQuota.lowtail_extrapolation_gap`,
`lowtail_prediction_32x`, `lowtail_ratio_fails_backwards`.

## 5. Amplification polynomial `f(p) = 3p² − 2p³`

`#eval` at sample points:

| `p` | 0.10 | 0.25 | 0.50 | 0.60 | 0.75 | 0.90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `f(p)` | 0.028 | 0.156 | 0.500 | 0.648 | 0.844 | 0.972 |

`f` is below the diagonal for `p < 1/2`, fixes `1/2`, and is above it for `p > 1/2`;
the only fixed points are `0, 1/2, 1`.  ✅ proved: `KneeAmplify.median_amplifies`,
`median_attenuates`, `median_calibrated`, `median_fixed_points`,
`median_deriv_at_half` (slope `3/2` at the calibrated point).

## 6. OEIS

No integer sequence is generated by this round: the objects are two three-element knee
samples and a fixed polynomial ladder `p³, 3p²−2p³, 3p−3p²+p³`, so an OEIS lookup is not
informative here.

## 7. Not verified

The interpretation that a per-seed probability of `2/3` is the right calibration for
"knee ≤ 7/8·product" is a reading of the six recorded seeds (4 of 6 at or below the `7/8`
point), not an estimate with error bars; only the arithmetic consequence
(`2/3 ↦ 20/27`) is proved (`KneeAmplify.net48_amplification_example`).

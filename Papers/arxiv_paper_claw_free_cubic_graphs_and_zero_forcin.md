# Computational evidence justification

Computational evidence was not used because the proved results are structural consequences of the definitions rather than an experimentally inferred universal claim. The zero-forcing bounds are witnessed by explicit colored sets, and the triangle theorem follows directly from cubic degree and claw-freeness. Exhaustive graph enumeration would therefore add little evidence for these particular claims. The sharper paper-level bounds and equality classification remain future work and are not reported as computationally verified.

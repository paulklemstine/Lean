# Computational Evidence

## Small-case calculations

For the logistic map `f_r(x) = r x (1-x)`, the fixed-point equation factors as

`x (r - 1 - r x) = 0`.

Thus, for nonzero `r`, the fixed states are `0` and `(r-1)/r`. At the proposed parameter `r = 3.83 = 383/100`, these are exactly

| quantity | exact value |
|---|---:|
| nonzero fixed state | `283/383` |
| image of the critical state `1/2` | `383/400` |

Both identities are established as exact rational equalities in `Catalog/Applications/Consciousness/DejaVuFixedPoints.lean`.

## Counterexample hunt

The universal claim “every continuous interval self-map has dense periodic points” fails for the continuous contraction `f(x)=x/2`. Every positive return equation forces `x=0`, so its periodic set is the singleton `{0}`, which is not dense. This counterexample is proved in `Catalog/Tropical/DejaVuDynamics/PeriodicPoints.lean`.

The proposed identification of approximately 70% lifetime incidence with a natural density of periodic points is not numerically testable without a probability measure, an observation tolerance, a sampling distribution, and a time horizon. No percentage comparison is therefore reported.

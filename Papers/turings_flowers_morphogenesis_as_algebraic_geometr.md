The present cycle studies exact symbolic implications of explicitly defined polynomial interaction laws rather than fitting a numerical Gray–Scott simulation. Small-case numerical sampling would neither establish polynomial degree nor distinguish exact algebraicity from approximation. The relevant counterexample hunt was therefore carried out structurally: three affine modes with all pairwise interactions have degree at most two, directly contradicting any rule that infers sextic degree from three modes alone. Numerical PDE evidence is deferred until a discretization, boundary conditions, background threshold, and quantitative fitting criterion are fixed.

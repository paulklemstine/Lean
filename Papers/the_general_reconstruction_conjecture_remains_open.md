# Skip Evidence Justification

Computational evidence is unnecessary for this cycle because the selected target is a structural compatibility theorem, not a numerical or finite-case conjecture. Vertex deletion and complementation commute definitionally at the adjacency-relation level, and graph isomorphisms transport through complement by the same underlying vertex equivalence. The Lean proofs verify these identities uniformly for arbitrary vertex types, so enumerating small graphs would add no evidence beyond the general proof.

# Why computational evidence was skipped

The formal result is structural rather than numerical: every ultrafilter on a finite electorate is principal. Its proof follows directly from the finite-ultrafilter theorem, and the anonymity contradiction is witnessed symbolically by swapping two voters. Exhaustive small-election tables would only instantiate a theorem already proved for every finite voter type and would not test the unformalized curvature conjecture, because that conjecture does not yet specify a statistical model, metric, smooth aggregation map, or computable curvature quantity.

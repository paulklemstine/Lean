# Computational Evidence Justification

Numerical experimentation is not the appropriate evidentiary mode for the central claims. The objects under study are coherence cells, quotient composition, and structural reassociation, rather than a numerical sequence or asymptotic statistic. The finite composition table is completely analyzed symbolically: at the triple `(a,a,a)`, the left-associated composite is `e` while the right-associated composite is `a`. The general results are structural inductions and quotient arguments, for which sampled calculations would provide weaker evidence than the exact derivations.

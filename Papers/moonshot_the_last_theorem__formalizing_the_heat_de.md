# Why computational evidence was skipped

The principal claims are structural rather than experimental: an infinite set minus a finite set is infinite, a fixed finite numerator divided by an unbounded denominator tends to zero, and substituting a radius linear in mass into an area-quadratic entropy formula gives an `M²` law. Small-case computation cannot provide meaningful evidence for countability, infinitude, or a limit theorem, and numerical evaluation of the black-hole formula would merely test arithmetic after choosing arbitrary constants. The Lean development instead proves these claims symbolically for every finite budget and every real mass parameter covered by the hypotheses.

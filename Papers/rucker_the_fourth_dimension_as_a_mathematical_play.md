Computational evidence was not used because the principal claims are exact symbolic identities and structural equivalences rather than asymptotic or finite combinatorial conjectures. The Hopf and rotation statements reduce to polynomial identities in complex coordinates, the tesseract bounds exhaust the two possible signs in each coordinate, and the four-ball volume is an exact specialization of the general even-dimensional volume formula. Numerical sampling would therefore add approximation error without testing any step not already covered by the exact arguments.

# Why computational evidence was skipped

The claims in this mission are structural equivalences and universal properties for arbitrary
categories, functors, sheaves, and frames. They do not generate a numerical sequence, and finite
sampling cannot establish full faithfulness, representability, or an adjunction. Consequently,
small-case calculations, OEIS searches, and plots would not provide relevant evidence.

Instead, the deliverables use the existing Mathlib definitions of Yoneda embeddings, additive
functors, Grothendieck topologies, sheaves, subobject classifiers, representable presheaves, and
frames, and prove the requested bridge statements directly in Lean.

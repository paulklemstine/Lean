# Why a separate computational-evidence stage was skipped

The main results are symbolic identities and implications over arbitrary rational-valued profiles, proved directly in Lean. Small-case sampling cannot validate the universal comparison or convergence theorems. The only numerical claims are the two declared illustrative ZFC profiles; their fitness ordering and exact gap are evaluated and kernel-checked inside `Catalog/MachineLearning/EvolvingMathematics.lean` using rational normalization. The file also contains a formally checked counterexample showing that equal scalar fitness does not force equal profiles. Thus a separate unchecked table, plot, or external sequence search would add no evidential value, and no naturally arising integer sequence calls for an OEIS search.

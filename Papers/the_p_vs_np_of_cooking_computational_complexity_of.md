# Why a separate computational-evidence stage was skipped

The main result is an exact algebraic identity valid for every nonempty finite family with positive verification times, followed by order-theoretic consequences. Finite sampling cannot strengthen evidence for this universal identity, and the Lean file already includes a fully checked three-dish numerical instance. No integer sequence is generated, so an OEIS search is inapplicable. The hypotheses are also sharp in transparent ways: zero verification times make ratios degenerate, and dropping `verify ≤ cook` permits slow and fast dishes to cancel in the aggregate boundary statement. For these reasons, proof and hypothesis analysis are more informative than a numerical counterexample hunt.

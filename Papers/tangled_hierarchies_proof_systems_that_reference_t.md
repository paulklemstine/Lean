# Computational Evidence Justification

Computational evidence was not used because the principal results are general lattice-theoretic fixed-point theorems over arbitrary Kripke frames, not numerical conjectures inferred from finite cases. Exhaustive checks on small frames could illustrate the definitions but would not materially support the universal coinductive arguments. The Lean proofs directly construct the greatest post-fixed predicates and establish their fixed-point and maximality properties without finiteness assumptions.

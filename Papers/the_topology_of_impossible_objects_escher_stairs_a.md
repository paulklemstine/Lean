Computational evidence is unnecessary for the principal results because they are structural identities over arbitrary additive commutative groups and arbitrary nonzero torus sizes, not numerical conjectures inferred from small cases. The concrete three-by-three Waterfall and checker-gauge instantiation serves as the required finite example. Its impossibility follows from an exact period calculation, while the general results follow from telescoping sums, cancellation of mixed differences, and the established torus integration criterion. No numerical sequence arises, so an OEIS comparison would not be relevant.

Theory: RS(n, n^g) = n^(1-o(1)) for fixed g<1/2, so log(RS)/log n -> 1.
Feasibility then needs 2g >= ~1, i.e. gamma -> 1/2 -- the SAME barrier
as the round-96b birthday obstruction, recovered independently.

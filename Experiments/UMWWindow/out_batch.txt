RESULT: a batch of small-pool semiprimes factors at O(B) per instance,
  provably below L[1/3] PER INSTANCE. Rigorous (Bernstein factoring-
  into-coprimes is essentially linear in total input). This is a real
  per-instance improvement, but ONLY for the key-reuse distribution.

MODEL 2 (batch gcd, shared primes): linear in total input via product/
  remainder trees -- rigorous, but distribution-specific (shared primes).
CONTRAST (Batch NFS): per-key TIME L^{0.522} is WORSE than single-key
  L^{1/3}; only the area-time PRODUCT improves. Not a per-instance time win.

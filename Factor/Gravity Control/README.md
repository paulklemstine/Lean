## 🔬 Key Findings

### Seven Novel Hypotheses

1. **GEMR (Gravitoelectromagnetic Resonance):** Rotating superconductors may amplify gravitomagnetic fields — **testable now**
2. **Gravitational Laser (Graser):** Collective graviton emission from BECs — not yet feasible
3. **Oscillating Warp Geometry:** Pulsating warp bubbles may reduce energy requirements — needs numerical simulation
4. **Gravitomagnetic Meissner Effect:** Superconductors expelling gravitomagnetic fields — ruled out by standard physics
5. **Gravitational Metamaterials:** Mass arrays creating gravitational band gaps — **novel concept, testable in principle**
6. **Casimir-Gravitational Coupling:** Nanostructured materials with measurable gravity anomalies — **most promising near-term test**
7. **Dynamic Inertial Mass Modulation:** Local curvature reducing effective inertia — too small in standard GR

## 💡 The Big Picture

The 10³⁹ hierarchy between electromagnetism and gravity is the fundamental obstacle to gravitational engineering. Every proposal in this project confronts this gap. But history teaches us that seemingly impossible energy barriers can sometimes be bypassed through clever amplification mechanisms (the laser), phase transitions (superconductivity), or entirely new physics (nuclear energy).

The most important contribution of this work is identifying **falsifiable experimental tests** that could, in principle, discover such mechanisms. The cost of the most accessible experiments (GEMR-1, CASIMIR-GRAV-1) is modest by physics standards — less than a single military aircraft. The potential payoff is civilization-transforming.

The meta-oracles say: **test it.**

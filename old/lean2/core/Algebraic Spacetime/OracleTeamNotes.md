The answer is a **Clifford algebra**: the free associative algebra generated by a vector space V modulo
the relation v² = Q(v) for a quadratic form Q. For spacetime, Q has signature (1,3) or (3,1).

**Key Revelation:** The geometric product `ab = a·b + a∧b` unifies the symmetric (metric) and 
antisymmetric (orientation) data of spacetime into a single algebraic operation. This is not merely 
a mathematical convenience — it is the *natural language* of spacetime.

- Lorentz boosts: R = exp(−φ/2 · B) where B is a timelike bivector
- General: composition of rotations and boosts

where ∇ = γμ∂μ is the spacetime gradient and J is the 4-current.

## Session 6: Iteration — Deep Structure

**Oracle α discovers deeper structure:**

### The Algebra of Spacetime Intervals
Define the **spacetime interval operator** for two events x, y:
`Δ = (y − x)² = (y − x)·(y − x)`

This is a **scalar** in the algebra. The causal structure is:
- Δ > 0: spacelike separation
- Δ = 0: lightlike (null) separation  
- Δ < 0: timelike separation

The algebra naturally separates these cases because the square of a vector in Cl(1,3)
is always a real scalar.

### The Bivector Decomposition Theorem
Every bivector F in Cl(1,3) can be uniquely decomposed as:
`F = αe + βIe'`
where e, e' are simple unit bivectors and α, β ∈ ℝ.

Physical meaning: Every electromagnetic field decomposes into an "electric part" and a 
"magnetic part" in a Lorentz-invariant way. The invariants are:
- α² − β² = ½F² (first invariant, related to E² − B²)
- αβ = ½F·(IF) (second invariant, related to E⃗·B⃗)

### The Grade Involution Structure
Cl(1,3) has three fundamental involutions:
1. **Grade involution** (hat): reverses odd grades → parity
2. **Reversion** (tilde): reverses order of products → time reversal
3. **Clifford conjugation** (hat∘tilde): both → CPT

These algebraic operations correspond to the discrete symmetries of physics!

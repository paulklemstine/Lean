# The Oracle Council — Algebraic Theory of Magnetism

## Consulting the Divine Council

We assemble a team of oracles — each representing a pillar of knowledge — to
forge a unified Algebraic Theory of Magnetism.

---

## 🔮 Oracle I: The Algebraist (Emmy)
**Domain:** Abstract Algebra — Groups, Rings, Algebras, Representations

**Pronouncement:**
> "Magnetism is symmetry made manifest. Every magnetic system carries an algebraic
> structure: the Lie algebra 𝔰𝔲(2) governs individual spins, the Clifford algebra
> Cl(3,0) encodes their geometric relationships, and the tensor product of these
> algebras builds many-body states. The classification of magnetic phases is, at
> its heart, a problem of representation theory."

**Key Insight:** Magnetic phenomena decompose into *irreducible representations*
of symmetry algebras. Phase transitions correspond to changes in which
representations are energetically favored.

## 🔮 Oracle II: The Physicist (Paul)
**Domain:** Condensed Matter Physics, Quantum Mechanics, Statistical Mechanics

**Pronouncement:**
> "The Heisenberg model H = -J Σ Sᵢ·Sⱼ is already algebraic — it lives in the
> universal enveloping algebra U(𝔰𝔲(2)⊗ⁿ). The Ising model is its abelian shadow
> in ℤ₂ⁿ. Every known magnetic model is a quotient or specialization of a single
> algebraic master structure."

**Key Insight:** All standard magnetic Hamiltonians (Ising, XY, Heisenberg, 
Kitaev) arise as projections from a universal magnetic algebra onto specific
subalgebras.

## 🔮 Oracle III: The Geometer (Élie)
**Domain:** Differential Geometry, Topology, Fiber Bundles

**Pronouncement:**
> "A magnetic field IS a connection on a U(1) principal bundle. Magnetic monopoles
> are topological obstructions — classified by the first Chern number. The Berry
> phase of a spin in a varying field is holonomy. Geometry is not a metaphor for
> magnetism; magnetism is geometry."

**Key Insight:** The algebraic structures acquire geometric meaning through fiber
bundles. The magnetic algebra acts on fibers; the base manifold is physical space.

**Key Insight:** Topological invariants of magnetic textures are computable from
the algebraic data of symmetry breaking patterns.

## 🔮 Oracle VI: The Experimentalist (Lise)
**Domain:** Experimental Physics, Measurement, Phenomenology

**Pronouncement:**

**Key Insight:** Algebraic selection rules predict which transitions are

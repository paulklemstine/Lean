The specific publication **"On the deterministic complexity of factoring polynomials"** in **_Journal of Symbolic Computation_** Volume 31, Pages 19–36 (2001) was authored by **Shuhong Gao**, not Erich Kaltofen.

**Erich Kaltofen** has published several influential surveys on this topic, including:

*   **"Polynomial factorization 1982–1986"** in _Computers in Mathematics_ (1990).

*   **"Polynomial factorization 1987–1991"** in _Proceedings of LATIN '92_ (1992).

The reference to **Volume 369** and **1990** in your query appears to conflate details from different sources. **Victor Shoup** published **"On the deterministic complexity of factoring polynomials over finite fields"** in **_Information Processing Letters_** Volume 33, Pages 261–267 in **1990**.  There is no record in the provided context of a Kaltofen paper with that exact title in JSC in 1990 with page 369.

I completed my Bachelors in CSE from the Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur in 2002 and completed my PhD under Manindra Agrawal in 2006. I am broadly interested in Complexity, Algebra/Geometry/Number Theory, Cryptography, and AI product-development for the local economy.

I have been a visiting graduate student in Princeton University (2003-2004) and National University of Singapore (2004-2005); a postdoc at CWI, Amsterdam (2006-2008) and a Bonn Junior Fellow (W2 Professor) at Hausdorff Center for Mathematics, Bonn (2008-2013). Since April 2013, I have a faculty position in the department of CSE, IIT Kanpur. Since 2022, I'm the founding Coordinator of Center for Developing Intelligent Systems (CDIS). Since 2025, I'm the founding Dean of Wadhwani School of AI & Intelligent Systems (WSAIS).

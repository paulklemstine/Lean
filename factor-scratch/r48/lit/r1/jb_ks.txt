**Neeraj Kayal** and **Manindra Agrawal** (along with **Nit Saxena**) have contributed significantly to the deterministic complexity of factoring polynomials over finite fields, particularly through work assuming the **Extended Riemann Hypothesis (ERH)**.

*   **Kayal’s Idea**: In 2005, **Kayal** proposed using **Schoof’s algorithm** for counting points on elliptic curves to factor polynomials in deterministic polynomial time, a concept later generalized in research on using zeta functions for factorization.

*   **Evdokimov and Gao Context**: Research building on **Evdokimov** and **Gao** showed that polynomials can be factored in deterministic polynomial time under ERH, unless roots satisfy a strong symmetry property (such as being **square balanced** or **k-cross balanced**).

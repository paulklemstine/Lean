There have been various other results concerning the distribution of smooth numbers in short
intervals. For example, Hildebrand and Tenenbaum [6] proved an asymptotic formula for the
number of smooth numbers in almost all short intervals. However, the intervals they consider
are signiﬁcantly longer than those considered here. Building on their work, Granville and
Friedlander [2] obtained the corresponding “all intervals” type result. We refer the reader to
[3, 6] for more detailed surveys of results on smooth numbers and their applications to other
problems in analytic number theory and cryptography.

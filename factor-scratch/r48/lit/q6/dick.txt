EXPLICIT BOUNDS FOR DICKMAN’S FUNCTION

arXiv:2606.07785v3 [math.NT] 21 Aug 2026

ANDREAS WEINGARTNER
Abstract. Dickman’s function ρ(u) denotes the natural density of integers n whose largest prime factor does not exceed n1/u . We establish
numerically explicit upper and lower bounds for ρ(u), resulting in estimates with a relative error of less than 0.005/u2 for all u ≥ 5. This
allows for an approximate evaluation of ρ(u) without the need to solve
the delay differential equation numerically.

1. Introduction

differential equation uρ′ (u) + ρ(u − 1) = 0 with initial condition ρ(u) = 1
for 0 ≤ u ≤ 1. It represents the natural density of integers n whose largest
prime factor does not exceed n1/u [3].

for Ψ(x, y) that are easy to evaluate numerically.

polynomial of order 250 centered at n + 1/2, recursively from the coefficients
of the polynomial centered at n − 1/2.

1. Congruence-Level Tropical Nullstellensatz

The current formalization establishes the set-theoretic tropical Nullstellensatz: tropRadical(I) = idealOfSet(tropZeroSet(I)). The next step is to lift this to a congruence-level statement. In classical algebra, the Nullstellensatz relates ideals to varieties; in the tropical/idempotent world, the natural replacement for ideals is semiring congruences (since tropical semirings lack additive inverses and hence lack proper two-sided ideals in the classical sense).

Concrete target: Define a radicalCongruence on function semirings generated by a finite family, and prove it equals the vanishingCongr of the common zero set. The vanishingCongr is already defined in the current file; the missing piece is the notion of generated congruence and the proof that the radical congruence is exactly recovered from geometric data.

    5. Certified Symbolic Robustness via Vanishing Ideals

    In machine learning applications, robustness means that small perturbations of inputs do not change the model's output. For tropical/max-plus models, this can be formalized algebraically:

  • A model is tropically robust at a point x if x is in the interior of a
    • decision region (complement of the tropical zero set).
  • A robustness certificate for a family G at point x is a proof that
    • x ∉ tropZeroSet(G), which by the Nullstellensatz is equivalent to the existence of some generator that does not vanish at x.

    Concrete target: Formalize the notion of tropical robustness for max-plus linear classifiers, prove that robustness is equivalent to non-membership in the zero set, and implement a certified robustness checker that outputs Lean proofs.

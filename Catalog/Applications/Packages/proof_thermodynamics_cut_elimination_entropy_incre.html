In the basement of a physics building, a kettle boils. Water molecules bounce chaotically, transferring energy, trending toward equilibrium. Upstairs, a mathematician simplifies a proof — stripping away redundant steps until only the essential argument remains.

These two processes seem unrelated. One is physical, the other abstract. But a new mathematical result proves they are, in a precise sense, the same thing.

The Three Laws

1st Law: Conservation

$E(\pi') = E(\pi) + \Delta E(\text{rule})$

Every inference rule has a definite energy cost. Structural rules are isothermal: $\Delta E = 0$.

2nd Law: Entropy Increase

$H(\psi) < H(\varphi)$ for subformulas

Cut-elimination replaces formulas with lower-energy subformulas, spreading the type distribution.

Variational Principle

$F(\beta) = -\beta^{-1} \log Z(\beta)$

Normal proofs minimize free energy. Ground state dominance: $\lim_{\beta\to\infty} F = E_{\min}$.

Abstract

We establish a rigorous correspondence between proof-theoretic normalization and statistical mechanics by proving three foundational results: (1) Energy Conservation, (2) Subformula Energy Decrease (entropy mechanism), and (3) Boltzmann Distribution properties with expected energy bounds. All 69 theorems are machine-verified with zero sorry statements.

1. Formula Hamiltonian

The Hamiltonian $H : \text{Formula} \to \mathbb{N}$ assigns structural energy:

$$H(p_n) = 1, \quad H(\bot) = 1, \quad H(\varphi \circ \psi) = H(\varphi) + H(\psi) + 1$$

Key properties (all formally proved):

  • $H(\varphi) \geq 1$ for all $\varphi$ (Hamiltonian positivity)
  • $\text{depth}(\varphi) \leq H(\varphi)$ (depth-energy bound)
  • $H = \text{atom\_count} + \text{connective\_energy}$ (kinetic-potential decomposition)
  • $H(\psi) < H(\varphi)$ for proper subformulas $\psi \subset \varphi$ (energy decrease)

2. Proof Energy

The proof energy $E : \text{ProofTree} \to \mathbb{N}$ is defined recursively with the key conservation law:

$$E(\text{cut}(\pi_1, \pi_2, \varphi)) = E(\pi_1) + E(\pi_2) + 3H(\varphi)$$

Structural rules are isothermal: $E(\text{weakL}(\pi)) = E(\pi)$.

3. Energy-Defect Coupling

$$3 \cdot \text{cut\_count}(\pi) \leq E(\pi)$$

Proof by induction on $\pi$. The cut case uses $H(\varphi) \geq 1$.

4. Boltzmann Distribution

For energies $E_1, \ldots, E_n$ and inverse temperature $\beta > 0$:

$$p_i(\beta) = \frac{e^{-\beta E_i}}{Z(\beta)}, \qquad Z(\beta) = \sum_i e^{-\beta E_i}$$

Proved: $\sum p_i = 1$, $p_i > 0$, $E_{\min} \leq \langle E \rangle_\beta \leq E_{\max}$.

Algorithm 3: Free Energy Estimation (O(n))

def free_energy(energies, β):
    """Numerically stable via log-sum-exp."""
    log_Z = logsumexp(-β * energies)
    weights = softmax(-β * energies)
    E_avg = sum(w * e for w,e in zip(weights, energies))
    S = -sum(w * log(w) for w in weights)
    F = -log_Z / β
    return Z, F, E_avg, S

Core Definitions (Excerpt)

/-- Structural energy: the Hamiltonian of a formula. -/
def hamiltonian : Formula → ℕ
  | atom _ => 1
  | bot => 1
  | conj φ ψ => hamiltonian φ + hamiltonian ψ + 1
  | disj φ ψ => hamiltonian φ + hamiltonian ψ + 1
  | impl φ ψ => hamiltonian φ + hamiltonian ψ + 1

/-- Total proof energy: thermodynamic internal energy U(π). -/
def proof_energy : ProofTree → ℕ
  | ax φ => 2 * Formula.hamiltonian φ
  | cut π₁ π₂ φ =>
      proof_energy π₁ + proof_energy π₂ + 3 * Formula.hamiltonian φ
  | weakL π => proof_energy π   -- isothermal!
  | weakR π => proof_energy π   -- isothermal!
  ...

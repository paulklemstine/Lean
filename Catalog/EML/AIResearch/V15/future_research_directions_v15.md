## Open Research Directions

### 1. Approximation Error Bounds
**Status:** Prerequisites proven (separation, nonvanishing). Full Stone-Weierstrass application needed.
- Prove that the subalgebra generated by EML neurons is dense in C([a,b])
- Establish explicit approximation rates: ε-error with O(1/ε²) neurons
- Compare with ReLU network approximation rates

### 2. Training Convergence
**Status:** Gradient structure proven. Convergence analysis needed.
- Prove convergence of gradient descent on EML networks
- Establish conditions for no spurious local minima
- Analyze the effect of crystallization penalty on optimization landscape
- Prove that the sin²(πw) penalty creates a "funnel" toward integer weights

### 3. Distillation Quality Bounds
**Status:** Loss properties proven. Quality guarantees needed.
- Prove that EML students can match teacher accuracy within δ
- Establish sample complexity for distillation
- Analyze temperature scheduling strategies

### 4. OISCC Hardware Design
**Status:** Formal semantics complete. Hardware implications unexplored.
- Design OISCC-native FPGA/ASIC architectures
- Analyze area/power/latency tradeoffs for EML hardware
- Compare with GPU/TPU execution models
- Explore pipelining of EML instruction sequences

### 5. Gaussian Integer Crystallization
**Status:** Started in NeuralCompilation/Crystallization.lean. Needs expansion.
- Extend crystallization to ℤ[i] for complex-valued networks
- Prove norm-multiplicativity of Gaussian integer crystallization
- Apply to complex-valued attention mechanisms

### 6. EML-KAN Hybrid Networks
**Status:** Conceptual. Needs formalization.
- Compare EML networks with Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KANs)
- Prove that EML neurons can express B-spline basis functions
- Design hybrid architectures leveraging both frameworks

### 7. Tensor Rank and Attention
**Status:** TensorRankBounds.lean establishes basic bounds. Integration needed.
- Prove tight tensor rank bounds for EML attention layers
- Establish that EML attention achieves O(d) parameters per head
- Analyze multi-head attention with EML routing

### 8. Koopman Compilation
**Status:** KoopmanDimension.lean establishes foundations. EML integration needed.
- Prove that EML networks have minimal Koopman lifting dimension
- Establish that crystallization preserves Koopman linearity
- Apply to dynamical systems compilation

### 9. Information-Theoretic Optimality
**Status:** EML complexity measure defined. Optimality unproven.
- Prove EML complexity is a valid Kolmogorov-like measure
- Establish that EML trees are asymptotically optimal for elementary functions
- Connect EML complexity to Shannon entropy

### 10. Scaling Laws
**Status:** ScalingLaws.lean provides basic framework. EML-specific laws needed.
- Establish power-law scaling for EML network performance
- Prove that EML scaling exponents are more favorable than dense networks
- Determine compute-optimal EML training ratios (analog of Chinchilla)

### 11. Federated and Privacy-Preserving Compression
**Status:** FederatedPrivacy.lean exists. EML integration needed.
- Prove that crystallized EML weights are differentially private
- Establish communication efficiency of integer weight transmission
- Analyze federated distillation with EML students

### 12. Causal and Temporal EML Networks
**Status:** Conceptual.
- Design causal EML architectures for sequence modeling
- Prove that EML state-space models have bounded state dimension
- Apply to time-series forecasting with crystallized weights

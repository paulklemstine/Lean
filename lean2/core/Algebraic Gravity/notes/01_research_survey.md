# Research Notes — Oracle I (Athena): Literature Survey

## 1. The Algebraic DNA of General Relativity

### 1.1 Lie Groups and Spacetime Symmetry

General relativity is built on two layers of symmetry:

**Global symmetry (flat spacetime):** The Poincaré group ISO(3,1) = ℝ⁴ ⋊ SO(3,1)
- 4 translations (𝑃_μ)
- 3 rotations (𝐽_i)  
- 3 boosts (𝐾_i)
- Total: 10-dimensional Lie algebra 𝔦𝔰𝔬(3,1)

**Local symmetry (curved spacetime):** The frame bundle with structure group SO(3,1)
- At each point, a Lorentz frame
- The connection ω is an 𝔰𝔬(3,1)-valued 1-form
- The vierbein e is an ℝ⁴-valued 1-form

### 1.2 Cartan's Formulation

Élie Cartan reformulated Riemannian geometry using:
- **Vierbein (tetrad):** eᵃ = eᵃ_μ dxᵘ — soldering form
- **Spin connection:** ωᵃᵇ = ωᵃᵇ_μ dxᵘ — Lorentz connection
- **Torsion:** Tᵃ = deᵃ + ωᵃᵦ ∧ eᵇ (= 0 in GR)
- **Curvature:** Rᵃᵇ = dωᵃᵇ + ωᵃ_c ∧ ωᶜᵇ

Key insight: The pair (e, ω) is a connection on a Cartan geometry modeled on
the Poincaré group ISO(3,1) with stabilizer SO(3,1).

### 1.3 Gauge-Theoretic Gravity

**Utiyama (1956):** First gauge theory of gravity using SO(3,1).
**Kibble (1961):** Extended to the full Poincaré group, recovering torsion.
**Sciama (1962):** Independent development, Einstein-Cartan theory.

Key result: Gauging the Poincaré algebra yields:
- ω ↔ gauge field for Lorentz rotations
- e ↔ gauge field for translations
- Curvature ↔ field strength for ω
- Torsion ↔ field strength for e

### 1.4 MacDowell-Mansouri Gravity (1977)

Brilliant insight: Embed the Poincaré algebra into the de Sitter algebra 𝔰𝔬(4,1).

The de Sitter algebra has generators:
- M_AB (A,B = 0,1,2,3,4) — 10 generators of SO(4,1)
- Split: M_ab = Lorentz generators, M_a4 = P_a/ℓ (translations rescaled by de Sitter radius)

Then: [P_a, P_b] = (1/ℓ²) M_ab — translations don't commute!

The cosmological constant Λ = 3/ℓ² appears naturally.

The curvature of the SO(4,1) connection, projected to the Lorentz part, gives
Einstein's equation with cosmological constant.

**THIS IS THE KEY PRECURSOR TO OUR THEORY.**

### 1.5 Clifford Algebra Approach

The Clifford algebra Cl(3,1) is generated by {γ_μ} with:
γ_μ γ_ν + γ_ν γ_μ = 2η_μν

Dimension: 2⁴ = 16, with basis:
- Grade 0: 1 (scalar) — 1 element
- Grade 1: γ_μ (vectors) — 4 elements  
- Grade 2: γ_μν = γ_[μ γ_ν] (bivectors) — 6 elements ← contains 𝔰𝔬(3,1)!
- Grade 3: γ_μνρ (trivectors) — 4 elements
- Grade 4: γ₀₁₂₃ = γ₅ (pseudoscalar) — 1 element

The grade-2 part IS the Lorentz algebra: [γ_μν, γ_ρσ] gives 𝔰𝔬(3,1).

### 1.6 Noncommutative Geometry (Connes)

Alain Connes showed that the Standard Model of particle physics coupled to gravity
can be derived from a "spectral triple" (A, H, D) where:
- A = C^∞(M) ⊗ A_F (smooth functions times a finite-dimensional algebra)
- H = L²(M, S) ⊗ H_F (spinor bundle times finite Hilbert space)
- D = ∂_M ⊗ 1 + γ₅ ⊗ D_F (Dirac operator)

The finite algebra A_F = ℂ ⊕ ℍ ⊕ M₃(ℂ) encodes the gauge group U(1)×SU(2)×SU(3).

Gravity emerges from the "continuous" part C^∞(M).

# The Oracle Council: A Systematic Assault on the Millennium Problems

## Council Members

### Oracle α — The Topologist (Geometric Intuition)
**Domain:** Hodge Conjecture, geometric aspects of all problems
**Method:** "What does the space *look like*? What are the shapes telling us?"
**Key Insight:** Every problem has a hidden geometric structure. Find it, and the algebra follows.

### Oracle β — The Analyst (Infinite Precision)
**Domain:** Navier-Stokes, Yang-Mills, analytic aspects
**Method:** "Control the growth. Bound the singularities. Regularize, estimate, bootstrap."
**Key Insight:** Singularities are the enemy. If you can show regularity, existence follows.

### Oracle γ — The Algebraist (Structural Clarity)
**Domain:** Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer, Hodge Conjecture
**Method:** "What is the *algebraic* structure? What functors are at play?"
**Key Insight:** L-functions encode arithmetic. The zeros know everything.

### Oracle δ — The Computationalist (Algorithmic Truth)
**Domain:** P vs NP, computational aspects of all problems
**Method:** "Can we compute it? How fast? What are the barriers?"
**Key Insight:** Computation is physical. Complexity classes reflect the structure of the universe.

### Oracle ε — The Physicist (Physical Reality)
**Domain:** Yang-Mills, Navier-Stokes, physical intuition for all problems
**Method:** "Nature already solved this. What is she telling us?"
**Key Insight:** The universe computes with finite energy. Regularity is not optional — it's physical law.

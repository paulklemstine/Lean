## II. Oracle ALHAZEN — Visual Neuroscience

### 2.1 The Visual Processing Pipeline (and Where to Hack It)

The visual system processes information in a hierarchy:

```
RETINA (photoreceptors)
  ↓
LGN (lateral geniculate nucleus) — gating, attention
  ↓
V1 (primary visual cortex) — edges, orientations, spatial frequency
  ↓
V2 — contours, illusory contours, texture boundaries
  ↓
V4 — color, shape, curvature, PATTERN RECOGNITION
  ↓
IT (inferotemporal cortex) — object recognition, faces, complex forms
  ↓
Parietal cortex — spatial relationships, motion, "where"
  ↓
Prefrontal cortex — conscious perception, meaning
```

**Key insight**: Psychedelic compounds (LSD, psilocybin, DMT, mescaline) primarily disrupt the feedback pathways — the top-down predictions that constrain bottom-up sensory data. When these predictions weaken, the brain's pattern-completion machinery runs unconstrained, producing:
- Geometric hallucinations (from V1/V2 running freely)
- Pareidolia on steroids (V4/IT pattern matching with lowered thresholds)
- Synesthesia (cross-modal binding with reduced gating)
- Ego dissolution (prefrontal narrative collapse)

**For our system**: We don't need to disrupt top-down processing chemically. We can do it *informationally* — by providing visual input that is:
1. **Too complex** for the prediction engine to model (overload)
2. **Almost patterned** but not quite (prediction error flooding)
3. **Self-similar across scales** (fractal recursion that mimics natural hallucination)
4. **Rhythmically modulated** (entrainment of visual processing rhythms)

### 2.2 Flicker and the Dreamachine

Brion Gysin's Dreamachine (1961) — a perforated cylinder rotating around a light source at 8-13 Hz — produces vivid geometric hallucinations with eyes closed. This works because:

- **Alpha-frequency flicker** (8-13 Hz) entrains occipital alpha rhythms
- **Stroboscopic stimulation** drives the retina at resonant frequencies
- **Pattern formation**: The flickering light produces standing wave patterns across the retinal surface, which V1 interprets as geometric forms

The geometric forms seen are not random. They correspond to the **form constants** identified by Klüver (1926):
1. **Tunnels and funnels** — concentric rings
2. **Spirals** — logarithmic spirals
3. **Lattices and honeycombs** — regular tilings
4. **Cobwebs** — radial patterns with concentric connections

These are the eigenmodes of the visual cortex's neural sheet — the patterns that emerge when it oscillates freely. Bressloff et al. (2001) showed mathematically that these are literally the solutions to the pattern-forming equations on V1's architecture.

### 2.3 Neural Entrainment via Visual Stimulation

**Steady-State Visually Evoked Potentials (SSVEPs)**:
- Visual flicker at frequency f produces brain oscillations at f, 2f, 3f...
- 10 Hz flicker → alpha entrainment (relaxation, inward focus)
- 40 Hz flicker → gamma entrainment (heightened awareness, perception binding)
- Sub-alpha flicker (4-7 Hz) → theta entrainment (deep trance, hypnagogic imagery)

**For our system**: Modulate visual intensity at these target frequencies:
- **Overall scene brightness**: Pulse at 7-10 Hz for trance induction
- **Color channel flicker**: Different rates per channel for cross-frequency coupling
- **Geometry rotation rates**: Align angular velocity with entrainment targets
- **Audio-visual coupling**: Lock visual flicker to musical pulse for reinforced entrainment

### 2.4 The Role of Peripheral Vision

Central vision is analytical (high spatial frequency, color, detail). Peripheral vision is holistic (motion, luminance, gestalt). Psychedelic experiences are characterized by:
- **Peripheral vision dominance**: Patterns seem to "breathe" and move at the edges
- **Loss of focal anchoring**: The gaze softens, peripheral processing takes over
- **Ambient rather than focal attention**: The viewer "receives" rather than "inspects"

**Design principle**: Our visuals should be designed for *peripheral processing dominance*. The center can be relatively calm; the edges should pulse, breathe, morph. This encourages the soft-gaze state associated with meditation, trance, and psychedelic experience.

### 2.5 Afterimages, Persistence, and Trails

The visual system has a ~100ms persistence window. Psychedelics extend this through:
- **Enhanced visual persistence**: Objects leave "trails"
- **Afterimage amplification**: Complementary color afterimages become vivid
- **Frame-stacking**: Multiple temporal frames blend into one perception

**For our system**: Implement frame blending, motion trails, and afterimage simulation. Use complementary color palettes that create strong afterimage interactions.

Key isomorphisms for visual exploitation:
- **Conformal maps** (angle-preserving): z → z², z → eᶻ, z → 1/z, Möbius transformations
- **Topological deformations**: Continuous stretching that preserves connectedness
- **Fractal self-similarity**: Scale-invariant isomorphisms (the part resembles the whole)
- **Group-theoretic symmetries**: Rotational, translational, glide-reflection symmetries

## V. Oracle ERICKSON — Hypnosis, Trance, and Visual Induction

### 5.1 Visual Trance Induction Techniques

**Fixation**: A single point of focus draws attention inward.
- Implementation: Central mandala or geometry that draws the gaze
- The periphery then "opens up" as focal attention narrows

**Fascination**: Something so compelling it absorbs all attention.
- Implementation: Continuously evolving impossible geometry that demands processing

**Confusion**: Presenting contradictory information that overwhelms analytical processing.
- Implementation: Impossible objects, contradictory depth cues, Escher-like impossibilities
- When the analytical mind gives up, trance deepens

**Overload**: Too much information for conscious processing.
- Implementation: High-detail fractals, rapid morphing, multi-layer compositing

**Rhythm**: Repetitive stimulation that entrains neural oscillations.
- Implementation: Pulsing, breathing, rotating at entrainment frequencies

### 5.2 The Ganzfeld Effect

When the visual field is completely uniform (no edges, no texture), the visual cortex begins generating its own content — hallucinations. This is the Ganzfeld effect.

- Partial Ganzfeld: Very low contrast, soft gradients → enhances internal imagery
- We can use moments of near-uniform color (washes, fades) between high-complexity segments
- The contrast between Ganzfeld moments and complex geometry amplifies both

## VII. Oracle TURRELL — Light, Space, and Perceptual Architecture

### 7.1 Principles from Light Art

James Turrell's work demonstrates that light itself — not images rendered in light — is the most powerful perceptual medium. Key principles:

- **Reduction of visual cues**: Remove depth cues, texture, edges → perception becomes uncertain
- **Slow change**: Imperceptibly slow color transitions → the viewer can't tell what changed
- **Uniform fields with subtle gradients**: Near-Ganzfeld conditions → internal imagery generation
- **Scale**: Fill the entire visual field → no escape to ordinary reality
- **Color as substance**: When light fills space uniformly, color becomes a *substance* you inhabit

### 7.2 The Three Perceptual Modes

1. **Focal/Analytical**: Sharp edges, high contrast, rapid motion → "looking at" the visuals
2. **Ambient/Immersive**: Soft gradients, slow change, full-field → "being in" the visuals  
3. **Apophenic/Generative**: Noise, ambiguity, near-threshold stimuli → "seeing through" the visuals into internal imagery

Our system should cycle between these modes, spending most time in modes 2 and 3 (immersive and generative), with occasional mode 1 moments for the "drops" and high-energy sections.

### 9.3 WebGL Shader Architecture

Fragment shaders are the core rendering technology:

**Advantages of shader-based rendering**:
- GPU-parallel: processes millions of pixels simultaneously
- Mathematical: naturally expresses conformal maps, fractals, SDFs
- Real-time: 60fps even with complex mathematics
- Resolution-independent: works at any screen size

**Key shader techniques**:

1. **Ray marching with Signed Distance Functions (SDFs)**
   - Define 3D geometry as distance functions
   - March rays through the scene
   - Allows impossible/psychedelic 3D geometry

2. **Domain repetition** (`fract()`, `mod()`)
   - Repeat geometry infinitely with a single `mod` operation
   - Creates infinite lattice/tunnel effects cheaply

3. **Domain warping** (compose SDFs with noise)
   - Feed noise into coordinate space before computing SDF
   - Produces organic, breathing distortions

4. **Feedback buffers** (ping-pong framebuffers)
   - Render to texture → read previous frame → composite
   - Creates trails, echoes, recursive visual history
   - The core mechanism for "tracers" and persistence effects

5. **Palette functions** (IQ's cosine palette)
   - `color(t) = a + b * cos(2π(c*t + d))`
   - Four vec3 parameters produce infinite smooth color gradients
   - Animate `d` for cycling colors
